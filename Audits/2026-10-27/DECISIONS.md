## D1. The last line comes first

"She didn't count them." must appear in the trilogy **only** as the last line of Book 3 ch 86.

- Reword the earlier verbatim uses: ch 37 lines 320 and 322, ch 50 line 215, ch 63 line 249.
- Reword the close variants: ch 11 line 151 ("She didn't count.") and ch 47 line 138 ("She didn't count anything.").
- Maren may still leave something uncounted. She just mustn't say so in that sentence, or anything close to it. Better still, show it: she watches; she lets the number go.
- Other characters' "He didn't count things" lines (chs 64, 72, 78) may stay, because they are Calder's and about Calder.

## D2. Book 3's mirrored map is fixed in the prose

One rule: **going east from the Wend, you are in Saltreach.**

- Hearthmere lies west of the Wend. That includes Hallowmere, the Greenwold and the Candle Downs.
- **The Ysse is the Plains' river** (the Wyldmarch's own). It is not Hearthmere's border.
- **The border Rowan shuts** is the **upper Wend at the Greenwold road's ford**, where Rowan's post stands (ch 10).
- **Tova's clans** cross the Ysse fords off the burning Plains, then come west across Saltreach's empty scrub and moor (the "eastern march" in the draft becomes this country) to the Wend.
- **Greyhold to Hallowmere** is *west* (and a little north).
- **Hallowmere's people** go *east* toward the Wend and Greyhold, and cross at Liss's ferry (ch 41).
- **From Coldtarn,** the Mere lies *west*.
- The breath's glass line runs from the Thornwood west, with every station before the camp *east* of the Wend.
- Chapters to change: 4, 7, 10, 11, 23, 41–45, 49–53, and the Part 1 and Part 4 ledgers.

- **Wendmouth** is about a hundred leagues south of the hub, as in Books 1–2.

- **The market:**

  - The Ostrey barge is stopped a few leagues below the hub.
  - "Twelve leagues" becomes "ninety leagues" or similar wherever it means the hub to the sea.
- **Chs 74 and 75:** sights of "the mouth" from the hub become far-sense, through Orrun or Aurveth, or are cut.
- **Ch 76:**

  - From the *Kittiwake*'s masthead at the Staithe, Kit sees the Lady and the Bell rise and go down together **four leagues below the Staithe, where the sea has come up the river**.
  - It stays her sighting alone, and it stays ambiguous.
  - Every "off the Wend's mouth" in the docs becomes "in the lower Wend, where the sea came up the drained bed below the Staithe". Idony's "both under the sea" (82) still holds.

- **Wording:**
