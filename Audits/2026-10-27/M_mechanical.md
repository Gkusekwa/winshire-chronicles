# Mechanical checks (all 253 chapters, prose outside the notes blocks)

- **Headings and notes:** every chapter opens `# Chapter N: Title` matching its file name (ch 26's "Why Me?" keeps its question mark only in the heading; the file name can't hold one). Every chapter has a notes block.
- **POV order:** correct in all three books. Book 1 alternates S/F strictly. In Books 2 and 3, S and F alternate strictly with each other, and the other POVs never come two in a row.
- **Reveal rules in prose:**
  - "Council of Immortals" / "Immortals": 0.
  - "seventh relic": 0.
  - "elemental": 0.
  - "Book 1/2/3" in prose: 0.
- **Em dashes:** 0 in all three books.
- **"the way" similes** ("the way you / a / the …"):
  - Book 1: 179 (about 6 per 10,000 words).
  - Book 2: 77.
  - Book 3: 2.

  Book 1's audit cut these by about a third, and Book 3 was drafted with none, so Books 1 and 2 read differently from Book 3 here.
- **"Not X. Y." fragments** (a line starting "Not …." followed by a sentence):
  - Book 1: 49.
  - Book 2: 143.
  - Book 3: 117.

  This is the strongest tic left in Books 2 and 3.
- **"It wasn't X. It was Y."** (rough match, some false positives): Book 1 14, Book 2 14, Book 3 7.
- **Style-guide banned words:** "in that moment" once (B1 ch 29 L162). Every "the weight of" is literal.
- **"almost smiled":** Book 1 6, Book 2 6, Book 3 3.
