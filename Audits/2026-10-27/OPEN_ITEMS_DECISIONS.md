*The author's instruction: "compose and close all open items". These decisions were drafted by Claude under that instruction and are binding on the fixers. Trimming stays held on the author's word ("no trimming just yet"). Publishing is settled: ebooks only (author, Oct 2026).*

## Book 1

- **O1. Haverly.** He bonded Kesh at fifteen at his family's mews, before he came to Greyhold. He arrived two years ago. Rewrite ch 61:91, and any line implying he was at a school at fifteen, to match.
- **O2. Ch 8, Coldtarn** now lies about 5 leagues above Varrhold. From Harrow's End that is about 12 leagues by the Way and the beck. Make the journey take two days up and two days back.
- **O3. Ch 14.** Ulric comes down the Way from the Ram's Head side as the Atlas allows. The fixer checks the Atlas and adjusts the wording only.
- **O4. The lion's attacks.** There are three in canon: shore, stream and tower. Each count must match the number of attacks that have happened by that chapter. Fix ch 23 and the ch 29 notes to match.
- **O5. Ch 45.** Cut the new hint "As if somebody had had it written already". No payoff is planned.
- **O6. Ch 68.** The feint stays. Add one line from the twins' side: the feint had to cost something, or the gate wouldn't believe it. That explains the drawer's two releases in ch 69.
- **O18. Ch 50, Calder's confession to Bel, becomes a scene** in place of the summary. He says why he kept quiet about the chain: naming it meant naming what was coming for the farm and for Wren. Use the existing staging. No new facts.

## Book 3

- **O10. The two Staithes.** The drovers' ford eight leagues below the hub is renamed **the Sheepford**, from ch 58 on, in prose, notes, ledgers and plan. Tolly's Staithe at Greyhold, and "the staithe" in chs 4, 7 and 11, stay as they are. Ch 60's line telling the two apart becomes: "the Sheepford, the drovers' summer ford eight leagues below the hub, that the barges called the Staithe until Kit named it again". Keep it short.
- **O11. The six roads (chs 77–78).** The six columns come up out of the Vale by different roads, and all of them join the Drover's Way **between Harrow's End and the Fellwood**. Orrun lies across the Way at the Fellwood's foot, where the roads have become one, not at Harrow's End. Restage with the smallest changes.
- **O12. The Bell's move (ch 76).** Add one line of Kit's guesswork: the Bell came up the drained bed in the night after the river ran backward, following the water it had always followed.
- **O13. Flight time.** Coldtarn is about 17 leagues from the hub, about two hours for a Titan-class dragon. The prose's "half a day" becomes "two hours", or the like.
- **O14. New numbers.**
  - Maren's 412 paces for the Mother and 38 for Orrun are **approved** as her private counts.
  - The **twenty-nine nights** on the ketch are checked against the days and corrected if wrong.
  - "**Six thousand and twelve**" at the Sheepford is checked against any later total and reconciled.
- **O15. New beats approved:** Odo's cracked runner giving at the last pull (ch 71), the Rusk sentence (ch 58), and the Nessa beat (ch 60).
- **O16. The Ashlings** are approved as they stand in the Atlas data, reaching west to Cotter's Cross.
- **O21. Ulric's death is shown, not reported.** Dramatise it in the chapter where it happens, in that chapter's POV. If that POV couldn't witness it, keep the report but give it a short, concrete account from a witness, Sigge, in scene. No new facts.
- **O22. More room on the far shore.** Before each gift (chs 37, 39), add one short beat, 150–300 words, where the bonded character and the beast face what's coming, in voice.
