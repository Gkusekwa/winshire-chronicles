# Response to the two developmental reviews (27 Oct 2026)

*Two outside reviews arrived during the fix pass: one on Book 1 and the trilogy's architecture, and one on Books 2–3. This file records what was done with each point. The author ruled "no trimming just yet", so every cut to word count or structure is noted below and left for later.*

## Already fixed in this pass (it agrees with the audit)

These were all in the 27 Oct audit and are applied (see `DECISIONS.md`):
- Corvin's chain-words now need his hand on the iron (Book 2 chs 80 and 83).
- "On a roof" now reads "in a barley field" (Book 2 chs 86 and 88).
- Wynn is a man throughout, and Sorrow lives; the hound that dies is another knight's.
- "She didn't count them." appears only as the last line.
- The mirrored map is corrected: west of the Wend is Hearthmere, east is Saltreach.
- Liss moves her ferry to the eddy.
- The lower Wend is about ninety-five leagues.
- "The seventh" is never used for the relic.
- Calder can write in Book 3.
- Orrun has two shadows before the gift.

## Improved now, as the reviewers asked

- **Book 2 ch 84, the climax, is expanded**, with sensory weight in the vault, the relic going home and the five answering. It was the shortest chapter in the trilogy.
- **The archive-bird gets one quiet scene with Idony before it dies** (Book 2), so its loss lands as the loss of a mentor.
- **Book 3 ch 35, the Hall of Light:** Maren and Calder's conversation is deepened, and the fading of the Grey Mother and the Wayfarer lands as a bereavement.
- **Kit's "No price" (Book 3 ch 79)** is checked and, where thin, tied to Jory and to what she has seen.

## Adjusted

- **"Cut the 'Not X. Y.' pattern by 60%" / "eliminate at least half".** This pass cut about a third across all three books. Going further is a line-editing pass best done with the author reading aloud (Style Guide, "Process"). It is the first item on the line-edit list below.
- **"Refer to the relic as 'the sixth stone'."** Canon calls it the control relic or the sixth relic. The prose now uses "the control relic", "the relic" or "the coal in its back", never a number.
- **"Ensure Book 1 never shows Calder reading unaided."** Agreed. The audit found no Book 1 breach. Book 2 carries the literacy arc and Book 3 now honours it.

## Commented on, not acted on

- **Book 1 structural trims to about 250,000 words:** condensing the Wendmouth chapters (32–38), merging the island visits (21–29), speeding up 41–48. **Held by the author's ruling ("no trimming just yet").** The audit agrees that Part Three is the slackest stretch of Book 1, so this is worth doing in a later revision.
- **Maren and Calder meet only at ch 78 in Book 1.** This is the series' design, and the echoes do the connecting work. No change. The reviewer's point about stamina is real, but it is better met by trimming later than by an earlier meeting, which would break the apart rule's logic.
- **More agency for Calder before ch 50, and more of the chain's guilt in chs 4, 10, 16 and 24.** The chain already recurs in his pocket (chs 10, 12, 20) as a weight he notices. Adding more beats is a revision choice for the author. Suggested if trimming happens, so that the cut chapters carry more of it.
- **Cracks in Maren's stoicism (chs 25–29) and more of Tobin's discontent early on.** These are good notes for the author's own pass. They need new beats, not fixes.
- **Distinct dialogue voices** (Tobin hurried, Liss warm, Cassian seductive). A real risk in a long book. Best handled in the author's read-aloud pass. Flagged in the line-edit list below.
- **Two editions: an unabridged ebook and a simplified print edition.** **Settled by the author (Oct 2026): one edition only, never split, the same text in every format.** `Publishing.md`, the README, the Style Guide, the Tales plan and the Series Outline are updated. The reviewer's trim to about 250,000 words is held ("no trimming just yet"). How the full length fits print is now an open publishing question.
- **Book 2's front-loading** (Part One averages about 4,900 words a chapter, Part Seven about 2,200). Expanding ch 84 helps. A full rebalance means trimming Part One, which is held.
- **Maren waits in the wings in Book 2's Part Five (Sarovel).** Noted for the author. Her Sarovel chapters (59, 64, 66) carry the Grace failing, but could carry more of its human cost.
