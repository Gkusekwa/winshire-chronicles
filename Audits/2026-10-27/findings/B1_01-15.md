# Findings: Book 1, chapters 1–15
Reviewer summary: Chapters 1–15 are clean on the settled rules. Maren and Calder cast nothing, and nothing breaks a reveal rule: no Titan by name, no Council, no elementals, no book numbers, no em dashes. The day count holds from the fair (day 0) to the night of day 5, and the A1/APPLIED fixes are in place. What's left is mostly plot logic and geography: a reply that comes back from Ashford too fast (5), Teo's missing escort and the direction Ulric's men ride at the ford (14), and sightlines from Upper Rush (2, 4). There are also a few age and notes slips, and some repeated chapter shapes.

## Critical
None found.

## Major

### [B1-05-a] Rowan's reply confirms an Ashford licence in under an hour
- Where: Book 1 ch 5, lines 139 and 217
- Quote: "They waited at the bend for most of an hour" / "The magistrate at Ashford confirms it was issued at the request of the Millbrook carriers' guild"
- Problem: Kesh's round trip to Greyhold takes under an hour, and Ashford is 19 leagues west of Greyhold. Rowan can't have had the magistrate confirm anything in that time. Pell, who reads everything twice, would notice. Readers will too, unless the speed is meant to show that Greyhold already knew about the licence, and nothing plants that.
- Evidence: Bible/Atlas.md:32 (Greyhold to Ashford, 19 leagues straight, 24 by road); ch 5:139.
- Fix: Make it a record already held, which also sharpens the seal thread: "The licence is genuine. Greyhold has the magistrate's copy; it came under his seal three days ago, at the request of the Millbrook carriers' guild, and the men holding it are guild road wardens. We're to let them be."

### [B1-14-a] Teo's own men never arrive, then turn up at dusk
- Where: Book 1 ch 14, notes line 11; prose lines 48–50 and 248
- Quote (notes): "His six men were to follow at mid-morning." / (prose) "They brought Teo up to the hold at dusk, on a hurdle, with his own men walking beside it"
- Problem: In the notes, Teo's escort should have reached the ford by mid-morning, before Calder arrived "an hour before noon" and found the ford empty. The prose never says where they were or how they came to be carrying him at dusk. Since the murder depends on Teo being alone, a careful reader will ask.
- Evidence: ch 14 notes:11; ch 14:50 ("Nobody there yet. The banks empty."); ch 14:248.
- Fix: Change the notes to "his men were told noon". Add one line at 248: "His own six had come down from the hold at noon, as he'd told them, and found the ford empty but for the dead."

## Minor

### [B1-01-a] Haverly's age doesn't fit Kesh's six years
- Where: Book 1 ch 1, line 43 (and ch 3 line 45)
- Quote: "He was twenty."
- Problem: Kesh chose him at fifteen (ch 3, Ruling 25), and later chapters say "six years" (27:55, 61:95), which makes him 21. Check also: ch 61:39 has him coming "to Greyhold at fifteen" and staying six years, which fits badly with his being a second-year in Maren's cohort (ch 9:49, ch 11's paired trials).
- Evidence: ch 3:45; ch 27:55; ch 61:39, 61:95; Rulings_41-79.md:39.
- Fix: In ch 1, "He was twenty-one." In ch 61:39 (outside this range), drop "had come to Greyhold at fifteen" in favour of "had come to Greyhold two years ago with a falcon he'd had since fifteen".

### [B1-01-b] Calendar canon says Book 1 starts at day 0, but chapters 1–10 come before it
- Where: Book 1 ch 1–10 (no day numbers in their notes); canon docs
- Quote: "Book 1 runs from the harvest fair at the end of summer (day 0)"
- Problem: Coldtarn falls on the new moon before the trial's new moon (day 14), so it is about day -15. Ch 10 is Coldtarn + 11, about day -4, and the trial is day -1. Ch 1–2 are about three weeks before the fair. The prose (ch 1 "end of summer", ch 9 "long autumn light") fits that, but World, Series_Outline and the Plan all say the book opens on day 0. Doc only, but later reviewers working from "day 0 is the start" will misdate Part One.
- Evidence: Bible/World.md:239; Bible/Series_Outline.md:19; Book 1/Chapter_Plan.md:7; Decisions_Ch11-20.md:8,10; ch 8:23 ("eleven nights since the pyre was lit"); ch 10:36.
- Fix: In World.md Calendar, add: "Chapters 1–10 fall in the three weeks before the fair (about days -23 to -1; Coldtarn's new moon is about day -15); Harrow's End's harvest fair is day 0." Make the same edit in the Outline and Plan.

### [B1-02-a] Greyhold seen from Upper Rush past a hill (Check)
- Where: Book 1 ch 2, lines 177 and 247–261; ch 4, line 173
- Quote: "past the bend where the Wend went out of sight behind the hill" / "A row of them, high up, too regular for houses. Lanterns on a wall."
- Problem: From Harrow's End, Upper Rush is behind a hill at a bend. Greyhold is downstream of Harrow's End, about six leagues from Upper Rush. The same hill should hide Greyhold's wall from the Upper Rush bank, yet Calder and Joss watch the lanterns there (ch 2), and Teo sees "Greyhold's grey smudge" from the paddock by day (ch 4).
- Evidence: Rulings_41-79.md:26 (Upper Rush to Tolly's Staithe about six leagues; the Staithe is opposite Greyhold); Atlas.md:171, 177.
- Fix: In ch 2:177, drop "behind the hill": "upriver, north, past the bend." In ch 2:247, "small and steady, very far off downriver, were lights."

### [B1-02-b] Bel can't have been looking at Calder's face for thirty years
- Where: Book 1 ch 2, line 231
- Quote: "had been looking at that face for thirty years"
- Problem: Ch 4 makes Bel three years younger than Calder ("since she was six years old and he was nine"). With Calder at 32, that makes her 29.
- Evidence: ch 4:209; ch 2:181.
- Fix: "had been looking at that face all her life."

### [B1-04-a] Ch 4 notes disagree with the ch 3 prose and with ch 4's own prose
- Where: Book 1 ch 4, notes line 9
- Quote: "in Chapter 3 he has the cart news secondhand from Penner's lad" / "Lisska flies over with Imra; Calder doesn't know their names yet"
- Problem: The ch 3 prose now says "Hollin's lad" (3:127). In ch 4 itself, Teo names both "Imra" (4:143) and "Lisska" (4:151).
- Evidence: ch 3:127; ch 4:143, 151.
- Fix: In the notes, "Hollin's lad", and "Teo names Imra and Lisska; Calder meets them in Chapter 6."

### [B1-06-a] A sellsword called "Fen" two pages from "Fenn"
- Where: Book 1 ch 6, line 77
- Quote: "What's in your crates, Fen?"
- Problem: In a Calder chapter, a reader is likely to take this line as addressed to Calder Fenn.
- Evidence: ch 6:77; Calder is "Fenn" throughout.

### [B1-06-b] Who has been bringing the levy (Check)
- Where: Book 1 ch 6, lines 27–35 and 87 (against ch 16 line 216)
- Quote: "It's never been you before." / "saw Varrhold for the first time in four years"
- Problem: Ch 16 says Gorm Askell "counted Calder's barley sacks at the hold every autumn for ten years". That reads as though Calder brought them himself, against Ada and ch 6.
- Evidence: ch 16:216; ch 24:30 ("counted the barley sacks every autumn for ten years").
- Fix: In ch 16:216, "who'd counted Harrow's End's barley sacks at the hold every autumn for ten years".

### [B1-08-a] The trip home from Coldtarn doesn't add up
- Where: Book 1 ch 8, line 209
- Quote: "He rode home on the wool bales in the dark, two days, and on the second night the cart stopped at the Ram's Head"
- Problem: The Ram's Head is the halfway point. If they stop there on the second night, the trip takes three days, not two. The cart left Coldtarn near dark, so it can't reach the Ram's Head on the first night either.
- Evidence: Atlas.md:254 (Ram's Head, seven leagues from each end); Atlas.md:15 (cart, 7 leagues a day); ch 8:201.
- Fix: "He rode home on the wool bales, the best part of three days, and on the second night the cart stopped at the Ram's Head".

### [B1-08-b] Sigrun "sixty years in this Clan" at 76
- Where: Book 1 ch 8, line 163
- Quote: "I've been in this Clan sixty years."
- Problem: Sigrun is Clan-born (Ragnald's great-niece) and 76. Sixty is close to her years as a rider (59), not her years in the Clan.
- Evidence: Characters.md:96, 171; Rulings_41-79.md:15 (Ruling 11).
- Fix: "I've ridden for this Clan near sixty years."

### [B1-09-a] Eleven second-years on the bench against eleven bonded "this year" (Check)
- Where: Book 1 ch 9, line 49 (against ch 1 line 85)
- Quote: "There were eleven of them on the bench that morning, the second-years" / "There were eleven of them this year."
- Problem: Ch 1 counts eleven bonded students this year. Ch 9 puts eleven second-years on the bench, and that number includes the unbonded Maren and Tobin. Either ch 1 is counting bonded students across all years (not clear from the text), or one of the numbers is wrong.
- Evidence: ch 1:85; ch 9:49; ch 5:19 ("eight students").
- Fix: In ch 9, "There were fourteen of them on the bench". Or in ch 1, "There were eleven in the yard this year, across both years."

### [B1-13-a] Ch 13 notes give a different source for Maren's old letters
- Where: Book 1 ch 13, notes line 10 (against prose line 62 and notes line 13)
- Quote (notes): "her father taught her the old letters from the river-watch boundary stones"
- Problem: In the prose, he teaches her from a milestone in a hedge near Ashford, with letters he got from an Isvaren Reader. The Magic touches line in the same notes agrees with the prose.
- Evidence: ch 13:62; ch 13 notes:13.
- Fix: In the notes, "from a milestone on the Hearthmere side".

### [B1-14-b] Ulric arrives from the wrong side and his men ride toward the hold (Check)
- Where: Book 1 ch 14, lines 166 and 216 (against ch 16 lines 190–192)
- Quote: "Ulric came a quarter of an hour later, with his six, over the near rise" / "Ulric's men up the Way toward the hold, Arn's men across the moor to the west."
- Problem: Ulric is living at Varrhold, up the Way, yet he comes over the near (Harrow's End) rise, the same side as Calder. After the fight his men ride toward the hold and Arn's go west across the moor. By the next day Arn holds Varrhold and Ulric is outside its gate, then gone east to Skarrow. The directions run backwards.
- Evidence: ch 14:22, 48, 120; ch 16:78–80, 190–192; Atlas.md:262 (Skarrow, east side of the Scarfell).
- Fix: In ch 14:166, "over the near rise, up from the Ram's Head, where he'd lain the night". In ch 14:216, "Ulric's men away east over the moor, Arn's up the Way toward the hold."

### [B1-14-c] Calder misremembers the fair
- Where: Book 1 ch 14, line 64 (and ch 16 line 38)
- Quote: "while its rider bent down and told Wren she danced better than any girl at the hold"

- Evidence: ch 12:57, 75, 162–166.

## Craft

### [B1-05-c] Four of the first ten chapters end with the lead lying awake
- Where: ch 5:265, ch 8:215, ch 9:213, ch 10:218 (ch 15:150 is a close fifth)
- Quote: "didn't sleep for a long time" (9) / "and didn't go back to sleep" (10)
- Problem: The same closing beat keeps coming back, and it flattens the endings of Part One. Ch 8's dream is the only one that earns its place.
- Fix: Keep ch 8's ending. End ch 9 on "She thought: there's someone over there who does that, too." End ch 10 on Rook's line in the dream, and cut the waking.

### [B1-10-c] Ch 10 replays ch 6 beat for beat
- Where: ch 6:113, 139, 235, 243 against ch 10:44–48, 92, 106, 110
- Quote: "Fenn! The bee man!" (both) / "said the barley was damp" (6) / "Damp barley" (10)
- Problem: Both chapters run the same sequence: wain up the Way, the steward's damp barley, Teo's greeting, the cliff path, Lisska's head a hand's width away, Dag's "Late". The callbacks are deliberate, but there are enough of them that ch 10 reads as a rerun until Rook arrives.
- Fix: Keep Dag's "Late" and Lisska's hello. Cut the steward exchange to one line, and start the cliff scene on Teo's ask.

### [B1-15-c] Ch 15 repeats ch 13's bird-silence beats
- Where: ch 13:141–149; ch 15:62, 80
- Quote: "the birds came back one by one" (both chapters)
- Problem: This is A1's weak-chapter note, and the repeat is still close to word for word.
- Fix: In ch 15:80, cut "and the birds came back one by one". The heron is enough.

### [B1-10-d] The "not my business" refrain is used three times, twice in one scene
- Where: ch 4:189; ch 10:148, 200
- Quote: "It's never my business. That's why we're still here."
- Problem: Using it twice in ch 10 blunts the gate moment, where it matters most.
- Fix: Cut it at 10:148 and keep "he walked out onto the stone at Coldtarn with his hands empty" alone.

### [B1-13-c] The narrator states the book's design out loud
- Where: ch 13:185
- Quote: "she was the only person on either bank who could see them both at once"
- Problem: This is the author's frame, not something Maren could think. She doesn't know what happened at the ford.
- Fix: "she knew that both of them had just started."

### [B1-08-c] Small tics noticed in passing (mechanical pass to confirm)
- Where: "heart going like a drum" (6:189, 8:215); "It wasn't X. It was Y." (8:23, 8:87, 8:109, 15:74); about 40 "the way" simile frames remain across chs 1–15 (for example 1:17, 6:123, 8:179, 14:159).
- Problem: The style rule now bans "the way" similes outright, but A1/APPLIED only cut about a third of them.
- Fix: Leave these to the mechanical pass. Of the tics, the ch 8 dream's "heart going like a drum" is the one to cut.
