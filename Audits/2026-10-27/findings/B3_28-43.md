# Findings: Book 3, chapters 28–43
Reviewer summary: Part Three is strong. The far-shore chapters hold the mythic voice, the two gifts land, and Maren's counting arc pays off cleanly in ch 40 and 43. The serious problems are mechanical: twice Idony calls the control relic "the seventh" (a settled-rule breach); the ship and basket counts in 28 and 38 don't add up; the far shore's road map can't hold the ninety-three's walk; and several compass bearings in 41–43 point the wrong way against the Atlas. Ch 43 also reads as if Orrun's walk under the sea lasted days, not thirty-two days.

## Critical   (breaks the story, canon or a settled rule)

### [B3-35-a] The control relic is called "the seventh"
- Where: Book 3 ch 35, line 271; ch 36, line 45 (and ch 36, line 111)
- Quote: "The control relic," said the old man. Under his breath. "The seventh. The one from Hearthmere's vault." / "The relic. The seventh. The coal in the Last Titan's back."
- Problem: Settled rule 2 says the control relic is never called "the seventh relic". Here Idony calls it "the seventh" straight after "the control relic", which is the same thing in other words. Ch 36 line 111 makes it worse. "The Unbound. The seventh ring" runs straight into "even with it grown into its back", which welds the relic to "the seventh ring". The canon count is also against it: six relics remain and the control relic is "the sixth" (Bible/Series_Outline.md:129, 131).
- Evidence: BRIEF.md rule 2; Bible/Series_Outline.md:129–131.
- Fix: ch 35:271 → "The control relic," said the old man. Under his breath. "The one from Hearthmere's vault. The coal in its back." ch 36:45 → "The relic. The coal in the Last Titan's back." ch 36:111 → "Theirs," he said. "The Unbound. A person can't hold it. A bound beast can't. A chained Titan can't, even with the stone grown into its back; ..."

## Major      (a continuity or logic error a careful reader would notice)

### [B3-28-a] The Bell's ship count doesn't add up
- Where: Book 3 ch 28, lines 120, 154–160, 328
- Quote: "Eleven ships became ten." / "It took the other six ships and threw them." / "Nine of the eleven ships. Four off the Bell's back. Five on the reefs... Two drifted away north"
- Problem: Rennick's three have gone and the *White Heron* has sunk, so ten ships meet the Bell (4 capsized + 6 flung = 10). The tally then says "nine of the eleven" and lists 4 + 5 + 2 = 11 hulls lost, from a fleet of ten.
- Evidence: ch 28:30 (eleven plus Rennick's three), 64–70, 90–98, 120.
- Fix: line 328 → "Nine of the ten ships. Four off the Bell's back. Five on the reefs either side of the road, flung there by the first wave. The tenth drifted away north, waterlogged, empty; they found her in the afternoon, and nobody aboard."

### [B3-28-b] Maren counts fourteen ships on a night when ten are left (hand-off from ch 27)
- Where: Book 3 ch 27, line 384 (night 126, ch 27 notes line 21)
- Quote: "counted them. Eleven. Then three more, behind, close together."
- Problem: By night 126 Rennick's three black ships have turned back (d.121–122) and the *White Heron* has sunk (d.123). The fleet is ten ships with no escort astern.
- Evidence: ch 28:64–70, 90–98, 120.
- Fix: ch 27:384 → "She stood on the hill-top at the end of the world and counted them. Ten. In a ragged line, and nothing behind."

### [B3-38-a] "Nine people" are lost from two baskets that each carried twenty
- Where: Book 3 ch 38, lines 224 and 238 (and 248)
- Quote: "Low over the outer spit with twenty in each basket" / "Nine people in the baskets. Two riders. Two dragons."
- Problem: Two full baskets at twenty each makes forty people, not nine. Line 72 sets "twenty people to a pair", and the notes and ledger both use nine.
- Evidence: ch 38:72, 224; Part3_Ledger.md line 26.
- Fix: keep the nine and change line 224 to "coming in slow with the last of the Saltings' old folk, nine between the two baskets, and still too low because the baskets were wet and heavy". Or keep twenty and make line 238 "Forty people in the baskets", with line 248 "Two of the forty, alive, clinging to one."

### [B3-39-a] Kharrak and Lisska guard the ninety-three on the road while they're at the hall
- Where: Book 3 ch 39, lines 30–32
- Quote: "With Kharrak and Lisska lying awake at either end of the line."
- Problem: On days 143–144 all three dragons are at the Hall of Light with Calder. They landed there at noon 142 (ch 34:235–237), Aurveth lies outside the columns (ch 35:95, 243), the horse walks "past the farmer's dragons" on day 144 (ch 36:255), and Calder leaves with "his three" at dawn 145 (ch 37:28–38).
- Evidence: ch 34:235–237; ch 36:255; ch 37:28–38.
- Fix: line 32 → "They'd slept on the road. Sitting up. In turns. With Jory walking the line all night, waking anyone who lay down."

### [B3-39-b] The far shore's road can't carry the ninety-three to the lagoon in a day and a half
- Where: Book 3 ch 39, line 30; ch 32, lines 168–170; ch 31, line 305; ch 27, line 374
- Quote: "Up the western road from the crossroads, a day and a half" / "Jory said it must be the one that came up from the lagoon."
- Problem: Taken together, the chapters put one road through lagoon → Mother's hill (two days' walk, ch 27) → hall (a day SW, ch 31) → crossroads (a day off, ch 39:92, 156). The ninety-three, children among them, would walk four days or more, right through the hall where Maren, Calder and the elders are on day 143, and nobody notices. Jory is also back at the crossroads by day 145 (line 40), which he can't be on foot. Ch 32:168 has the road "came in from the west" although the lagoon lies north-east.
- Evidence: ch 27:374 (lagoon NE of the hill); ch 31:305, 311; ch 32:168–170, 330; ch 39:92, 156; Part3_Ledger.md line 19 ("The Hall of Light lies between the Mother's hill and the Wayfarer's crossroads").
- Fix: make the lagoon road a separate road. ch 32:168–170 → "Another road came in from the north. Paved. Grey. Jory said it must go up to the lagoon. To the Needle. Kit's road, by another way." Keep the hall on the north-east road. In ch 39:40 add that Calder fetched Jory: "Jory told him at the lagoon on the hundred-and-forty-fifth, when Calder came down for him on Aurveth."

### [B3-39-c] The lion leaves the ridge on day 148 in ch 39 but on day 147 in ch 40
- Where: Book 3 ch 39, lines 394–412; ch 40, lines 25–35, 111
- Quote: ch 39: "Aurveth saw it at dusk on the hundred-and-forty-eighth... Moving. Walking. Down off the ridge... He watched it reach the lagoon's shore. And go into the sea." ch 40: "He carried them down from the hall in a day... At dusk on the hundred-and-forty-seventh."
- Problem: In ch 40 Orrun reaches the lagoon at dusk 147 and walks into the sea at dusk 148. Ch 39 shows the whole walk off the ridge and into the sea in the one dusk of 148.
- Evidence: ch 40:25–35, 111; Part3_Ledger.md line 9.
- Fix: ch 39:404–410 → "Through the hills. The hall's ridge, the columns, empty. And on the lagoon's shore, the grey warmth, sitting. Then moving. Into the water."

### [B3-40-a] Orrun counts the piers "up" with the numbers going down
- Where: Book 3 ch 40, lines 175–179 and 261
- Quote: "*Forty-one,* he said. *Going the other way now. Counting up.*" / "*Thirty. Twenty-nine. Twenty-eight.* Counting up."
- Problem: The milestones count down toward the far shore. The shelf pier is *into the light, one* (ch 32:32), and going home Jory counts "One!... Eleven!... Forty-one" (ch 42:101–113). Walking home, Orrun's first pier should be One, and "counting up" can't run 30, 29, 28.
- Evidence: ch 32:32; ch 42:101–113; ch 28:12 ("eleven" after "twelve").
- Fix: line 175 → "*One,* he said, the first night." Line 179 → "*One,* he said. *Going the other way now. Counting up.*" Line 261 → "*Twenty-eight. Twenty-nine. Thirty.*" (Notes line 16 should match: "*Two. Three.*")

### [B3-41-a] Tova leaves the Greenwold "when the Titan lay down in the Mere", twenty-five days after it did
- Where: Book 3 ch 41, line 49 (and notes line 12) against line 139
- Quote: "Tova had brought them out of the Greenwold on the hundred-and-twentieth, when the Titan lay down in the Mere" / "Where it had lain down on the ninety-fifth day"
- Problem: The chapter dates the Last Titan's lying down in the Mere twice and differently: d.120 and d.95. The Part Two ledger has it walking into the Mere on d.94.
- Evidence: ch 41:139–141; Part2_Ledger.md line 22.
- Fix: line 49 → "Tova had brought them out of the Greenwold on the hundred-and-twentieth, when the Antlered came down into the Thornwood and the oaks were too near it." (Or drop the Titan clause and keep the date.)

### [B3-42-a] Compass errors on the flight home
- Where: Book 3 ch 42, lines 257 and 267; ch 41, line 343
- Quote: "He looked east, up the Wend, where the river went inland from its mouth past Wendmouth" / "Over the Saltreach bank, over the Way, over farms he knew. West of the camp. A long way west."
- Problem: The Coast Road runs Wendmouth, Gullhaven, Corsa, Blackwater, and Hearthmere has "the Wend on its eastern border", so from Corsa the Wend is west. The camp lies "between the river and the Way" (ch 41:27), so a flight over the Way passes east of the camp, not west. Rowan's sighting (ch 41:343) repeats the error.
- Evidence: Bible/Atlas.md:62, 70; ch 41:27.
- Fix: ch 42:257 → "He looked west, along the coast, to where the Wend came down past Wendmouth into the sea. A long way up it, on the river's bank, smoke." ch 42:267 → "East of the camp. A long way east." ch 41:343 → "a long way east of the camp."

### [B3-42-b] The burning relic is "east" of Coldtarn and "in the middle of everything"
- Where: Book 3 ch 42, line 473; ch 43, line 358
- Quote: "*And east,* said Aurveth. *A long way. In the middle of everything. Something burning.*" / "north-west, toward the middle of everything. To the Mere."
- Problem: The coal is the relic in the Last Titan's back in the Mere (ch 43:370–374). The Mere is in Hearthmere, west of the Wend, so from Coldtarn above Varrhold it lies west or south-west, not east. And "the middle of everything" for the Mere sits too close to the settled "middle of the world" (the island in the Wend, the hub, near Coldtarn). The reader may take it for the hub, which Atlas reserves for the old capital.
- Evidence: Bible/Atlas.md:49, 70, 78–79, 281; BRIEF.md rule 5 ("the middle of the world").
- Fix: ch 42:473 → "*And west,* said Aurveth. *Across the river. In the Mere. Something burning.*" ch 43:358 → "north-west, toward Hearthmere's heart. To the Mere."

### [B3-43-a] Ch 43 forgets the thirty-two days under the sea

## Craft      (at most 8)

### [B3-40-b] One stone-opening simile reused in three chapters and two POVs
- Where: ch 39:416; ch 40:135; ch 42:203
- Quote: "as water opens round a boat's bow" (39 and 40, word for word); "as water opens and closes round a boat" (42)
