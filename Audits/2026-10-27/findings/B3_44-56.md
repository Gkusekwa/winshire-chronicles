# Findings: Book 3, chapters 44–56

Reviewer summary: The range is strong. Kit's homecoming, the Antlered kill, the glass-line and Wren's question all land, and the reveal, magic and meeting rules mostly hold. The biggest problems are geography. The drafts put the Candle Downs and the Greenwold between the Thornwood and the Wend. Ch 46 has the Lamp arrive from the wrong side for a camp on the Saltreach bank. Ch 53 onward puts Liss's ferry house at the eddy, but Book 1, Book 2 and the opening of Book 3 put it under Greyhold, about three leagues downriver. There are also two hard timing errors (Calder's homecoming as told in ch 43; Idony's two-day barge from Blackwater), one possible breach of the elemental limit in ch 52, and a scatter of count and direction slips.

## Critical   (breaks the story, canon or a settled rule)

### [B3-52-a] Idony's "nothing new in the ground" goes past the elemental limit (Check)
- Where: Book 3 ch 52, lines 206–212
- Quote: "Anything that might come up out of a dead Titan's breath, as grass comes up out of a burned field." / "Nothing comes free. Not while one of them's left to take it."
- Problem: The brief makes "nothing new in the ground; nothing small" the limit. These two sentences add a mechanism: once no Titan is left to take a dead one's breath, something new grows from it. That explains in advance the stone creature in ch 86. It's a judgement call, so I've marked it Check, but as written it reads as an explanation.
- Evidence: BRIEF rule 2 (elementals); ch 52 notes line 15 (the limit wording).
- Fix: Keep line 206 down to "For anything new. In the ground. Anything small, stirring, where the great thing had been." Cut line 212's second sentence, so that it reads "Nothing comes free."

## Major      (a continuity or logic error a careful reader would notice)

### [B3-45-a] Calder came home on about day 149, not "three days back" from day 182
- Where: Book 3 ch 43, line 144 (just before the range), against ch 45, lines 35–41, 81 and 221–225
- Quote: "Home," said Brede. "Three days back. Over the Marches at dusk."
- Problem: Brede speaks on d.182, so this puts Calder's return at about d.179. Ch 45, though, has him ten days on Ada's pallet from about d.150, Kharrak cracking Teo's ledge on d.160, and Arn pouring at Coldtarn on d.160 while Calder sleeps in the cave. The three dragons were seen going home at dusk on d.149.
- Evidence: Book 3/Notes/Part3_Ledger.md:23 (echo, dusk 149: three shapes going north-east toward the Scarfell); Chapter_Plan.md:9 ("comes home the day after the gift"); ch 45 notes line 12.
- Fix: ch 43:144: "Home," said Brede. "A month back. Over the Marches at dusk. The whole camp saw."

### [B3-45-b] Maren already "sees" the coal among the five eggs, two chapters before "only me"
- Where: Book 3 ch 43, line 374 (echo), against ch 45, lines 317–323
- Quote: "And on its back, among five dark shapes, something burning. Like a coal." / "*He feels it... He can't see where... Only me. And you, through me.*"
- Problem: The plan's point for ch 45 is that nobody else can see the coal, and ch 68 depends on that. Ch 43 gives Maren, through Orrun, the five shapes and the burning coal among them, which is close to a sighting. Ch 45's "He can't see where" then sounds like a correction.
- Evidence: Chapter_Plan.md:205 ("nobody else can see it, and that will matter (68)").
- Fix: ch 43:374: "And on its back, somewhere, something burning. Like a coal." Drop "among five dark shapes" so that only Aurveth ever shows the eggs.

### [B3-46-a] Idony's barge reaches the camp from Blackwater in two days
- Where: Book 3 ch 44, lines 323–325 (leaves d.194); ch 46, lines 47 and 57 (arrives d.196); also ch 46, line 417 (Kit's second barge at the camp on d.203, having been at Blackwater on d.195)
- Quote: "On the hundred-and-ninety-fourth." / "The old man came the same day."
- Problem: Blackwater (295,178) to Wendmouth (183,204) is about 115 leagues of coast. Wendmouth to the camp is about another 90 leagues up the Wend. That is around ten days even at coastal ship speed plus a fast barge. Kit's eight days with a stop for Keepers is also impossible. Ch 46 needs Idony there for three days' writing before the council on d.201.
- Evidence: Atlas.md:18–19 (barge downstream 20 leagues/day, ship along the coast 30/day); Tools/atlas/world.py:255, 262 (Wendmouth, Blackwater).
- Fix: Have Lisska carry Idony and Corrie north-west when she brings Jory on d.187 (ch 44:207). She "went... north-west, over the cliffs" with two passengers on her neck, and Idony is set down at the camp on the way. Ch 46:57–59 then reads "He came off the green one's neck before her wings were still". The barge in 44:323–333 carries Nye and the salt fish only. Kit's second barge (46:417) moves to about d.212, or loads the Keepers on a later run.

### [B3-46-b] The Lamp's arrival puts the camp on the wrong bank
- Where: Book 3 ch 46, lines 29, 131, 143 and 145
- Quote: "Out of the east. Over the downs on the Hearthmere bank." / "from the eastern bank to the western" / "onto the eastern meadow. Into the camp."
- Problem: The Hearthmere bank is west of the Wend, and the camp is on the Saltreach (east) bank. A Lamp coming from the east (it went north-west from the Worldsend's foot in ch 44:355) would reach the camp without crossing. If it crosses east to west, it ends on the far bank, not in the camp's eastern meadow. Line 29 also brings the lion up the west bank, away from the camp. That conflicts with the council held thirty paces from his head (47:180).
- Evidence: Book 3/Notes/Part3_Ledger.md:23 ("The Accord's camp... on the Saltreach bank of the Wend"); Atlas.md:70 (the Wend on Hearthmere's eastern border).
- Fix: line 29 "Up the Wend's east bank"; line 131 "Out of the west. Over the downs on the Hearthmere bank, with the sunrise full on it"; line 143 "from the western bank to the eastern". It has come round in the night. If dawn-from-the-east must stay, use line 131 "Over the downs on the Saltreach bank" and have it walk down the river on the light rather than across.

### [B3-49-a] The Candle Downs and the Greenwold put between the Thornwood and the Wend
- Where: Book 3 ch 49, lines 30 and 52; ch 50, line 447; ch 51, line 207; ch 52, lines 56, 100–104 and 158; ch 53, lines 33 and 99–101 (and notes line 13)
- Quote: "Eastward, over the Marches, over the burned Downs and the empty Greenwold" (49:52); "Under the Downs. Under the burned Greenwold, under the Marches, the Wend" (50:447); "The third at the Candle Downs' east end" (52:56)
- Problem: The Candle Downs (south of the Mere) and the Greenwold (north of Hallowmere) are both in Hearthmere, west of the Wend. The Thornwood is north-east of the hub. The flight east, the breath's route west, and the glass stations "a day apart" along the clans' road all cross them in the wrong order. In ch 52 the camp's glass (station 5) is logged after the "Downs' west end" (station 4). Clans leaving a Saltreach-bank camp for the east don't take "the Hearthmere road". The Sun Road also runs from the Sunward Hills to Hallowmere and never meets the Downs' southern edge, so the Vintner's Gate "opened onto the Downs" (53:101) has no place on the map.
- Evidence: Atlas.md:50–51, 59 and 82; Tools/atlas/world.py:81, 83, 97 and 107 (polygons and the Sun Road line); Atlas.md:149 (the Wyldmarch as "the wild east").
- Fix: Make it consistent. The clans' road and the glass line from the Thornwood run west through the Wyldmarch–Saltreach border country and the Marches to the camp. West of the Wend they go to Greyhold's hill, then the Candle Downs' or Greenwold's edge, then Sallow End. Rename stations 2–4 (for example "the Saltreach Track's head", "the Ashlings' north edge", "the Wend's far bank"), reorder 50:447 to "Under the Wyldmarch. Under the Marches, the Wend. Under the burned Downs", and change "Hearthmere road" in 49:30 and 51:207 to "the Saltreach Track" or "the east road". For 53, say only "the clans' road home" at d.240. Place the Gate where the Sun Road leaves the Sunward Hills for Hallowmere, and accept that Maren's ride there is longer.

### [B3-53-a] Liss's ferry house moved to the eddy, three leagues from where canon puts it
- Where: Book 3 ch 53, lines 371, 377 and 419; ch 55, lines 26 and 44 (also ch 57, line 37, just after the range)
- Quote: "Where the river bent below Greyhold's fallen hill" / "Liss's ferry house stood on the Hearthmere bank above the eddy, as it always had." / "three leagues up the Wend, to the eddy"
- Problem: The ferry house is at the stone jetty under Greyhold's wall, and the eddy (where the island was) is about three and a half leagues upriver of Greyhold. Book 3 ch 1 still has the ferry house on the jetty road by the fallen hill. The camp was three leagues below Greyhold's hill, so moving it to the eddy is about six or seven leagues, not three.
- Evidence: Book 1/Notes/Rulings_41-79.md:26 (Ruling 18); Atlas.md:175; Book 2/Notes/Part7_Ledger.md:13 ("walks three leagues upriver to the eddy where the island was"); Book 3 ch 1, lines 366–368; Book 3/Notes/Part3_Ledger.md:23.
- Fix (author's call): 53:371 "Where the river had split, three leagues above Greyhold's fallen hill"; 53:377 "Liss's ferry stood on the Hearthmere bank above the eddy now. She'd moved scow and rope up from the jetty in the summer, when the hawser went across"; 53:419 and 55:26 "six leagues". Ch 55's hay-barn becomes "the barn above Liss's new landing". Ch 57:37's "his old room" becomes the room she kept for him in the new house.
