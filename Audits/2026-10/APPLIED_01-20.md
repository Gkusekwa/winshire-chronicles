No files were renamed and no other files were touched. Originals were snapshotted before editing, and the counts below compare that snapshot with the current files. Net change: 81,184 → 80,333 words (−851), almost all of it cuts.

## Style pass

- **Folk-saying openers (A1 M8).** A1 lists 13 in my range: 1, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 11, 13, 14, 16, 18, 19, 20.
  - **Kept whole (5):** 1 (Part One opener), 8 (Coldtarn), 13 (Part Two opener), 16 (the ford/pyre), 18 (the mill).
  - **Cut to one to three sentences (7):**
    - 4: "tells lies for thirty… shovel".
    - 6: thirty days of tables, which the "thirty nights" later in the chapter needs.
    - 10: the no-sheep saying, which Rook's "sheep" line pays off.
    - 11: the first sentence only.
    - 14: rewritten to avoid "the weight of"; the place paragraphs stay.
    - 19: Pell's two ways, both of which the chapter quotes later.
    - 20: "one life and two voices" plus the "four" line.
  - **Cut (1):** 3. The ferry-legend proverb is gone, and the chapter opens on Liss and the rope. The rope-charm fact (Magic touches) is kept.
  - I checked all 79 chapters for later references to the cut material and found none.
- **"the way you / the way a" frame:** cut or converted about a third in every chapter, mostly in action and where two frames sat close together. Ch 17's three "sleeper" frames became one, and Ch 10's two "picture" frames became one. Frames that work as mythic turns or carry character were kept, for example Ch 1 "a crack in your own ceiling", Ch 6 "across a frozen river", Ch 8 "a word in a language you used to know", Ch 19 "brush a fly off a table".
- **Over-explained emotion** (cut where the scene already shows the feeling):
  - Ch 5: "That was what made it so hard to watch." and "It wasn't mockery. That was what made it bad. It was recognition."
  - Ch 6: "felt foolish and relieved in equal parts".
  - Ch 7: the repeat of "waited for every face", which line 81 already says.
  - Ch 9: "She found, in fact, that she rather liked it." and "She didn't know, yet, whether that made them alike."
  - Ch 14: "He knew it was stupid as he said it."
  - Ch 15: "Danger wasn't the reason… Because".
  - Ch 16: the doubled "heard it come out of his mouth".
  - Ch 19: "It seemed stupid."
  - Ch 20: "That was the worst of it."
- **Repeated cadences trimmed:**
  - The "and didn't know why" chapter endings in 8 and 9.
  - Ch 4's closing "he still hadn't told anyone", which echoed Ch 2's last line.
  - Ch 13's first of two "like a held breath" (A1's weak-chapter note on 15).
  - One of three "I remember everything" in Ch 20 (M12). The setup and the payoff stay.
  - Two "heart going like a drum" (8, 15).
  - Ch 1's doubled "anyway".
- **"Not X. Y." fragments:** the cuts are in 2, 4, 17, 19 and 20, aimed at narrator-level corrections that were redundant. Examples: Ch 2's "Not bent, not snapped. Cut." right after "cut clean through"; Ch 19 "Not faded. Stopped."; Ch 20 "Not gone. Still." and "Not fear. More like". Fragments in dialogue, in deathbed italics, and those carrying an image (Ch 1 "Not toward the river. Down.", Ch 2 "Not cloud.") were kept.
- No new plot, facts or scenes. No em dashes and no banned phrases were introduced.

## Notes for the author and for later ranges

1. **Two Penners in Ch 3.** A1's M2 wording brings in "Penner's lad", who comes down from Upper Rush, but the same chapter has "Old Penner from Millbrook". Either they are one family with a lad up at Upper Rush, or the lad needs another name.
2. **Ch 33's spelling** should be "stanching-word", to match 19 and 21 (m22).
3. **Bible/Characters.md:** A1 M13 asks for Pell's wyvern to be "bonded" and the place to be "the coast road below Stillhaven". This is outside my range; A3 M7 notes that Characters:152 may already follow the prose.
4. **Ch 37 and 39 notes** say Maren's father's hound was killed "on the Saltreach road". Ch 1 says "on the far bank", which agrees. Nothing to change here; this is only a check for the 21–40 agent.
5. **Ch 11 still has "The sun went down behind Greyhold"** while Maren is in the west channel opposite Redwatch. Greyhold is three leagues south, so the sun is setting roughly over the Hearthmere bank rather than literally behind Greyhold. It is harmless, but "behind the Hearthmere bank" would be more exact if the author wants it.
