- **"for the rest of her life"**: cut in 63 and 69; kept in 77 only.
- **"ten paces"**: paraphrased or cut in 63, 64, 67, 69, 70 (×2), 72; trimmed 73 from 10 to 3 and 78 from 4 to 2.
- **"almost smiled"**: cut in 61, 65, 75 (×2), 78; kept Pell's in 69.
- **"That was the worst of it"**: cut the only one (61) along with the line that explained it, leaving the cap's one use to 41–60.
- **"Two hammers, crossed." standalone**: kept 71 (Odo's punch); folded 73's into the sentence.
- **"Not X. Y." fragments**: 60 → 40 (cut or merged; kept the ones doing work, like "Not barred." and "Not the town. Not yet.").
- **Simile frame**: about a third of the "the way…" similes cut or turned into a plain "like" in each chapter, mostly in action (e.g. 63 the falcon's turn, 68 cold water, 69 the drawer's "push a door", 72 the knife, 75 Kesh, 76 the trap-door). Callbacks and character-world similes kept (Calder's cows and sheep, Maren's river).
- **Over-explained emotion and related tics**: trims in 61 (the arm "must have hurt"), 63 ("She couldn't have said… She knew."), 65 (repeated "it couldn't understand"), 67 ("couldn't have said why"), 68 and 70, 72, 76 (superlatives: "the loudest/quietest/gentlest thing … ever", "Lower than … ever felt"), 71/73/74 ("She'd always think that afterwards", "She'd think about it afterwards…", "She'd see it all her life", "He'd think about it later…"), 77 ("She knew it wasn't hers"), 78 (Maren's recap of the Titan shortened, since 77 already shows it; a duplicate "rope sings before it parts" simile removed).
- **Door similes** (A2 table): 69, 71 and 76 removed; Haverly's dying "like a door shutting" left.
- No em dashes, banned words or new stock phrases added. Spelling as already used.
