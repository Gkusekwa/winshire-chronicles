## Book 1 prose

1. **No casting by Maren or Calder in Book 1.** Apply A1 C1, C2, C3 (Ch 5, 19, 21) as written, and update those chapters' "Magic touches" notes to match. Bond gifts are not casting.
2. **Maren's years (A1 C5): option B.** Joined the river watch at 17; **eight winters** on the river watch; two years at Greyhold; age 27. "four winters" → "eight winters" (1, 3, 9, 67 and anywhere else); Ch 13 "four years" → "eight years" where it means the river-watch habit; Ch 5 and 11 "four years" → "two years"; Ch 15's "eight years ago" stays.
3. **Island trips start at Redwatch (A1 C7).** Ruling 7 stands, and the map now matches it: Redwatch is on the Hearthmere bank opposite the island, half a league across the west channel; Greyhold is about three leagues downriver. Apply A1's fixes for 11, 29, 35, 37, 39.
4. **Haverly's trial (A1 M1):** use the first fix (a known exception: out of uniform, without the pin, "you were never there").
5. **Haverly's bonding age (A1 m16):** fifteen. Change Ch 3's "fourteen" to "fifteen" and leave 61; check Ch 27's "six years" against it.
6. **Renames:** Merrow's ox-beast **Holm → Dun** (Ch 37, 39 notes, 61, and anywhere else the ox is meant; never touch Sigrun or Ragnald Holm). The Ch 33 sergeant **Brask → Tasker**.
7. **Ch 16 bonded riders (A1 m17):** "ten or eleven of them".
8. **The other Titans (A2 C2, C3):** cut both lines exactly as A2 says. The pull "toward somewhere a long way off" is the only hint left.
9. **The keys (A2 C6):** the Watch-captain's key (Thorne's) opens the black door (where it is turned once for each of the Watch, on the night they're sworn, as Ch 51 and 75 have it) *and* fits the knight's lock below. Callis took it from Thorne at the gate, and Cassian turns the knight's lock with it (77). Corvin's key for the Ruin's lock is older: its bow is cut with the same hooked marks as the boss (74). Where he got it is a Book 2 thread.
10. **The countdown (A2 M5):** use the candle option. The twins count to marks on a candle or glass, not breaths, and Cassian says "four" only when the flame touches the mark.
11. **Odo's collars (A2 M6):** ordered "in the spring, before the summer".
12. **The drawer (A2 M7):** she is Halloran's drawer, lent to Delling. Calder recognises her from Gallows Quay (34).
13. **Rowan in the breach (A2 C5):** add the line that she took every rider back up the River Road to Redwatch at dusk.
14. **The Fellow (A2 m15):** he dies when the floor drops (76). Tie him to Isvaren in Calder's view with A2 m11's line.
15. **Rook's ledger (A2 setup 2):** plant one line in 72 or 74 that Rook's ledger went into Delling's chest (Book 2 ch 7 and 14 need it).
16. **Ch 67 (A2 M28):** give Maren the one act A2 proposes (halfway down toward Rowan's column; a greyhart scents Orrun; she turns back).
17. **Deathbeds (A2 M18):** keep the "Don't talk" routine for Pell only. Apply A2's suggestions for Hallam and Haverly.
18. **Day count and calendar:** the prose stays as it is. Book 1 runs from the harvest fair (day 0, end of summer) to sunrise on day 144, in early spring. The day numbers in the notes are kept.

## Style pass (each prose agent, in its own range)

Edit surgically, keeping the voice (lean and mythic; see Style_Guide.md and Voice_Samples.md). Never add new plot. No em dashes. Never introduce the banned stock phrases.
- **"the way you / the way a …" simile frame:** cut or convert about a third of them in each chapter, especially in action. Prefer no comparison, or a plain "like".
- **Over-explained emotion:** apply every instance the reports name (A1 M7; A2 M19–M23). Elsewhere, cut a sentence that names a feeling the scene has already shown.
- **Folk-saying chapter openers (Ch 1–40 only, A1 M8):** keep about one chapter in three. In the others, cut or shorten the proverb paragraph so the chapter opens in scene. Don't write new scenes.
- **Echo retellings (A1 M9):** each echo adds one new fact or a distortion, or it's cut down to a line. Apply A1's specific trims (31's refugee monologue, 33's scout, the stream-retellings). Cap "twenty paces" and "roared like the end of the world" at one anchor use each, plus at most two light uses.
- **Caps for 41–79 (A2 tics table):** "That was the first thing" ≤ 2; "for the rest of her/his life" only in 77; "ten paces" only in 42, 55, 73, 78 (paraphrase the rest); "almost smiled" ≤ 3; "That was the worst of it" ≤ 1; "Not X. Y." fragments down by about a third; "Two hammers, crossed." as a standalone line ≤ 2.
- **Repeated images (A1 M12, M14; A2 M23):** apply as written.
