# Structural rewrites: Book 1, Ch 24, 25, 35

Brief: A1 "Weakest chapters" (items 1, 2, 4), approved by the author. Only these three files were edited. Word counts are prose only (below the first `---` after the notes). Originals were backed up in the session scratchpad before editing. Each file's Status line now says "structural revision", and the Draft notes describe the new shape.

Checks on all three: no em dashes; no "the way you / the way a" simile frames left (24 had 3, 25 had 1, 35 had 2); none of the Style Guide's banned phrases; no new plot facts.

## Ch 24, Hunted (Calder): 6,138 → 4,996 words (−19%)

**Structure.** The four gate scenes are now two.
1. **The Clan.** Opens on day 14 with Sten Raske arriving. Gorm Askell's visit on day 13 is told as a short flashback at the head of the scene, keeping the keeper terms, Kharrak's "I'll keep him in my teeth", and "Then Arn Varr will come and fetch them. And you… He remembers that." Raske's offer, Aurveth's cruel line about Sigrun and her apology, Calder's "no to all of it", "Then who?" and "there's no middle of the river" all stay.
2. **The outsiders.** Corrin Ash in the afternoon and the coin man at dusk are now one continuous scene, with Calder on the mounting block in between. The coin, the ring of six, "Is it later now?" and the culvert stay. Lisska names him "the sea man" here, which 28 and 32 call back to.

**Trims and sharpening.**
- The confession to the dragons is kept, but its recap of the chain, bucket and ford (all things the reader has seen) is down to one paragraph.
- Hob's betrayal is kept whole. Calder's list of memories of Hob is cut to three (the fair, the bracken, the gate) before "*All right,* Hob had said".
- The flight, the Fellwood, Crow Scar and the echo are lightly tightened.
- Similes converted to Calder's world: "like a man at a horse fair", "the posts of a tithe barn", "still as a hare in its form", "like weather making up its mind".

**Facts kept for later chapters** (grepped 25–79):

## Ch 25, The Midden (Maren): 3,836 → 2,870 words (−25%)

**Structure.** The two Hale scenes (the midden on day 17, the archive on day 19) are now one Hale scene at the midden at dusk on **day 19**. It comes after the day-18 island trip, which stays where it was, so Lisska's sighting in 24 still holds. The chapter now runs: Grieve, the barn, the rumours, the beasts, the island, then Hale.

**The friction.** Hale opens by saying he has read her notebook, so he asks only for what she didn't write down. That kills the recap. Each exchange then either adds something or shows a refusal:
- The layered hollow, "dug, and filled, and dug again" (new to her as she says it).
- "How did you know to ask that?", which he won't answer.
- "It's had three goes." / "Why hasn't it?" / "Ask it."
- She tells him she sat with it last night, which he didn't know.
- "Does it dig?" / "Why does the archive want to know…?"
- The verse and the oath's age (new).
- "What's below, Warden?" / "*Do not ask*" (he refuses with the oath itself).
- The farmer question, then her one new item for him: the man with the boots, and "twenty times".
- "Will you tell me why?" / "No."
- His offer of the old orders in the archive, which she takes as the price of her promise.

**"Too carefully"** now lands once, in the narration: "He asked it too carefully. Every word set down like a man crossing a weir in the dark…" Two other beats were cut: the "choosing each word off a shelf" line and "much too lightly". The closing reflection, the lost key and "found she wasn't hungry", is kept as the chapter's closing image, now at the midden rather than on the archive stair, and supper rather than dinner because it's evening.

**Facts kept:**
- Grieve, the Long Barn, the thirty-one beasts, and the Darrow girl's hound (27, 39).
- The licensed-word line.
- Hale's look, keys and long black key (35, 49, 51).
- Rowan showed him the notebook.
- The hollow, and "does it dig" (53).
- The oath older than the orders, and forty years (27).
- "I'd like to see it. One day. Before I die." / "You could come." (35).
- "I'd like to tell you about [the old orders]" (27). It is now said at the midden as an invitation to the archive.
- The day-18 trip from the Redwatch boathouse, with Turnip, Ottery's "my own boat", and the bird going over.
- Haverly's story and "I do".
- The hunters' talk of the farmer, and Maren's "Yes, Warden."

**Moved or removed, and why it's safe:**
- **Hale's first appearance moves from day 17 to day 19.** Nothing later cites day 17 or "the third evening" (grepped).
- **The archive setting of the farmer question is gone.** 27 starts the archive afternoons itself. 27's notes still say "Hale in the archive, 'I'd like to tell you about the old orders'". That is notes-only drift and the prose agrees, but the author may want to touch it.
- **Dropped:** the recap of all three attacks, the lion's ground effects at the spit and the bridge, Hale's "You've told me a great deal more than you know", and his line about the old orders before Greyhold. That line's substance survives in his invitation. Nothing later quotes any of these.

## Ch 35, The Mentor (Maren): 3,551 → 2,747 words (−23%)

**Structure.** The chapter now climbs to Hale's slip.
- **Pell** is cut to the new part: three lines, ending "I'll take it up myself."
- **Rowan** is cut to the new part: the river doesn't know her name, "So someone told him", and the margin note beside the boats. The re-asking about the roadblock is gone. 43 quotes "Then someone told him" and the margin; both kept.
- **The Lesson Wall bench** moves **before** the archive, from noon on day 53 to noon on day 52. Hale's "He said your name", which used to be the archive's opening re-report, now opens the bench in one line, with the knock-at-the-door look. The Vey report is cut to what Maren doesn't know. The two-places line, the relief and then the shame, "Why?", and "Because he's three… the last time it was so…" all stay. She waits him out and gets nothing. He ends it by sending her to the archive for the oath.
- **The archive** that afternoon has the door, the history of the oath and the slip, as before. Her choice not to press him now plays against the bench ("At noon she'd sat on the bench and waited him out and got nothing… She had him now"). The key image from the old bench ending ("the one she knew now would fit a lock as big as a man's hand") moves to her last look back from the archive door.
- **The island** that night stays as the coda. The duplicated passage where she thinks about the door was cut.

**Closing image changed, deliberately.** The old ending (Hale limping off from the bench, then "Nobody came to tell her") belonged to the third report scene, which the brief named as part of the problem. The chapter now ends on the lion lifting its head toward Greyhold, the hum, and "She sat there until it got up and went." The key image survives at the end of the archive scene. The opening (writing the report, the line set alone on its page) is kept.
