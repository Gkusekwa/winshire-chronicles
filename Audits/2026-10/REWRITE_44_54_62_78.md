# Structural rewrites: Book 1 ch 44, 54, 62, 78

The author approved these structural rewrites. Only the four chapter files were edited. The word counts are for the prose below the notes block. The notes blocks (Beats, Carried forward and Draft notes) were updated wherever the structure changed what they describe. All four chapters still have no em dashes, none of the banned words, and no new "the way you / the way a" similes (several old ones were converted to "like" or "as" in the passages touched). "Ten paces" now appears only in 78.

## 62 Before the Storm (required): 3,379 → 3,526 words

- **Order of days.** The earlier fix for M9 had left the tents section (day 133) after the packing section (days 133–134). The tents now come first, on the afternoon of 133, and the packing follows from them. The chapter now runs strictly in order: 131 ford → 132 Morrow dies, Gunna, the night of writing → 133 morning papers, 133 afternoon tents → 133–134 packing and Fisk → 134 Bel → 134 night on the Scar → 135 smoke.
- **Cat recap cut.** The paragraph retelling the bridge memory (the white road, the tower, the cat, the partner bowing, the cat lowering its head) is gone. Calder now names the cat in one line, and Aurveth's answer (the M3 wording) is unchanged.
- **One forward line per scene.**
  - Ford: Gunna watches Calder, not Morrow (a plant for her).
  - Morrow's death: it ends on the riders coming down with ropes.
  - Gunna: she says she'll come for the written words in the morning and won't wait long.
  - Writing: Ada says "She said she'd not wait," and Calder runs for the Scar.
  - Papers: if the yards won't hear Gunna, the ring and chains are all he has left.
  - Tents: "It's starting" (unchanged).
  - Packing: Fisk tells him to get his own people on Liss's boat.
  - Bel: "You'll walk us down to the Staithe, the night before" (this matches 64).
  - Scar: Kharrak reports more fires, nearer.
  - Smoke: "Two days to the ferry" (unchanged).
- **Kept:** Morrow's dying, Gunna's reading plan (Varrhold, then Skarrow), Ada's "say it aloud" ruling, Bel's decision and the ferry date of 137, Calder's two reasons for staying, and the forge echo.
- **Facts moved or changed (all inside this chapter only):**
  - Gunna's pickup moves from "at dawn" to the morning; she is waiting on the Scar. Walking two leagues from Bel's after a night of writing couldn't reach the Scar by dawn.
  - The Garrows go on the evening of 133, once the tents are down, and the Hollins the next morning (they went the same day before). This ties to Tessa's decision in 54: they had been waiting on the ferry and took their cart to the Staithe to wait there. Nothing outside 62 dates either family's departure.
  - "Watched it from the Scar over three days" becomes "two days" (133–134).

## 78 Underground (required): 3,020 → 3,114 words

- **The meeting is untouched**, from the opening to "Twice. So I wouldn't forget."
- **Recap cut:** "There's no time… She told him. Fast. In pieces." and everything after it, down to Aurveth's "I knew". That covers Cassian's death told again, the list of the dead (Rook, Hale, Pell, Thorne, Lucan, Haverly), "both turned", "the island fell / the arch, gone", and Maren's long account of the Titan (sleeper, kin, door-keeper, chains, hate).
- **What replaces it** (only what's new between them):
  1. **Kin.** Orrun goes up the rubble to Aurveth and smells her. The bridge memory rises in her, now with Ennis's face (70). She bows her head to the stones, as Ennis bowed, and the lion lowers his, as the cat did (28). Kharrak bristles. Lisska relays that he's kin too, far back. "I'm not kin to anything." Kharrak lies down.
  2. **What each sees.** Maren, dry: Lisska talks about him all the way as if he'd know everything, and "I thought you'd know more." "So did she." Calder sees her hands, and blood to the elbow that isn't hers.
  3. **What's still unsaid.**
     - She asks only whether the other one turned his key. Calder answers: his hand was on the wrist, it turned anyway, and Corvin went up the stair. 79 needs her to know this.
     - He doesn't ask about Cassian, and she doesn't say.
     - He has the overheard talk from under the arch (68) on his tongue, and the cavern shakes and he holds it back: "It would keep. Till there was a sky over them."
  4. **The one new fact for Calder:** Maren's two-line version of what Orrun touched ("something that wants the world down"). Aurveth closes her eye: she has known since the ice. This pays off 44.
- **Getting out** is kept: Aurveth can't fly, Kharrak's "Then I'll DIG", Orrun's "up". One beat is added: Maren gives the lantern to Lisska, because in 79 Lisska carries it.
- **Kept for 79 and Book 2:**
  - the five links handed back
  - Wren alive at the ferry house, and her hello, twice
  - the first anchor-stone falling, with five chains left
  - Aurveth's wound and wing
  - Orrun opening the rock
  - "Fenn." / "Ashdown."
- **Kept untold:**
  - what he heard under the arch (Book 2 ch 2)
  - Ada's third verse and the arch's last line, "its own kin kept the door" (both told at sunrise in 79)
  - Rook's death, now dropped from 78 entirely. The A2 table only noted that 78 "doesn't tell" the arch; nothing later needs Maren to learn of Rook here.
- **Removed facts, and why that's safe:** the list of the dead and Maren's long account of the Titan. Both are on the page in 72–77 from the POV that witnessed them. 79 has Maren think of the Ruin's lock "with the farmer's hand on his wrist… Gone up an old stair", and that is still told.
- **Style:**
  - "ten paces" ×2 (down from 3; one became "across the gap")
  - converted: "the way a cart-bed trembles", "the way the floor of a barn moves", "the way you know weather", "the way her mother talked", "The way Aurveth had laid her throat"

## 44 The General's Silence (lighter): 2,677 → 2,584 words

- **Movement.** Aurveth's silence now pushes him into a decision. She sets him down above Bel's at dawn and goes on alone. Lisska asks what they'll tell his mother and Bel. Calder decides: nothing to anybody, and no claw on the island until Aurveth says; Kharrak gives his word. In the kitchen Ada asks what's on the river, and he says "Ice." She doesn't ask again. The old flat close ("She didn't speak the whole way. He didn't ask.") is replaced.
- **What it draws on:**
  - Kharrak's wish to dig, and Aurveth's threat (both already in the chapter)
  - Ada's refusal in 40, "Don't ask me again, Calder" (now recalled during the singing; 40 is in the Carried-forward)
  - the hearth-words (40, 46)
  - It leads straight into 46, where Bel asks and he says "I don't know… He told himself it was true."
- **Repetition cut:**
  - Aurveth's second "Do not ask me" and the standalone "*Do not ask.*" line after it
  - the second singing of "*Keep the watch below… And do not ask*" and the "*Do not ask me,* she'd said" echo
  - the recap paragraph during the singing (a voice in Crow Scar, the vast bound thing, bone set badly), reduced to the arch carving and Ada's refusal
  - "*Keep the watch below.*" repeated after it
- **Checks against 46:**
  - The two "do not ask"s that 46 counts on the ice are still there: Aurveth's line and the song.
  - The single millwheel that 46 calls back to is kept.
  - Aurveth still says nothing about it until 46.
- **Fixed:** Lisska "in the snow beside him" (she was a league and a half off) now reads "beside Kharrak".
- **Converted:** five "the way" similes, and named feelings trimmed ("frightened" in the narration).

## 54 The Gathering (lighter): 4,250 → 4,578 words

- **Brask** is now a bargain, not a speech:
  - Calder presses with "That's not who."
  - Brask trades the chest for his legs ("Your legs are his").
  - He gives the price on Calder and the charm-seller, and Kharrak presses down.
  - He holds back the knight with the lion until Calder gives his word that he'll walk.
  - The release at dawn is now Calder keeping that word against Kharrak ("You gave it. I didn't." / "Then you'll keep mine.").
  - Every fact Brask gave before is kept: Delling's company, no ring, ten silver, the man with the chest, the woman with the charm cart, two hundred alive for the farmer and for the knight with the lion, "Like you were a pair", Delling's farmer-first plan, everything going to the island. 64 and Book 2 ch 7, 14 and 30 rely on these.
  - The charm-seller is placed "at the Ram's Head", and she's still only "a woman with a cart", so she can't be confused with Halloran's drawer (58, 68).
- **The meeting** now ends on one decision instead of "Nobody decided". Tessa Garrow: the Garrows are going over, not on the ice with Davy, but on Liss's boat once the river's out and she's running. Wat gives way. Fisk's coda is unchanged.
  - **Consistency:** 55 breaks the ice on 119, and 62 has the ferry running again by 133, when the Garrows go first. 56's "when the ferry runs" for Wren now has a model, and 64 still has a near-empty Staithe on 137. No one leaves before 62, so nothing in 55–60 changes.
- **Growth:** about 330 words, all in the Brask exchange and the decision.
- **Style:** "Lisska's bewilderment" became "Lisska, going round and round"; two "the way" similes converted.
