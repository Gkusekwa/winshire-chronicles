# People, Places and Words

*Everyone here is as you first meet them. Nothing is said of what comes after.*

## Who's who

### Maren and Calder

**Maren Ashdown**: twenty-seven, a second-year at Greyhold, the knight school on the Hearthmere bank. She came late, after eight winters on the river watch. Unbonded, and she says she never wanted a bond.

**Calder Fenn**: thirty-two, a barley farmer at Harrow's End on the Saltreach bank. A widower with a young daughter. He pays his dues, keeps his head down and talks to animals like friends.

**The monster of the Old Ruin**: what both banks call whatever keeps the island in the river. Something breathes in its trees at night. Nobody lands to find out what.

**Aurveth**: Sigrun Holm's dragon, the largest of the three. Bronze gone green with age, with a gold eye the size of a cartwheel hub. Older than the hold, Sigrun says.

**Kharrak**: Dag Rorke's dragon. Rust-red, heavy in the shoulder and crossed with old scars. He doesn't like anybody.

**Lisska**: Imra Sallow's dragon. Lean, dark green and quick, with pale green eyes. She teases, and she likes anything small that isn't afraid of her.

### Greyhold

**Instructor Pell**: the weapons instructor. Long-boned and grey, with two fingers gone from his left hand. His one lesson is *subdue, don't kill*.

**Lucan Haverly**: twenty-one, gifted, well taught and bonded. Maren's rival for first lance. He means well, and he has never had to wait for anything.

**Kesh**: Haverly's falcon, who chose him when he was fifteen. She opens her wings at the worst moment because she thinks it's funny.

**Tobin**: nineteen, all elbows, Maren's friend. Unbonded like her and much louder about it.

**Ide Darrow and Bracken**: a quiet girl in Maren's year and her hound, brindled and waist-high, with one torn ear.

**Knight-Captain Ilse Rowan**: small, neat and grey, with ink worn into one finger. She sets the students' trials from a bare room at the top of the north tower.

**Warden Osric Hale**: past sixty, stooped, on an ash stick, with a ring of old keys at his belt. He keeps Greyhold's archive and asks careful questions.

**Captain Marta Thorne**: a captain at Greyhold. Her boar is black and the size of a hay cart, and she is the only person it doesn't want to kill, most days.

**Grieve**: master of the stables and the Long Barn, forty years at Greyhold. He is not a knight and will tell you so.

**Merrow**: an old knight, ten years retired from the gate, who sits in the Long Barn every morning and talks to his ox-beast.

**The grey cat**: the size of a calf, and owned by nobody. It sleeps in a different stall every night.

**Turnip**: the stables' old piebald, with one white eye and a dried turnip tied to his bridle. He is what the late-oath candidates get.

### Millbrook and the river

**Liss Ashdown**: Maren's mother. She has run the ferry from Greyhold landing to Tolly's Staithe for thirty years and knows every story on the river.

**Tam Ashdown**: nineteen, Maren's younger brother, fair where she is dark. Apprenticed to the smith at Millbrook.

**Maren's father**: a bonded knight. When a poacher's bolt killed his hound, he lasted four days. Maren was fourteen.

**Odo Marl**: the Millbrook smith and Tam's master. Huge and bald, with burned forearms and a laugh like a bellows. He complains about the price of charcoal.

**Sergeant Ottery**: Maren's old sergeant on the river watch at Redwatch. He never gets up for anyone below a captain.

**Tolly and Greta**: Tolly works the eel weirs at Tolly's Staithe, the Saltreach end of the ferry. Greta smokes the eels.

**Gannet**: a Saltreach boatman out of Wendmouth, with a silver ring in each ear and a smile that arrives first. He deals in live beasts.

**Davin**: the potboy at the Kettle in Millbrook. Sixteen, and he can read when most of the village can't.

### Harrow's End and Upper Rush

**Ada Fenn**: Calder's mother. She has a saying for every weather and knows more of the old songs than anyone in the village.

**Wren**: Calder's daughter, nine. She stores things up and asks about them later. She isn't afraid of dragons.

**Senna**: Calder's wife, dead of a fever five years before the book begins. Wren has her long, level look.

**Bel**: Calder's younger sister, married at Upper Rush. Quick and blunt, and she can read his face.

**Joss**: Bel's husband, a big, slow, gentle man who breeds dray-beasts.

**Pip and Nan**: Bel and Joss's two little ones, a boy and a girl.

**Sorrel**: Joss's young dray-mare, the colour of wet sand. She settles for Calder.

**Mabbin and Old Grey**: Joss's two grey dray-beasts, taken from his barn the night Upper Rush burns.

**Hob**: the miller at Harrow's End. His prices are how he says hello.

**Bramble**: the Fenns' cow. She has no opinions about the Clan.

**The Garrows**: old Garrow and his knees; his son Wat, who plays the drum at the fair; and Wat's boy Davy, ten and a half.

**Hollin**: an Upper Rush farmer who loses his ox-beast in the raid and says out loud what others only think.

**Old Fisk**: keeps the Eelweir, Harrow's End's fishing landing at the mouth of the beck, with his sons.

### The Dragon Clan at Varrhold

**Haldor Varr**: the Clan's chief. He is dead as the book opens, and his pyre smoke lies over the hills.

**Ulric Varr**: Haldor's brother. The best warrior in the Clan, with the most men in his hall. He never raises his voice.

**Arn Varr**: Haldor's elder son, about twenty-five. He has his father's hawk nose and his father's temper, and he claims the chair by birth.

**Teo Varr**: Haldor's younger son, twenty. Warm and well liked, and the one both sides will listen to.

**Sigrun Holm**: Aurveth's rider. An old war-leader with a scar through one eyebrow, who has ridden for the Clan near sixty years.

**Dag Rorke**: Kharrak's rider. Squat and scarred, head shaved, always cleaning his axe. To him, everyone is late.

**Imra Sallow**: Lisska's rider, and a scout. Small, wiry and laughing, and she can't pass a crowd without showing off.

**Gorm Askell**: the hold's steward. Long and sour, with ink on his fingers. He counts every sack twice and says the barley is damp.

**Rook**: one of the hired hands Ulric has taken on since the funeral. A quiet man with a face you forget. He says he is a drover from the coast.

**Hallam Brecc and Morrow**: the oldest rider on the ledges and his old black dragon, greying at the scales. They keep the old law that no bonded rider fights kin.

**The priest**: the Clan's priest, old and grey, with a staff. At Coldtarn it is his place to ask, "Who pours?"

### The Restoration

**Vale**: the name the crowds give the Restoration's leader. A warm, grey-eyed speaker who can fill the Tidesteps at Wendmouth.

**Absalom Reede**: a travelling bookbinder, grey and soft-spoken, who mends ledgers up and down the river and talks at the Kettle about the one realm.

**The coin man**: a soft-spoken stranger in a good dark coat who pays in new silver.

**The Captain**: a broad, short-haired Saltreach woman who brings a band of hunters across to the island by raft.

### Others

**Captain Idris Vey**: small, neat and spectacled, with a soft voice. His sellsword company out of Wendmouth holds the Tide Council's commission for the Marches.

**Oswin Rusk**: the Halloran house's factor on Gallows Quay, plump and neat in dark red. He writes every price in a little book.

**Brack**: landlord of the Ram's Head, huge and silent. He settles fights by standing up.

**Tuft and Fell**: wool-carting brothers from Ewe Hollow who can talk about sheep for two days.

## Places and words

**Winshire**: the old name for the whole land, from before it split into six kingdoms. Most people only meet it in songs.

**The Six Kingdoms**: what people say now. Book 1 lies where two of them meet, Hearthmere and Saltreach.

**Hearthmere**: the kingdom on the west bank of the Wend. Green and rich, with a crown and its knightly orders, where bonded and unbonded knights serve as equals, or so the charter says.

**Saltreach**: the far bank. A league of port-cities with no king, which everyone calls a kingdom anyway. It has no knights; sellswords do its fighting.

**The Marches**: the border country along the Wend, on both banks.

**The Wend**: the river between Hearthmere and Saltreach. It runs brown, and in winter it freezes from the edges in.

**Greyhold**: the knight school on the Hearthmere bank, where the Wend bends. A quarry, then a fort, then a school. The charter over its gate says the bonded and the unbonded serve as equals.

**The Old Ruin**: broken arches and the stump of a tower on a wooded island where the Wend splits, upriver of Greyhold. The stumps of an old bridge show in a dry summer. The river watch may not land there, and river folk keep off it.

**The river watch**: Hearthmere's boat patrol on the Wend. Its long boats work out of the towers on the River Road.

**The River Road**: the Hearthmere bank road from Millbrook north to Northwatch, with a tower every few leagues. Redwatch, of red sandstone, keeps the watch's boats.

**Millbrook**: a Hearthmere river village below Greyhold, with a great mill, a smithy, a tavern called the Kettle, and a jetty where Saltreach boats tie up without asking.

**The Brackenwood**: the old royal forest west of Greyhold. Taking a beast out of it is a hanging offence.

**Harrow's End**: Calder's village on the Saltreach bank. Thirty-one houses, a mill, and a shrine to the gods at the crossroads. Every field looks across the water at the Old Ruin.

**Upper Rush**: four farms in a hollow above the river, two leagues upriver of Harrow's End.

**Hollow Beck**: the stream that comes down from the Scarfell past Varrhold, turns Hob's wheel, and meets the Wend opposite the Old Ruin.

**The Drover's Way**: the sheep and grain road from Harrow's End up Hollow Beck to Varrhold. Two days by cart, with the Ram's Head inn at the halfway.

**The Scarfell**: high moor and black crags north-east of the Marches. Dragon country.

**Varrhold**: the Clan's hold under the Scarfell's cliffs. Black-stone longhouses inside a turf rampart, a chief's hall roofed with shed dragon scale, and above it the Roosts, dragon caves scorched black at the mouths.

**Coldtarn**: a black tarn high above Varrhold that never freezes. The Clan burns its dead on the shore and gives them to the water.

**Wendmouth**: the port-city at the mouth of the Wend, a hundred leagues south. Anyone may speak on its Tidesteps, and anything with a pulse is sold on Gallows Quay.

**Beast**: a creature with enough mind to bond. A cow or a heron is only an animal.

**Dragon**: four legs and two wings. The Clan breeds them by the hundred and names them, but a dragon chooses its own rider.

**Wyvern**: two legs, two wings and a barbed tail, smaller than a dragon and not clever. Saltreach companies tame them with a hood, a hook and an empty belly. Don't call a dragon one in front of the Clan.

**Dray-beast**: the farms' working beast, eight feet at the shoulder and more, three times the weight of a horse. Not a horse. Some farmers swear their greys understand them.

**Duskcat**: a long, low cat, black or smoke-grey, with eyes like lamps. It lives at the far end of the Brackenwood, where nobody goes.

**The Dragon Clan**: holds the Saltreach side of the Marches from Varrhold. Its warriors ride dragons. Its chiefs come from the Varr, whose badge is a black hound.

**Dues and the levy**: what Marches farmers pay the Clan, grain at harvest and wool at shearing. In return the Clan keeps the sellswords out of the valley. A levy is whatever more the hold sends for, as when a chief dies.

**Bond**: a beast's choice of a person, never the other way round. The two speak mind to mind and feel what the other feels. Without a bond, people and beasts can't understand each other.

**One life between two**: the oldest law there is. When one of a bonded pair dies, the other usually follows.

**Binding**: forcing a beast to obey from outside, with chain, pain and will. It looks like a bond and isn't one. It is forbidden, and a binding is never a bond.

**The unchosen**: people no beast has chosen. At Greyhold, the unbonded.

**Late oath**: how the knight schools take a few older, unbonded candidates. Their names go at the bottom of the lists.

**First lance**: the student who rides at the front of the autumn patrol and is read first at muster. No rule says it goes to the bonded. It does anyway.

**The oath**: said at noon every day at Greyhold, bonded and unbonded together. *I will hold the river. I will keep the road. I will stand between the weak and what would harm them, and I will keep the watch below, and I will not ask what I keep.*

**Subdue, don't kill**: the first rule of the orders after the oath, carved over Greyhold's armoury door. Never kill a beast if there is any other way.

**Noted**: one word on a report sent up the orders. Pell says it means nothing will be done. Rowan says it means nothing yet.

**Licensed words**: Hearthmere's magic. Healers, lamp-tenders and knights may say only the words their licence allows, and Greyhold's lamps burn without oil. Unlicensed words earn a fine.

**The Restoration**: what Wendmouth calls the people following a man who says the six kingdoms are rotten and the land should be one again, as it was before the six. Its watchword is *One again*.

**The old song**: Ada's lullaby, *Before the six there was one*. The old ones in it are the gods, who walked the land unbound. It ends *till the six are one again*.
