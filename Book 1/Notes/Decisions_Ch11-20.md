# Decisions made while drafting Chapters 11–20 (to carry into the Bible and later chapter notes)

All of these are proposals until you accept them.

## Timeline
- Rowan sets the trial on day -1. Harrow's End's fair is **day 0**; Maren lands on the island at dusk on day 0. The ford is noon on day 4 ("four days from tonight"). Teo's pyre is dusk on day 5. The Clan splits on day 6. Riders come to Harrow's End's green on day 7, and that dusk Calder, at the Eelweir, sees the patrol boats ("two days they've been at it"). Maren hides from the boats on days 6 and 7; on day 8 they don't come. The battle, the bonds and the lion's waking all happen on the night of day 8. Chapter 20 is day 9.
- Maren first hears the breathing on night 5, so by the battle she has heard it four nights.
- The new moon (Rowan's deadline) is day 14, six days after the battle night. Full moon about day 29.

## Calder's side
- **The ford on Hollow Beck** is two leagues below Varrhold, about eight leagues up the Drover's Way from Harrow's End. Calder and Hob leave on Hob's cart at two in the morning and arrive an hour before noon; they're back at the Eelweir in the small hours of day 5. On day 5 the hold sends for Calder mid-morning and he rides up before noon for the dusk pyre.
- Teo's pyre: one night's burning at Varrhold. Ulric stands outside the gate and is not let in. The three dragons circle the pyre and call.
- Imra gives Calder back his coat. The chain is still in the pocket.
- Rook drew his wages the night before the ford, left before light toward the river, and stole a horse. Calder lies for the first time ("he owes me money"). This is where the guilt starts.
- The split: Arn holds Varrhold. Ulric goes to his own steading, **Skarrow**, east of the Scarfell (a new place, to add to the map), with most of the fighting men. Dag and Imra side with Arn. Sigrun goes with Ulric as the Clan's war-leader.
- **The dragons in the war:** the Clan keeps hundreds of dragons in the Roosts; about a dozen are bonded. Old Clan law: no bonded rider fights kin. After Teo's death the other bonded pairs shut themselves in the Roosts and won't fly for either side. Sigrun, Dag and Imra, the war-leader and her two, break the law, so the war has three bonded war dragons (Hob: "three war dragons, and a cliff full of them sitting on their hands").
- Steward **Gorm Askell** speaks for Arn. A red-bearded man (unnamed) speaks for Ulric and pays in silver.
- Hob sells forty sacks of flour to Ulric. **Wat Garrow** (old Garrow's son, father of ten-year-old Davy; his wife Tessa is west-side born) and the Ashby widow side with Arn. Calder sells to both sides at the old price and won't name anyone.
- The battle: Ulric's carts come at night for the flour. Arn fires the mill and the flour. Sigrun comes in on Aurveth.
  - Dag is taken off Kharrak by Aurveth.
  - Kharrak, falling, rakes Aurveth's back and kills Sigrun.
  - Imra is shot through the throat by a **crossbow** bolt from beside the hedge. The Clan doesn't use crossbows. Who fired it is still open.
- Calder's injuries: broken ribs, a gash on his left leg, a cut on his head. He gets them when Kharrak's tail takes out the byre wall.
- The bonds take in this order: Kharrak, Aurveth, Lisska. As each one forms, he lives that rider's last moment:
  - Dag: fury.
  - Sigrun: "not like this, not by him".
  - Imra: cold, and Teo's name.
- **The marks:** three marks over his heart that overlap like coins. Aurveth's is bronze and broad. Kharrak's is rust-coloured and jagged. Lisska's is green and small.
- **How the bond voices are written:** in italics, with no quote marks. Aurveth uses long sentences, Kharrak uses fragments, and Lisska asks questions. They hear what he says to them and feel what he feels, but they don't hear every thought.
- Aurveth is "more than three hundred years old". Before Calder she had been bonded three times (her first lost from memory; Ragnald Holm, died 61 years ago; Sigrun, about 59 years, who was 76 at the mill), and had felt two of those bonds break; Sigrun's is the third.
- Ada sees the marks and gives a look that recalls the way she sings the song's last verse too fast. This sets up Chapter 22.
- A lone Clan rider sees the three dragons alive at midday on day 9, and from then the news is out.
- Bramble gets out of the byre; Ada finds her in the beck pasture on day 9.
- At dusk the dragons burn their own riders where they fell. Kharrak burns Dag among the roofs below the mill.

## Maren's side
- The island:
  - The tracks are four-toed, as broad as a round shield, with claws.
  - The hunters' camp is on the north-west shore under a bluff. It holds eleven coils of chain, forty stakes, a cage and a lean-to.
  - She moves into the tower and hides the skiff in the reeds of the west channel.
  - From night 5 she hears the beast breathing eleven times a quarter of an hour, and faster when the dragons call.
- She hides from the Greyhold patrol boats on days 6–7. Fenna, a student in her year, and Brannoch are aboard.
- **The hunters:** twelve of them, led by a woman with a Saltreach voice called **the Captain**. They burn clay pots of sweet honey-smoke to keep the beast asleep. That explains the cold fires.
- With the twelve is a thirteenth man, giving no orders: **Rook is with them.** Maren doesn't know his name and notes only his boots with the brass eyelets. He says "We won't need to." Use this in Chapter 21, and in 58 when Calder sees Rook again.
- **Scale of the lion (your decision):** about 10 ft at the shoulder in Book 1 ("nearly twice a man's height"), towering over people. Its forefoot is as broad as a round shield. It grows toward dragon size over the series. Titans are mountain-sized.
- **The lion's power (your decision):** earth and stone, visible elemental magic that grows with its size. In Book 1 it shows only faintly: the ground heaves when it turns over, pebbles jump and stone cracks when it roars. Later the earth itself moves. Its mane is iron-grey, nearly white at the roots, and its eyes are pale.
- **The chaining goes wrong.** Three men die: one crushed, one opened by a claw, one caught in their own jaw-trap. Maren puts out the leaf fire in the hollow. The beast doesn't wake.
- Her skiff is cut loose and lost downstream, so she's stranded.
- **The lion wakes** at the dragons' death-screams. It roars, which is the roar Calder hears, then smells the honey-smoke on her cloak. The trailing stake jams in the bridge stones. She has its throat at sword's length and lowers the sword. It strikes her once, with its claws half out: three cuts down her left shoulder and arm, and she goes into the west channel. She climbs onto the first pier of the old bridge.
- **Pell's sword is lost in the river.** This matters for Chapter 23 and for Pell later.
- The lion tears the stake free and opens its forefoot. It limps south along the west shore toward the spit with one loop of chain still on, which is the wound in Chapter 21.

## Name fixes made after drafting
- Harrow's End's alehouse is **the Wheel**, which Hob keeps by the mill. **The Kettle** is Millbrook's tavern on the Hearthmere bank.
- Harrow's End's landing is **the Eelweir**, at the mouth of Hollow Beck opposite the Old Ruin, kept by **old Fisk** and his sons. **Tolly's Staithe** is the ferry's Saltreach jetty opposite Greyhold, about five leagues downriver of Harrow's End; Tolly lives there and doesn't appear in Harrow's End scenes.
- The grown farmer is **Wat Garrow**; **Davy Garrow** is ten, Wren's friend.
- Harrow's End is about a league and a half across the water from the island. On the Saltreach bank the roads are the river lane and the Drover's Way; the River Road is only the Hearthmere bank road.
- Arn's steward is **Gorm Askell**. **Odo Marl** is still Tam's smith master in Millbrook.
