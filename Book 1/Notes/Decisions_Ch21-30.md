# Decisions made while drafting Chapters 21–30 (to carry into the Bible and later chapter notes)

## Your decisions (world rules)
- **The lion's size:** about 10 ft at the shoulder in Book 1, towering over people. It grows toward dragon size over the series. Titans are mountain-sized.
- **The lion's power:** earth and stone, visible elemental magic that grows with its size.
  - In Book 1 it is faint. The ground heaves when it rises or turns. Pebbles jump and stone cracks when it roars. The earth groans under the tower when it rams it. The ground hums like a struck bowl when it watches Aurveth fly over.
  - Later the earth itself moves.
- **The forests:** Winshire's forests are vast and thick enough to hide a dragon. The new forest is **the Fellwood**: oak at the bottom, black pine higher up, covering the lower Scarfell east of Harrow's End for leagues. Crow Scar stands up out of it. Bears there stand taller than a house's eaves, and boar grow the size of dray-beasts.
- **Beast scale:** apart from small personal beasts (falcons, hounds, martens, cats), most beasts are 8 ft or more.
  - Dray-beasts 8 ft and more at the shoulder. Greyharts (white war-mounts) 9 ft. The lion about 10 ft ("nearly twice a man's height").
  - Dragons: Lisska about 12 ft at the shoulder and 40 ft nose to tail, the smallest but far bigger than a dray-beast, bear or boar; Kharrak about 16 ft; Aurveth about 20 ft. Wyverns are smaller than Lisska.
- **The Clan's dragons:** hundreds in the Roosts, about a dozen bonded. Under the old law no bonded rider fights kin, so after Teo's death the other bonded pairs shut themselves in the Roosts. Sigrun, Dag and Imra broke the law, so the war has three bonded war dragons. Arn about 200 spears, Ulric about 450.
  - **Chapter 4 (done, commit d7e7829):** dray-beasts now stand "eight feet at the shoulder and more, taller than a man could reach".
- **The lion and Maren can't talk.** Respect is a process, and the lion tries to kill her more than once. She defends herself, hurting it only where it does no harm.

## People (new or defined)
- **Captain:**
  - The Captain (hunters): Saltreach voice. She has gone to Wendmouth for money, and says she'll come back "with men enough to take this whole island apart".
- **Clan, Saltreach and Marches:**
  - **Sten Raske:** Ulric's red-bearded man.
  - **Gorm Askell:** Arn's steward.
  - **Lieutenant Corrin Ash:** Captain Idris Vey's company, out of Wendmouth, hiring at Cotter's Cross under the Tide Council's standing commission (the Council itself hasn't heard yet). Carries a hooded hawk.
  - **The coin man:** soft-spoken, in a good dark coat, pays in new silver with the ring of six struck on the coin's edge. Not Rook. He bought Calder's road and hour from Hob.
  - **Hesketh Coley** (old, deaf) and **Rue** (charcoal burners under Crow Scar). Rue's man died carting for Ulric at the mill.
  - **Wat Garrow:** old Garrow's son, Davy's father; wife **Tessa**, and a baby. His cows, his farm. **Davy Garrow** is ten (Ch 2); Calder taught him to tickle trout, and saves him in Ch 30.
  - **Old Fisk** and his sons keep **the Eelweir**, Harrow's End's landing at the mouth of Hollow Beck. (Tolly lives at Tolly's Staithe, opposite Greyhold.)
  - **Benna Hode**, 80, burned in her house by Ulric's men.
  - **Hessa:** Calder's grandmother, who kept a jackdaw.
- **Greyhold:**
  - **Grieve:** Master of Stables. Unbonded, forty years at Greyhold.
  - **Ide Darrow:** the Darrow girl, a second-year in Maren's year. Her hound is **Bracken**: brindled, waist-high, one torn ear.
  - **Captain Thorne:** her black boar is the size of a hay cart.
  - **Merrow:** retired knight with an ox-beast.
  - **Vasey:** the greyhart's rider, who breaks his wrist.
  - **Fenna** and **Brannoch:** river watch.
  - **Warden Osric Hale:** past sixty, tall and stooped, short white beard, bad hip, ash stick. Wears a ring of keys with one long black key among them. Keeps the archive. **Unbonded** (proposal).
- The "Warden" title is kept for the inner circle, so the stables are run by the "Master of Stables".

## Places
- **The Fellwood:** the new forest under the Scarfell east of Harrow's End.
- **Crow Scar:** a black crag in the Fellwood with a cave three-quarters up its face. It is already in `marches_items.py` for chapter 16; move it to chapter 24 and draw it standing out of the forest.
- **Skarrow:** Ulric's steading, east of Varrhold over the Scarfell, about thirteen leagues from Crow Scar.
- **The Eelweir:** Harrow's End's landing at the mouth of Hollow Beck, opposite the Old Ruin.
- **Low Garth, the Rathe farm and Benna Hode's:** west-side farms burned by Ulric's men.
- **The Long Barn:** at Greyhold, against the north wall. The midden is behind it.
- **The archive:** under the north tower at Greyhold.
- **The Wheel:** Harrow's End's alehouse. It burned with the mill on battle night (chapter 18); what was left of it burned again in chapter 30, along with Hob's house, the Garrows' farm and the cottages by the beck.
- **The island landing:** on the south spit, the tumbled paving of an old landing stair comes down from the fort's south wall. The collar-chain was staked at its foot (chapter 21).

## Story threads
- **Hob:** he sold Calder's road and hour to the coin man. Calder pulls him out of his burning house; Hob says nothing.
- **Wren's blue ribbon** is on Lisska's left horn.
- **Ada's seventh verse:** taught her by her father's mother in her own house, sworn on that hearthstone. Ada has admitted it exists but won't sing it "till I'm sure".
- **The chain:** Calder has told the dragons about it, and about Rook. Kharrak: "Then we'll find out. All of them."
- **Aurveth's partners:** first unknown; Ragnald Holm (died 61 years ago; she lay a year on Coldtarn's shore, then a year in her cave); Sigrun, chosen at the Roosts about two years after, just come of age, rode her 59 years. Bonded three times before Calder.
- **Aurveth's memory:** the white bridge, the Oneroad and the fire on the tower. The great grey cat on the tower steps, to which her first partner bowed. She believes "someone has cut it out" of her memory.
- **The bond's reach:** speech carries 3–4 leagues; feeling carries much further.
- **Holding three:** headaches and nosebleeds. In chapter 30 their rage floods into him and he nearly beats a man to death. Lisska stops him.
- **Hale's interest:** he goes still at the dug-and-refilled hollow, the ground's response and the dragon shadow. He asks whether it was bronze.
- **Rowan:** wrote something in the margin of her ledger.
- **Hearthmere knights:** at Harrow's End's second burning, a line of about twenty, mostly on horses with a greyhart or two, watched from the far bank all night and didn't cross. One lifted a hand.
