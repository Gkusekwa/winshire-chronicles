## The twins (Restoration leader)
- The crowd calls him **Vale**. In public, Cassian Vale speaks on the Tidesteps at Wendmouth: warm, laughing, touches people, remembers names.
- **Corvin Vale** directs the Ferriby raid from the smithy roof, unmasked, with signals relayed by a boy with a whistle, "like a game of stones". He is the thin man from the Chapter 5 roadblock.
- Both have a long pale face, dark short hair and grey eyes. Corvin is still; Cassian is warm.
- Corvin knows Maren's name: "Ashdown. I hear you sit with lions." Rowan concludes someone inside told him.
- Nobody in Book 1 knows there are two until late. Calder can't make the hundred leagues add up. The carter says: "Then he's got wings."
- The inner circle hears it too: on day 53 Hale tells Maren that Vale spoke on the Tidesteps at noon on the day of Ferriby, and files it under the river's old "man in two places" story. Maren lets it go.

## The Restoration's method (new, proposal)
- **Forced binding:** a beast in a six-dot collar is drugged with honey-smoke while a man in grey chants over it. An unchosen man with an iron ring kneels with his hand on the beast's head and says "You're chosen now".
  - The beast's eyes go empty, "like windows".
  - The beasts fight without sound or pain, all moved "as if by one hand".
  - This is canon's "a binding is not a bond".
  - A binding gives the beast the binder's will, not his mind (36: "Only what he wanted. His will, put on the beast like a collar."). One handler can drive a pack: a man in grey calls the time for fifty beasts in the camp fields (38), and a man in grey in a barge stern moves six hounds with his arm (39).
- **Camps:** along the drove road up the Wend there are refugee camps that give out soup and blankets, with drill fields behind them full of bound beasts and young men with rings.
- **Signs of a real bond:** a bound hound looks up at Kharrak passing overhead, and for a moment something of its own comes back into its eyes. This is a hint for later.
- **The rings:** iron rings stamped with six dots, given "when you stand up". Odo Marl made a gross of them, and "half my trade this year is mending their chain".
- **Absalom Reede:** a travelling bookbinder at the Kettle who runs the Restoration meeting in Fennick's tithe barn behind Millbrook's mill.

## Inside Greyhold
- A rider with Greyhold's seal cleared the river a second time, for Ferriby, in a hand Ottery doesn't know. Rowan's new order: boats move only on her own written order.
- **Hale's secrets:**
  - **The black door** at the end of the archive: three steps down, black oak with three iron bands, no handle, and a lock as big as a man's hand. The long black key on Hale's belt fits it.
  - **The Watch list** begins "From the closing". Each Watcher's name has a door-mark beside it, and the list ends "Given into the keeping of the crown" in the year of the charter.
  - **His slip:** "the inner circle keeps a watch nobody else is told…"
  - **The Last Titan:** the river lullaby everyone knows ("the last great beast lay down below"; "hush now, the last one's sleeping") once had a Watch story behind it, now forgotten, found only in the black book's margin and on an old Millbrook ballad-sheet. "The Watch had a name for it. The Last Titan." Said once, not explained, and never called one of the old ones. Hale stops at "too big, too angry, too…" and goes grey when Maren speaks the verse. He believed "the last sleeps below the stone" was an epitaph, and never read "let no hand wake it" because the ink had run on that line in her notebook.
  - **Why he's afraid:** "Because he's three…" The archive's oldest books say that when the old signs come all at once, "the last time it was so…" He stops. It's about omens, not another man with three bonds.
- **Merrow** (91) and his ox-beast **Dun** die together on day 56. They are the gentle face of one life between two. Merrow swore Hale into the inner circle forty years ago, when Merrow was past fifty, and "kept the watch sixty years".
- **Ide Darrow and Bracken:** Ide is a second-year in Maren's year; Bracken is brindled, waist-high, one torn ear. Bracken is killed at Redwatch by a crossbow bolt (crossbows again). Ide withers over three days, and her mother comes too late, on the afternoon of day 65; Ide dies at dusk that day. The cat nobody owns lies on her bed from the second night.
- **Pell** has a torn leg and a crutch. He brings back a bound hound's six-dot collar, which goes to Rowan, who writes.
- Haverly apologises to Maren for not standing beside her.

## Calder
- **Captain Idris Vey:** small, neat, about fifty, grey-gloved, spectacles, soft voice. His company is 120 men with bolt-throwers. His two hooded wyverns are **Sable** and **Hask**, a little smaller than Lisska.
- **The Chimney:** the escape route out of Crow Scar, a split through the crag, wide at the bottom and narrowing to a squeeze in the middle, to a ledge on the sheer east face barely big enough for the three dragons and Calder.
- **Wendmouth:** about a hundred leagues from Greyhold, about 120 by the drove road; ten-odd leagues a day on foot.
  - The four trading houses are Marrow, Quill, Strand and Halloran.
  - The Tidesteps.
  - The Low Lantern doss-house.
  - The Crane tavern.
  - Gallows Quay's Halloran beast-office, run by factor **Oswin Rusk**.
- **What Calder frees at Gallows Quay:**
  - The hounds, martens and white hump-ox.
  - A wild wyvern.
  - A **unicorn colt** from Sarovel, silver-black. It looks back at him "as if remembering him".
- **What he leaves:** the **phoenix feather** that never cools, in Rusk's window. The glass over it is warm.
- **The price on his head:** 200 silver from the Halloran house and the Tide Council, with a likeness posted on the Tidesteps, at Cotter's Cross and on the post of Harrow's End's crossroads shrine.
- **Mabbin** comes home to Joss and Sorrel at Upper Rush on the night of day 63; Ada and Wren are there at Bel's.
- **Kharrak** after Wendmouth: silent for four days, then on day 55, at the second camp, "Those are what they want us to be", then "Don't. Or take me."
- **Aurveth's memories:** the Oneroad milestones count to the bridge, the middle of the world ("Nineteen to the bridge"). Her first partner's voice comes back, but not his face. She was hatched in the old hatching caves above Coldtarn.
- **Lisska:**
  - Nine years ago Imra landed her on the island on Brann Holt's dare, and she remembers the arch.
  - She can now "push" memories through the bond on purpose.
  - Aurveth reads the old script by instinct: "the last … great … sleeps below the stone … let no hand wake it" (Maren had already found "great", Ch 13). Lisska's memory goes before the last line, so Calder does not get "keep the watch below"; that waits for Ada's verse in Chapter 46.
  - Lisska sheds a wing scale on day 72, as broad as a round shield and thin as slate, still warm. Calder keeps it inside his coat (it is the warm scale Maren finds in Chapter 43).
- **Aurveth** says only that the island "cut" her; nothing yet about what is below (saved for Chapter 44).
- **Ada:** goes white when Calder says the verse, and won't explain: "Not yet. Don't ask me again, Calder. Not tonight."
- **Harrow's End** is half empty, with nine of thirty houses lived in. Hob has vanished. Wat Garrow is rebuilding, with old Garrow, the Ashby widow and old Fisk and his sons at the Eelweir. The soft-spoken coin man came asking at Ada's door, so Ada and Wren are at Bel's at **Upper Rush**.
- **The old hatching caves** above Coldtarn are the dragons' winter lie-up.
- Calder will cross the ice to the island alone when the Wend freezes.
