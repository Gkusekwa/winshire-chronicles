# Rulings made during the Chapters 41–79 review (drafter's calls; flagged for the author)

*All confirmed by the author (Oct 2026), as drafted.*

1. Pushing a sight to a bonded dragon on purpose (Ch 42) is allowed, the same way memories are shown on purpose: effort, intent, consent. Live sight is not shared otherwise.
2. The crossed hammers are Odo Marl's stamp. Confirmed.
3. "Three hundred years" is the chains' age; Aurveth's age stays "more than three hundred". No link is stated.
4. Watch is a subset of the inner circle. Rowan is inner circle, not Watch; Thorne is Watch.
5. Dragons can speak to each other mind to mind as kin, without riders, within sight. Aurveth hears Morrow (60). Humans hear only their own bonded.
6. A bound beast whose binder is gone goes slack and follows any hand; it may slowly half-wake (Old Grey). Proposal.
7. Redwatch stands on the Hearthmere bank opposite the island, half a league across the west channel (Atlas moved to 174.3, 101.9). Greyhold is about three leagues downriver of it. Ch 25, 29, 35, 37, 39 changed to match.
8. The army's route: Cotter's Cross → Ram's Head → down the Drover's Way → Harrow's End → the island.
9. "Brother" for a sister's husband is ordinary Marches usage.
10. Wendmouth stays 100 leagues straight, 120 by road (as reconciled after 11–40); Ch 50 restored to "a hundred and twenty leagues on the drove road".
11. Sigrun was 76 at the mill (17 + 59).
12. Rowan's room is at the top of the north tower (Ch 11 changed).

# Rulings after the 71–79 review
13. Kin-speech (ruling 5) extends to the great sentient beasts: the lion and a dragon can speak mind to mind as "far kin". Humans still hear only their own bonded.
14. There are two keys to the black door: the archivist's (Hale's) and the Watch-captain's (Thorne's). Callis takes Thorne's.
15. The Titan's pull "toward somewhere a long way off" is the only Book 1 hint of the five sleepers.
16. Lisska spends day 143 hidden at the Brackenwood edge watching the lion, too frightened to go down; she comes to Maren after the bond (Ch 73).

# Rulings from the October 2026 revision
*The author's decisions, binding on the revision. Each changes or fixes canon, and each is carried into the Bible.*

21. No casting by Maren or Calder in Book 1: Maren's settling-word and stanching-word (Ch 5, 19, 21) are cut, and bond gifts are not casting. The rule in `Magic.md` §10a stands.
22. Maren joined the river watch at 17 and served eight winters on it, then two years at Greyhold; she is 27 ("four winters" becomes "eight winters").
23. Island trips start at Redwatch (Ruling 7 stands): Redwatch is on the Hearthmere bank opposite the island, half a league across the west channel; Greyhold is about three leagues downriver.
24. Haverly's trial across the river is a known exception: out of uniform, without the pin, and "you were never there".
25. Kesh chose Haverly at fifteen (Ch 3 changed from fourteen), at his family's mews at home, before he came to Greyhold. He came to Greyhold two years before ch 61.
26. Renames: Merrow's ox-beast Holm becomes **Dun**; the Ch 33 sergeant of the Redwatch tower, Brask, becomes **Tasker**.
27. Ch 16: "ten or eleven" other bonded riders, to agree with the eleven pairs on the ledges.
28. The other Titans: the hints in Ch 77 ("There were destroyers once…") and Ch 79 ("It wants them awake") are cut; the pull "toward somewhere a long way off" is the only hint (Ruling 15).
29. The keys: the Watch-captain's key (Thorne's) opens the black door and turns the knight's lock, once for each of the Watch on the night they're sworn; Callis took it from Thorne at the gate and Cassian turned the knight's lock with it (77). Corvin's key for the Ruin's lock is older, its bow cut with the same hooked marks as the boss (74); where he got it is a Book 2 thread. Hale's key is the archivist's.
30. The countdown: the twins count to marks on a candle or glass, not breaths; Cassian says "four" only when the flame touches the mark.
31. Odo's hundred collars were ordered "in the spring, before the summer" (65, 71).
32. The drawer at the gate is Halloran's, lent to Delling; Calder recognises her from Gallows Quay (34).
33. Rowan took every rider who could sit a horse back up the River Road to Redwatch at dusk on day 143, so she isn't in Greyhold when the gate falls (75).
34. The Fellow, the ring-broken Isvaren drawer, dies when the cavern floor drops (76); Calder's view ties him to Isvaren.
35. Rook's ledger went into Delling's chest (72 or 74), for Book 2.
36. Ch 67: Maren goes halfway down toward Rowan's column; a greyhart scents Orrun, and she turns back.
37. Calendar: Book 1 runs from the harvest fair at the end of summer (day 0) to sunrise on day 144, in early spring; midwinter is about day 88. The prose seasons govern; the day numbers in the notes run fast after midwinter and are kept as an order, not a dated calendar. Book 2 runs about a year from early spring (midsummer about its day 98, midwinter about day 281); Book 3 runs from spring to the end of the next winter, its final battle in deep winter.
38. *The Six Who Sleep* is retired as a title option. "The Unbound" stays both Book 1's working title and the seventh ring's name for now (superseded by 45).
39. `Book 2/Chapter_Plan.md` (revision 4) and `Book 3/Chapter_Plan.md` (revision 1) replace the Outline files. Book 2 has three POVs (S Maren, F Calder, R Idony); Book 3 seven (S, F, R, G Rowan, C Arn, U Tobin, M Kit Merrin, a new Blackwater reef-pilot whose family carries Maren and Calder over the sea). Both books have since been drafted (see their plans).
40. **These are beast books, not dragon books** (the author). Dragons are one kind of beast among many, the Dragon Clan's. Every kingdom's own beasts carry weight in its chapters, its set pieces and the war; the sky is shared (dragons, Worldsend rocs, Isvaren stormkites, Saltreach wyverns, every tower's falcons; `Bestiary.md`, The sky).
41. **The lock's sentence** (Book 2 ch 81, approved): "One hand shut it. Two hands open it together. What is opened together is kept together, or it is not kept."
42. **Book 2's climax** (approved): Maren sends Orrun and every shadow to the cellars and gives up the door to save the people there (Book 2 ch 83–84).
43. **Corvin's key and the older stair** (approved): the twins' father, the unchosen knight of the gate, found both while digging a drain under the grooms' yard (Book 2 ch 76–77).
44. **The Bridle fails on one cause**, Idony's wrong stroke (Book 3). Tam refuses to forge its six-hollow ring and is sent off the works.
45. **Titles:** the seventh ring keeps "the Unbound"; Book 1's working title is *The Watch Below*; Book 2's Part Seven is "Under the Hill".
