# Rulings made during the Chapters 41–79 review (drafter's calls; flagged for the author)

1. Pushing a sight to a bonded dragon on purpose (Ch 42) is allowed, the same way memories are shown on purpose: effort, intent, consent. Live sight is not shared otherwise.
2. The crossed hammers are Odo Marl's stamp. Confirmed.
3. "Three hundred years" is the chains' age; Aurveth's age stays "more than three hundred". No link is stated.
4. Watch is a subset of the inner circle. Rowan is inner circle, not Watch; Thorne is Watch.
5. Dragons can speak to each other mind to mind as kin, without riders, within sight. Aurveth hears Morrow (60). Humans hear only their own bonded.
6. A bound beast whose binder is gone goes slack and follows any hand; it may slowly half-wake (Old Grey). Proposal.
7. Redwatch stands on the Hearthmere bank opposite the island, half a league across the west channel (Atlas moved to 174.3, 101.9). Greyhold is about three leagues downriver of it. Ch 25, 29, 35, 37, 39 changed to match.
8. The army's route: Cotter's Cross → Ram's Head → down the Drover's Way → Harrow's End → the island.
9. "Brother" for a sister's husband is ordinary Marches usage.
10. Wendmouth stays 100 leagues straight, 120 by road (as reconciled after 11–40); Ch 50 restored to "a hundred and twenty leagues on the drove road".
11. Sigrun was 76 at the mill (17 + 59).
12. Rowan's room is at the top of the north tower (Ch 11 changed).

# Rulings after the 71–79 review
13. Kin-speech (ruling 5) extends to the great sentient beasts: the lion and a dragon can speak mind to mind as "far kin". Humans still hear only their own bonded.
14. There are two keys to the black door: the archivist's (Hale's) and the Watch-captain's (Thorne's). Callis takes Thorne's.
15. The Titan's pull "toward somewhere a long way off" is the only Book 1 hint of the five sleepers.
16. Lisska spends day 143 hidden at the Brackenwood edge watching the lion, too frightened to go down; she comes to Maren after the bond (Ch 73).
