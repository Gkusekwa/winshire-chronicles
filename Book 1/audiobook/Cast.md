# Cast: The Watch Below

Voices for a multi-voice production. A single-narrator production uses only **Narrator**, who then carries every line; the notes still tell the narrator how each character sounds. Lines are counted from the annotations (speech, bond speech, thoughts, remembered words, verse). Speakers without a role of their own share the Man, Woman and Child voices; crowds and choruses go to the Narrator. The mapping lives in `Tools/audiobook/cast.py`.

**Bond speech** (italics in the book: beast and bonded human, mind to mind) is the book's signature sound. Keep each beast's voice distinct and keep bond speech a touch closer and quieter than spoken dialogue, so a listener always knows when words are passing through the bond.
