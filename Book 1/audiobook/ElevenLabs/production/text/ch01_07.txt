[calm] Haverly followed her look. He was quiet for a moment.

“That’s Clan land, isn’t it,” he said. “Over there.”

“Further north. That’s farms.”

“Why would anyone burn farms?”

[hushed] She didn’t answer. She was counting the fires. Two. Then, while she watched, a third, small and far back from the water, like someone striking a light in a dark room. [pause]

[tense] Out on the river a lantern was coming across from the /ˈsɔːltriːtʃ/ bank, low and steady, as a lantern moves on a ferry when the woman poling it has done it ten thousand times. /ˈmɛərən/ watched it until it was halfway, and then until it was close enough that she could hear the pole knock the hull, and only then did she find that she’d been gripping the stone hard enough to hurt.
