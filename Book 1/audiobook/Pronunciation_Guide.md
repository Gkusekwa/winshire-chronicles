Spellings are approximate; stress the capitalized syllable. Add approved forms to the chosen voice engine’s pronunciation dictionary before final rendering.

## Notes for production

- “th” in *Staithe* is the unvoiced sound in *thin*.
- “kh” in *Kharrak* is a suggested rough breath sound; a plain “k” is an acceptable engine-friendly fallback.
- Keep the apostrophes in possessive place names silent; the pronunciation guide gives the full spoken form.
- Check names in context. A spelling may recur as a person, place, title, or creature.
