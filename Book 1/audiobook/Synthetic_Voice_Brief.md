# Synthetic voice brief

Use a relaxed, natural English (UK) read with clear diction and an unhurried pace. Keep the delivery intimate and conversational rather than announcer-like.

The chapter SSML now carries an `AI CUE` XML comment for each paragraph and explicit 450 ms paragraph pauses where no longer pause already exists. Cues cover narration, dialogue, action, tension, reflection, titles, questions, and emphasis. Read and apply the comments as performance directions, but never vocalize them. Honor existing SSML breaks as silence.

Character distinctions should remain subtle: Maren measured and observant; Calder earthy and wry; Aurveth patient and deliberate; Kharrak terse; Lisska curious and quick; Orrun slow and deep but intelligible; Wren clear and even. Do not invent accents or overstate emotions. See `AI_Narration_Instructions.md` for the full cue protocol and `Pronunciation_Guide.md` for provisional name pronunciations.
