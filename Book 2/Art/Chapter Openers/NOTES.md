## POV signs

- **Maren (S)**: a single small ring, as in Book 1.
- **Calder (F)**: the three-dot triad, as in Book 1.
- **Idony (R)**: a small tide-glass, two arcs meeting at a waist between a top and a bottom cap (`tideglass()`). It is used for nothing else in the book.

Chapter 86 carries both the ring and the triad (they sit at opposite ends of the envoys' table); chapter 35 sets Calder's triad among the stars as Starwatch's "one comes with three".
