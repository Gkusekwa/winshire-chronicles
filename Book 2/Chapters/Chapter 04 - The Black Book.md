In the knight's lock chamber the lined-up rings of script still burn white rim to boss *(Book 1 ch. 77)*, on ground where every lamp is dead: the lock is old magic, the old ones' own work, not a rune that feeds on the Deep, so dead ground doesn't touch it (Magic §2, §5). She copies the sentence into the black book, stroke by stroke, without being able to read it. On the ledge Orrun stops: below where the chains were, something shut, "like a held breath". Rowan offers a pardon if Orrun leads the crown's hunt. No.
