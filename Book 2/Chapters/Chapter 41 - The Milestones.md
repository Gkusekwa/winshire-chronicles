The six ends are the six great upwellings his college has always marked as anomalies: the sleepers. Neither had the pattern alone. The next end is west, at the Scald, and where all the lines meet, at the bridge, is the held breath. Nobody knows what that is. **The race begins** *(canon: a race, not a hunt)*.
