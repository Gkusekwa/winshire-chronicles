He shouts Fisk's old boatman's wind-word from the jetty with Lisska in the air, and nothing happens: Greyhold's bank is dead ground and the ferry is on running water, and a charm needs neither (Magic §10 rule 5). **His casting fails.** Lisska does it alone: she spends all her wind in one gust, and the ferry's driven onto the stone jetty. He feels her empty herself through the bond and goes down; he wakes an hour later on the bank with Lisska lying across his legs, too tired to fly. Fisk's eel-word never worked on the river either *(Book 1 ch. 70)*. Behind the flood, the Restoration's companies, crossed into Hearthmere over the summer, hold Ashford and are stripping the Candle Downs; Rowan's knights are fighting them village by village with steel, because nothing else works on Hearthmere's ground.
Echo of the other's story: Liss, wringing out her skirts: her daughter writes once a month, short, from wherever the lion is.
