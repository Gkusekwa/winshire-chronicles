## Places and words

### Kingdoms

**Hearthmere**: the green kingdom west of the Wend, of knightly orders, charters and a crown at Hallowmere. Every working there was licensed, and its better streets had lamps that burned without oil. Since the Titan flew, the whole kingdom is dead ground.

**Saltreach**: a league of port-cities along the southern coast and the Wend's east bank, with no king. The trading houses and pirate captains sit together in the Tide Council at Corsa, which stamps charms as "warranted" and sells contracts for anything. Inland are farmers, drovers and the Dragon Clan.

**Isvaren**: the high, cold north, ruled by its colleges of learned magic from Vael. Rank follows learning, and bond gifts are registered as "wild power". The colleges' Wardens keep the law, with snow lynx at their heels.

**The Wyldmarch**: the wild east of forest, grass and mountain, where clans live under councils of elders. Beasts are kin: never hunted, traded or bound. Its Keepers protect beasts and people, and its charms are knots and hearth-songs.

**Kessmoor**: the cold north-west of coal, iron and canals, run by guild engineers from Ironhythe. It calls the Deep "the Bleed", has banned spoken magic, and calls its own runes engineering.

**Sarovel**: the warm south-west, a bond-monarchy with its seat at Solenne. Status follows the bond, and the unbonded are "the unchosen". Its word for the Deep is the Grace, which it says flows through the chosen.

### Places

**The Wend**: the border river between Hearthmere and Saltreach. Its valley is the Marches. A spoken charm won't cross running water, so nothing said across it lands.

**The Old Ruin**: the old realm's bridge-fort on its island in the Wend. It fell into the catacombs when the seal opened, and the river now pours into the hole where the island stood.

**Greyhold**: Hearthmere's knight school on the Wend, built over the way down to the chains. Its inner circle, the Watch, kept that secret; their rank is Warden. Liss Ashdown's ferry house stands at its landing.

**Hallowmere**: Hearthmere's capital on the shore of the Mere, where the knightly orders sit in council with the crown.

**Varrhold**: the Dragon Clan's hold in the Scarfell moors, with the Roosts, its dragon eyries, cut into the cliff above. Ulric's steading, Skarrow, lies on the moor's far side.

**Cotter's Cross**: a rough market town on the Saltreach bank where the Drover's Way meets the Saltreach Track: inns, a beast pen, and a hiring yard where sellswords wait for contracts.

**Wendmouth**: the port at the mouth of the Wend, run by four trading houses who hate one another. Its beast market is on Gallows Quay.

**Vael**: Isvaren's city of colleges on the north shore of Glasswater, a lake under the Iron Fells. Its towers are slender blue-grey slate, each college lit in its own colour.

**The Long Library**: Isvaren's oldest archive, a single hall half a league long, cut into the hill above Vael.

**The Thornwood**: the Wyldmarch's deep forest of oak, black pine and thorn, where the old lions came from. The Hollow Ring, twelve standing stones sacred to the clans, lies deep inside it.

**The Hallow Plains**: Wyldmarch grassland running east to the Worldsend, with great herds, horse clans, and standing stones the clans say mark where beasts are buried.

**The Scald**: a basin of fused glass near the Hearthmere border, where the Kessmoor town of Orrin stood until a disaster forty years ago. Kessmoor keeps a cordon round it, and has banned magic ever since.

**The Sun Stair**: the Bond-King's palace on its cliff above Solenne's bay, with its long stair down to the sea.

**The Drowned Bell**: a reef two leagues off the end of Gullhaven's shingle spit on the Saltreach coast. At low spring tides you can hear what sounds like a bell.

**The Oneroad**: the old realm's highway, mostly broken now, its milestones cut in the hooked script. It crossed the Wend at the Old Ruin.

### Beasts

**Greyharts**: Hearthmere's white, elk-like war-mounts, nine feet at the shoulder, bred by the crown for its knights.

**Duskcats**: black, panther-like cats. A Wyldmarch Keeper with a duskcat is the clans' night watch.

**Stonehorns**: heavy, rhinoceros-like beasts of the plains and scrub. The horse clans count their herds the way farmers count weather.

**Tuskers**: mammoth-like great beasts of the north. They talk in a rumble felt more than heard, and Isvaren hires their herds, by agreement with the herd, to haul over the passes.

**Ironbacks**: plated, boar-like beasts of the Iron Fells, the size of a barn. Fire doesn't burn them, and Kessmoor's foundries harvest their plates.

**Unicorns**: lean, deer-boned, grey to silver-black, with a single spiral horn of living bone, cloven hooves and a temper. Sarovel's crowned beast.

### Magic

**The Deep**: the Isvaren colleges' name for what every kind of magic draws on. It rises slowly from far underground, pools in deep places and in old-realm stone, and has tides. Nobody can say where it comes from.

**The toll**: what every working costs, taken from the place, the worker or a beast. Heat leaves the body, hands shake, noses bleed. Draw too hard and a worker burns out: grey in a night, the words never working for them again.

**Dead ground**: ground whose Deep is used up or gone. Charms there are only words, runes go hungry, and a drawer draws on himself. Bonds still hold, because a bond isn't the Deep.

**The six disciplines**: the classes of magic as Isvaren counts them. Charms are spoken words, short and close. Runes are the hooked letters cut into stone or iron. Drawing pulls raw force up through the body. Shaping changes what a thing is, for a while; healing is shaping. Tide-reading measures the Deep's rise and fall with tide-glasses. Binding is forbidden.

**Binding**: forcing a beast with honey-smoke, words, will and a collar. A bound beast obeys, with no mind-speech and no shared life. Kill the binder and it is free.

**Bond**: a beast's choice of a person, made only by the beast. The two share one life and speak mind to mind, and when one dies the other usually withers. A bond brings gifts from the beast, such as a dragon's tolerance for fire or stone that moves for a lion. A binding is never a bond.

**Honey-smoke**: smoke of sleepwood resin cooked with honey. It opens a beast's self, bonded or not, so a binder can get in.

**Old magic**: what only the old ones of the songs could work, with nothing between them and the Deep. People can find what is left of it and copy its forms, but never make it, and dead ground doesn't touch it.

**The stir**: Idony's word for what happens round a place where the Titan lies down and draws: the old stone fills and hums while the country round it goes dead.

**The surge**: a flood of the Deep. Lamps burn white, charms take too hard and won't stop, and drawers burn out. Hearthmere had one on the night the seal opened.

**The drain**: the famine that follows when the power under a place goes. Its magic fails, as Hearthmere's did at dawn when the Titan flew.

**Rings**: Isvaren's grades of learning, iron rings worn on the working hand with a notch for each grade, from Hand at the first ring through Reader, Scribe, Fellow and Master to Keeper of the Script at the sixth. A ring can be broken for a crime.

**Quickened edge**: a blade with runes struck into the steel at its quenching. It cuts what plain steel won't, takes a toll from whoever uses it, and goes dark on dead ground. The Haverly family has one.
