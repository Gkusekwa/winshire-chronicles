# Book 2, Part One: shared drafting ledger

*Three drafters write Part One at once: Maren (1, 4, 6, 9, 11), Calder (2, 5, 7, 10, 12) and Idony (3, 8). Before writing a scene that touches another POV's days or people, read this file. After deciding any fact another chapter could contradict (who is where on which day, what someone said, a name, a wound, a number, a beast), append one line here under your name. Keep lines short. The newest line wins only if it doesn't contradict the plan; if two lines clash, follow the plan and note the clash.*

## Route distances (settled, Oct 2026; follow the Atlas)
- Tolly's Staithe → Harrow's End by the bank track: about 4½ leagues.
- Harrow's End → Varrhold by the Drover's Way: 14 leagues (Atlas: 11 straight, 14 by the Way). The Ram's Head is halfway (7); Teo's ford on Hollow Beck is a league above it (8 from Harrow's End, 6 below Varrhold); the falls on Hollow Beck, the edge of Clan ground, are about 3 below the hold.
- So Tolly's Staithe → Varrhold is about 18½ leagues. "Fourteen leagues by the Way" always means from Harrow's End.

## Maren (1, 4, 6, 9, 11)
- Ch 1 (day 0): Rowan's column is 38 knights and students, Wynn (hounds Ash, Sorrow), Corbet (takes the wain back), surgeons Hallard and Aske; the beast-chain's quenched words are dead; groom Daw Pruett dies on the bank. The Restoration army on the Harrow's End hill breaks up by itself, unled. Maren carries Hale's black key on a rag and the uncleaned river watch sword. Calder and the dragons walk down the River Road at noon; Rowan turns her back.

- Ch 4 (days 1–4): 53 graves on the hill west of the walls, beasts with partners (Haverly+Kesh, Thorne+boar; Pell, Hale alone); Grieve reads the names. Tam brings Odo (both legs splinted) to the ferry house on a door; Tam's Restoration ring is gone. Grieve gives Maren Haverly's sword (letters dark). Maren writes MAREN ASHDOWN in plain letters under Hale's name in the black book and takes it. The cavern: half full of still black river; six iron rings with stumps of chain; Callis on a door in irons (both arms broken); Cassian's body on a plank. The lock's sentence still burns (old magic); she copies it in charcoal into the back of the black book. Orrun: "Something is shut there. Like a breath held." Day 3: council rider; Cassian's body hung in chains under the charter on the gatehouse, board: THE RESTORATION'S LEADER. Day 4: Rowan offers pardon/first lance if Orrun leads the crown's hunt; Maren refuses; Rowan guesses she's Watch (Rowan never was).
- Ch 6 (days 6–10): Millbrook's sweet well sour; Widow Rudd's words don't take. Council writ: Sir Aldous Pryne and twelve men take the Fenns to Greyhold's fort granary (split inner wall); Old Grey waits at the gate. Rowan's hint: "not a hand's width". Odo directs: three iron ties, rubble facing on the granary's back wall; Tam argues at the gate. Orrun opens the wall. Liss ferries them over without a lantern; greys and Orrun swim; landed before dawn day 9. Day 10 dawn: Lisska finds them in a Fellwood hollow above the Staithe (Aurveth fallen twice; Calder near the Ram's Head). Wren: "We're all here… Tell him. Twice."
- Ch 9 (days 10–14): off the Way; north through the Fellwood by old charcoal-burners' tracks two leagues east of it. Day 11: the swath (a league wide, NE, trees flat, crowns NE; ground drunk-from, thin not dead). Night 12: the Roosts' cry, "goes up at the end"; Ada: "She's reached the hold." Ada's hearth-word takes faintly on this bank. Day 12: a Fellwood bear backs off from Orrun. Day 13: five deserters at a beck-crossing; one looses at Maren; Orrun kills two, three run. Quarrel: "You are not my hand." / *You are not my teeth.* They sleep back to back. Day 14: Ada tells Maren about Senna (Senna Holt, from by the Lowe; fever five years ago). Wren: "Is he sorry?" "I don't know." Wren walks beside Old Grey throughout (rides him from day 15, ch 10).
- Ch 11 (days 15–17): onto the old drove track at its last climb (not the Drover's Way, which runs past the falls to the west); Wren climbs onto Old Grey from a boulder and rides him. The edge (above the Fellwood, ~3 leagues SE of Varrhold; falls on Hollow Beck visible to the west). Gunna Tarne/Tamsin, Hulda Sarr/Harl, Leif and his young brown. Gunna bows to Orrun the old way; Tamsin (312 years old) calls him "the cat on the steps"; he says *Yes*. Calder's message via Gunna. Night 15: Ada's word catches at once on the moor; Varrhold's smoke; Maren doesn't go. Day 16: Sorrel lame (stone in off fore-hoof); Bel's thanks ("Joss would've liked you"); Ada teaches Maren the hearth-word (two fingers, three turns, word on the third turn, the catch); fifteen tries, smoke. The stone: flat, grey, palm-sized, white ring round it; given to Wren, "I don't know" what for. "My road's the other way," twice. Before dawn day 17: tremor from the east; Orrun lies against her again (quarrel not mended); "We go." Ada lifts a hand.
## Calder (2, 5, 7, 10, 12)
- Ch 2 (days 0–1): the walk takes the afternoon; at dusk the ferry house (stone house under Greyhold's river wall; guide-rope to Tolly's Staithe). Wren and Liss there; Ada, Bel, Pip, Nan come down from the Long Barn after dark with Mabbin, Sorrel and Old Grey (who stands behind Wren). On Liss's table: Rook's six-knife; the dead shooter's iron ring on a string (B1 ch 60); the island chain; the five links. He tells Maren everything from under the arch. She tells him the one at the school is dead (doesn't say she did it). Ada's hearth-word does nothing on the Hearthmere bank. Nothing said about tomorrow.

- Ch 5 (days 4–6): the council's herald (horn's loudness-word dead): Saltreach refugees to go back within ten days; Calder outlawed, 100 crowns alive. Abe (Fisk's youngest, 14) brings news: Harrow's End stripped by unpaid companies; Arn's riders burned a steading at Skell Head, Ulric's two at the Lowe; Fisk stays "to tell it after". Ada's keeping-word over goat's milk takes faintly at Tolly's Staithe, nothing on the Hearthmere bank. Aurveth's horn-wound (from the ironback, B1 76) festering. Wren stays with Liss; Lisska to visit "every few days"; Old Grey rests his chin on Wren's head. "I'll write." "You can write two." Dawn day 6: "Your road's that way." "So's yours." Aurveth swims the Wend (Kharrak under her, wing held up) to Tolly's Staithe; Varrhold eighteen leagues from the Staithe, fourteen of them on the Way (see Route distances).
- Ch 7 (days 6–10): Tolly's Staithe → Ewe Hollow turn → Harrow's End (4½) → up the Way to the Ram's Head (7 more); 11½ of the 18½ leagues done by dusk day 10. Day 7: sixteen deserters at the Ewe Hollow turn; a warranted bolt in Aurveth's neck plates; Coll Brask talks them down; Delling has the paymaster's chest (and Rook's book) at Cotter's Cross; "Delling sells anything". Aurveth's first fall above Harrow's End; night 7 with Fisk at the Eelweir. Day 8: crossroads shrine, three-faced figure, a copper. Day 9: second fall at the Skell Water; Lisska sent to Wren; Ram's Head sacked, sign on one chain, charm-board in the mud. Dusk day 10 in the paymaster's yard: Lisska's news; "Maybe not bad."
- Ch 10 (days 11–15): Ram's Head → Varrhold 7 leagues (1 to Teo's ford, 6 more), two days. Mid-morning day 11 at Teo's ford, Aurveth stands in the water remembering; night 11 in a sheepfold under the falls (edge of Clan ground). Dusk day 12 at the hold: every dragon on the Roosts cries, going up at the end ("They are saying I came home"). Gorm Askell limping. The ten pairs of the ledges come down and stand behind Calder (Gunna Tarne/Tamsin, Harl, the boy Leif and his young brown dragon). Arn (in black, thinner) at the gate: price, the three come back to the Clan and Calder with them, flying when the Clan flies; Calder says yes. Setters: Ivar Leck (dun dragon Brindle) and two old women. Night 12: rot cut from the horn-wound with a Kharrak-heated knife; herd-word on the dressing. Day 13: wing set with ropes over the oak beam, five dragons holding her; setting-song by a dozen voices; the bone knits a little ("more than I've had out of it in ten years"). Day 14, the hall (east fire only): Calder tells Arn Rook's words; knife and iron ring on the table, Arn won't touch them; "A dead man's words in a living man's mouth aren't proof." "Bring me the book." Don't say Teo's name in the hall. Day 14 evening: Lisska reports the beck killings; that night Calder asks Gunna to fetch the Fenns to the hatching caves above Coldtarn; "Hallam would have gone." "Hallam's dead." Day 15 dawn: Gunna, Tamsin, Harl, Leif go down. Dusk: Lisska: Wren up on Old Grey's back on the old drove track's last climb (not the Way), lion beside her.
- Ch 12 (days 17–25): day 17 Lisska saw the lion go east, heather moving behind it with no wind. Day 18 the old hatching caves above Coldtarn (4 leagues from the hold): warm floor, steaming spring to the tarn, rows of stone hatching bowls, a few unbonded dragons wintering at the back. Wren: "She wouldn't come in" (twice); gives Calder the stone; he keeps it with Rook's knife and the iron ring. Ada's word catches before her hand finishes. Day 20: the spring pens on the lower ledges (oak bars, chosen pairs, keepers with mutton barrows); the chief's chair (black oak, carved dragons) under a grey cloth since Teo's burning; Arn sits beside it; a chief is named when both halves pour at Coldtarn. Calder given a grey Clan cloak, no thread. Day 21: Ulric's rider (lean, white scar through an eyebrow): "Tell the farmer I listened to the end. Tell him when he's proof, I'll come and look at it." Day 22: Tuft and Fell from Cotter's Cross: 2–3,000 unpaid there; Corvin "came up out of the ground by an old stair under the Ruin", in the big room upstairs at the Fleece, ring of six painted on the inn sign; Reede printing bills in the back; Gorm reads one: THE LAMPS WERE A CRIME… THE ONE REALM IS COMING. COME TO THE CROSS. Calder keeps it. Day 23 Arn: "Go and buy it, then" (no riders, no silver). Day 24 first ride on Kharrak (Dag's saddle-place); Calder holds the red tide from inside; "It was different." Day 25 dawn south with Kharrak and Lisska; Aurveth stays. Cotter's Cross four days' walk from Varrhold, under a day by air. The Fenn longhouse stands empty, door open (not burned).
## Idony (3, 8)
- Ch 3 (night 143 to day 3, Vael): the Gallery of Glasses (312 glasses; Hearthmere's are 201–203 etc.); every glass breaks at midnight; his 11 green bottle-glasses survive. Sunrise: Hearthmere flat/dead; a line of dips NE at the speed of a bird; "the walker"; "a held breath" under the river hill. The speaking bird is called "the Old Reader" in Vael (no name; calls Idony "ferryman" fondly, "Reader Vael" when displeased); hasn't flown in living memory; one eye milky. Dane (Blue College, Fifth ring) impounds ledgers and glasses on day 2 with two Wardens and two snow lynx. Idony's copies are on oiled silk sewn into his winter coat.
- Ch 8 (days 12–25): the Hall of Rings; Quell (Fifth ring, half-cut sixth notch): the draw "not a subject for study"; Dane's expedition and the holding-lamps (runes read *be mine*); Nessa's ferry *Heron*, lantern owl, tin Lady-token; departure day 20 with stormkites; day 25 the two recut glasses (turned east) show the drain in the Thornwood, and the walker on those same two glasses, south-west of the Thornwood, going north-east; recorded only in Idony's silk copy. The college body is the Council of Colleges (not "Council of Tides").
