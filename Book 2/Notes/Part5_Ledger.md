## Maren (55, 57, 60, 63, 65)
- Ch 55 (days 290–305): day 287 the Titan's ring of dots over the Western Deep breaks and swings SE toward Sarovel's coast; day 290 it comes in over the shore. Leaving the Scald day 290: Pike at the gate without looking up from a drawing (\"Send me the day... For the next one.\" \"There isn't a cage at the next one.\" \"Not yet\"); Moss gives Orrun a new sealed paper (*travelling on the Office's account to Sarovel. Not to be hindered*; \"in Sarovel is nothing at all... I'll be sorry not to know how it ends\"). Route: S on the Iron Road to the border, SW across Hearthmere's dead western downs (ash-coloured, empty farms, a village with snow in its rooms; Orrun silent two days); day 295 the first real hill: Sarovel's ground bright (**the Grace**: \"The Deep, with the sun on it\"; the glass stands very high; Orrun: \"It is sweet. Too sweet. Like honey left in the sun\"). The Sunward Hills (vineyards with vines cut to black stumps, grey olive terraces, white walls, warm afternoons). At Highcrest onto **the Sun Road** (paved, wide, white with limestone dust; Solenne to Hallowmere). **Stage-houses** every half-day: long white limestone house with red tiles round a courtyard, a columned beast-stable grander than the house with a gilded carved crest over every stall, a small white domed **stage-chapel** with a gilded sun over its door. Day 296 the first below Highcrest: the stout aproned keeper kneels in the yard at the sight of Orrun, then everyone (his wife, grooms, a boy with a bucket, a merchant, a silk-clad woman with a gold-coated bonded hound in a jewelled collar, on one knee). Idony: an old lion of the songs outranks a unicorn: she's higher than the Bond-King. \"Lady of the Lion.\" Maren remembers Greyhold's lines (\"at the back of every line\"). A young groom silently nods Tobin (pale ring-band, no beast) to **the servants' gate** by the kitchen door; Maren goes with him; supper with the **unchosen** (bread, olives, a bean stew with something red that burns); an old straw-haired stable-hand: \"You came in our gate... Why?\" \"Because he did.\" She pushes the bread across. Idony eats in the house (given the best room for arriving with her; a hand, if anything, in Sarovel). **The liturgy** at dusk, sung three times with a small bell before and after: *Praise to the Sun on the Stair, / who lit the first lamp / and gave it to the crown, / and keeps it burning / while the crown keeps faith.* Idony: the palace's stair to the sea has a shrine at its foot where the crowned line worships; \"I don't know what [the first lamp] means. I've never known.\" Every stage the same; she stops saying \"get up\"; always the servants' gate; by the third the grooms wait for her; by the fifth the mud-brick quarter's unchosen gather outside it to watch, not kneeling. Days 297–298 two nights at Highcrest (Orrun: the sweetness \"like walking all day in a bee-hive\"); a gold-and-white liveried household man asks for \"the Kessmoor measurer\": Lady Aurel asked the household to find him; the Bond-King wants a reader for the cracking gold collars in the palace stables. Dane is at court selling the Bond-King the College's chain-craft (cage and chain, for Sarovel's own beasts); Corvin a guest of Lady Aurel's house. Idony: \"if he's going to show it to somebody, I want to be in the room... My glasses need to be near... the shrine at the bottom of the stair.\" \"He wants you. He'll take you.\" \"I know. That's why I can get in.\" Idony is grey at the temples now. Tobin teaches him say-it-twice (\"The farmer's girl does it\"): \"I'll come. I'll come.\" Day 300 **Solenne** from the last hill: a curving blue bay; white limestone terraces, domes, colonnades, gilded tower-tops; the palace (hundred windows, gilded dome) on the cliff at the bay's western end with **the Sun Stair** zigzagging down the cliff to the water; the brown mud-brick unchosen quarter below the walls; the Sunward Hills behind; and the Titan lying grey on the ridge above the palace, wings folded like a roof: the stir has begun (\"Two weeks. Perhaps less\"). At the landward gate a white-and-gold four-horse palace carriage takes Idony (\"The low quarter.\" \"The low quarter.\"). Maren, Tobin and Orrun take a one-door thatched mud-brick horse-stable at the quarter's edge from an old unchosen man with no horse (\"nice to have something in it again\"); Orrun lies along the front wall with his head in; Maren sleeps against his head; Tobin in the loft. Echo, day 302 at a low-quarter wine-inn: a loud drunk Saltreach captain (brass-buttoned sea-coat, salt in his beard): the dragon farmer laid a red book on the Tidehall table with three dragons on the sea-steps; the Halloran house fell before noon; the fat factor fled on the morning tide \"with his breeches round his knees\"; \"Can't even read, they say.\" Maren, back turned: *He can read. Slowly. He reads my letters himself.* Day 304 feast-day procession down the palace's upper stair: nobles with gold hounds, jewelled great cats, too-long-legged coal-eyed not-quite-horses; at its head **the royal unicorn** (lean, deer-boned, taller and narrower than a horse, cloven hooves ringing on stone, silver-grey chalked white for the feast, spiral living-bone horn gilded, a wide gold collar cut with marks; Bestiary: not a white horse) with the crowned Bond-King in white and gold on its back; its eyes empty. Orrun: \"Kin. Far kin. It is not there... at all.\" He wants to go to **the Elderwood** (a day N of the city; the king's; foresters hang poachers): \"There are more... They are frightened... Of the one on the hill. And of the stair... I want to ask them... Why that one is not there.\"
- Ch 57 (days 305–310): lions painted on the low quarter's doors and shutters all week, at night, nobody says who: mud, ochre, cooking-pot soot, and one in gold leaf scraped off something on the wine-inn's door ("You can't miss him"). Tobin stays with the old stable-owner and the unchosen grooms (he talks to everyone and they listen, as nobody at home did): "Somebody should be here. When Idony comes." Day 305 north up the Gilt valley's west side out of the vineyards to **the Elderwood** (a day; the king's forest; two old grey gibbets at the road's end, iron still on, bones in one: no poacher hanged in a year): enormous evergreen oaks with rope-twisted grey trunks and hard shining leaves, sponge-barked cork-trees, black-trunked split yews, steep hills, no paths, a century of leaves underfoot; the Grace thick (every leaf too green, the air sweet enough to ache the teeth, gold bars of light in midwinter). Orrun: watched since the gibbet; "I am not a hunter. They know." A clearing where an old oak fell. Dawn 306 **Lisska** (flight 5) crashes down through the canopy, frosted: she followed the painted lions to the low quarter; Tobin saw her on the stable roof, pointed north and shouted a word over and over ("the skinny one is very loud"). **Calder's page** (charcoal on the back of something printed, hand better, lines level): Maren sees only the biggest letters at the bottom, *LISS SAYS COME HOME.* / *LISS.* (her mother can't write); she sees the words *Corvin* and *Blackwater* without reading; folds it into her coat behind the black book with his first letter: "Because if I read it I'll go... I can't go home yet." Lisska will wait for an answer ("you always say you'll write") on a great grey oak branch above the clearing. Dusk 308 **eleven wild unicorns** (lean, deer-boned, long fine legs, narrow heads, cloven hooves, silent on leaves; silver-grey, two nearly black with silver like ash on charcoal; spiral horns of old-ivory living bone); the **old lead mare** nearly black, grey at muzzle and eyes, ribs showing, the last hand's length of her horn gone, cut clean and worn smooth, stops at ten paces. Orrun stands (tree-tall now, oak branches brushing his back), then lowers his head to the leaves the old way (as Gunna bowed to him on the edge); the mare lowers hers; they talk silently all night. Orrun's telling at dawn: they remember the old realm (the road, the white bridge, the cat on the steps; a forebear walked the road to the bridge "Before the kings"). Nine kings ago the first Bond-King came with sweet smoke in pots on poles and men, took a year-old foal too small to run, put gold round its neck and a hand on its head; the people below the walls were told it was the first unicorn bond in the world; when it died the son came back with smoke: nine foals, nine kings. The one on the stair now is the ninth, called in the herd *the one who was taken in the snow*: taken twelve winters ago, the mare's daughter's. "There has never been a king with a unicorn... There have been nine kings with a prisoner." The mare's horn: the fourth king's men sawed the tip off while she lay in the smoke going after a foal (a unicorn's horn is worth a farm); she came out, the foal didn't. **Their kin sleeps under the palace**: under the stair, under the shrine where the crown kneels, the Sun on the Stair: the one-horned sun-rayed sleeper of the temple walls, far kin to them "As Kharrak is mine"; the herd has always kept watch from the wood and the hills above the bay; nine kings never asked what they knelt on (Maren thinks: *And I will not ask what I keep*). They're frightened: the stone over the stair is filling like a cup; when the sleep is taken their kin will wake with the gold-collared one still on the stair. Orrun grieves kin to kin. Day 309–310 the mare touches the broken end of her horn once to his mane on the neck and the herd goes. One more night in the clearing; Maren holds the folded letter, feels the charcoal through it, doesn't open it. "Tomorrow. We go back to the city tomorrow." Ten days since Idony's "two weeks"; Orrun: "It is filling. I can feel it from here."

## Calder (56, 59, 62, 64, 66)
- Ch 56 (days 291–300): day 291 **Brask's letter** (dictated to a Wendmouth fish-docks clerk; Ada reads it: "He spells *proud* with a w"): the Council has him counting barrels in the seized Halloran warehouses; the seized Halloran ship *Gannet*'s log: day 268, three young bound dragons (bronze, black, red) brought from Solenne by sea, landed at Blackwater, "To be held for the gentleman's buyer" (underlined); "Don't come to Wendmouth. They still want your head at the fish-docks." (So they flew S with the case, gave it to Corvin at Solenne, and were shipped back E to wait out of sight.) Lisska: "He's selling them like sheep." Gunna (at the caves, Tamsin's head in her lap): "Blackwater" before he says it; she watched four young ones taken at the Roosts on midsummer night "all on our knees in it." **Gunna's idea**: thick linen folded in four with straps, over dragons' nostrils tied behind the jaw and over riders' faces, soaked in vinegar ("the opposite of sweet... If it doesn't let the sweet in so fast, perhaps there's time to come back up"); Tamsin recoils from the bucket; Lisska: "It stinks." "Good." Day 292 Greyhold landing (Clan grain crossing to both banks): Liss on her mended ferry (new boards in its back, new rope): she sees letters on Lisska's leg when Lisska rests on the ferry-house roof going west; "put something at the bottom for me... *Come home.* And my name, so she knows it's me and not you. Two words. You can spell those." Party: Calder on Kharrak, Lisska; **Gunna on Tamsin** (312, dun-gold); six riders of the ledges incl. **Harl**; Aurveth stays. A barrel of vinegar bought dear from a fishing village's herring-pickling sheds up the coast from the Saltings. Lisska must sing without her cloth: "In the smoke... All right." Dusk 296 **Blackwater**: a narrow inlet into the Worldsend's southern tail, sheer black cliffs, water stained black by peat-streams; tarred stilt-houses joined by plank walkways round its head; a dozen low fast flagless ships; sellswords' fires on the shingle; no harbour-master, toll or headland lights; on the north shore a big tarred boathouse on piles with an open sea-door, honey-smoke from its roof-vents. Dawn 297 from the landward cliffs, cloths on (Kharrak makes a kettle noise at the vinegar). The binders light two dozen pots on roof, walkways and shingle and throw burning honey-cakes on poles into the wind. Through the vinegar the smoke is a far sweetness, a tug (Kharrak: "like a dream I'm having somewhere else... It stinks too much to sleep"). Kharrak takes the roof off and throws the ridge into the water ("Not one hurt. The men on it jumped... Say it." "Later."). Tamsin settles across the sea-door like a hen on a nest. Inside on the plank floor over the black water, the bronze, black and two-year-old red chained to iron rings in the piles, thinner, collars gone dirty grey, a ring of honey-pots, a binder at each head. Lisska hangs in the smoke over them and sings the mother-hum while going under ("I didn't stop"). The black lifts its head; Calder drops ten feet through the roof (leg through a board), knocks its heavy binder into a honey-pot (rolling in coals), drives the collar-pin out with Rook's knife and his palm (it falls through a gap into the water): the black's eyes come to the edge, not through. Harl drops in with a smith's punch and hammer from the hold's forge and does the red's pin (its binder runs; Calder trips him): the same. **The bronze's binder** (thin, pot-burned face scarred red down one cheek) puts a long thin knife into the soft place under its jaw where the throat plates don't meet; it makes no sound and goes down ("If I can't have it, you can't"); Tamsin's tail, eyes barely open, sweeps him through the shed wall into the inlet; the hired men pull him out alive and throw down their weapons. Lisska crawls out of the smoke onto the shingle: "Did they hear?" "Two of them." Captured: three binders alive (one drowned), eight hired men. At the beach fire, Gunna's hand on Tamsin's jaw behind him, the pot-burned binder: the dragons are for **a buyer at Solenne**; **Corvin** is at the Bond-King's court and dines with him; selling the Clan's young bound dragons to Sarovel's court for the Highcrest beast-lords; "The price was to pay for something else... He's always paying for something else... He says that's the first rule. Don't ask." The bronze burned on the beach at dusk on driftwood and the roof-planks, lit by Kharrak's low breath; the stilt-house folk watch, nobody comes near. Echo, day 299 at the one jetty: a Sarovel wine-ship's crew: a gold lion with a rock mane painted on the Sun Road gate at Solenne and at every stage-house, for the Lady of the Lion who went in at the servants' gate every stage "with some skinny lad nobody'd bonded"; the unchosen paint her lion on their low-quarter doors, in gold or in mud. *Of course she did.* Night 299 his letter, a whole page, charcoal on the back of a Corsa Council notice, the letters going uphill less: Blackwater, the cloths, the two, the bronze and the knife; *The man who pays them is at Solenne with you. He is selling our young ones to the court. He has a buyer there.* / *Be careful of him. I know you will be. I am writing it anyway.* / biggest letters: *LISS SAYS COME HOME.* / *LISS.* Dawn 300 the black puts its nose against Lisska's side once ("It knew I was there. Just for a moment"); **flight 5** west ("There's a lion on the gates. I'll find her"). Harl and two riders bring the black and red home slowly up the coast to the caves.
