# Book 2, Part Seven: shared drafting ledger

*Same rules as the earlier part ledgers. The plan wins over the ledger if they clash; note the clash.*

## Maren (79, 81, 84, 86)
- Ch 79 (day 354): dawn Maren and Orrun up the old stair by the old ones' worn tread; Liss's lamp relit twice more with Ada's word (hands numb to the wrists, carried like a cup); Wynn + three knights + hounds at the head; Joby asleep against Garran Vale's stone; nobody came up. **The cage strung in the night 353–354**: six chains from iron eyes in the rock below the inner wall in black curves over the river to six house-sized iron posts on the Saltreach bank upriver of the Staithe; white lines inside every link. **Four Kessmoor heartstone engines** (barn-sized, up the Wend by barge in the autumn): two in the yard on timber beds, two by the Saltreach anchors, idling, steaming, pipes and rods to the chain-ends. **Dane's twelve College binders** in blue along the inner wall's top above the eyes. Rowan on the north tower. **Envoys' camp** on the slope above the Staithe behind the anchors (Kessmoor iron wheel, Blue College blue, a gold sun on white for Sarovel, others); Maren doesn't go over. Landing: Millbrook's lower lane, Ferriby stragglers, the crown company's camp-women, all Greyhold's grooms. **~70 won't cross**: the poorest of the lower lane and the river-flats reed huts; **Mother Ketch** (new: ~80, walnut face, heron eyes; the river took her man and two boys off the eel-weir thirty years back): "I'll not go on it... We'll go in the stone." Rowan lets them into **the cellars** (knocked-through vaults under the inner wall). Thurl: "The cellars are the strongest roof on the hill... It's the wall above them that's empty... If it goes, it goes outward. Toward the river... Not down." "You're sure." "No." Mother Ketch: "You talk like her." Noon they go in (bundles, blankets, hens); **Joby Marsh stays with them with a lantern** (his grandson, 12, crosses). **Tam's last link** half-struck and grey on the anvil; he lays his hammer on it: "It's not going to be wanted." Odo: "It was never going to be enough." Tam carries Odo's chair down the jetty road on his back. **Liss's last crossing** at dusk: Odo's chair lashed to the thwarts, the last cart with Odo's good anvil and tools, Joby's grandson, a Ferriby woman with a goat, Tam with the bow lantern ("Come over." "No."). Liss: "Come over." "No." "Then come home after." Maren returns the lamp: "You said bring it back." "I said it twice." At the Staithe: Kharrak and Aurveth on the shingle, a man at the post; Liss perhaps takes his hand; Maren doesn't cross. Liss stays over. The barge makes one last trip back for the cage; nothing else crosses; the rope hums empty. **Echo, night**: from the jetty, lines of dragon-fire coming down off the Scarfell's rim toward the hill above Harrow's End, more than she can count; Orrun: *They are all coming... Every one on the high ledges. The old ones. The young ones. The ones who never fly... What was on his page.* Rowan's lantern burns all night on the north tower.
- Ch 81 (day 355, morning to early afternoon): Maren and Orrun on the water-gate stair when the Titan lands (the hill shudders); *It is on it... I can feel the chains. They are very bright.* The cavern awake: black water shivering in rings; knight's lock blazing; scaffolds shaking, dust; in the near wall the backs of the eye-bolts (black, body-thick) with **hair-fine cracks spreading, white with chain-rune light**. **Corvin's party** at the shut place, in by **an old arch in the far corner** opened from the other side: Corvin, Callis, Idony with a lantern, **four binders** (pots, short spears), **eight band men** (crossbows, spears). **The shut place, smooth in ch 69, now has rings of hooked script rising out of it from inside, gold**, the same rings as the knight's lock, pressed up by the Titan above. Corvin: "Read it." Idony, finger on the stone, in the common tongue: **"One hand shut it. Two hands open it together. What is opened together is kept together, or it is not kept."** Maren knows the shapes (charcoal copy from the knight's lock, ch 4; *together* found on the old stair). Corvin: "Two hands... Its. And mine." Maren: "Or mine. And the lion's." "Then we'll see whose." **Callis** comes at her with a short sword in his bent left hand; she doesn't draw Haverly's edge; takes the sword off him barehanded (into the water); puts him down; he gets up. Corvin: "Keep the lion off the door." Maren: "One." **One shadow** stands up out of the cavern floor beside Orrun before the shut place (it goes out of her like blood); bolts bounce; binders' smoke and words do nothing (bonded beast; a floor can't be bound); **a binder's spear into the shadow's shoulder** (she feels it). **The river comes in**: the near-end roof over the stair splits; the Wend pours in as a house-wide brown column; flood across the cavern. **Callis swept in**, arms useless; **Maren pulls him out by the collar** with Orrun's help onto the stair's lowest steps; he says nothing, gets up, and wades back along the ledge to Corvin, shouldering the wall. Orrun unmakes the shadow; **the spear-wound comes back into his own shoulder** (she feels it): *It is nothing.* Water to the stair's fifth step; Corvin's lanterns still on the higher shut-place ledge. **Echo**: *The farmer... Far up. Where the island was. Where the river turns... Three of them. And him. Coming down. Through the water... He is coming down after it.*
- Ch 84 (day 355, mid-afternoon): the last of Idony's hold dies in the iron; the Titan shakes its legs once like a dog shedding water; **it leans its brow on the clay-soft shut place and the stone goes in like wet sand**: a short round passage; **the held breath** comes out past her, a long warm wind smelling of nothing, up through the rock into the open air (every lamp left on the hill flares once and goes out for good: she hears later). **The vault**: round, domed, the size of Liss's kitchen, plain grey stone without a mark; a waist-high plinth; **the control relic**: a grey old-realm stone ring a cartwheel across, warm, **six hollows in its rim: five egg-shaped, the sixth round and empty** (as if something round once sat there: unexplained, Book 3). Maren in the opening with Haverly's edge (letters still dark though the hill is live: old magic, not hers); **she cuts once at its brow; the blade rings like a hammer on an anvil, no mark**; it doesn't notice her; she steps aside (she'd chosen it upstairs). **Corvin first**: from the arch along the ledge, Callis behind, roll under his arm, through the gap by the Titan's jaw; left arm in its sling; **right hand on the ring**: "Now." **It takes his hand**: the stone rises over fingers, hand, wrist like water in a basin; he pulls and his hand stays, grey stone in the ring's rim; **his arm ends at the wrist in smooth sealed grey stone, not bleeding**; he doesn't scream; Callis drags him out. (Old magic isn't for people.) **The Titan takes the ring** gently in its jaws (as she saw it hold the egg at the Scald), Corvin's stone hand a lump in the rim, lays it in its back among the five; the stone grows over it: **six**. **It speaks to Orrun once through the rock** (cellars empty; the seventy out on the broken hill, Joby last with his lantern); Orrun makes it words for her: ***You kept it well, little brother. / It is not yours to keep any more.*** Orrun doesn't answer. **Echo** via Orrun's eyes on the hill: the inner wall gone, vaults open to the sky, people grey with dust, Mother Ketch on her feet; **Kharrak with a man still in his claws, Lisska hanging in the air, Aurveth's head coming up off the rubble: all three go still at once, heads toward the river, like hounds hearing a horn; Calder's hand at his chest where the marks are.** The Titan turns and goes away up the cavern toward the Ruin end with six in its back. Maren alone in the vault, hand on the warm plinth, sword dark on the floor; Corvin on his knees in the arch held up by Callis; Idony on the ledge with his grey hands in his lap.

## Calder (80, 82, 85, 88)
- Ch 80 (night 354 – about noon 355): Liss at the Staithe post took his hand: "You're the farmer." "Yes." "You write like a child." "I know." Tam takes Odo up the Way in the dark with the cart; Liss stays in an empty fisher's hut at the Staithe "To see my rope" (flint fire). First light through the envoys' camp: **Engineer Garth Tolley** (new: Pike's deputy, square, red-bearded); Dane's Blue magister (thin, anxious, holding glasses to the light; unnamed); a brown Sarovel street-tribune of Solenne's low city sent by Tobin **with a letter for Maren** ("Then I'll wait"). **The whole Clan on the hill above Harrow's End**: 120+ dragons (every pair on the ledges, Gunna's and Hulda Sarr's, Ivar Leck on Brindle, Leif and his young brown, Arn's thirty, Ulric's half in at midnight). Rowan sends **Wynn + eight knights on the barge with hounds and greyharts** (white, elk-like, nine feet, great antlers) to keep several hundred late crossers, envoys' servants and the curious moving up the Way. **Sunrise: the Titan comes down** out of the SW over the downs, the light going under it, wings like ragged old stone across half the sky, twelve chain-ends, Bell harpoons black in its side, five grown-over places; lands in the Wend half a league upriver of Greyhold, brown water higher than the river wall; walks down the river chest-deep under the chains toward Greyhold. **Rowan's horn; the cage springs**: four engines roar (fireboxes white, black smoke, drums turning); the chains rise off the river and close like a net; Dane's twelve binders on the inner wall speak the oath's form with the hold-words; every link's runes white; the chains close on back, neck, shoulders; it stops, held, head up toward the hill; the envoys breathe out. It pulls, the chains brighten (*They feed on what they hold*: Liss told him at the post, from Odo); the Saltreach anchor-posts groan but are new live quarry stone; the hill's eyes hold. Calder and Kharrak start the frozen crowd up the Way; **Ash and Sorrow work the crowd like sheep**; knights lift the old and lame onto greyharts (one carries three); Ulric's riders land in front of stalled carts. **Mid-morning the Restoration's last band** (~30 men, carts, the one binder Corvin left them, the bound Ferriby oxen) comes out of the Brackenwood to loot the empty Greyhold landing (ferry house, grooms' cottages, crown camp); **Arn's red and twenty pairs come down in a ring**; the band runs up the River Road into the Brackenwood without a blow; the binder throws down his pot; the oxen walk into the river one by one and come out with full eyes: the last of the Restoration's war. It holds: the Titan stands under six white chains, head low, not pulling; Aurveth from the Staithe: *It isn't fighting. It's waiting... For the hill.* **Echo**: Lisska on the empty ferry-house roof feels through the stone the lion far below going down a stair into black water lit gold, with a small figure with a dark sword: *They're going down.*
- Ch 82 (day 355, noon to early afternoon): the Titan stops waiting and **draws** on the hub through the river, bank and hill; the chain-runes feed on the draw too, too white to look at; the inner wall's emptied stone can't carry them: frost-cracks round the eyes. **Eye 1** tears out (cart-sized, with rock) and its chain whips into the river; eye 2; **eye 3 pulls a house-wide slab of the inner wall out and down the slope into the river** (outward, as Thurl said); **eye 4 drags the near yard engine** across the yard on its timbers, through the broken wall face and over: **Dane** on its footplate, gloved hands on the governor wheel, doesn't let go; goes over and under it; the firebox bursts on the slope, heartstones scattered white like coals. **Rowan's three short notes**: the crown's men knock the pins from the two Saltreach drums and her knights from the last yard engine's; the chains run out slack off the Titan; she gets the knights, the crown company, ~30 engine-firemen and a few hidden grooms' loft-boys down the jetty road onto the barge and across; last off on foot, surcoat torn, looking back at **the cellar door, now under the fallen slab**. The Titan turns and walks three leagues upriver to **the eddy where the island was** and goes down into it; the eddy becomes a whirlpool. Aurveth followed him from the Staithe: *It's going home. Under. Where it lay... Where the lion is.* Lisska: *She's down there.* **Kharrak, Lisska, Aurveth dive** through the whirlpool, past the Ruin's walls on the river-bed, through the hole where the Ruin's floor went in Book 1, into air: **the long cavern** the Titan lay in, Ruin to Greyhold: ribbed roof like a whale's inside, half full of black water, at the Ruin end the anchor-stones with iron eyes and the rusted stumps of the first chains. Kharrak's fire lights it. The Titan ahead, back scraping the roof (falls behind it), toward a small gold light at the Greyhold end. **The wyverns**: Corvin's bound pair (smaller, thinner than Vey's; iron collars, broken chains), chained in the old way's arch, binders drowned in the flood, binding broken by the running water; Lisska's kin-voice (*Come. You're safe.*) finds nobody: *There's no one there.* Kharrak's fire-wall drives them screaming into the old ways. **Meeting**: Kharrak lands on the high dry ledge before the shut place **ten paces from the lion** (wound in his shoulder); Lisska: *He says you're late.* Maren, back to the wall, palm on the gold rings, soaked, Haverly's edge dark; Idony at her shoulder with a lantern; Corvin's men drawing back toward the far-corner arch; Corvin in the arch watching. "You kept it." "Not yet." **Echo**: the stone screaming under her hand (high, long, like torn iron), the rings turning faster; the Titan's head coming out of the dark down the cavern.
- Ch 85 (day 355 mid-afternoon to dawn 356): on the broken hill his three go still (Kharrak in the air with a river-flats fisher in his claws; Lisska hanging; Aurveth's head up on the wall); **his marks burn** (bronze, rust, green): **the call**, a pull going out from under the river over all Winshire like a ring on a pond; the dragons lean into it like trees in wind; Kharrak sets the fisher down very carefully. **Aurveth names the five as they turn toward the Marches**: *The stag. In the east. Where the forest walks / The cold one. North and west. Under the ice / The one that burns. West / The bright one. South. With the horn / The one with the bell on its back. In the sea. Far down... Five... They're turning. Toward here.* **Kessmoor's cage breaks**: *The one that burns... It's in iron... Was... It's out.* (He feels her feel it.) The pull passes; Lisska drops shaking onto the rubble: *It called and I nearly went.* Kharrak: *I didn't* (he did, a little). Aurveth: *We're not what it called. We're only near enough to hear.* The hill groaning. Aurveth stays (*Bring them up*); **Calder and Kharrak, Lisska behind, go back down the river-breach fall**. Titan gone up the cavern; Idony grey on the ledge; Maren in the vault door, sword picked up. **In the far-corner arch a lantern going up a stair behind it** (as from the Ruin's rim a year ago): Callis ahead with the lantern against his body; **Corvin**, left arm in sling, **a long old roll-case under the sling arm**, right arm ending at the wrist in grey; doesn't look back; gone (canon). Calder doesn't follow. Maren: "Go. Through the water. The way you came." "You..." "He's coming for me." **Orrun comes down out of the rock**, one lion, swaying, shoulder wound black, **mane white at the roots all along his neck**; she puts Idony on his back and closes his fingers in the mane; climbs up behind; one look at Calder; the lion goes into the rock. Calder into the black water as the roof comes down; out through the breach into the Wend at dusk. **Greyhold's hill falls in**: north tower, yard, inner wall, south tower, gate and charter folding into the ground, a huge grey dust-cloud over the river; **the river wall above the jetty slides into the river; the ferry house stands, cracked, roof half off.** Night on the Saltreach bank at the Staithe: Aurveth brought the last people off the hill before it went, three at a time; the seventy grey with dust; Joby's lantern out at last; Mother Ketch on an upturned boat; Rowan silent by the barge; Liss in her hut doorway; the rope still tied on both posts. **Echo, dawn 356**: a league down the Hearthmere bank below Millbrook's woods the hillside opens; the lion comes up out of the ground like a swimmer, a grey-haired man on his back, a woman walking beside with her hand in his mane. Liss sees, hand over her mouth. Calder doesn't go. The lion-woman lifts her hand; he lifts his.

## Idony (83, 87)
- Ch 83 (day 355, early afternoon): Idony had been on a cord held by a band man; the man went into the flood and let go; Idony's feet took him along the ledge to Maren (fourth time); Corvin let him. The Titan's head out of the dark (eyes each as big as the Vael ferry); **it puts its brow gently to the shut place** beside the lion; rings turn faster, the scream higher; **the stone gives**: softens like wax near a flame, the rings blurring: old magic giving way (canon). **Corvin reads the appendix** in the arch, the roll spread across a kneeling binder's back, palm on it: *hold*, *hold fast*, *turn back*, every word right and every word wrong (weight wrong on the closing hooks, turns a breath late); the chain-ends (still a working, still feeding) **bite** into the Titan's forelegs like wire into a tree; it screams (heard through the hill), rears; its back goes into the roof at the near end under the inner wall; **the cellar vaults break** (*The stone people*). **Maren chooses in one breath**: "Go. All of you. Up. The cellars." *The door.* "I know." *If I go up, the door...* "I know. Go." Hand off the shut place. To Calder: "The cellars. Under the inner wall. Seventy people... Go up. Through there. Tear the hill open from above. He'll open it from below." Calder, Kharrak, Lisska (green) and Aurveth (old bronze) go up through the river-breach. **Orrun goes into the rock like a diver into a pond**; **three shadows** stand up out of the floor and follow. Maren stays with Haverly's edge drawn, dark. **Idony speaks the chain-words rightly**: palm on the inside curve of the nearest swinging chain-end (house-tall links) on the Titan's foreleg; not *tighten*, not *bite*: **_Hold fast_. _Be still._**, weight where it belongs; not for Corvin; the Titan goes **still, held like a horse by a bridle**, brow a few paces from the soft stone, eyes open. The hardest working since the first chains (a hundred voices then; one now); it's him going out, not the Deep. Maren's hand on his back; "**Four**" (a fourth shadow: five lions); **she stops breathing; he counts to ten; she breathes**. **Echo**, in gasps: a lion under each vault holding the roof on his back; Orrun opening the wall up into the hill; Calder and Kharrak tearing the slab and rubble off from above; a lion carrying a child in his jaws like a cat with a kitten, one with three on his back, up through stone onto the hill; dragons lifting them down to the river and the barge; "Together... They're not speaking to each other. Not a word. They're just doing it." "Joby's last. With his lantern... Out. All of them. Out." **The burnout**: he lets go and it comes back: hair, beard, face grey in a breath; his right hand shaking and never stopping; *hold* aloud is only a word; he knows everything and none of it will work for him again (canon: survives). He sits on the ledge. Maren stands over him, grey-faced, swaying, sword dark. The Titan's legs unlock; it lowers its head to the clay-soft shut place; Corvin closes the roll in the arch; nobody at the door but a woman with a dark sword and a grey man who can't lift his hands.
