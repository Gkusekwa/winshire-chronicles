## Maren (79, 81, 84, 86)
- Ch 79 (day 354): dawn Maren and Orrun up the old stair by the old ones' worn tread; Liss's lamp relit twice more with Ada's word (hands numb to the wrists, carried like a cup); Wynn + three knights + hounds at the head; Joby asleep against Garran Vale's stone; nobody came up. **The cage strung in the night 353–354**: six chains from iron eyes in the rock below the inner wall in black curves over the river to six house-sized iron posts on the Saltreach bank upriver of the Staithe; white lines inside every link. **Four Kessmoor heartstone engines** (barn-sized, up the Wend by barge in the autumn): two in the yard on timber beds, two by the Saltreach anchors, idling, steaming, pipes and rods to the chain-ends. **Dane's twelve College binders** in blue along the inner wall's top above the eyes. Rowan on the north tower. **Envoys' camp** on the slope above the Staithe behind the anchors (Kessmoor iron wheel, Blue College blue, a gold sun on white for Sarovel, others); Maren doesn't go over. Landing: Millbrook's lower lane, Ferriby stragglers, the crown company's camp-women, all Greyhold's grooms. **~70 won't cross**: the poorest of the lower lane and the river-flats reed huts; **Mother Ketch** (new: ~80, walnut face, heron eyes; the river took her man and two boys off the eel-weir thirty years back): "I'll not go on it... We'll go in the stone." Rowan lets them into **the cellars** (knocked-through vaults under the inner wall). Thurl: "The cellars are the strongest roof on the hill... It's the wall above them that's empty... If it goes, it goes outward. Toward the river... Not down." "You're sure." "No." Mother Ketch: "You talk like her." Noon they go in (bundles, blankets, hens); **Joby Marsh stays with them with a lantern** (his grandson, 12, crosses). **Tam's last link** half-struck and grey on the anvil; he lays his hammer on it: "It's not going to be wanted." Odo: "It was never going to be enough." Tam carries Odo's chair down the jetty road on his back. **Liss's last crossing** at dusk: Odo's chair lashed to the thwarts, the last cart with Odo's good anvil and tools, Joby's grandson, a Ferriby woman with a goat, Tam with the bow lantern ("Come over." "No."). Liss: "Come over." "No." "Then come home after." Maren returns the lamp: "You said bring it back." "I said it twice." At the Staithe: Kharrak and Aurveth on the shingle, a man at the post; Liss perhaps takes his hand; Maren doesn't cross. Liss stays over. The barge makes one last trip back for the cage; nothing else crosses; the rope hums empty. **Echo, night**: from the jetty, lines of dragon-fire coming down off the Scarfell's rim toward the hill above Harrow's End, more than she can count; Orrun: *They are all coming... Every one on the high ledges. The old ones. The young ones. The ones who never fly... What was on his page.* Rowan's lantern burns all night on the north tower.
- Ch 81 (day 355, morning to early afternoon): Maren and Orrun on the water-gate stair when the Titan lands (the hill shudders); *It is on it... I can feel the chains. They are very bright.* The cavern awake: black water shivering in rings; knight's lock blazing; scaffolds shaking, dust; in the near wall the backs of the eye-bolts (black, body-thick) with **hair-fine cracks spreading, white with chain-rune light**. **Corvin's party** at the shut place, in by **an old arch in the far corner** opened from the other side: Corvin, Callis, Idony with a lantern, **four binders** (pots, short spears), **eight band men** (crossbows, spears). **The shut place, smooth in ch 69, now has rings of hooked script rising out of it from inside, gold**, the same rings as the knight's lock, pressed up by the Titan above. Corvin: "Read it." Idony, finger on the stone, in the common tongue: **"One hand shut it. Two hands open it together. What is opened together is kept together, or it is not kept."** Maren knows the shapes (charcoal copy from the knight's lock, ch 4; *together* found on the old stair). Corvin: "Two hands... Its. And mine." Maren: "Or mine. And the lion's." "Then we'll see whose." **Callis** comes at her with a short sword in his bent left hand; she doesn't draw Haverly's edge; takes the sword off him barehanded (into the water); puts him down; he gets up. Corvin: "Keep the lion off the door." Maren: "One." **One shadow** stands up out of the cavern floor beside Orrun before the shut place (it goes out of her like blood); bolts bounce; binders' smoke and words do nothing (bonded beast; a floor can't be bound); **a binder's spear into the shadow's shoulder** (she feels it). **The river comes in**: the near-end roof over the stair splits; the Wend pours in as a house-wide brown column; flood across the cavern. **Callis swept in**, arms useless; **Maren pulls him out by the collar** with Orrun's help onto the stair's lowest steps; he says nothing, gets up, and wades back along the ledge to Corvin, shouldering the wall. Orrun unmakes the shadow; **the spear-wound comes back into his own shoulder** (she feels it): *It is nothing.* Water to the stair's fifth step; Corvin's lanterns still on the higher shut-place ledge. **Echo**: *The farmer... Far up. Where the island was. Where the river turns... Three of them. And him. Coming down. Through the water... He is coming down after it.*

## Calder (80, 82, 85, 88)
- Ch 80 (night 354 – about noon 355): Liss at the Staithe post took his hand: "You're the farmer." "Yes." "You write like a child." "I know." Tam takes Odo up the Way in the dark with the cart; Liss stays in an empty fisher's hut at the Staithe "To see my rope" (flint fire). First light through the envoys' camp: **Engineer Garth Tolley** (new: Pike's deputy, square, red-bearded); Dane's Blue magister (thin, anxious, holding glasses to the light; unnamed); a brown Sarovel street-tribune of Solenne's low city sent by Tobin **with a letter for Maren** ("Then I'll wait"). **The whole Clan on the hill above Harrow's End**: 120+ dragons (every pair on the ledges, Gunna's and Hulda Sarr's, Ivar Leck on Brindle, Leif and his young brown, Arn's thirty, Ulric's half in at midnight). Rowan sends **Wynn + eight knights on the barge with hounds and greyharts** (white, elk-like, nine feet, great antlers) to keep several hundred late crossers, envoys' servants and the curious moving up the Way. **Sunrise: the Titan comes down** out of the SW over the downs, the light going under it, wings like ragged old stone across half the sky, twelve chain-ends, Bell harpoons black in its side, five grown-over places; lands in the Wend half a league upriver where the island was, brown water higher than the river wall; walks down the river chest-deep under the chains toward Greyhold. **Rowan's horn; the cage springs**: four engines roar (fireboxes white, black smoke, drums turning); the chains rise off the river and close like a net; Dane's twelve binders on the inner wall speak the oath's form with the hold-words; every link's runes white; the chains close on back, neck, shoulders; it stops, held, head up toward the hill; the envoys breathe out. It pulls, the chains brighten (*They feed on what they hold*: Liss told him at the post, from Odo); the Saltreach anchor-posts groan but are new live quarry stone; the hill's eyes hold. Calder and Kharrak start the frozen crowd up the Way; **Ash and Sorrow work the crowd like sheep**; knights lift the old and lame onto greyharts (one carries three); Ulric's riders land in front of stalled carts. **Mid-morning the Restoration's last band** (~30 men, carts, the one binder Corvin left them, the bound Ferriby oxen) comes out of the Brackenwood to loot the empty Greyhold landing (ferry house, grooms' cottages, crown camp); **Arn's red and twenty pairs come down in a ring**; the band runs up the River Road into the Brackenwood without a blow; the binder throws down his pot; the oxen walk into the river one by one and come out with full eyes: the last of the Restoration's war. It holds: the Titan stands under six white chains, head low, not pulling; Aurveth from the Staithe: *It isn't fighting. It's waiting... For the hill.* **Echo**: Lisska on the empty ferry-house roof feels through the stone the lion far below going down a stair into black water lit gold, with a small figure with a dark sword: *They're going down.*
