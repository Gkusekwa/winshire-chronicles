## Idony (83, 87)
- Ch 83 (day 355, early afternoon): Idony had been on a cord held by a band man; the man went into the flood and let go; Idony's feet took him along the ledge to Maren (fourth time); Corvin let him. The Titan's head out of the dark (eyes each as big as the Vael ferry); **it puts its brow gently to the shut place** beside the lion; rings turn faster, the scream higher; **the stone gives**: softens like wax near a flame, the rings blurring: old magic giving way (canon). **Corvin reads the appendix** at the ledge's edge below the arch, the roll spread across a kneeling binder's back, his good palm on the runes of the nearest chain-end (the chain-end tears from his hand when the Titan rears, and he goes back into the arch): *hold*, *hold fast*, *turn back*, every word right and every word wrong (weight wrong on the closing hooks, turns a breath late); the chain-ends (still a working, still feeding) **bite** into the Titan's forelegs like wire into a tree; it screams (heard through the hill), rears; its back goes into the roof at the near end under the inner wall; **the cellar vaults break** (*The stone people*). **Maren chooses in one breath**: "Go. All of you. Up. The cellars." *The door.* "I know." *If I go up, the door...* "I know. Go." Hand off the shut place. To Calder: "The cellars. Under the inner wall. Seventy people... Go up. Through there. Tear the hill open from above. He'll open it from below." Calder, Kharrak, Lisska (green) and Aurveth (old bronze) go up through the river-breach. **Orrun goes into the rock like a diver into a pond**; **three shadows** stand up out of the floor and follow. Maren stays with Haverly's edge drawn, dark. **Idony speaks the chain-words rightly**: palm on the inside curve of the nearest swinging chain-end (house-tall links) on the Titan's foreleg; not *tighten*, not *bite*: **_Hold fast_. _Be still._**, weight where it belongs; not for Corvin; the Titan goes **still, held like a horse by a bridle**, brow a few paces from the soft stone, eyes open. The hardest working since the first chains (a hundred voices then; one now); it's him going out, not the Deep. Maren's hand on his back; "**Four**" (a fourth shadow: five lions); **she stops breathing; he counts to ten; she breathes**. **Echo**, in gasps: a lion under each vault holding the roof on his back; Orrun opening the wall up into the hill; Calder and Kharrak tearing the slab and rubble off from above; a lion carrying a child in his jaws like a cat with a kitten, one with three on his back, up through stone onto the hill; dragons lifting them down to the river and the barge; "Together... They're not speaking to each other. Not a word. They're just doing it." "Joby's last. With his lantern... Out. All of them. Out." **The burnout**: he lets go and it comes back: hair, beard, face grey in a breath; his right hand shaking and never stopping; *hold* aloud is only a word; he knows everything and none of it will work for him again (canon: survives). He sits on the ledge. Maren stands over him, grey-faced, swaying, sword dark. The Titan's legs unlock; it lowers its head to the clay-soft shut place; Corvin closes the roll in the arch; nobody at the door but a woman with a dark sword and a grey man who can't lift his hands.
- Ch 87 (days 357–362): first morning the pen goes across the page in a long shaking line and drops; he doesn't pick it up. Not craft*"). **D.357 noon Calder** on Kharrak: "She says you need a hand." "Two." "I write slow." "So do I now." Every two hours day and night Idony reads lean/angle (from knife-scratches on the sill)/height aloud at the window on **Joby Marsh's too-short stick**; Calder rules columns with Liss's bread-board edge and writes big square figures. **The Titan lies on the Scarfell** (came up out of the Ruin end of the cavern the night the hill fell); five dips moving toward it from east, north-west, west, south and the sea. Idony reads alone after, aloud, and remembers the numbers. **D.360** the eastern dip (the stag) reaches the Scarfell, then the north-west, then the west (the Furnace); **d.361** the south (Horned Sun) and last, slowest, the sea (the Bell). **Midnight 361: all four glasses breathing together, up, held, down**: "All six. As one." **D.362** Joby brings a proper peeled hazel stick. **Echo, dusk 362**: from the ferry-house door, past the grey heap of Greyhold, the empty Way, to the Scarfell's rim: **six shapes rising off the moor together like a startled flock, turning east toward the Worldsend**; gone into the dark. Maren, back from the river, stands at the jetty's end with her hand in the lion's mane, watching the same way; he doesn't call to her. He sits with his shaking hand flat on the farmer's pages and says the last reading aloud through the open door "so somebody would have heard it."
