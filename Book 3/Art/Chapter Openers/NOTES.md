Module: `/home/claude/audit/repo/Tools/art/book3_openers.py` (each Canvas is seeded `30000 + n`). Run `python3 book3_openers.py OUTDIR`.
Outputs: `svg/chapter_NN.svg`, `png/chapter_NN.png` (1500 px wide, white background), `contact_1.png` (1–44), `contact_2.png` (45–86).

## POV signs (the same in every chapter)
- **S Maren**: the single ring (as in Book 1)
- **F Calder**: the three-dot triad (as in Book 1)
- **R Idony**: the tide-glass, two arcs meeting at a waist and capped top and bottom (an hourglass)
- **G Rowan**: the blade, an upright stroke with a short crossguard and a pommel dot (Haverly's edge)
- **C Arn**: the pouring cup, a bowl on a short stem ("Who pours?")
- **U Tobin**: the hand's block, a small open square (a hand's work)
- **M Kit**: the sail, a mast with a three-cornered sail over a short hull

When both protagonists are in a chapter (34, 35, 61, 68), the ring and the triad stand side by side.
