# Chapter 33: The Works

Beats: Ironhythe. The Accord's "fallback cage" is going up in the yards: a ring of heartstone engines and great chain, the first chains again in iron (Magic §5, §7). Sarovel's hands do the heavy carrying. Tam and Odo are here as Hearthmere smiths; Odo, in his chair, says which links will fail, and Pike has him moved off the works. Tam forges the Bridle's great chain until the order comes to forge its central ring with six hollows in its inner face. He made collars for binders once *(Book 1)*, and he won't make a thing he can't see the use of. He refuses, and Pike sends him off the works to the Marches with the wagons. Nobody asks why a smith would refuse good pay; Tobin notices. At night Tobin sees a man with stiff arms who was a knight at Greyhold when Tobin was a student: Callis. Callis begs him: "I'm nobody here." Tobin writes to Rowan that a Restoration man is in the works (see 41); Pike holds his three thousand's wages and bread, so he says nothing to anyone else.
Echo of another story: Tam at the next forge, asking if anyone's heard from his sister.
