## The story so far

*The Watch Below*

They say the Last Titan was only a story. In the Marches, where the Wend runs between Hearthmere and Saltreach, it was a story with a war in it. The killing of Teo Varr set the Dragon Clan against itself. Maren Ashdown, the oldest unbonded student at Greyhold, failed her trial on the forbidden island rather than harm the grey lion she found there. Calder Fenn, a barley farmer on the Saltreach bank, took the bonds of three dragons as their riders died, and was hunted for it. Under Hearthmere lay its secret: a Titan chained for three hundred years, its bled breath lighting every lamp. The Restoration, the twins Cassian and Corvin Vale, opened the seal to take it. Maren bonded the lion, Orrun, by giving herself into the binding, and killed Cassian at one lock. At the other, Calder's hand was on Corvin's wrist as it turned. They met underground and watched the Titan tear free and fly.

*The Hunt Beneath*

Hearthmere woke to dead ground. Calder told Maren what he had overheard under the arch, that the twins had arranged Teo's death, and they took separate roads.

Maren followed the Titan, because Orrun would not let his kin go. It went from temple to temple, one in each kingdom, and at each it took a relic, a hollow egg of old stone, into the plates of its back. Under the Hollow Ring were carvings of six great beasts asleep. One by one the sleepers woke. The Antlered rose at the Moot on Lake Ysse, where Keeper Hesper and her duskcat Hush died. The Pale Coil came out of Glasswater and drowned lower Vael. The Furnace came up molten under the Scald, and Kessmoor's heartstone cage, fed on beasts' hearts, closed on it and held. The Horned Sun broke Solenne's palace cliff into the bay. The Bell was the reef itself, and its wave took Gullhaven's spit and two hundred and ninety lives.

In Vael the tide-reader Idony Vael had seen every glass in his college break. The Long Library's old speaking bird, his only friend, showed him its oldest roll: his own College of Tides had sold Hearthmere its chains and its noon oath. Then Corvin's hunters killed it and took the roll, and Idony's ring was broken for leaving the colleges' binding. On the one night Maren and Calder shared there, Calder's milestones and Idony's charts made one map: the old roads run on the Deep's lines, and their six ends lie over the six sleepers. Under the Scald the carvings told the rest. The song's "till the six are one again" was the temple's own line. The Titan was waking the five to command them.

At Solenne, Idony's word freed the royal unicorn, and it killed the Bond-King. Mother Ysa rowed the last boat out and didn't come back. Corvin took Idony to read the chain-words. Tobin stayed with Solenne's unchosen, and Maren, knowing now that the sixth relic lay in a vault beneath Greyhold, went home to keep the door.

Calder's road was the Clan's. Corvin raided the Roosts with honey-smoke and took four young dragons, and Tor Brecc and his dragon Skell died there. Arn and Ulric poured their ash together, one hand each, and gave Calder his errand: bring the four home, and the names of whoever paid for Teo. A Saltreach house had paid. He said so in Hallowmere's square and before the Tide Council, the house fell, and the Restoration's army broke unpaid. He brought three young dragons back, and Wren's voice woke one of them: Ildra. On the coast Vey died on the reef, and the Clan lifted Gullhaven off its marked roofs.

The Titan came home a year after it flew. The crown's second cage failed, Magister Dane died in his engines, and Rowan let go of the chain to save her people. In the cavern, Corvin's chain-words brought the roof down on the seventy in Greyhold's cellars. Maren gave up the door and sent Orrun and all his shadows up through the rock for them. Idony spoke the chain-words rightly, held the Titan until the cellars were empty, and burned out. The control relic took Corvin's hand, the Titan took the relic, and five answered. Kessmoor's cage broke. Greyhold's hill fell in, and Corvin got away with Callis and the roll.

At Liss's table the envoys of broken places agreed no single kingdom could stand. "Then we stand together," said Ulric. "Or we drown one at a time." The five came to the Last Titan on the Scarfell, and at dusk the six went up together and flew east into the Worldsend. Maren watched them go from the ferry-house jetty. Calder sat down at Bel's table and began to write.

## Who's who

*As they stand when Book 3 begins.*

### The seven, and their beasts

**Maren Ashdown**: Greyhold's late-oath student, made Watch by Hale before he died; she broke her oath for the lion and refused the crown's pardon; she counts.

**Orrun**: the ancient grey lion, her partner; nearly Aurveth's size now, white at the roots of his mane; stone opens for him, and he can raise shadows of himself, each as strong as he is, four at most and only once.

**Calder Fenn**: a barley farmer of Harrow's End, bonded by accident to three dragons; there is still a price on him in Hearthmere; he has just learned to write.

**Aurveth**: the eldest of his three, a great bronze; she has outlived three partners, flies slowly on a wing that mended crooked, and through her he has felt the Deep itself.

**Kharrak**: the broad, scarred red; speaks in bursts, thinks restraint is cowardice, and keeps choosing it anyway.

**Lisska**: the lean green far-seer; jokes, carries word to Maren through Orrun, and her kin-voice once reached a bound dragon.

**Idony Vael**: a tide-reader of the College of Tides at Vael, about thirty-one, his ring broken; burned out under Greyhold, grey-haired, his hands shaking for good; he still knows everything and can still read a glass.

**Ilse Rowan**: Greyhold's Knight-Captain, unbonded; she commands the forty knights left at the river, and let go of the crown's chain to save them.

**Arn Varr**: the dead chief Haldor's elder son, unbonded, flying a Clan red; proud, grieving, and sharing the Clan with his uncle.

**Tobin**: Maren's unchosen friend from Greyhold; once a Restoration man, he walked into the Scald ringless and now holds Solenne's low city with its unchosen.

**Kit Merrin**: about forty, captain of the salvage ketch *Kittiwake* and pilot of Blackwater's small, indebted Merrin house; she talks prices the way other people talk weather.

### The Accord's kingdoms

*Hearthmere*

**Liss Ashdown**: Maren's mother, the ferrywoman; her house below the fallen hill is cracked and standing, and her rope still runs across the Wend.

**Tam Ashdown**: Maren's brother, a smith; forged chain for the crown's cage and left the last link on the anvil.

**Odo Marl**: Millbrook's smith, in his chair since Book 1; laughs like a bellows, complains about charcoal, knows which links will fail.

**Davin**: once the Kettle's potboy, now clerk to Hallowmere's street tribunes; reads aloud, slowly and loud.

**Wynn**: a knight of the gate, with his hounds Ash and Sorrow.

**Joby Marsh**: an old unchosen groom of Greyhold, who went down into the cellars with the seventy and a lantern.

**Mother Ketch**: eighty, of Millbrook's lower lane; she would not go on the river and went into the stone instead.

**Lord Ambrose Thale**: President of the crown's council in Hallowmere; old, thin, careful.

*Isvaren*

**Master Beatrix Quell**: a Master of the College of Tides, who knew about the hole under Hearthmere all along.

**Magister Ansel Corrow**: a thin, anxious magister of the Blue College, Dane's man, who did not deny the College's part.

**Nessa Vael**: Idony's sister, who works the family ferry on Glasswater and stayed to rebuild it.

*Kessmoor*

**Agathe Pike**: Master Engineer, who built the heartstone cage at the Scald and never asks what anything costs.

**Edmund Kaye**: First Guildmaster of Kessmoor's Assembly; small, bald, quiet, never without a ledger.

**Garth Tolley**: Pike's deputy, square and red-bearded; Kessmoor's envoy at Liss's table.

**Isak Thurl**: the Kessmoor engineer who strung the crown's second cage and read the cracks in Greyhold's wall.

*Sarovel*

**Seraphin**: the Bond-King's son, nine, carried out of the burning palace; the tribunes rule in his name.

**Perrin Sade**: a street-tribune of Solenne's mud-brick quarter, sent to the river by Tobin.

**Mattias**: a tribune who speaks for Sarovel's unchosen.

**Lady Ismay Aurel**: a court mage of the old Bond-King, in white and gold.

*The Wyldmarch*

**Elder Tova**: an elder of the horse clans.

**The Keepers**: the Wyldmarch's wardens of beasts and groves, with their wolves and bears; they burned their own sleepwood groves so no binder could have them.

**Mistress Orla Marrow**: Speaker of the Tide Council at Corsa, in blue silk, her salt-grey hair in a net; she read Calder's Gullhaven page aloud to the Council.

**Master Aubrey Strand**: of the house that bought the Bell's contract and lost Vey and four ships on the reef.

**Oswin Rusk**: the Halloran factor who struck the Restoration's ring-of-six silver, and fled on a fast ship when the house fell.

**Nell Tallis**: Sable's rider, lean and freckled with a burned ear; she has led Vey's wyvern company since the reef.

**Sable and Hask**: the company's two wyverns, tamed, never bonded.

**Coll Brask**: a sellsword out of Wendmouth who knows everyone, and once took Calder into the Halloran mint.

**Gannet**: the Wendmouth poacher whose smile arrives first; he has a grudge against the lion-woman and is bosun to Kit Merrin now.

**Jory Merrin**: Kit's brother, nineteen, quiet, the best pilot of the family.

**Hessa Pane**: a salt-wife of Gullhaven, with her tin cup of brine.

### The Clan

**Ulric Varr**: the uncle, the Clan's best warrior, once called Teo's murderer; he poured with Arn at the black tarn and stands beside him now.

**Gunna Tarne and Tamsin**: an old rider of the ledges and her grey-green dragon, three hundred and twelve years old; burned at the Roosts, they carried eleven people at Gullhaven in one flight.

**Gorm Askell**: Arn's steward; long, sour, ink-fingered, sure every levy is damp.

**The Clan's priest**: old and grey, with a staff; the one who asks "Who pours?"

**Ildra**: a young dragon stolen by Corvin's binders and brought back to herself by Wren's voice.

**The black and the red**: the other two young dragons brought home from the binders, still empty-eyed.

**Ada Fenn**: Calder's mother, who sings the old songs, wrote his first account and taught him his letters.

**Wren Fenn**: Calder's daughter, who makes everyone say it twice.

**Bel**: Calder's sister, widowed in Book 1, who keeps the long table by the warm spring in the hatching caves.

**Pip and Nan**: Bel's children.

**Old Grey**: Joss's dray-beast, bound once and driven to kill his master; now he follows Wren of his own will.

### Others

**Corvin Vale**: the surviving twin, Hearthmere-born; his right hand is gone, turned to grey stone at the wrist; he has the oldest roll and the chain-words.

**Callis**: the knight of the gate who opened Greyhold's river gate in Book 1; both arms badly set; Maren pulled him out of the flood and he went back to Corvin anyway.

**Old Fisk**: keeper of the Eelweir at Harrow's End, who sat out the flood on his roof "to tell it after".

## Places and words

**Winshire**: the old name for the world of the six kingdoms, kept by ancient beasts, old songs and a few scholars.

**The six kingdoms**: Hearthmere, the knights' kingdom, dead ground since the Titan flew; Saltreach, the lawless league of ports ruled from Corsa; Isvaren, the kingdom of colleges at Vael; Kessmoor, the kingdom of engines, which bans spoken magic and calls the Deep the Bleed; Sarovel, the bond-kingdom at Solenne, whose Grace has failed; and the Wyldmarch, the clans' country of forest and plain.

**The Clan**: the Dragon Clan of the Scarfell, on the Saltreach side of the Marches, ruled from Varrhold by the Varrs; its dragons nest on the Roosts.

**The Marches**: the border country along the Wend, with Greyhold's fallen hill on the Hearthmere bank and Harrow's End on the Saltreach side.

**The Accord**: the alliance the kingdoms will have to make; when Book 2 ends it is one sour sentence at Liss's table.

**The Deep**: the breath of the sleeping Titans, rising through the rock; every kind of magic in Winshire is borrowed from it.

**The toll**: what a working costs, taken from the place, from the worker, and for the bonded from the beast; at worst a worker burns out.

**Dead ground**: ground drawn empty of the Deep, where words are only words; Hearthmere is dead ground, and so is every kingdom a sleeper has left.

**Running water**: it breaks a spoken charm and a drawer's pull; Saltreach cuts its sea-charms into hulls as runes.

**The six disciplines**: charms, runes, drawing, shaping, tide-reading and binding.

**Bond and binding**: a bond is a beast's choice, one life shared between two; a binding is forced on a beast with smoke, words and a collar, and leaves it empty-eyed.

**Bond gifts**: power that comes from a bonded beast, not the Deep, so it works on dead ground; Orrun's stone, Aurveth's deep sight.

**Kin-speech**: dragons speak mind to mind as kin, and the great beasts as far kin; a person hears only their own bonded.

**Shadows**: another Orrun raised from the ground, as big and strong as he is; its wounds come back into him, and Maren is spread across every body.

**The hooked script**: the old realm's square writing with hooks at the strokes' ends, on milestones, chains and temple walls; few can still read it.

**Old magic**: what the old ones of the songs made themselves: the temples, the relics, the lock under Greyhold; dead ground can't touch it, and it isn't for people.

**Honey-smoke**: sleepwood resin cooked with honey; on the wind it opens any beast it reaches, bonded or not, to a binder.

**Heartstone**: Kessmoor's fuel, the plates and hardened hearts of harvested beasts; a heartstone engine never touches the Deep.

**The stir, the surge and the drain**: the three stages at every temple: the Titan drawing on it, the flood of wild magic when a relic is taken, and the famine when the sleeper rises and goes.

**The relics**: hollow eggs of grey old-realm stone that each held one sleeper's sleep; all five are grown into the Last Titan's back.

**The control relic**: the sixth relic, a stone ring with six hollows, once kept in the vault under Greyhold; it commands the five, and now it is in the Last Titan's back too.

**The chain-ends**: the broken ends of Hearthmere's first chains, still on the Last Titan's legs and still saying *hold*; whoever can speak to their runes has a grip on it.

**The Last Titan**: chained under Hearthmere for three hundred years and freed at the end of Book 1; the tales called it the last of its kind.

**The Antlered**: the Wyldmarch's sleeper, stag-shaped, antlered like a winter wood; the forest walks with it.

**The Pale Coil**: Isvaren's sleeper, a vast pale serpent of cold that lay under Glasswater.

**The Furnace**: Kessmoor's sleeper from under the Scald, plated and molten, a burrower that turns ground to glass; broken iron still hangs from it.

**The Horned Sun**: Sarovel's sleeper from under the Sun Stair, white-gold and horned like a unicorn the size of a hill.

**The Bell**: Saltreach's sleeper, a reef-backed leviathan the size of an island, the drowned temple's bronze bell still ringing on its back.

**The Oneroad**: the old realm's road, six ends running out from the bridge by the Old Ruin, a numbered milestone on each; its roads follow the Deep's own lines.

**The eddy where the island was**: the hub where the Oneroad's six roads met, three and a half leagues above Greyhold; the Old Ruin's island is gone, the river turns over its stones, and the Last Titan's cavern lies beneath.

**The drowned road**: a line of broken old-realm piers, each with its milestone, running south-west from the Drowned Bell's reef across the Shattered Sea.

**The Shattered Sea**: the reef sea off Saltreach's south-west coast, its islands the old road's broken piers; only Blackwater families pilot its channels, and the Bell's waking closed half of them.

**The Western Deep**: the open ocean to the west; one ship came back out of it with half its crew, who said they had seen a mountain walk on a far shore.

**The Worldsend**: the mountains at the eastern edge of the world, where the six went.

**The Tide Council**: Saltreach's rulers at Corsa, trading houses at one table and pirate captains on the Bench; it hires its armies, and to it a Titan is a contract.

**Salt**: Saltreach swears its contracts on a pinch of salt on the tongue; break one and you are unsalted, and no one will deal with you again.

**The Restoration**: the Oneward, the Vale twins' faction, sworn to "One again" under the ring of six; its army broke unpaid in Book 2, and Corvin is what is left of it.

**Keep the watch**: Greyhold's noon oath was a many-voice charm on the chains, written by the College of Tides with "and do not ask" in it; Maren says it her own way.

**The pour**: the Clan's rite for its dead, the ash poured into the black tarn at Coldtarn, and the priest asks "Who pours?"
