## Places and words

**Winshire**: the old name for the world of the six kingdoms, kept by ancient beasts, old songs and a few scholars.

**The six kingdoms**: Hearthmere, the knights' kingdom, dead ground since the Titan flew; Saltreach, the lawless league of ports ruled from Corsa; Isvaren, the kingdom of colleges at Vael; Kessmoor, the kingdom of engines, which bans spoken magic and calls the Deep the Bleed; Sarovel, the bond-kingdom at Solenne, whose Grace has failed; and the Wyldmarch, the clans' country of forest and plain.

**The Clan**: the Dragon Clan of the Scarfell, on the Saltreach side of the Marches, ruled from Varrhold by the Varrs; its dragons nest on the Roosts.

**The Marches**: the border country along the Wend, with Greyhold's fallen hill on the Hearthmere bank and Harrow's End on the Saltreach side.

**The Accord**: the alliance the kingdoms will have to make; when Book 2 ends it is one sour sentence at Liss's table.

**The Deep**: the breath of the sleeping Titans, rising through the rock; every kind of magic in Winshire is borrowed from it.

**The toll**: what a working costs, taken from the place, from the worker, and for the bonded from the beast; at worst a worker burns out.

**Dead ground**: ground drawn empty of the Deep, where words are only words; Hearthmere is dead ground, and so is every kingdom a sleeper has left.

**Running water**: it breaks a spoken charm and a drawer's pull; Saltreach cuts its sea-charms into hulls as runes.

**The six disciplines**: charms, runes, drawing, shaping, tide-reading and binding.

**Bond and binding**: a bond is a beast's choice, one life shared between two; a binding is forced on a beast with smoke, words and a collar, and leaves it empty-eyed.

**Bond gifts**: power that comes from a bonded beast, not the Deep, so it works on dead ground; Orrun's stone, Aurveth's deep sight.

**Kin-speech**: dragons speak mind to mind as kin, and the great beasts as far kin; a person hears only their own bonded.

**Shadows**: another Orrun raised from the ground, as big and strong as he is; its wounds come back into him, and Maren is spread across every body.

**The hooked script**: the old realm's square writing with hooks at the strokes' ends, on milestones, chains and temple walls; few can still read it.

**Old magic**: what the old ones of the songs made themselves: the temples, the relics, the lock under Greyhold; dead ground can't touch it, and it isn't for people.

**Honey-smoke**: sleepwood resin cooked with honey; on the wind it opens any beast it reaches, bonded or not, to a binder.

**Heartstone**: Kessmoor's fuel, the plates and hardened hearts of harvested beasts; a heartstone engine never touches the Deep.

**The stir, the surge and the drain**: the three stages at every temple: the Titan drawing on it, the flood of wild magic when a relic is taken, and the famine when the sleeper rises and goes.

**The relics**: hollow eggs of grey old-realm stone that each held one sleeper's sleep; all five are grown into the Last Titan's back.

**The control relic**: the sixth relic, a stone ring with six hollows, once kept in the vault under Greyhold; it commands the five, and now it is in the Last Titan's back too.

**The chain-ends**: the broken ends of Hearthmere's first chains, still on the Last Titan's legs and still saying *hold*; whoever can speak to their runes has a grip on it.

**The Last Titan**: chained under Hearthmere for three hundred years and freed at the end of Book 1; the tales called it the last of its kind.

**The Antlered**: the Wyldmarch's sleeper, stag-shaped, antlered like a winter wood; the forest walks with it.

**The Pale Coil**: Isvaren's sleeper, a vast pale serpent of cold that lay under Glasswater.

**The Furnace**: Kessmoor's sleeper from under the Scald, plated and molten, a burrower that turns ground to glass; broken iron still hangs from it.

**The Horned Sun**: Sarovel's sleeper from under the Sun Stair, white-gold and horned like a unicorn the size of a hill.

**The Bell**: Saltreach's sleeper, a reef-backed leviathan the size of an island, the drowned temple's bronze bell still ringing on its back.

**The Oneroad**: the old realm's road, six ends running out from the bridge by the Old Ruin, a numbered milestone on each; its roads follow the Deep's own lines.

**The eddy where the island was**: the hub where the Oneroad's six roads met, three and a half leagues above Greyhold; the Old Ruin's island is gone, the river turns over its stones, and the Last Titan's cavern lies beneath.

**The drowned road**: a line of broken old-realm piers, each with its milestone, running south-west from the Drowned Bell's reef across the Shattered Sea.

**The Shattered Sea**: the reef sea off Saltreach's south-west coast, its islands the old road's broken piers; only Blackwater families pilot its channels, and the Bell's waking closed half of them.

**The Western Deep**: the open ocean to the west; one ship came back out of it with half its crew, who said they had seen a mountain walk on a far shore.

**The Worldsend**: the mountains at the eastern edge of the world, where the six went.

**The Tide Council**: Saltreach's rulers at Corsa, trading houses at one table and pirate captains on the Bench; it hires its armies, and to it a Titan is a contract.

**Salt**: Saltreach swears its contracts on a pinch of salt on the tongue; break one and you are unsalted, and no one will deal with you again.

**The Restoration**: the Oneward, the Vale twins' faction, sworn to "One again" under the ring of six; its army broke unpaid in Book 2, and Corvin is what is left of it.

**Keep the watch**: Greyhold's noon oath was a many-voice charm on the chains, written by the College of Tides with "and do not ask" in it; Maren says it her own way.

**The pour**: the Clan's rite for its dead, the ash poured into the black tarn at Coldtarn, and the priest asks "Who pours?"
