# Book 3, Part One: Lights over the Worldsend: shared drafting ledger

*Same rules as Book 2's part ledgers. Before writing a scene that touches another POV's days or people, read this file. After deciding any fact another chapter could contradict (who is where on which day, what someone said, a name, a wound, a number, a beast), add one line under your name. The plan wins over the ledger if they clash; note the clash.*

## Maren (1, 4, 7, 11)
- Ch 1 (days 2–5): Maren at the ferry house since Book 2's day 362; envoys (Rowan, Tolley, Corrow, Perrin Sade) still there; Liss's rope running the Marches home. **Rowan's forty knights** (all that's left of the river's; Wynn with Ash and Sorrow; a woman with a falcon; three greyharts; most unbeasted). Orrun barn-sized and more, mane white at the roots, shoulder stiff in the mornings; **two shadows** dig the north side, slabs laid in rows along the slope's foot; Joby Marsh and twelve grooms with barrows. **D.2 the forge**: anvil split horn to heel, **Tam's half-struck link still on the horn**, his hammer under it (Maren keeps the hammer; leaves the link). **Isak Thurl** stayed with six Kessmoor men to write down what happened to the engines; rod: "Dead... Not drained... There's nothing under it." **D.3 the yard engine** on its side at the slope's foot, firebox burst, heartstones grey on the grass; **Dane** under it, gloved hands on the broken-off governor wheel (gloves taken off to free him); Orrun and shadows lift the engine a hand's breadth; buried by the fifty-three graves on the hill west of the walls; **Corrow** says the College's words; Thurl has the copy of Dane's letter to Ironhythe saying the anchors would hold. **D.3 the archive**: long table split, hanging lamp in black-glass pieces, **four dented iron chests** of the Watch's rolls and duty books (to Liss's outhouse; Rowan: "Nobody will read them now." "The reader will."). **The lintel**: the archive's three-block door-frame of inner-wall stone, lintel cracked across; two knights and four grooms inside; she holds it with her **left hand flat on the post, Orrun's earth through her, no word** (dead ground); it drops a finger's breadth and stops; all out, Joby last; it falls when she lets go. **Toll: her left hand grey and numb fingertips to wrist**, can't feel Orrun's mane; back by dawn, stiff and pale; she doesn't tell Liss; Liss sees. **D.4 Mother Ketch** on a donkey, sits on a lion-stone all day: "It fell. It held, though. Long enough... he picked up Sal Dobbin's boy like a cat with a kitten." **Rowan's offer** at Garran Vale's stone (the old stair's mouth under twenty feet of fall): captain of the knights ("Thorne's key went with Callis"), "captain of the road"; "No... I'm the lion's." "That's a rank too." **Echo, dusk d.4**: Lisska down the valley, a breath on the ferry-house roof, nothing on her leg; kin-speech via Orrun: the farmer's at the caves above the cold water writing everything from the beginning, started on the last day of the year at a table by a spring; Wren says you said twice and that was right. **The lights**, night d.4 and after: far east past the Scarfell on the world's rim, a white-gold dawn-glow at midnight, breathing, until true dawn; brighter each night; Orrun: *They are drinking... Out of the mountains... There was a great deal of the Deep in those mountains. There will not be.* **D.5** digging stops. **The charter stone** stood up against the ferry-house wall on d.3, face to the road; Maren reads its first line for the first time: *The gate shall stand open to all who would serve.* Orrun sleeps a day and a night.
- Ch 4 (days 8–10): night 8 Idony shows Maren the margin: "Light. Something that only gives. That doesn't drink... the only thing on the chart that doesn't." **Orrun's memory** (head in the kitchen door): a different, wider brown river, no ferry, no hill; a plain grey door in rock; tall not-people standing at it in a ring; a very young lion, mane not grown, lying before it; *They didn't die... They put me there and told me what to keep. And then they went... Into the light... West... toward where the sun goes down. Over the sea... We are going into the light. Keep the door.* Maren: "That's a song." *Yes. Somebody made a song of it.* Liss: "My mother sang that." Davin writes *the lion says* beside the margin. **No lights at midnight 8**. **Dawn 9 Idony: they've come down**: lean east-south-east; **the breath broken into six, out of time**, "like six people sleeping in one room"; onto the Hallow Plains. **Noon 9 a horse-clan rider** of the Wyldmarch (grey mare lathered to the hocks; kneels to Orrun the clans' way), sent by **Elder Tova** of the horse clans because the Keepers remember the lion-woman from the Moot: the six came down two days ago at the Worldsend's foot; **grass goes black where they walk; the Plains' standing stones crack, heard a league off**; every clan running west (horse clans, stonehorn clans, Keepers out of what's left of the Thornwood), **ten thousand on foot and more**, every stonehorn and mare; west is the only living land (mountains east, the High Crown north, a hundred dead leagues south); **riders turned back at the fords by crown knights with hounds**. **The council's answer to Rowan** (first in a month, blue seal): *The border of the kingdom is closed to the clans of the Wyldmarch and their beasts... No herds, no beast-folk, no Keepers... The knights of the Greenwold road shall turn them back.* Rowan: "The council doesn't believe in Titans. The council believes in grain. There isn't any." **Maren's answer to the tribunes**: *I can't come. I'm going east. / Tell the city the truth. Something is coming, and the council has shut the door on the people running from it. / Maren Ashdown.* Davin: "On the corn exchange steps. Twice." (He goes back d.10.) **D.10 on the jetty road before the forty knights Rowan forbids it** as senior knight at the river; "There's no school... There's no hill"; outlawed by name with a price; "Then I'll be outlawed." "I'll be the one who has to sign it." **Rowan turns her back**, then without turning: "The Greenwold road. That's where they'll put the knights... the only road from the Plains that'll take a herd. Don't go by the Greenwold." Wynn offers; Maren: stay, "Rowan will need you." Liss packs bread, cheese, salt; Maren leaves the brass lamp; "You always come home before... After's different. Come home after." Maren goes east at noon by the old drove track over the downs. **Echo, dusk 10** on the downs: thirty-plus lines of dragon-fire coming down the Wend low from the north past the fallen hill, on south past Millbrook: the farmer and the Clan. *To his sea.* The far eastern rim dull red.
- Ch 7 (days 10–26): east over the downs by old drove tracks (not the Greenwold road); **d.16 crosses the Ysse** (Hearthmere's eastern march, green, cold, fast out of Isvaren) at a drove ford north of the Greenwold road, unseen (drovers' marked stones, a cairn). **The Plains dead**: grey grass, grey stones, no hum, nothing alive for two days. **D.18 the clans**: stonehorns (grey, rock-plated on shoulders and back, cart-sized, horns curving forward like ploughshares) with herd-men and poles; mares with children two and three to a saddle; Keepers in green with wolves. **Elder Tova** (new; renamed from the plan's Ysolt: horse clans; older than Ada; grey hair cropped like a man's; furrowed face; root-knotted hands; bay mare with a white face; slow, weighed speech). **Keeper Rhun** (new: broad grey Keeper woman, a wolf at each knee, knew Hesper): Hesper died at the Moot with her cat holding the last lane; Hesper had said a lion would come out of the west with a woman on it. **Dusk 18 the six together**, from a ring of nine grey stones: the Plains burning black in a spreading band from the Worldsend's foot; the Last Titan (six newer places in its back); the **Antlered** with a walking forest (oak, thorn, ash pulling up roots); the **Pale Coil** coiled in a ring of rime; the **Furnace** lying flat on glass with **Kessmoor's broken chain still hanging off it**; the **Horned Sun** making half a league of day; the **Bell** dragging itself over dry plain, the bell ringing faintly; all moving west slowly. Orrun watches his kin and says nothing. **Days 20–23 the four Ysse fords** (the Greenwold road's is the fifth, southern, crown-held): river high with snowmelt; **Orrun at the northern ford and three shadows at the others** lie across the current upstream and **lift the bed into stone sills**: fords shallow from chest to waist (no word: dead ground, running water); **stonehorns breast the current shoulder to shoulder below the sills**; people cross in their lee; mares carry children; **wolves run the flanks** (Rhun calls them with a throat-sound like wind over stones); four days and nights. **D.22 the beast-flood** (wild horses, plains elk, a hundred feral stonehorns, wolves, deer, hares, a bear) at the southern ford; a league off at the second ford **she kneels, both hands on the turf, and pulls Orrun's stone through her: a quarter-league ridge of grey rock rises slanting across the flood's path**, man-high; the flood turns north along it. **Toll: no feeling soles to knees**, can't stand; *It never tells me which.* **D.24 Orrun carries her** across the last ford; shadows sink; sills go. Tova: "I've never seen a stonehorn turn for anything... Only a day?" **Dusk 24 a crown knight-captain** with twenty riders and hounds from the Greenwold road sees ten thousand across, looks at the lion, rides back ("He'll tell the council"). **Echo, d.25**: a 15-year-old horse-clan scout from the south: farmers say many dragons went down the Wend toward the sea ten days and more back; "There's a coast there that needs him." **D.26** feeling back, pins then cold; she walks at the column's head west into Hearthmere, six leagues a day.
- Ch 11 (days 44–50): **The eastern march** (a hundred leagues of grey downs between the Ysse and the Greenwold); stonehorns cropping to the dirt, ribs showing; column a league and a half long; children walking; **Tova counts the dead at dusk: eleven since the Ysse** (old people, a herd-man gored by a frightened bull, two infants in one night from cold; their mother walks at the tail with empty hands); "On the Plains in a hard winter we'd lose eleven." **Maren's feet cold in the mornings since the ridge** (an hour before she feels the ground); Orrun: "You're walking strangely." **D.45 a Greyhold falcon** (she knows its jess-bells). **D.47 the border**: the Greenwold's oak wall; the barricade's trunks in the ditch, the ditch planked; **Rowan alone on her greyhart** (white blaze) where the road enters the oaks; her knights, Crale's old dozen and the crown men **in two lines facing across the road "as if waiting for a wedding"**. Rowan: "You're outlawed. By name. Five hundred crowns... I signed it." "I know you did." "And I'm Marshal of the Border... The road's in my keeping." She turns side-on; **Orrun lies down at the planks opposite: the clans walk in between the lion and the knight** all afternoon and into torchlight (carts, mares, herd-men, stonehorns turning their eyes to the lion, Keepers, wolves at Rhun's throat-sound; a woman holds her baby up to a pikeman; the empty-handed mother last). Neither speaks. Tova: "Is that your captain?" "She's the crown's marshal." "She looks like a captain." **Rowan's terms at Tova's fire** (to Tova, not Maren): the Greenwold is the crown's hunting forest, unhunted a year, grey grass in the glades; the clans stay inside the wood (its western edge is the crown's line now); no grain; **"the aurochs are the crown's, and the crown isn't using them." "We'll take the aurochs." "Not all of them."** The jetty road unsaid. Rowan, leaving: "Your feet... I watched you. See to them." Orrun: "She watched you. All afternoon." **D.49 Dickon** (new: Liss's grandson, twelve, on a fat ferry pony with a white nose, five days alone): **Idony's readings in Liss's big round hand** (Davin back in Hallowmere): the Bell split d.25; **the other five divided at noon on d.41** (the glasses went still a long breath, then six breaths, all different): the Pale Coil north toward Isvaren; the Furnace (chain on it) south-west toward Kessmoor; the Horned Sun further south-west toward Sarovel; the Antlered back east toward the Wyldmarch; the Bell to the sea; **the Last Titan west toward Hearthmere: "each going home... its home is under you"**; "He says he's sorry it's slow"; P.S. the lights are gone off the mountains. Tova: "Then we've walked the wrong way." "You've walked the only way there was." **D.50 Kessmoor's call**: a rider in black and grey with a brass collar-clasp on an unbonded black horse; **printed sheets** ("To every kingdom... To the lion's knight, and to the dragons' farmer. The Hall of Measures at Ironhythe. The sixtieth day. Kessmoor will host. Agathe Pike, Master Engineer"); Maren named only as *the lion's knight* (Kessmoor printing round an outlaw); Rowan's says *the Marshal of the Border, for the crown*. Rowan: "Go... Kessmoor's found you a name." **Tova will go for the Wyldmarch.** **Echo**: Dickon's bargeman's tale: a gale off Corsa ~d.39, the chain winch broken with the herring fleet outside, **Lisska held the chain up out of the sea in her claws for an hour** while the boats ran in; Orrun can't hear her (too far). At the planks: "Thank you. For the road." "It isn't yours. It's theirs... I'll be the one who has to answer for it." "You always are." "Yes."

## Calder (2, 6, 9, 12)
- Ch 2 (days 3–6): d.3 = Book 2's day 365: the account's first line that morning, second after noon: *It was the end of the harvest fair, and I had a daughter asleep in the house, and nobody had ever told me anything.* Wren: "You'll need more paper." **The caves as a house**: Bel's hangings, the long table rebuilt, a hearth of hatching-stones under a roof crack, Pip and Nan sleep in a hatching bowl under Clan cloaks. **The black and the red** by the warm spring: still empty-eyed, but eating since the six went up, flying short round the tarn and back; hiss at anyone within a length except Wren ("They know I'm the one who talks about barley"). **Ildra** taller than Old Grey, pale-bellied, follows Wren with Old Grey; "She's not mine. She just comes when I talk." **Aurveth** lies across the cave mouth (head in by the hearth), horn-wound weeping; Ivar Leck packs it with moss and honey each morning; *Everything weeps when it's old. Ask your mother.* **Night 3 the hearth-word**: damp turf; Ada's swollen knuckles can't make the third turn; Calder with his hand hot from Kharrak's jaw (*You're never careful*): fire to the cave roof in a sheet, the roof black wall to wall, Pip's blanket alight (Bel beats it out, swearing); **his right palm blistered heel to fingertips, can't close it**; Ada: "That's not the hearth-word... It's the hearth-word shouted. Down a well. By a dragon... It's for a kettle." Bel: "Next time, ask me for a coal." D.4 morning Lisska sent to the river ("Go and see"). **D.4 noon Arn and Ulric** together; **Ildra walks across the cave and stands behind Arn's bench**, he pushes her nose away ("Go back to the girl"). Arn: the Clan will fly wherever needed, both halves agreed at the chair ("Nobody argued for a whole morning," says Ulric. "First time since Teo"); the riders want the three under the Clan's thread "As they were Teo's"; "They're not mine to give." Kharrak: *No.* Aurveth: *I'm tired... they can put their thread on Kharrak if they can catch him.* **Ulric's coast news**: the salt-pan farmers put Gullhaven's people off their land (no seed corn); **a thousand or more on the Coast Road west toward Wendmouth** with nothing; **the Council's contract**: *for the taking and holding of the three dragons bonded to the outlaw Calder Fenn of Harrow's End, for the Council's account. Alive. Ten thousand gold* (nailed at Gullhaven, Wendmouth and every Coast Road toll gate); the Strand house wants its ships back; Marrow's reading of his page shamed the houses: "They hate you more for being right. That's how the coast is." Lights from the Scarfell far brighter: the whole eastern sky white at midnight; every dragon on the ledges humming. **Echo, dawn 5**: Lisska's picture: the heap, rows of stone, three lions lifting, the lion-woman holding a cracked stone up with one hand, people under her arm; *Her hand was grey after.* **D.6 Lisska east, high**: back at dusk; the picture: the Worldsend's peaks, a valley full of light like molten metal, snow and ice gone to bare black rock for leagues, **six shapes in a ring, heads in, drinking**; **his eyes bleed** from both corners; blind till midnight; Ada bathes them. **Aurveth**: five lie heads in; **the Bell lies with its head south toward the sea**: *It wants its water... it'll go to your sea.* "The coast, then." Ada: "Go and explain yourself. Again." Wren will write for him.
- Ch 6 (days 12–20): Calder's father used to drive barley down the Way to Wendmouth each autumn and list what the gates cost. D.12 Gullhaven's hill empty (put off on the last day of the year: "nothing for the spring"). **D.13 the column** on the Coast Road west of Gullhaven, two leagues long, **~1,200**: old women, salt-boys, net-menders, ownerless carts, a few donkeys, a goat; **Hessa Pane** (arms brown and scabbed now): "You're late"; **her mother, the eldest salt-wife**, in a hand-cart pushed by two salt-girls, tin cup in her lap; **Old Nan Gorrie stayed** with a farmer's widow at the salt-store farm who'd known her as a girl. **D.14 Sallow Bar** (new: forty-house fishing town on a gravel bar; timber bridge over a tidal creek; chapel to the Drowned Lady with a blue-painted door): board *a copper a head, a silver a cart*; people paying in blankets, a net, a kettle, boots, a fiddle; **Hessa sells her mother's tin cup** for the cart. Kharrak lands on the bridge (tail in the creek); forty pairs on the bar (one on the chapel roof, which falls in). **The Gaffer** (new: one-eyed sellsword, leather coat, face like a boot left out in the rain; twenty crossbowmen; holds the gate this week). **Ulric pays the toll from the Clan's purse** over Calder's protest ("That's how the coast is"). That's my price"; **tosses back the cup**; **tears the notice off his post and gives it to Calder "For a keepsake"**; "I'd not stand under any of them... Not every gate's got a man on it who can count." Hessa puts the cup back in her mother's hands. **A ship's clerk: a berth Wendmouth to Corsa costs a year's barley**, per person; "For a year's barley, the Council will see to anyone." **Decision at the fire** (with Ulric, Hessa, **Abel Tarrow**, new: old drowned harbour-master's clerk with a stick): not Corsa, not Wendmouth: **inland to the Marches, Harrow's End and the fallow farms on the Way, and his own land**; "You put it in the ground and swear at the weather." **D.16 Wendmouth's gate** (stone bridge, iron bar, **Saffery house** red and black shutters; gate-captain counts coppers into a ledger): **two bounty men** in the crowd, **a brass net-gun** and **a hooded bound hound** with a smoke-pot man; **Lisska drops off the gatehouse wall** on the net-gun man (net lands on the gate-captain); Calder steps off into the creek, **the hound follows into running water and its eyes fill**; it runs; Ulric's riders hold the men; the smoke-pot goes in the harbour; Saffery apologises and charges toll for the dragons on the wall. **Echo, d.16**: a sailor up from the Staithe on a salt-barge: the lion-woman went east into the Wyldmarch against the crown's order, forbidden on the jetty in front of everyone; "Six days back"; the council will outlaw her. **D.17–20 the lift**: war-harness baskets from the gravel bar outside Wendmouth up the Wend, over the Reedmarsh and Cotter's Cross, up the Way to Harrow's End, **two hundred a day**, old, children, sick first. **D.20** Gullhaven families sit in his top field's winter stubble; Hessa's mother on a blanket with her cup. *The only thing on the whole coast that hadn't cost anything was a dragon standing on a roof with a white cross on it.*
- Ch 9 (days 22–28): **D.22 Calder to Corsa** with Kharrak, Lisska and Ulric (Aurveth stays at Harrow's End); lands on the Tidehall's sea-steps; **the docks come out and cheer him** (dockers, fishwives, hulk-folk wading ashore): the man who brought down the Hallorans; **a house clerk spits on the sea-steps** at his feet. D.23 Lisska sent north to look at the six. **The great room explained**: **the Houses' Table** (a seat per house over a set tonnage; most ships nearest the speaker; lose ships, lose places; **Strand moved three places down** since the reef; **the Halloran chair taken out and burned on the quay** by dockers after the fall); **the Captains' Bench** by the door (battered oak, dead captains' names cut in its back; seats won by holding a stretch of coast, lost the same way; boots on the table's end). **D.24 the sitting**: Calder unfolds **the Gaffer's torn notice** before Marrow: "Here I am... And there they are... Take them." Nobody moves. **Aubrey Strand** (harder since the reef): the contract stands; "I intend to." **Captain Hal Rennick** (new: holds the Saltings' channels; big, laughing, gold ring in one ear): "Make it a hundred thousand and I'll think about it... From very far away." Calder's ask: **empty the low harbour** (hulks on the mud inside the chain, Corsa's poor) before the Bell comes home (Aurveth: it's turned to its sea). **The Ostrey woman** (grey, top of the table): **"It's herring season"**; ~2,000 hulk-folk are the gutters; six-week season; "Who pays that?" / "Who pays the wave?" / "It's not on any contract." No member moves; the houses vote as one since the Hallorans fell. **Marrow privately**, in a back passage: reading his page cost her two votes and a window (a brick with his name on it); she's speaker because both ends of the table fear her, because she's never asked them for what they wouldn't give; **"Bring me a contract I can pass"**; "I haven't got another window." **D.25 gull-hawks** onto the harbour-master's perch from Blackwater, Wendmouth, Rennick's ship on the Saltings, a Gearmouth ship a month out; the boy: "A gull-hawk always knows the ship... Ask for Tallis.* **Nell Tallis** (burned ear; company mark a grey glove on black) refuses **Strand's factor**: the company took the Clan's coin for the children at Gullhaven; **the captain's rule: no contract against the coin's payer** ("The captain's dead." / "The rule isn't."); to Calder: "Can you pay?" "No." "Everyone on this coast loves you, and nobody on this coast can sell you anything." (Plant for ch 15.) **Echo, dusk 28**: Lisska back onto the Tidehall roof: **the six have split**; five together moving west, slower, near the Wyldmarch's western edge; **the one with the bell left them three days ago (Aurveth) and drags itself south-west alone toward the sea**; slow on land. His eyes don't bleed (she didn't go close). Ends looking at the board on the wyvern quay.
- Ch 12 (days 60–65): **Calder from Corsa** (left d.58) on Kharrak with Lisska, west along the coast and up the Coldrun; Ulric and the forty pairs at Corsa, Aurveth at Harrow's End. Ironhythe: a hundred chimneys, brown smoke-roof, iron sheds, black canals, **green alchemical lamps lit at noon** (Lisska: "Something that smells"). Kaye: **Kessmoor's Western Deep ships are needed**; no kingdom will carry her. **Echo**: in the gallery among Sarovel's grey-smocked hands, a young man looking up at the clock, not the table; Maren looks up once; Calder doesn't know him (Tobin). **Dawn 65 in the yard**: the lion along the wall, bigger than Kharrak; **Lisska and Orrun talked all night** (the chain in the gale; the stonehorns at the river). **The tip**, unasked: "Blackwater... The clock says noon.

## Idony (3)
- Ch 3 (days 6–8): **Davin** (17; Kettle's potboy, corn-exchange clerk's boy, now clerk to Hallowmere's street tribunes) comes down the King's Road d.6 on a grey tribunes' mule (crown-blue saddle-cloth gone brown) with **a letter from the tribunes for Maren: come to Hallowmere and tell the city what's coming; the council won't**; stays to write for Idony (round clerk's hand; reads aloud loud and slow). Envoys at Liss's table a fortnight on (Rowan, Tolley, Corrow, Perrin Sade): **Tolley's semaphore: the First Guildmaster calls every kingdom to Ironhythe, the Hall of Measures, "when the six come down"**, and wants to know first; **Corrow: no lantern owl back from Vael for nine days** ("Owls don't get lost"); Perrin: Solenne will send whoever can walk; Rowan: the council thanks her and sends paper; Davin: "The council's got no paper left. The tribunes have it." **The glasses** (four, one cracked) lean east hard; **all four breathe together, slowly lengthening**: in-breath 40 count on d.1, now **in 60, held 20, out 30**; "like the tide going out"; Davin: "Before a wave" (Millbrook's old men: the river going down before the big flood); **the west reads flat as a table**. **D.7 the margin**: the silk from his coat lining begins with **his copy (made at 20) of the Gallery's founding chart of the College of Tides** (200-some years old); at its **south-west edge, over the Shattered Sea, a steady rise with no breath**, and beside it ***error* in five hands across two centuries, then his own**; "It means nobody understood it. So they said it was wrong"; "Like a lamp. Left burning. In an empty house." Rumours written beside it: **Abner Quayle's *Margery*** (a mountain walking on a far shore, out of the Western Deep); **d.8 a salt-boatman** up from Wendmouth: **far-stone**, thumbnail flakes of old grey stone off a small, indebted Blackwater salvage house's wreck-cargo from the Shatters, sold on Gallows Quay as warranted old charms at two silver, "one in ten of them works." **Echo, night 7**: Maren's left hand on her cup, fingertips grey to the first knuckle, gripping so hard it doesn't shake; "The lintel... Nothing. That's the trouble... It comes back by morning. Less, every time." **D.8 the chart's first line**: *The east is being drunk. The west has been. Something in the south-west has never been drunk at all.* Davin reads it back twice ("The farmer's girl does that. Liss told me").

## Rowan (10)
- Ch 10 (days 30–45): **D.29 the council's summons** at Liss's ferry house (blue wax): Captain Ilse Rowan "to answer for a knight of her school" (Crale reached Hallowmere d.27). **D.30** she rides with **twelve knights and Wynn** (Ash and Sorrow); the rest of the forty stay at the river under **Sir Aldous Pryne** (Book 1). Dead ground the whole way; the hounds the only thing still working. **D.32 Hallowmere**: noon bell nobody faces; bread queues round corners; lamps glass with dust inside; sweet wells brackish; Mere grey, low, smelling of mud. **The lost girl** (five) in the tanners' lanes: Wynn puts **Ash** on her clog; a quarter of an hour through dyers' lanes and a hide-yard, half the lower town following the hound "as if it were a lamp being carried"; she's in a dry cistern under a grate (went in after a cat). Old man: "How does it still do that?" Rowan: **not the Deep: "a hound and a man who've agreed about something... It isn't in the ground."** "Can I have one?" **D.33 the council** in the crown's chapter-house (faded orders' banners): **Lord Ambrose Thale**; **Sir Hamon Crale** (new: the ch 7 knight-captain; heavy, red-faced, honest, frightened and ashamed of it): ten thousand across the Ysse, the lion "the size of a barn... It looked at me." Decision: **the Greenwold line holds; the clans stay in the eastern march** (between the Ysse and the oaks); no grain. **The outlawry**: Maren Ashdown outlawed by name, **five hundred crowns**; needs her school's captain: **Rowan signs** ("Ilse Rowan, Captain, Greyhold"; she's never said she'd do a thing and not done it: the bay colt at nine, collarbone). "What are his knights to do?" "Turn them back." "With what?" No answer. **Davin** on the steps (read Maren's letter to the square d.20; tribunes asked four times; council stopped answering): "Did you sign it?" "Yes." "She said you would." **Night 33, the crown's chapel**: twelve glass lamps on the altar step (the gods' gift to the crown), dusty; a tallow candle; she has never prayed; lights a candle from habit; **the chain**: three notes on her horn at noon of B2 day 355, pins out, the crown's chain run out ("the second she'd held for the crown, and the second that had gone"); she counted the seventy in the cellars dead and let go anyway (they lived: the lion tore the hill open, Joby brought them up; a groom swam the Wend to tell her); **she'd do it again; "That was the trouble"**: a knight who'd let go of the crown's chain would let go of the crown's order. Sits until the candle goes out. Doesn't pray. "Back to the river?" "No." **Days 34–38 east through the Greenwold** (oak tunnel; aurochs among the trunks; hounds keep close). **D.38 the crown's post** where the road leaves the oaks for the grey eastern downs: barricade of three-deep oak trunks, ditch, sixty crown men, Crale's dozen knights with hounds. "Take it down." Crale: "That's against the council's order." "Yes. Write it down." **Greyharts in the traces** (never draught beasts; did it because their riders asked); the post's ox-team brought up unasked; the crown men join; by dusk the road open, ditch planked. Crale's honest report; **she signs under the line**: "Now it's mine." **D.42 Thale's letter**: "Since you have opened the road, you will keep it": **Ilse Rowan, Marshal of the Border**, every crown man east of the Greenwold; Crale relieved; no lord would go; "There is no grain"; **"The matter of the knight Ashdown is pending"** (Thale's word for Calder, B2 ch 46); the crown's seal (crown and twelve lamps). She folds it once and keeps it against her chest: **a seal is a seal** (plant: faith in a sworn word). Wynn: "Marshal." "Captain'll do." **Echo, dawn 45**: a falcon and an outrider: two days off on the downs a dozen leagues east, ten thousand and more, stonehorns, horses, wolves on the flanks, "A lion. The size of a barn. With her on it." She goes to see to the greyharts.

## Arn (5)
- Ch 5 (days 8–12): **Varrhold's hall**: two long tables, **red thread left, grey right**, the **chief's chair** (black oak, dragons carved up the legs and arms, one across the back worn smooth) at the top between them under a plain grey wool horse-blanket cloth since Teo's burning; Arn sits on the bench beside it; they've poured together twice and nobody's said a name. D.8 evening Arn gives the riders Calder's answer and Aurveth's joke: red side silent; **Bjarke** (new: young, loud red-thread rider of the high ledges); **old Hulda Sarr** laughs ("That's her to the bone"). **Gorm Askell's ledgers**: Aurveth bred on the high ledges in Rurik's grandfather's time, three Clan partners; Kharrak bred in the spring pens in Haldor's time, mutton eleven years; *Teo Varr. The green, from the spring pens. Bonded in his sixteenth year.* "Say it to the green one." "She'd bite me." **D.9 the pens**: a hundred-plus kept, unbonded dragons, oak bars, keepers with mutton barrows; **every dragon silent and facing east since dawn** ("stopped humming at dawn. All at once. Like a door shut": old keeper **Leir**, new). **Noon 9** a grey-thread rider from the eastern watch: the six are down on the Plains; black smoke along the eastern rim. **Ildra** (came down with Wren and Ada on Old Grey to see Calder off) follows Arn round the pens a length behind; **sent back four times**; the fifth time Wren leads her off by the stub of horn over her eye: "She likes you." "She's not mine." "That's what I say." **Gunna Tarne** on the pen rail with her clay pipe, half her face scar, arm stiff from the shoulder, Tamsin's wing black and thin at the trailing edge: "That's not a dragon that's lost. That's a dragon that's found something... not one of these has ever followed you a length." **Echo, d.9 afternoon**: Calder in the yard arguing aloud (Kharrak mustn't burn the toll gates; Lisska must eat the sheep, thin since the reef; Aurveth how far she can fly); Bjarke and three young riders laugh on the steps; **all three dragons turn and look at them at once**; Bjarke sets his cup down very carefully. **Night 9 at the chair**: Calder stands as a petitioner between the tables, asks for half the flights (Gullhaven's thousand on the road; the contract; the Bell turned to its sea); Arn: "The Clan's place is the Scarfell"; **Ulric: "Teo would have gone"**; it galls Arn; "Half... You take them... Forty pairs. Not one more... I hold the Scarfell." "Thank you." "Don't." **Dusk 10** forty pairs (red and grey mixed), Calder's three among them, Ulric leading on his grey, go down the Wend breathing fire to light the way. Ildra in the empty hall's porch; he doesn't send her away; doesn't call her. **D.11–12**: smoke in the east thicker, then moving west toward the border; Arn doubles the edge watch; Bel: ready since the autumn, "where would he like them moved to"; Ildra sleeps in the hall's porch; he steps over her tail.

## Tobin (8)
- Ch 8 (days 15–45): **the three royal olifants** (tusks sawn short for doorways) haul white rubble out of the burned city in chain traces for a cartwheel loaf each at noon ("They moved now. When they were fed"). **The low city** (mud-brick quarter under the white city's walls) run by **twelve tribunes** elected by shouting in the fish-market; Tobin isn't one. **Sorrel Isle regency** in the lighthouse keeper's cottage: **Seraphin** (nearly ten, thin, grave, a scar on his cheek from the night he was carried out), **two tribunes and Old Sebold**; **Lady Ismay Aurel** survived the palace in a ruined white gown and teaches the boy his letters every afternoon; Mother Ysa under the light. **No food**: the seed was in the palace granaries (now in the bay); d.15 a week left, less. **D.20 Inspector Harl Moss** up the Sun Road with two iron-wheeled Kessmoor carts, one of dark square bread ("to show the contract's real"): **three thousand hands for Ironhythe's works**; **iron Kessmoor coin every seventh day, a loaf a day a head, a roof in the works' sheds, a year or as long as the work lasts**; "there'll have to be an alliance... an alliance will need engines. And engines need hands." **Sebold laughs at "hands"**: "a hand is a man who does the work out of sight while a lord takes the credit for it upstairs... now you've come all the way down the Sun Road to make us hands again." "I've come down the Sun Road with bread." "That's the joke." Two days' argument; tribune **Mattias** (new) won't be a hand again; a woman: "Will you eat your pride, Mattias? Is there enough of it for the children?" Tobin: Kessmoor is "cold. And they write everything down. And they pay what they say they'll pay"; **"I'll go... I'll lead it... You can stop being a hand. You can't stop being dead."** **Moss writes every one of 3,000 names**, trade, age, children ("Kessmoor writes people down... We write down what you can do"). Moss: Ashdown's on the Wyldmarch border with the clans; the council may outlaw her. **Echo, night 24**: a Sun Road stage-keeper's boy brings **Maren's letter** left at his father's stage in the winter: *Tobin. / I'm going home. The farmer's coast is next. / Keep the watch. / M.* (in his shirt). Perrin went north in the winter with Tobin's letter and hasn't come back. **D.25 three thousand go north** with a dozen of the court's old hands in brown; Sebold stays (too old); **the olifants stay** for the rubble and the boy (Tobin: "Keep the road. Nobody can make you move"; it feels his face with its trunk). Sun Road north, grey; lions still on the stage gates; stage-keepers kneel at the brown robes and get up embarrassed; six to seven leagues a day; a baby born in the Sunward Hills; an old man buried under a cairn above Highcrest; a boy with a broken leg on a cart; **Moss's bread at every third stage, on the day**. **D.45** at the Iron Fells' foot: a hundred-plus black chimneys, iron-roofed sheds in rows, and on a rise the **square stone clock tower of the Hall of Measures** (white face, black hands). "Hands. Come on. They'll want to write us down."
