*Same rules as Book 2's part ledgers. Before writing a scene that touches another POV's days or people, read this file. ## Maren (14, 17, 21, 24, 27)
- Ch 14 (days 76–80): **The road** (told back): out of Ironhythe d.65 south-east, Orrun's long lope, twenty leagues a day and more; **d.69 Liss's ferry house**: Idony packed and waiting two days on the bench (the founding chart in oilcloth, two tide-glasses in wool, Liss's letters); Liss: "Bring him back. He eats nothing. He'll talk you deaf... Bring yourself back while you're at it"; **Idony rides behind Maren tied on with a strap Liss made**; down the Wend's west bank, across at the Staithe, east on the Coast Road; **round Wendmouth by night** (Saffery's gate, bounty men); past Gullhaven's empty hill (spit gone; a blue chapel door face-down in the grass); **round Corsa inland by the Saltreach Track** (d.73, from a hill: forty dragons turning over it; she doesn't go in). **D.76 dusk down the black cliff path**: sellswords back away from their fires; **the stilt-houses pull up their ladders one after another round the inlet**; ships slip moorings and stand off; Orrun lies among the abandoned fires: "It's very black... Like the river under the hill." Idony laughs: "They pulled up the ladders. Against a lion." **Maren has three silver and eleven coppers, and the Accord's brass seal.** **D.77 dawn Kit rows across alone** and looks at Orrun "as if he were a cargo": "You're the one who wants to go west... I'm the one who can take you"; Kessmoor's ships draw four fathoms and couldn't pass the second milestone. **Gannet** in the second boat: "Mistress student knight"; the cub "worth a gold piece... Bill of sale and everything." "It was worth more than that to the cub." Kit: "Don't lend him anything." **D.78 the price** in the stern cabin (Orrun on deck; the ketch sits lower): (1) **the Accord's warrant on Ostrey's paper** to clear the Merrin debt due day 100 ("Twenty-two days from now"; else Ostrey sells her to the Council as a coal-barge); Maren weighs Kaye's "and keep a record" and the farmer's "song", and **seals it** (*The Accord, for the war's necessary costs. The lion's knight.* in Kit's hand; Maren signs her name); (2) **labour: the reef-stone**; (3) **passage both ways and the rule kept by all aboard, lion included**; the road kept secret; Kit doesn't know why the rule (Hal "didn't drown of breaking it. He drowned of a squall"); breakers "I don't take them home". **Salt on the tongue**: a chipped clay dish of coarse grey sea-salt from the galley; a pinch each from the same dish: ***Salt between us. What's sworn is kept, or neither of us is fit to drink with.*** **Idony takes a pinch as a witness** ("Third century... Corsa's founding charter... I read it at Vael when I was twenty-two... Witnesses can taste. It's in the charter"); "Is he always like this?" "Yes." Gannet witnesses from the doorway, no salt. **D.79 Idony in the hold** on the far-stone ballast: **his tide-glass climbing on water** for the first time in its life. Kit talks prices instead of talking; Maren loses every argument. **Maren's left fingertips grey and numb** since the ford; she keeps the hand in her coat on the seal; Kit sees and says nothing. **Echo, dusk 80** on the cliff top: far west over the Saltings **a green speck turning**; Orrun: "Too far. She can't hear me... She's looking at the sea. Something in it." The ladders let down again one at a time. Twenty days to the hundredth.
- Ch 17 (days 84–86): **Dusk 84** Orrun hates the sea ("It moves... and never goes anywhere") and **swims out, badly, head high, ears flat**, to the near reef (Kit: "I'd not have believed that if I'd been paid"); Kit at the tiller, Jory at the oars, Maren in the bow; Idony on the *Kittiwake*'s deck in a blanket. Maren kneels on the reef's top, both hands on wet weeded rock; **two shadows rise out of the reef** and go down into the channel without a splash, shoulders under the block's ends; **she pulls the stone up through herself**; the block comes out of the sea streaming. **Idony shouts from the deck: *Into the light, and nothing!*** "Somebody carried a shore-stone out to the end of the road and stood it on the road's first pier!" **The Bell, a league out**: a long low reef-shape where there's none, moving west, water piling white along it "like a storm walking"; then **one deep bell-note under the sea**, felt through the reef; the shadows hold. **Kit: "That's my father's water."** The shadows walk the block along the channel and **set it face-up on the eastern reef's shoulder above high water**; they sink back into the rock. **Toll: left arm grey and numb fingertips to elbow**, a line "as if she'd dipped her arm in ash"; she can move it but not feel it; rows back one-armed; **it doesn't come back by morning** ("It's not coming back." "I know." "You didn't say." "No." "I knew anyway"); she puts it in her coat. **Jory sounds five fathoms.** **Dawn 85 Kit touches one grain of salt: "Done... That's the second thing"**; the first done with the seal; the third the crossing. **Noon 85 Fosse** presents the Accord-sealed paper ("I've had it read"); saw from his window the grey stone on the eastern reef; Jory told the beach about five fathoms; **he adds it up aloud**: the Merrins' road west, "*Rocks.* For eighty years"; "**Why would the lion's knight pay a war's necessary costs to go and fetch rocks?**" Ostrey accepts the warrant (the Tide Council signed the Accord); **the debt against the *Kittiwake* is cleared**; **within the hour Fosse's gull-hawk goes west to Corsa**. Kit: "That's what your seal cost." "You named the price." "I did... I didn't think to price that." **Days 85–86 loading**: casks, salt pork, biscuit in tins, lamp oil, salt; **the far-stone ballast restowed flat along the keel so the lion lies level on it**; Gannet works best of anyone and smiles at Maren. **Echo, dusk 86**: **Lisska lands on the roofless boathouse walls** (Calder sent her on the tavern word): kin to kin through Orrun: **Corsa's chain is up; the bell one passed Corsa this morning going west and turned in off the Saltings, lying close in; "it's going home. To where it slept. And then it'll come back out"**; "he'd tell you to put it in your coat"; she flies back west to Corsa. Idony reads the ballast aloud below.
- Ch 21 (night 94 to 95): **The crew named**: **Ferris** (mate, grey, sixty, sailed with Hal; at the helm), **Lorn** (old, deaf in one ear, best on a sheet, feels wind-changes through the rope), **Tansy** (cook, broad, red-armed, sings), **Nye and Sim** (brothers, 16 and 17, aloft), **Corrie** (20, quiet Blackwater girl, taken on last year), and Gannet as bosun. **Moonless, an hour after midnight, on the ebb**; no lights; Nye lets the mooring go hand over hand; the ketch drifts down the inlet "like a log" past the houses' line (a pipe-smoking watch on Strand's ship sees nothing). Orrun: "I like it dark. It's like the hill." **Gannet at dusk: two joins in the boom's middle are rope, not iron, under a spar painted white** so the black ships can watch it; "I've been ashore every night. For news." "Let fall": the ketch "leapt"; **Ferris puts the stem square on the white spar; the lashing parts "like a bowstring breaking"**; **the boom's two halves swing in on the black ships' capstans to net her**. **Maren puts her right hand down the forehatch onto the far-stone ballast** (the only stone aboard; Orrun lying on it): *The cliff. The east one... It's rock.* / *I can feel it. Through this* ("as if the far-stone were a coal he could blow on"); **one shadow rises out of the eastern headland's cliff-foot and holds the boom's eastern end open in its jaws** against twelve men on a capstan; the western half scrapes along the port quarter and its cable goes under the counter; Ferris leans on the tiller and it doesn't catch; through. **A bolt from Rennick's eastern ship holes the mainsail** "the size of a door"; **Rennick laughs and raises a hand** "as a man raises his hand to a horse that's won a race he didn't have money on"; he doesn't loose again. **Toll: the grey climbs past the elbow to a hand's width below the shoulder; she can't feel the arm at all** ("like a sleeve with something heavy in it"). **Kit: "That's it. I'm unsalted. As of the chain."** Gannet: "And the crew?" "**Crew's crew. You're mine, not the League's. You signed my articles**... If they want you unsalted they'll have to catch you first." Idony slept through it on the lion: "Was it very exciting?" "Yes." **Dawn 95 the Shatters**: reefs and black rock teeth in every direction, white water, dark channels, the long south swell ("named for the split"). **Kit reads the turns from the book without Jory**, slowly, Ferris calling soundings, brothers aloft; **first pier in two days**. **Orrun silent on the ballast**: *No stone. For a hundred leagues. Every way... It's like standing on a coin. In a river.* Nothing else all day. **Echo, sunrise 95**: far back on the black cliffs above Blackwater **a green speck crying, the same long high note, over and over**; Orrun can't make words of it: *It isn't for us*; Maren knows something has happened at Corsa (Ulric; she won't learn it for a long time). **Noon: the first turn** (a tooth-rock and its shadow, as Abram drew it): "The book's right."
- Ch 24 (days 95–114): Out of the reef-maze by the book's turns on 95–96 into **open blue-black sea, no bottom found** ("Not even her father"). **D.97 the first pier**: square grey column out of open sea, broken at twice the mast's height, white with birds, the milestone upright on its top: **Idony through Kit's glass: *Into the light, forty-one***. Two or three piers a day, south-west; some awash, some taller than any tower, birds wheeling; **Idony reads every number before Kit finds it; after the tenth Kit waits for him first**. Maren: the piers held up **a causeway**, gone down when the world split. **Orrun feels each pier come and go** (*Stone. Real stone. Old. Like the arch... Gone... like stepping-stones. In a river*); **Kit won't sail the road at night** (nor Hal, nor Abram): **heave to on a long line beside a pier**; all sleep aboard; **at night beside a pier Orrun breathes long and slow "as if he'd come up for air."** **D.100 freight rates**: **Idony's grandmother (his mother's mother) kept the ferry across Glasswater under Vael's towers**, a flat scow on a rope, three generations; hemp, three-strand, a new rope every second spring; **a copper a head, two for a horse, a silver for a Warden's lynx ("the lynx paid for the rope")**; coffin as freight or passenger: Kit "Freight", Idony "**Passenger. Half fare... They'd paid enough**"; **Kit laughs out loud, the first time since Blackwater**; "She drowned three Wardens in forty years for not paying, and she was very soft about the dead." **Maren's toll spreads**: **d.99 grey over the shoulder and along the collarbone** to a finger short of her throat-notch; **d.106 a palm-sized grey patch on her left ribs under the arm: lying on her left side she can't feel her own heart** (she can on the right); coat fastened to the throat in the full sun; **Tansy (d.108): "You'll boil in that, knight." "I'm cold."**; Orrun sleeps with his breath on the patch. **D.104 dawn the Bell passes a league east**, a reef-shape moving south-west, white water, the bell under the sea; it crosses their bows and is out of sight ahead by noon; Kit: "It's going the same way"; Idony: "It slept on this road. Its temple was on it... Toward the light. Like us"; Kit: "My father's water." **D.110 the ninth pier** (low; its milestone leaning as if struck): **Kit heaves to close and drops a pinch of salt from the chipped dish into the sea; Ferris bares his head**; "On." **Echo, from d.98: beyond kin-range for the first time since the bond**: no hounds, horses, stonehorns, cats, foxes, Lisska, Kharrak ("like a fire in the next room"), Aurveth ("like a well"); *There's nobody... Every way. Only us*; seventeen days of it. **D.114 dusk *Into the light, two***; hove to; **a pale glow under the south-western horizon "as a town's lamps glow over the brow of a hill"**; Orrun lifts his head. *(Author note: the Bell's temple on the road's line favours the atlas's ~25 leagues off Gullhaven over the prose's 2.)*
- Ch 27 (days 119–126; 116–118 told back): Three days at anchor in the lagoon, nobody ashore. **D.119 landfall**: Kit, Ferris, Maren, Idony in the boat (Orrun swims): **the drowned causeway rises under the lagoon onto the beach and runs on inland as a paved grey way, a cart's width and more, straight between the dunes**; Orrun: *Stone. All of it. Everywhere*; **at the tide-line a waist-high milestone, sharp-cut: *Into the light*, no number** (Abram's last page); Idony wades to it, hands on it. **The city on the sand**: column-tops in rows standing in the sand "to their chins", flat roof-lines, a wall to the water, half-sanded doorways, **a great flight of hundreds of wide steps up into the biggest dune**; all humming; **no people, no bones, no tools, no rubbish: "as if everyone had got up one morning, and put everything away, and gone out"**. **Idony's glass on the steps climbs to the top and stays** ("It's full. All of it. The whole shore"); **his hands shake as an old man's, not a burned man's** (he doesn't remark it). **The beasts don't run**: small dun white-tailed deer smell Kit's coat and nose her hand ("They don't know what I am"); a hare washes its face by Maren's boot; **sea-birds land on Idony's shoulders, knees and shaking hand**; a red fox asleep in a doorway opens one eye at the lion; **a herd of small grey horses on the dunes** looks at Orrun and goes back to grazing; Idony: "They've never seen us. Not anybody. Ever." **Kit's rule: ashore by day only, back aboard before the sun touches the sea; one crew always in the boat at the road's end; casks filled in pairs at a spring under the steps** (sweet, cold, humming; Tansy: "tasted of nothing"). **Gannet ashore every day alone with a canvas bag, back with bag and pockets heavy** (Kit lets him: "Merrins took stone home"). **D.122 from the platform at the top of the steps** (paved, low wall, looking inland): green close-cropped hills rolling inland, the road a grey line into them; **on the farthest ridge a long grey great-cat shape the size of a hill, lying, rising, lying**; Orrun stands: *It's...* **D.123 Maren goes with him** against the rule's spirit: "I won't sleep. I'll stay awake"; **Kit tells her now: Abram's brother slept on the sand here and "They left him with his eyes open... I didn't tell you. I'm telling you now."** Idony forbidden, goes back aboard grumbling. **Two days inland on the road**: milestones every league, all *Into the light*, none numbered; deer walk beside them; something like a bear watches from a hill; **the light rises out of the grass and the road round them at night, warm as a banked hearth**; **she counts it: sixty rising, a long hold, sixty sinking**; nearly sleeps twice, bites her cheek (right side) till it bleeds; Orrun: *You're tired. Hold on to me.* **Noon 125 the hill's crown, a flat table of grey rock**: **the mountain: a great grey cat the size of a hill, forepaws out, head on them, a grey mane like dust or mist or smoke; eyes shut; light rising round her even at noon.** **Orrun lies down ten paces from her paws** (as at the Hollow Ring, Book 2 ch. 16, 18), head on his paws. **Her eyes open: gold, cartwheel-sized, older than Aurveth's**; through Orrun **Maren feels her know him "as a cat knows her own kitten in a heap of kittens in the dark"**; **Orrun makes a sound Maren has never heard: a kitten's**, over and over; Maren weeps (feels it only on the right side). They stay on the hill; Maren awake against a milestone, hand on the road. **Echo, after midnight 126 from the hill's north-east edge**: at the very rim of the dark sea **a line of tiny lights, ten, strung out, none behind: the Council's fleet sailing at night**; days off yet. Toll unchanged ashore (neck to jaw; ribs).

## Calder (15, 19, 22, 26)
- Ch 15 (days 66–78; plan's 66–76 stretched for the echo): **D.67** back from Ironhythe; Ulric and the forty pairs on **the old signal-field** above the Tidehall. **Ulric isn't bonded**: he flies behind **Sigge Brack** (new: young, silent grey rider of the Brack steading at Skarrow) on Sigge's grey, which the coast calls "Ulric's grey" (resolves chs. 6, 9). **The chain**: two round towers; tarred links as long as a forearm; lies on the harbour floor when down; **raised by a twelve-man capstan in the eastern tower's winch-house** (drum split in the gale and replaced with Gearmouth iron); **chain money** paid by tonnage at the tower window's iron grille to a clerk with the Council's book; a bell; the long iron roar. A ship-master thanks him for Lisska in the gale. **Chain-master Cobb** (new: old, deaf, exact; counts the links every time): the low harbour pays "Nothing. Nothing in it goes anywhere." **Forty-one hulks, two thousand and some people.** **Dorcas Kell** (new: the hulk-folk's spokeswoman, a gutter, fifty, forearms crossed with knife-scars; lives in the captain's cabin of the ***Merry Agnes***, a whaler forty years dead, families in her oil-holds): "Because this is the house... Leave your hulk and... by night there's someone worse on it... You'd not leave your farm." **Lisska on the roof: "Then take the boats. With them in."** **D.68 the chain-clerk's book, fourth page: *Any contract of the Council for labour in the harbour under the sum of one silver may be signed by the Speaker alone*** (the odd-jobs rule: a boy to scrape the sea-steps). **Calder writes the contract himself** (an hour): lift the hulks with the people in them **up onto the old ropewalk terrace behind the city, above the wave-mark**; gutters walk down to the herring each morning; the houses lose nothing; **price one copper**. **Marrow laughs** (nobody at the Tidehall has heard it; a passing clerk turns as if something fell); "You found the odd-jobs rule. The boy who scrapes the steps... Who read it to you?"; **signs alone** and pays **a Corsa copper with the ship-and-wave worn almost off** from her own purse: "A contract's not paid till the coin's crossed... Don't spend it"; "It costs them nothing at all." **D.69 Nell Tallis**: "Can you pay?" (she says it first) / "One copper"; "Any contract"; she takes it; **her five wyverns carry the lines under the keels**. **D.70–76 the lift**: Ulric shouting from the signal-field, laughing; first the coaster ***Cormorant*** (nine aboard, four dragons, "a sound like a boot coming out of a bog"; set upright in a timber cradle the hulk-folk built of the Council's wood; a woman laughs); **thirty-four hulks in seven days**; **Kharrak lifts the *Ostrey Pride*** (grain-ship, twenty years dead, eighty aboard) alone on one chain with Lisska calling the swing ("They screamed more at me than at the flying. I liked it"). **The last seven too rotten to lift** (the *Merry Agnes* among them): hauled up **the old slipway** on rollers by two hundred to a rope, **Dorcas's red ox Patience** (one horn broken, fifteen years on the fish-carts) and **a blind old stonehorn** from the scrub (mossy plates, chipped horn, white eyes; loose off a drover's herd on the Track twelve years; fed fish-heads; **comes when Dorcas bangs a pan**). **D.76 the low harbour empty** (bare mud, gulls, a child's shoe); house clerks stare; one writes in a little book; **the herring comes in and two thousand gutters walk down from the terrace**; "Nobody in the houses lost a fish." **Ulric** at dusk: Teo and Arn as boys making games of anything ("Arn always lost to Teo and never minded"); **"When the bell one's been and gone. I'll take you to Coldtarn... Show you where we pour... A man who lifts a harbour should see where we pour"** (plant 19, 22). **Echo, night 78, the Saltings Arms** (Nell buys him a drink with his own copper "to see what it was worth"): a Blackwater smuggler: the lion down the cliff path, sellswords walking backwards into the sea, ladders "Clack, clack, clack... Like a stick on a fence"; **"the Merrin woman rowed across to it... like it was a cargo she'd come to buy"**; Nell: "The ones who go further than they say." He doesn't smile. Forty-one hulks lit on the terrace above the wave-mark.
- Ch 19 (day 88; 86–87 told back): **D.87 noon Marrow rings the Tidehall's fire-bell herself** (nobody alive had heard it) and orders every street below the wave-mark up the hill; the docks go; **the houses load all night** (books, silver chests under guard; Strand green, Ostrey grey, Quill white; one house loads a harpsichord); **Rennick's three black ships slip out at midnight**. **Dawn 88 the last chain money**: Cobb lowers the chain for the houses' ships and **each pays at the grille by tonnage**; they go out east along the coast; the chain goes up again. Lisska: "They paid. To leave... It's stupid." **Mid-morning the sea goes out of the Saltings' channels all at once**; **the Bell comes out of the west across the Saltings' shallows**: a back like an island of black wet rock with weed, **the drowned temple's broken dome and columns on it, the green bronze bell the size of a house swinging and ringing *down* every stride**; a glassy grey-green wall of water ahead of it higher than the counting-houses; going east along the coast; Corsa in front. **Two hundred people sheltered at the Tidehall** (stone is safe): sea-steps, balcony, great room, copper roof; **Marrow at the top of the sea-steps sending them higher**; **Ulric waist-deep then chest-deep on the bottom step handing people up into the war-harness baskets of the forty pairs** (Sigge's grey nearest): "When they're up... You're no use to me on the stair." **The chain snaps like a whip; the eastern tower's foot goes with the winch-house: Cobb dies with his drum** (he'd counted the links that morning). **The water comes in to the wave-mark**: the houses' counting-houses along the front go under shutter by shutter; **the ropewalk terrace and its forty-one hulks stand above it**; Dorcas at the *Merry Agnes*'s rail holding a pan, not banging it. **The Bell's head comes in through the broken towers** (small deep old eyes, "Nothing so small as angry") **and turns toward the terrace**. **Calder's fire**: both palms on Kharrak's neck; no word ("A word was nothing out here"); *All of it... Take mine too*; he pushes what fire comes from into the dragon; **a river of fire across half a league of harbour into the Bell's face**, steam; **the head turns back to the sea**. **Toll**: palms stuck to Kharrak's plates and the skin left on them (raw to the wrist), eyes bleeding shut, no breath (*Breathe... Again*); Kharrak carries him out lying on his neck. **The backwash takes the Tidehall** (front wall, balcony, windows, the painted ceiling of ships); Nell's wyverns pluck the last off the roof's edge; the pairs take the balcony; **Marrow dies in the great room** seeing the last clerk down the back passage. **Ulric dies**: chest-deep on the steps, he lifts **a clerk's boy of six** up to Sigge, who pulls him into the saddle; the backwash takes the steps and the man (Sigge tells it the same words every time). **The Bell goes east along the coast** in the afternoon toward the mountains' end; Lisska follows a league. **Corsa** a brown ruin to the wave-mark; the chain in two pieces on the harbour floor; **the low harbour, emptied for one copper, has nothing on it to lose.** **Dusk: Sigge finds Ulric whole on the low harbour's mud, in a hulk's hollow, face to the sky**, and sits by him; Calder set down beside him, can't stand. **Night in the *Merry Agnes*'s captain's cabin, Dorcas's bunk; Dorcas wets cloths for his hands all night**, silent; Lisska on the cabin-top; **Kharrak along the terrace with his head turned south-west** (as at noon d.80 on the signal-field, with Lisska and, the riders say, Aurveth); *Nothing. Sleep.* **Echo**: refugees on the Saltreach Track all night; a sailor off a Blackwater coaster: **a salvage ketch at Blackwater fitting out for a long voyage, loading water for a month.** Calder: *Ask for a reef-pilot.*
- Ch 22 (days 95–100; 89–94 told back): **Told back**: Calder sick through d.89; **Dorcas binds both his hands to the wrist in clean cloth** ("You'll not hold a rein." "I don't use one." "Then go... We'll be here"); he flies north d.90; **the forty pairs stay at Corsa under Brede** (Book 2: old man of the ledges and his blue; Harl is Hulda Sarr's dragon). **Sigge flew Ulric home to Skarrow** (Ulric's steading: stone hall, turf roof, byres, a burn) d.89 lashed in a sail, **with the clerk's boy Pim, six**, who wouldn't let go of his coat. **Ulric's pyre at Skarrow lit d.90, seven days and nights (a champion's, not a chief's eleven)**; Calder there from the third night. **Lisska went east d.94 on her own, from Skarrow** to tell Maren of Ulric; reached Blackwater at dawn 95: the new chain broken in the middle, the ketch far off "going into the rocks"; called and called (ch. 21's echo); **d.96 she lands on Varrhold's hall roof**, thin: *It went out through their chain. - Ch 26 (days 112–118; 101–111 told back): **Aurveth withering since the pour**: her bronze dull "like a pan left out in the yard all winter", brown at the edges; **spine-plates standing proud** as the flesh goes; a sheep in two days instead of one; sleeps facing south-west; the holding wing weaker (drops and catches on the flight down). **D.110 Lisska saw the Berths fleet fitting out** (rooms built in holds; water for a month). **The terrace is a town** (a market between the hulks; gutters gut fish off boats on the shingle); forty pairs under Brede. **D.112 Brask** (Coll Brask; can't write; passes words "as if a word were a coin") on Blackwater's shingle with what's left of a company: **Kit's message, given him dusk 93, price paid: *Tell the farmer, if he comes. I've not told the knight.
