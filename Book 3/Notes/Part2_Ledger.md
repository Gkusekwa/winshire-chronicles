## Maren (14, 17, 21, 24, 27)
- Ch 14 (days 76–80): **The road** (told back): out of Ironhythe d.65 south-east, Orrun's long lope, twenty leagues a day and more; **d.69 Liss's ferry house**: Idony packed and waiting two days on the bench (the founding chart in oilcloth, two tide-glasses in wool, Liss's letters); Liss: "Bring him back. He eats nothing. He'll talk you deaf... Bring yourself back while you're at it"; **Idony rides behind Maren tied on with a strap Liss made**; down the Wend's west bank, across at the Staithe, east on the Coast Road; **round Wendmouth by night** (Saffery's gate, bounty men); past Gullhaven's empty hill (spit gone; a blue chapel door face-down in the grass); **round Corsa inland by the Saltreach Track** (d.73, from a hill: forty dragons turning over it; she doesn't go in). **D.76 dusk down the black cliff path**: sellswords back away from their fires; **the stilt-houses pull up their ladders one after another round the inlet**; ships slip moorings and stand off; Orrun lies among the abandoned fires: "It's very black... Like the river under the hill." Idony laughs: "They pulled up the ladders. Against a lion." **Maren has three silver and eleven coppers, and the Accord's brass seal.** **D.77 dawn Kit rows across alone** and looks at Orrun "as if he were a cargo": "You're the one who wants to go west... I'm the one who can take you"; Kessmoor's ships draw four fathoms and couldn't pass the second milestone. **Gannet** in the second boat: "Mistress student knight"; the cub "worth a gold piece... Bill of sale and everything." "It was worth more than that to the cub." Kit: "Don't lend him anything." **D.78 the price** in the stern cabin (Orrun on deck; the ketch sits lower): (1) **the Accord's warrant on Ostrey's paper** to clear the Merrin debt due day 100 ("Twenty-two days from now"; else Ostrey sells her to the Council as a coal-barge); Maren weighs Kaye's "and keep a record" and the farmer's "song", and **seals it** (*The Accord, for the war's necessary costs. The lion's knight.* in Kit's hand; Maren signs her name); (2) **labour: the reef-stone**; (3) **passage both ways and the rule kept by all aboard, lion included**; the road kept secret; Kit doesn't know why the rule (Hal "didn't drown of breaking it. He drowned of a squall"); breakers "I don't take them home". **Salt on the tongue**: a chipped clay dish of coarse grey sea-salt from the galley; a pinch each from the same dish: ***Salt between us. What's sworn is kept, or neither of us is fit to drink with.*** **Idony takes a pinch as a witness** ("Third century... Corsa's founding charter... I read it at Vael when I was twenty-two... Witnesses can taste. It's in the charter"); "Is he always like this?" "Yes." Gannet witnesses from the doorway, no salt. **D.79 Idony in the hold** on the far-stone ballast: **his tide-glass climbing on water** for the first time in its life. Kit talks prices instead of talking; Maren loses every argument. **Maren's left fingertips grey and numb** since the ford; she keeps the hand in her coat on the seal; Kit sees and says nothing. **Echo, dusk 80** on the cliff top: far west over the Saltings **a green speck turning**; Orrun: "Too far. She can't hear me... She's looking for the bell one. In the sea." The ladders let down again one at a time. Twenty days to the hundredth.

## Calder (15, 19, 22, 26)
- Ch 15 (days 66–78; plan's 66–76 stretched for the echo): **D.67** back from Ironhythe; Ulric and the forty pairs on **the old signal-field** above the Tidehall. **Ulric isn't bonded**: he flies behind **Sigge Brack** (new: young, silent grey rider of the Brack steading at Skarrow) on Sigge's grey, which the coast calls "Ulric's grey" (resolves chs. 6, 9). **The chain**: two round towers; tarred links as long as a forearm; lies on the harbour floor when down; **raised by a twelve-man capstan in the eastern tower's winch-house** (drum split in the gale and replaced with Gearmouth iron); **chain money** paid by tonnage at the tower window's iron grille to a clerk with the Council's book; a bell; the long iron roar. A ship-master thanks him for Lisska in the gale. **Chain-master Cobb** (new: old, deaf, exact; counts the links every time): the low harbour pays "Nothing. Nothing in it goes anywhere." **Forty-one hulks, two thousand and some people.** **Dorcas Kell** (new: the hulk-folk's spokeswoman, a gutter, fifty, forearms crossed with knife-scars; lives in the captain's cabin of the ***Merry Agnes***, a whaler forty years dead, families in her oil-holds): "Because this is the house... Leave your hulk and... by night there's someone worse on it... You'd not leave your farm." **Lisska on the roof: "Then take the boats. With them in."** **D.68 the chain-clerk's book, fourth page: *Any contract of the Council for labour in the harbour under the sum of one silver may be signed by the Speaker alone*** (the odd-jobs rule: a boy to scrape the sea-steps). **Calder writes the contract himself** (an hour): lift the hulks with the people in them **up onto the old ropewalk terrace behind the city, above the wave-mark**; gutters walk down to the herring each morning; the houses lose nothing; **price one copper**. **Marrow laughs** (nobody at the Tidehall has heard it; a passing clerk turns as if something fell); "You found the odd-jobs rule. The boy who scrapes the steps... Who read it to you?"; **signs alone** and pays **a Corsa copper with the ship-and-wave worn almost off** from her own purse: "A contract's not paid till the coin's crossed... Don't spend it"; "It costs them nothing at all." **D.69 Nell Tallis**: "Can you pay?" (she says it first) / "One copper"; "Any contract"; she takes it; **her five wyverns carry the lines under the keels**. **D.70–76 the lift**: Ulric shouting from the signal-field, laughing; first the coaster ***Cormorant*** (nine aboard, four dragons, "a sound like a boot coming out of a bog"; set upright in a timber cradle the hulk-folk built of the Council's wood; a woman laughs); **thirty-four hulks in seven days**; **Kharrak lifts the *Ostrey Pride*** (grain-ship, twenty years dead, eighty aboard) alone on one chain with Lisska calling the swing ("They screamed more at me than at the flying. I liked it"). **The last seven too rotten to lift** (the *Merry Agnes* among them): hauled up **the old slipway** on rollers by two hundred to a rope, **Dorcas's red ox Patience** (one horn broken, fifteen years on the fish-carts) and **a blind old stonehorn** from the scrub (mossy plates, chipped horn, white eyes; loose off a drover's herd on the Track twelve years; fed fish-heads; **comes when Dorcas bangs a pan**). **D.76 the low harbour empty** (bare mud, gulls, a child's shoe); house clerks stare; one writes in a little book; **the herring comes in and two thousand gutters walk down from the terrace**; "Nobody in the houses lost a fish." **Ulric** at dusk: Teo and Arn as boys making games of anything ("Arn always lost to Teo and never minded"); **"When the bell one's been and gone. I'll take you to Coldtarn... Show you where we pour... A man who lifts a harbour should see where we pour"** (plant 19, 22). **Echo, night 78, the Saltings Arms** (Nell buys him a drink with his own copper "to see what it was worth"): a Blackwater smuggler: the lion down the cliff path, sellswords walking backwards into the sea, ladders "Clack, clack, clack... Like a stick on a fence"; **"the Merrin woman rowed across to it... like it was a cargo she'd come to buy"**; Nell: "The ones who go further than they say." He doesn't smile. Forty-one hulks lit on the terrace above the wave-mark.

## Idony (16)
- Ch 16 (days 80–84): **The salvage hold** under the Merrin house (between the stilts; plank floor over black water; a boat slip; chisel-bench; flake-grit): **forty-odd far-stone blocks** on timber bearers, loaf-size to cart-length, faced on one side as only old-realm masons faced; **the hum comes to the teeth, not the ear**. **The script**: every faced block bears the milestone formula ***into the light*** **and a number** (*twelve*, *thirty*, *nine*). **The Merrin book** (sailcloth-bound, gone soft as skin; doesn't leave the ship, but Kit brings it down at dusk 80 and keeps her hand on it): Abram's drawings of each pier and its milestone copied stroke by stroke by a man who couldn't read them; bearings and turns; corrections in four or five hands; **the milestones count down**: *into the light, forty-one* at the first pier (two days out, south-west of the Shatters' edge) **to *into the light, one***; page *nine* marked *Hal. Squall. The boat.*; **the last page: a low pale shore, white sand, shapes like buildings, and *into the light* with no number**: "there's nowhere left to go"; **the numberless blocks in the hold came off the shore's buildings: "They're *here*."** Idony laughs till he coughs. **D.81 the glass**: on the hold floor among the stone it **climbs a third of its column and holds**; on the *Kittiwake*'s ballast only a finger; on the walkway in the air, **dead flat**; "It's not reading the world... It's reading the stone. **The stone's full**" of the undrunk breath (*something in the south-west has never been drunk at all*). **The charms**: **a flake is a thumbnail of live ground you can carry**; most cut too thin, most buyers know no word, most used on live ground anyway; **one in ten** = thick enough, a word known, ground dead enough; "Your great-grandfather was selling live ground by the pinch. At two silver. He was robbing himself." Kit: "Don't tell Ostrey." *(Proposal for Magic §2: far-stone as carried live ground.)* **D.82 the feather**: "Ask my grandmother. She's dead... From where we go... That's all anyone in my family has ever said about anything." He writes nothing of it. **Days 82–83 the bearings**: the founding chart on the bench with far-stone flakes for weights; Kit's old brass dividers walked by Maren from Blackwater's notch along Abram's bearings, forty-one down to one: **the point lands on the south-western margin, on the mark with *error* in six hands**: "They built the road to it." Kit reads *error* upside down (she can read that). **Maren writes for him** (square soldier's hand, letters set down "as if laying out kit"; neither mentions the slowness): the formula, the count, the numberless shore, the glass at a third, the stone full, the bearing, the mark, and **the last line, half to himself: *It answers a breath that is unbound.*** ("Write it down so I can find out"; he means the shore.) **D.83 Gannet** brings unneeded lamp oil and **reads the pages upside down from the ladder's foot, easily**; Idony notices he reads well and says nothing (plant: chs. 29, 44, 60). **His hands shake less among the stone**; he doesn't tell Maren (she keeps her hand in her pocket; fair). **Echo, noon 84**: through the slip, Kit and Maren on the walkway: the 85th (bottom of the spring tide) vs **the 84th, tonight**; "A day sooner costs extra... Days cost"; "Fine": Maren loses the fourth day running. "He'd never in his life been so happy."

## Kit (13, 20, 25)
- Ch 13 (days 60–66): **Kit Merrin** (about forty; lean, weathered, broken nose set crooked, early grey in black hair; talks prices like weather; **always sleeps aboard**, even in Blackwater). **D.60 the reef-stone** at the bottom of the spring tide, Jory rowing: a square-cut old-realm block **the size of a chapel** lying across **the throat of the Merrins' channel** a yard under at low water, hooked script on its top face, **a faint hum** through the water; it fell off **the drowned road's last pier** when the sea went out at Gullhaven (end of B2's winter). **Blackwater**: black cliffs into cloud, peat-black water, tarred stilt-houses leaning like drunks, a dozen smugglers' ships, sellswords' fires on the shingle; **no harbour-master, no toll, no chain** (the Council tried three times in Hal's life: one chain stolen, one sold back to the Council, the third harbour-master found floating in the spring); **the boathouse on the north shore still roofless** from the farmer's dragons. **The Merrin house**: tall stilt-house at the inlet's eastern end, **ochre shutters** (house colour), the salvage hold under it on the water. **The *Kittiwake***: two-masted, broad, shallow, built on Wenna's money to sit on a reef at low water and float off on the flood; crew of six. **The secret**: great-grandfather **Abram Merrin** found the drowned road ~80 years ago (broken old-stone piers south-west over the Shatters, each an island with a milestone of hooked script and a number; at the end, a shore); the Merrins bring back **far-stone** (grey, humming blocks), chipped into flakes sold through a Saffery man on Gallows Quay as warranted old charms, two silver, **one in ten does something**; never spoken of at table. Kit's been over six times with her father, twice since with Jory. **Abram's rule: never sleep ashore over there.** **Father Hal Merrin** kept it thirty years; **drowned eleven years ago at the ninth milestone** when the ship's boat went over on the reef a cable from the ketch; Kit brought her home alone with **Jory, eight**, who sat on the ballast and read the milestones by eye. **The Merrin book** (Abram's milestones and turns; four generations of margin corrections; Jory has never needed it). **D.62 Master Orme Fosse** (new: Ostrey's factor at Blackwater, the only house that keeps one there; grey coat, Ostrey's grey cuff, round spectacles, soft and pleasant, forgives nothing): the debt against the *Kittiwake* (signed end of winter, when the Bell shut half the Shattered Sea) **falls due on the hundredth day**, or the ketch is Ostrey's; "Thirty-eight days"; "what it is your family goes so far to fetch" / "Rocks." **Gannet** (about fifty, Wendmouth face, the smile first; came east in the autumn after Saffery cleaned the Halloran beast men off Gallows Quay; bosun; Kit trusts him as far as his contract). **Jory**: the fourth eastern channel is open but **two fathoms at the bar; she draws two and a half with ballast** and will roll over without it. Cabin: Hal's charts, Wenna's sea-chest, an old tin luck-tag over the door that never worked. **The feather**: grandmother **Wenna Merrin** (small, fierce, black-eyed, a gull's voice) showed it on the beach by the fire when Kit was ten (the ketch a keelless hull on the stocks): red to gold to brighter, warm as a hearthstone; Kit held it once; "From where we go"; sold the next week to the Halloran house for the keel; Kit saw it two years later in Rusk's window on Gallows Quay; Hal: "Never sleep ashore over there," as if it were an answer. **D.66 echo**: an Ostrey gull-hawk to Fosse's perch; a Corsa smuggler at the beach fire: the Accord at Ironhythe (Rennick's thumb), the lion's knight and the dragons' farmer side by side; **she asked for a ship west past the Shatters; Kessmoor said no; Rennick laughed; the farmer said it was a song**. **Gannet: "Ashdown... She took a cub off me. On the jetty at Millbrook. Two years back. The week before the harvest fair... I haven't forgotten."** Kit thinks of the lion that moves stone; "Thirty-four days"; tells Jory to get the book out and find how far Ironhythe is "by lion".
