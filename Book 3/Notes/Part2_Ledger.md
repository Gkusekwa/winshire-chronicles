## Maren (14, 17, 21, 24, 27)
- Ch 14 (days 76–80): **The road** (told back): out of Ironhythe d.65 south-east, Orrun's long lope, twenty leagues a day and more; **d.69 Liss's ferry house**: Idony packed and waiting two days on the bench (the founding chart in oilcloth, two tide-glasses in wool, Liss's letters); Liss: "Bring him back. He eats nothing. He'll talk you deaf... Bring yourself back while you're at it"; **Idony rides behind Maren tied on with a strap Liss made**; down the Wend's west bank, across at the Staithe, east on the Coast Road; **round Wendmouth by night** (Saffery's gate, bounty men); past Gullhaven's empty hill (spit gone; a blue chapel door face-down in the grass); **round Corsa inland by the Saltreach Track** (d.73, from a hill: forty dragons turning over it; she doesn't go in). **D.76 dusk down the black cliff path**: sellswords back away from their fires; **the stilt-houses pull up their ladders one after another round the inlet**; ships slip moorings and stand off; Orrun lies among the abandoned fires: "It's very black... Like the river under the hill." Idony laughs: "They pulled up the ladders. Against a lion." **Maren has three silver and eleven coppers, and the Accord's brass seal.** **D.77 dawn Kit rows across alone** and looks at Orrun "as if he were a cargo": "You're the one who wants to go west... I'm the one who can take you"; Kessmoor's ships draw four fathoms and couldn't pass the second milestone. **Gannet** in the second boat: "Mistress student knight"; the cub "worth a gold piece... Bill of sale and everything." "It was worth more than that to the cub." Kit: "Don't lend him anything." **D.78 the price** in the stern cabin (Orrun on deck; the ketch sits lower): (1) **the Accord's warrant on Ostrey's paper** to clear the Merrin debt due day 100 ("Twenty-two days from now"; else Ostrey sells her to the Council as a coal-barge); Maren weighs Kaye's "and keep a record" and the farmer's "song", and **seals it** (*The Accord, for the war's necessary costs. The lion's knight.* in Kit's hand; Maren signs her name); (2) **labour: the reef-stone**; (3) **passage both ways and the rule kept by all aboard, lion included**; the road kept secret; Kit doesn't know why the rule (Hal "didn't drown of breaking it. He drowned of a squall"); breakers "I don't take them home". **Salt on the tongue**: a chipped clay dish of coarse grey sea-salt from the galley; a pinch each from the same dish: ***Salt between us. What's sworn is kept, or neither of us is fit to drink with.*** **Idony takes a pinch as a witness** ("Third century... Corsa's founding charter... I read it at Vael when I was twenty-two... Witnesses can taste. It's in the charter"); "Is he always like this?" "Yes." Gannet witnesses from the doorway, no salt. **D.79 Idony in the hold** on the far-stone ballast: **his tide-glass climbing on water** for the first time in its life. Kit talks prices instead of talking; Maren loses every argument. **Maren's left fingertips grey and numb** since the ford; she keeps the hand in her coat on the seal; Kit sees and says nothing. **Echo, dusk 80** on the cliff top: far west over the Saltings **a green speck turning**; Orrun: "Too far. She can't hear me... She's looking for the bell one. In the sea." The ladders let down again one at a time. Twenty days to the hundredth.

## Kit (13, 20, 25)
- Ch 13 (days 60–66): **Kit Merrin** (about forty; lean, weathered, broken nose set crooked, early grey in black hair; talks prices like weather; **always sleeps aboard**, even in Blackwater). **D.60 the reef-stone** at the bottom of the spring tide, Jory rowing: a square-cut old-realm block **the size of a chapel** lying across **the throat of the Merrins' channel** a yard under at low water, hooked script on its top face, **a faint hum** through the water; it fell off **the drowned road's last pier** when the sea went out at Gullhaven (end of B2's winter). **Blackwater**: black cliffs into cloud, peat-black water, tarred stilt-houses leaning like drunks, a dozen smugglers' ships, sellswords' fires on the shingle; **no harbour-master, no toll, no chain** (the Council tried three times in Hal's life: one chain stolen, one sold back to the Council, the third harbour-master found floating in the spring); **the boathouse on the north shore still roofless** from the farmer's dragons. **The Merrin house**: tall stilt-house at the inlet's eastern end, **ochre shutters** (house colour), the salvage hold under it on the water. **The *Kittiwake***: two-masted, broad, shallow, built on Wenna's money to sit on a reef at low water and float off on the flood; crew of six. **The secret**: great-grandfather **Abram Merrin** found the drowned road ~80 years ago (broken old-stone piers south-west over the Shatters, each an island with a milestone of hooked script and a number; at the end, a shore); the Merrins bring back **far-stone** (grey, humming blocks), chipped into flakes sold through a Saffery man on Gallows Quay as warranted old charms, two silver, **one in ten does something**; never spoken of at table. Kit's been over six times with her father, twice since with Jory. **Abram's rule: never sleep ashore over there.** **Father Hal Merrin** kept it thirty years; **drowned eleven years ago at the ninth milestone** when the ship's boat went over on the reef a cable from the ketch; Kit brought her home alone with **Jory, eight**, who sat on the ballast and read the milestones by eye. **The Merrin book** (Abram's milestones and turns; four generations of margin corrections; Jory has never needed it). **D.62 Master Orme Fosse** (new: Ostrey's factor at Blackwater, the only house that keeps one there; grey coat, Ostrey's grey cuff, round spectacles, soft and pleasant, forgives nothing): the debt against the *Kittiwake* (signed end of winter, when the Bell shut half the Shattered Sea) **falls due on the hundredth day**, or the ketch is Ostrey's; "Thirty-eight days"; "what it is your family goes so far to fetch" / "Rocks." **Gannet** (about fifty, Wendmouth face, the smile first; came east in the autumn after Saffery cleaned the Halloran beast men off Gallows Quay; bosun; Kit trusts him as far as his contract). **Jory**: the fourth eastern channel is open but **two fathoms at the bar; she draws two and a half with ballast** and will roll over without it. Cabin: Hal's charts, Wenna's sea-chest, an old tin luck-tag over the door that never worked. **The feather**: grandmother **Wenna Merrin** (small, fierce, black-eyed, a gull's voice) showed it on the beach by the fire when Kit was ten (the ketch a keelless hull on the stocks): red to gold to brighter, warm as a hearthstone; Kit held it once; "From where we go"; sold the next week to the Halloran house for the keel; Kit saw it two years later in Rusk's window on Gallows Quay; Hal: "Never sleep ashore over there," as if it were an answer. **D.66 echo**: an Ostrey gull-hawk to Fosse's perch; a Corsa smuggler at the beach fire: the Accord at Ironhythe (Rennick's thumb), the lion's knight and the dragons' farmer side by side; **she asked for a ship west past the Shatters; Kessmoor said no; Rennick laughed; the farmer said it was a song**. **Gannet: "Ashdown... She took a cub off me. On the jetty at Millbrook. Two years back. The week before the harvest fair... I haven't forgotten."** Kit thinks of the lion that moves stone; "Thirty-four days"; tells Jory to get the book out and find how far Ironhythe is "by lion".
