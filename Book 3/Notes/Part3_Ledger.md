# Book 3, Part Three: The Far Shore: shared drafting ledger

*Same rules as Book 2's part ledgers. Before writing a scene that touches another POV's days or people, read this file. After deciding any fact another chapter could contradict (who is where on which day, what someone said, a name, a wound, a number, a beast), add one line under your name. The plan wins over the ledger if they clash; note the clash.*

## Maren (30, 34, 37, 40)
- Ch 30 (days 130–135; 126–129 told back): **Dawn 127 Maren falls asleep against the milestone after four nights awake; wakes at noon alive**: Orrun stayed awake (*I watched you. The whole time... The light came... It looked at you. And at me, awake. And went away*). **The bonded are safe while their beast keeps awake beside them**; she sleeps in watches of an hour or two while he wakes, and he (needing little) while she does: "They kept the watch for each other." **D.129 her name** comes through Orrun as a shape, rendered in Maren's tongue: **the Grey Mother** (Hesper: *The Grey Mother keeps the stones. That's what we say when the ground shakes.*; Tova under her breath at the fords; Hearthmere's chapel hearthstones); Orrun: *She's my mother* (as a thing always known, just remembered). She doesn't use words; she gives Orrun pictures and feelings. **D.130 noon the showing**: she lowers her gable-sized head across the ten paces; Orrun's head against her paw; Maren's hand on his shoulder: **(1) the one realm**, green to its edges, grey roads and milestones and piered bridges everywhere, people and beasts walking side by side unchained, **lions on tower steps bowing back**; **the old ones** (shapes too big to see whole, of every kind) **teaching the hooked script in light on the air**, people copying it into dust, clay, stone (*So we could talk... We gave them our tongue. Set down*); **(2) iron**: the marks struck into iron, **a collar** on a great grey cat with empty eyes, then many, then **chain** (*hold* inside every link), then **a chained Titan under a hill bleeding its breath into a city's lamps and wells**; **(3) the rising and the split**: the road cracking, the sea over it, ice, a bell under the sea; **(4) the sleeping**: on a black plain the old ones lay the six down under hills, sea, a lake, a mountain, a stair, a ring of stones, each with a glowing stone to hold its sleep **and one stone more over them all**; **each old one that gives goes thin, pale, into light; the plain nearly empty after, a few left going west** (*It cost us. Our bodies. We had nothing else to give*); **(5) the door**: the Wend, the island, the grey arch, **a young half-grown grey lion with too-big paws set down there by a vast thin grey shape**: ***Keep the watch*** (the one thing that comes in words: Orrun has kept it in words three hundred years). **Maren understands: the realm fell for doing what the Restoration does** (and the crown under Greyhold). **She asks if they'll come back and sleep the six again**: the Mother's eyes on Maren for the first time: ***We closed the door once... We have nothing left but light.*** **Echo, days 131–134**: the Mother looks at Orrun as a cub is looked at; **d.132 she licks him once from nose to tail-tip "as a wind goes over stone"**; through the bond Maren feels him being somebody's and **has to walk to the hill's edge and look away**. **D.132 a lock of her hair over the left ear gone white** (plant for 37). **Dusk 135 Idony up the road on the grey horse**: "Kit had no say in it. I'm a witness... I've come to read. Is there anything to read?" "Yes."
- Ch 34 (day 142; 137–141 told back): **Dawn 137 the Grey Mother stands for the first time** and walks south-west along the road (the paving sings under her; deer watch; birds rise and settle); Orrun at her shoulder unasked, Maren on him; Idony on the grey horse; Gannet on foot. **The hall** on the long ridge where the road crosses it: **a long double row of tall square grey columns, roofless; a paved floor clean as if swept; a long narrow pool down the middle a man's height deep, spring-fed at the south end, draining under the floor at the north; a great arch over the north doorway.** The Mother lies along the hall's east side outside the columns with her head on the floor between two of them by the pool; Orrun by her head. **The light writes the lock's sentence over the arch and it stays**: ***One hand shut it. / Two hands open it together. / What is opened together is kept together, or it is not kept.*** Idony goes very still: "**They wrote the lock... The same hand. *They* did. Not the crown. Not the first knights**"; silent an hour. **Dawn 140 the Lamp walks up the ridge on the sunrise**: white-gold, tall as the columns, horse-like and not, a mane of light streaming back in no wind, **one long straight horn "like a spear of noon"**; stands at the south end by the spring; the pool lies at its feet "like a sword"; Idony: Sarovel's priests "are very bad singers." **Dusk 140 the Lady up the spring's channel into the pool**: pale moon-on-frost light coiling the pool's length and further down than its bottom; **a heron-like head on a neck "like a river", eyes the colour of deep water**; **Idony kneels and can't get up for a long time** ("My grandmother prayed to her every night. On the ferry... A hundred years of us... I never did"). **D.141 the Mother writes: *We called. / Others did not answer. / They have gone out.*** Four. **Noon 142 Calder** (Kharrak huge, Lisska on a broken column-top, Aurveth dull and thin but flying straight) lands outside the western columns and walks in; **last met at Ironhythe's yard (d.65)**; he sees her grey neck, white lock, still mouth-corner; she sees **his palms scarred pink and shiny to the wrists and his voice gone to a crack**. "You came." "The old one was dying." Neither says *song*. **Nothing happens while apart**; the arch's letters flare when he enters; **the four lights gather over the pool into a cart-sized brightness that won't make words**; Idony: "It's waiting... *Two hands*... it wants you to stand together." **Calder holds out his scarred right hand across the pool's corner; Maren goes round and takes it with her right** (her left is dead). **The light forms; both feel it through their beasts**: ***Lion's bond. Dragons' bond. / Together. / Ask.*** **The Wayfarer is in the light without coming: "It speaks through them... It's in them" (Orrun); Calder's free hand goes flat to his chest each time it speaks (his marks go hot).** **Gannet** between two western columns since dusk 137 with his board, copying the arch and the pool's light as Idony reads. They keep holding hands "like two children at a fair". "Well." "You ask. You've got the voice."
- Ch 37 (day 146; 145 told back; echo dawn 147): **Dawn 145 Calder and his three go north-east to the lagoon for Jory, then back south-west to the crossroads**; at the western columns: "Two hands." "I know." **Dusk 145 the Lamp walks off the ridge's east end into the last light and the Lady sinks away down the pool's channel "like a tide", "to take a shape".** **Dawn 146 the Mother comes out onto the ridge and lies down facing Orrun**; grey script (Idony reads aloud so Maren has the words): ***Lion's knight. / He is yours. You are his. / One life. / What I give him goes through you. / Will you let it?*** **Maren thinks of Hollin Knowe** (Book 1 ch. 73: she walked into the bond-forcers' binding and offered her life in his place): "A binding took. It didn't ask... This asked." **"Yes." / *Yes.* / *Are you afraid?* / *Yes.* / *So am I.*** (truth this time). **The gift**: the Mother's jaw on the ground before his paws; light into the ridge, into Orrun, **through the bond into Maren as cold: into all her bones, even those she can't feel** ("Like the stone a long way down where nothing had ever been warm"); **stone flows up into Orrun from the ridge, the paving, the hall's columns, "as water goes up a wick"**; **he grows: barn, house, the hall's height, a hill (the size of the Mother's hill)**; **grey as old stone; mane gone to dust/mist/smoke like hers; eyes gone from gold to pale ("the colour of the sky at dawn"); no wings** (the Last Titan has stone-and-leather ones); **the far shore's stone flows round his paws "as water flows round a stone in a river"; it comes without pulling**. **The Mother thins (mane, body, paws, eyes last on Orrun) and goes out "like a lamp"; the grass flattened in her shape; the hall's hum stops "as a bell stops when someone puts a hand on it"; only Orrun hums now.** **He's himself: *Maren... I'm here. I'm still here*** (same voice, deeper, slower, "like stone settling... at the bottom of a well"; no rage); she weeps and **can't feel her face on either side now (the cold)**; *I'm not now.* **Noon: three shadows, each hill-sized and exactly as big as he: *They're me. All of them. Now.*** She watches them and lets the number stand. **Idony cries at the arch ("There wasn't anything to read"); Gannet's board blank all morning; his smile doesn't come.** **Afternoon in the empty pool's reflection: her white lock spread to a band of iron-grey over the left ear and temple "like Rowan's"; cold for the rest of her life.** **Echo, dawn 147**: Orrun now feels the whole far shore's stone "as she'd once felt the floor of a single room"; **through it, far south-west, three dragons screaming (one deep, one high, one old)**: *The farmer's.* *The other gift.* until they stop.
- Ch 40 (days 147–180; the walk from dusk 148): **D.147 Orrun carries Maren, Idony (and Gannet, clinging on behind) down from the hall in a day**, the road singing under a hill's half-league stride. **Kit at the lagoon's road end** ("That won't go in the hold"); the 93 along the *Kittiwake*'s rail. **Maren closes the contract**: "Passage there... I'm going back another road... I'm releasing you. From the back half"; **Kit and Maren each touch one grain from the chipped dish: "Closed."** Kit gives her **a skin of the steps' spring water, ship's biscuit in a tin (thirty-two), salt pork in a cloth, and a twist of paper with a pinch of grey salt: "You'll want something to taste."** **Idony stays with the ketch** ("I'll be useful. It's so rare"); **"Count for me... tell me when you come up how long it was... I'll want it for the chart." "I will."** **Dusk 148 Orrun walks into the lagoon and goes down at the shelf**: **the sea-bed stone opens ahead in a space his size and closes behind** (as under the Ruin, Book 1 ch. 79, when they came up in the reeds below Redwatch, at a cart's size then); **air comes with them, cold and stone-smelling: *The stone remembers. The air that was over it. Before the sea came. It kept a little. I'm letting it out as I go***; light only the faint pale of his eyes; **he walks inside the drowned causeway**, its cut faces and once *Into the light* on a wall; **he feels each pier pass overhead and counts them up: *One... Going the other way now. Counting up. Toward home.*** **The stone weeps fresh sweet water where he passes** (*Roads have wells*); she fills the skin daily; eats little (cold takes the hunger); **sleeps a great deal in his mane; he doesn't sleep.** **Losing count**: by sleeps (eight, nine), by biscuits, by refills; **about the twentieth day she stops: for the first time in her life she doesn't know the count** (the last time it mattered: seven days on the floor by her father's chair); *I promised him.* / *I'll tell him... something*: **she laughs, the first time since the ridge.** He stops saying the piers when she stops counting; she doesn't ask. **Echo, from about the fifth day: through Orrun, a long pale moon-on-frost light coiling in the black water beyond the walls, keeping pace: the Lady, *going the same way. Home. To fight... keeping us company*** ("as a heron flies beside a boat on a river"); mostly there. **Once she touches a grain of Kit's salt to her tongue: "The only taste in the world."** **The climb**: the road slopes up, the air warmer, walls paler, then green water and light; **they come up at low tide onto the Drowned Bell's reef**: black weeded rock, pools, **broken grey columns round his paws "like the stubble of a mown field", the dome fallen, no bell** (the Bell took it); **north, the low Saltreach coast and Gullhaven's empty hill**; **one fishing boat, a brown sail, stopped dead**. **The fisherman, rowing over at last, tells her the day: the 180th: thirty-two days.** "I didn't count it. Somebody told me."

## Calder (28, 32, 35, 39, 42)
- Ch 28 (days 119–129; the tow 128–137 summarized): **The fleet sails day and night** on Hal's charts. **Dusk 119 at *forty-one*** (the pilots sail straight past it): **Aurveth comes down on the sea beside Ostrey's flagship; Jory jumps from the rail onto her shoulder**; Rennick's two guards go back to their dice; Rennick laughs. **Jory rides Aurveth's neck from then on, calling each pier by its broken shape** ("*Forty. Bear west a point...*"); Calder shouts it down; nobody listens. **D.121 at the fifth pier Rennick's three black ships heave to and next morning turn back north-east**, signalling *Turn back. Danger*: "I don't sail at night where I can't see the bottom, farmer!... Good luck to you!" (**Rennick survives.**) **Night 123 the *White Heron*** (Quill, white pennants) **strikes a fallen pier in the dark; sixty drown; the dragons lift eleven off her rigging** by Kharrak's fire. **Celia Ostrey: "The Merrins sail this. So can we. We have paid."** **Night 127 near pier twelve the Bell rises under the four leading ships**, lifting them on its back, rings *down*; they go over; its ring-wave flings six onto the reefs either side of the road. **Jory: "Twelve to eleven! The road's whole!"**: **half a league of unbroken flat causeway a fathom and a half down, the only such stretch on the road** ("It's the shallowest thing on the road that won't break you"). **Kharrak and Lisska herd the flagship's longboat and jolly-boat onto it with fire**; they ground on the stone. **The second wave**: Calder's hand on **Lisska's** neck: *I know*; **her wind, never asked for, comes up through him** ("as if the sky itself were breathing into him through her") and goes out of her **as a wall of wind across the sea, tearing the wave's top off**; it passes under the boats, spent. **Toll: breath**: nothing comes in; Lisska frightened for the first time he's heard; **Kharrak lands on pier twelve's broken top and lies flat as a bed**; a thread of air at a time till dawn; **speechless till d.129; voice cracked and thin for weeks; never gets his breath back all the way (always short of it on a hill after)**. **Losses**: **nine of the ten ships** (four off the Bell's back, five on the reefs; the tenth found drifting empty); the Bell goes back down under the road. **Survivors: 63 in the two boats** (house servants, mostly children, a few rich in salt-white coats, **Master Loy** the pilot, grey-faced with his chart) **plus 31 lifted off wreckage: 94**. **Celia Ostrey goes down at her flagship's rail, calm; Aubrey Strand goes down with his ship.** **The tow, d.128–138**: boats rowed by turns, towed by Kharrak and Lisska on war-harness lines; Aurveth ahead with Jory; nights tied to piers (people sleep in the boats, dragons on pier-tops); water from the longboat's breaker and two rains caught in sails; **nobody dies on the tow**. **Echo, d.128 at pier eleven: *Into the light, eleven* and, knife-scratched under it, Kit's mark: a small ship with a K for a mast**: "She's been past. Days ago." **D.129 Calder's warning** to all ninety-four: "Nobody sleeps ashore. Over there... If you sleep ashore over there, you don't wake up... That's the price. Of the berth you paid for."
- Ch 32 (days 135–140): **Dusk 137 the last pier**; **the Bell lies silent at the shelf's edge west of the Needle**; Jory won't take two laden boats past it; **Abram's page near the book's end: *South way. Small boats.***: half a day south along the shelf's outer edge to **a shallow cut** (too shallow for the *Kittiwake*; a longboat at the top of the flood) into **a small blue bay on the shore's south coast**, where **a second paved road** comes down out of the hills into the sea; tide-line milestone *into the light* (Jory's first sight of it). **D.138 landfall of all 94**: deer, a hare, birds on shoulders; **some lie down on the warm sand at once; Calder (voice cracked) pulls them up**: "Nobody lies down... Don't sleep ashore." **Night: everyone in the anchored boats, sitting up, sleeping in shifts on each other's shoulders; Kharrak and Lisska awake in the shallows alongside; Calder awake on the beach against Aurveth** (he keeps the rule; doesn't know the bonded's exemption). **Piers Calloway** (new: young, handsome Corsa wine-merchant's son; "I paid six hundred gold to come to a land without Titans... a reef-pilot told a sellsword a tale") **sleeps in the dunes with a blanket; dawn: on his back in the marram grass, eyes open, smiling, dead**; a deer smells his face. **Buried by the beach's numberless milestone; Loy says something; the 93 believe.** Fish come to their hands in the shallows. **D.139 inland half a day on the southern road to the crossroads** where it crosses the western road running on north-east to the hall's ridge (the southern road runs on north to the lagoon, Kit's road by another way): **a grey paved square with a milestone at each corner; in the middle a shape of pale gold light taller than a tower: a long body, wings folded "as a heron folds them", three long necks, three heads looking down three roads**: **the three-faced god of the Harrow's End crossroads shrine** (dog-kennel-sized, a bowl for coppers, the carving worn by rain and hands). **Aurveth breathes properly for the first time since Coldtarn**: long, deep, slow, sides filling (bronze still dull, plates still proud): *Oh.* **Calder's two coppers: Marrow's (kept) and his own last, a Hearthmere copper with a lamp on it, which he sets on the south-corner milestone's flat top** out of habit. **The Wayfarer speaks in him through all three bonds at once, three voices that are one; his three bond-marks on his chest (rust-red, green, bronze) go hot**: ***Farmer.*** **"Why me?... I was cutting barley... There were riders on the Scarfell who'd waited their whole lives... And three of them came to me. In a mill. Dying. Why?"** ***Because you have room.*** Nothing more; the dragons understand (Kharrak's low chest-sound, Lisska's tilted head); he doesn't. **"Will you come?... and fight?"** ***No. I have only this left. It is for them.*** (He's afraid he understands.) **Dusk, through Aurveth: *The others are gathering. In the hall. Where the road crosses the ridge. North and east... The other bond is there. Come. Two hands.*** He thinks of the lock under Greyhold. **Echo, dusk 139 from the north-east corner: on the ridge to the north-east a long grey shape lying in the light and a smaller grey one beside it**: "The lion." He sits awake against Aurveth all night, feeling her breathe.
- Ch 35 (days 142–143): **Calder asks four gods "Can you put them back to sleep?"**; **the Lamp's script** (white-gold, sharp-edged, "cut out of noon"; Idony shades his eyes): ***No. / The houses are broken.*** (the temples) ***/ The stones are in the last one's back.*** (the relics: six, five dark, one like a coal) ***/ The sleeping cost us what we were. We have nothing left to pay with.*** **Dusk 142**: ***Two of us will take a shape again, and stand with you. / Two of us will give what is left, and go out. / To the lion. To the three.*** Calder feels it go into Aurveth "like a stone into a well" (not fear); Maren feels Orrun hear it. **They let go at dusk; the light goes out "like a lamp when the door opens"; they sit on the pool's edge a pace apart.** **The talk** (Idony asleep an hour by the arch for once; Gannet a dark shape at the columns): **she read his midwinter letter at Orrin three times, "Twice so as not to laugh at the spelling... For the rest of it"**; **his father paid the Clan's dues every year, kept his head down, died in his bed ("I thought so till I was thirty")**; "None of them were keeping my head down"; **her father: poachers killed his hound in the Brackenwood; he sat in his chair by the fire a week and died; she was fourteen on the floor by the chair**: "I grew up knowing exactly what a bond costs. And I went and got one anyway"; **Senna died of fever seven years ago, ill nine days; Wren was four**; "It felt like half of me went with her"; **his line: one life between four "like being a house with the doors open"; hers: "One life between two is a house with one door. And somebody always standing in it."** Her mouth: "It's paid" (he doesn't ask by whom). **Her fear: the Mother's gift will make Orrun a Titan, "The thing under Greyhold"** ("I saw it go out of its chains at the Ruin... at the Ring... at the shut place"). **His fear: "The old one's going to die"**: she's waited her whole life for something (Sigrun said so) "and I think it's the last thing she was waiting for." **Neither comforts the other.** **Morning 143 the plan** (hands joined again; the light returns at once): ***There is one stone over the six.*** (Idony: "The one from Hearthmere's vault. The coal in its back") ***/ We made it. Only one of us can hold it. / If one of us takes it out of the last one's back, the six will hear. / They will come into the light with the one who holds it. / They will stop, as we stopped.*** ***I will carry it.*** (the Lamp). Maren: "Someone has to get it there... Two of them fight. Two of them give. To us. So we can get it there." **Echo: Gannet between the western columns six days, a pencil stub worn to a thumb's width, writing every line Idony reads.**
- Ch 39 (day 147; 143–146 told back; echo dusk 148): **D.143–144 Jory leads the 93 up the northern road to the lagoon** (sleeping sitting up in turns, Jory walking the line all night; nobody dies); **Kit takes all 93 aboard the *Kittiwake*** (hold, deck, the two boats towed astern): "*in the boats, two at a time*"; **she charges nothing** (Jory finds that the strangest thing in the world). **D.145 Calder comes down at the lagoon for Jory on his way back from the hall; Jory rides Aurveth to the crossroads.** **Dawn 147 the gift**: the Wayfarer's three faces turn from the roads to the dragons; marks hot; ***Farmer. / Hold them. / What I am goes into them. Through you. / Three ways. One gift.*** "Yes." The light comes down "like rain coming off a roof all at once when the wind takes the gutter" into him and out three ways (the mill, the other way: "Not three deaths. Three lives"). **Kharrak's fire comes out white; the square's south-east milestone melts and pools like wax and cools to slag**: *Oh, I like that.* **Aurveth's deep sight**: through the hills to the ridge (the lion a grey hill-sized warmth with something small between its paws: *He's changed*); down through the sea-bed to the drowned road's piers, "each one a cold grey standing shape"; **far east over the sea a burning like a coal among five dark shapes** (she doesn't tell him). **Lisska's wind: she goes up and clouds come out of a clear sky and wheel round her in a ring a league wide; she carries the storm and throws it west over the sea, laughing**: *Did you see that?* **Aurveth's holding wing cracks "like a green branch" and straightens at the root; both wings spread full across two roads; the bronze comes back bright.** **The toll, through all three bonds at once**: fever from the marks out; **blood from his nose, then his eyes**; **his three bond-marks (bronze, rust, green) burned into raised hard ridged scars on his chest, "as if somebody had pressed three hot seals into his skin"** (for life); the dragons can't come near (Kharrak's heat cracks the paving; Lisska's wind flattens Jory; Aurveth sees his heart stutter); **Jory holds his head and feels his heart stop under his hand for a count of three, then start**; *Hold on.* **The fever breaks at noon**: "Here." **The Wayfarer thins; its middle head looks down at him at the last; it goes out "as a wind drops at evening"; the square's hum stops. His Hearthmere copper is still on the south milestone.** **Dusk 147 their size**: **Kharrak the size of a hill, deep forge-red, gold-eyed, white shimmer over his jaws**; **Aurveth larger, bronze shining, eyes the size of cartwheels now**; **Lisska still smallest, the size of the hall, pale-green-eyed, chin on a milestone "the size of a stool to her"**; all still themselves: *Bigger.* / *Did you see my storm?* / Aurveth: ***You nearly went... Don't. Not yet. I've only just stopped waiting.*** **Echo, dusk 148** (he slept a day lying against Aurveth, awake above him): **through her sight the hall's ridge empty, and the grey hill on the lagoon's shore with something small on its back, walking into the sea; the sea-bed stone opens before it "as a ploughed furrow opens" and closes behind, along the drowned road under the piers**: *The lion. Going home. Under.*

"Say it twice."**; "It's warm... It's humming... You brought it." **Ada puts her hand flat on his shirt over the scars, the first time since he was a child; says nothing; doesn't sing.** Aurveth awake on the tarn shore seeing through all of Winshire: *He's still walking. Under. The lion... And west. Across the river. In the Mere. Something burning.*

## Idony (31, 36)
- Ch 31 (day 136): **Gannet arrives at the hill's foot at dusk 135, an hour behind Idony**, bag half full of milestone flakes: "Kit sent me. To see you didn't fall off... That's not a rock"; **he stays awake all night by the fire** (thorn branches, dry grass) chipping flakes and whistling.
