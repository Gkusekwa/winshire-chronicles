# Book 3, Part Five: The Gathering: shared drafting ledger

*Same rules as Book 2's part ledgers. Before writing a scene that touches another POV's days or people, read this file. After deciding any fact another chapter could contradict (who is where on which day, what someone said, a name, a wound, a number, a beast), add one line under your name. The plan wins over the ledger if they clash; note the clash.*

The College sold my tide-charts to Kessmoor for forty years. Sarovel's Grace-priests had my lamp notes. The crown bought my Glasswater readings and never read them... Almost nobody reads it... whoever bought them has a great many lines in an old tongue, and Gannet's spelling, and my guesses in the margin. I wish them joy of it. It took me a year and a god to read it."** **D.310 dusk the last barge in (the last two hundred, the goat, Dace Overy's dog, which has adopted Kit); fifteen days, as on the slate.** **Echo: under the eastern rampart: the lion-hill along the wall's top at the river end, eyes shut, breathing slow "like the sea"; against his foreleg Maren asleep sitting up in Quell's too-big Warden's blue coat, bandaged hand in her lap, grey side of her face to the dusk. Jory: "Is she dead?" Kit: "No. She's asleep... She sleeps like that now."**
