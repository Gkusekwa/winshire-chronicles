## Rowan (55)
- Ch 55 (day 270; 249–269 told back): **The camp moved three leagues up the Wend to the eddy in one day, d.249** (Davin counted the lost boots). **Davin's count d.268: ninety-eight thousand** (the Sun Road column, the coast's last people, the last Marches farms). **The council now sits in Liss's hay-barn above the ferry house on the Hearthmere bank, looking down on the eddy** (Liss's goats moved to the next field, complaining; the door on trestles came up by cart). **Noon d.270 Rowan's chalk map of the Vale on the swept barn flags** (up half the night on her knees); all stand round it: Thale (on a stick now), Pike, Mattias, Rennick, Aud Hallin, Arn, Idony in his chair at the map's north edge, Maren at its foot (her right hand in her coat now too); **Keeper Rhun in the doorway with two wolves: "We'll stand. On your line. Where you put us. Not at your table."** **The line west to east: Hearthmere bank from the downs' foot: Hearthmere's knights (three hundred, with ones in from the north) and militia, greyharts carrying knights along the bend back to the river, hounds; then Sarovel: the sixty olifants as walls ("For their own. Not for a goad"), the unchosen on the white ex-bond-steeds behind them ("where they've something big to bolt behind"); then Kessmoor's batteries both banks round the hub ("Where the relic will be." "Agreed"); Saltreach bank from the river: Kessmoor's second battery, the Wyldmarch next to the river (stonehorns, Keepers' bears and wolves, clans), Isvaren (Aud Hallin's thirty Wardens with snow lynx and twelve unbonded, untamed sabrecats from the College's pens at Vael, down the Iron Fells with the last Wardens d.261, following the Wardens who fed them), Saltreach at the east end where the wall meets the scrub (Rennick's sellswords, Black Ruth's and the Cranes' wyverns on the ground till wanted; "Paid. By the Accord's chest. On my seal").** **Air: each over its own (the Clan wherever Arn says over centre and both banks; rocs over the Wyldmarch; stormkites over Isvaren calling turns for all; Nell Tallis's wyverns over Saltreach's end); "Nobody owns the sky. You share it." River: the Lamp, the Lady in the water, the lion, the three.** **Idony on the wards: *Keep the watch* is the old College jailer's charm *Keep the watch below... do not ask* with the chains taken out; said at noon by every voice on the line; two wards, one per bank, never across the Wend; the Vale is the deadest ground in Winshire, so the Lamp's and the Lady's light ("the Deep in the open air") feeds them "like oil into a lamp... While the voices don't doubt. And while the light holds."** **The Bridle minute of d.147 read aloud by Davin. Pike: the Bridle finished on its flats at Ironhythe, footings laid; Ostrey's sleepwood resin, bought through Blackwater on the Accord's warrant, in sealed casks; both only on the Marshal's word, only if the Lamp fails; the batteries hold their sectors and don't leave the line for the Bridle unless Rowan says; nothing taken off the field before the end. Pike: "Agreed... Of course, Marshal... Agreed." Rowan notes she agreed to everything, fast, and is glad ("She'd have been worried if Pike had argued").** **Calder flew in from the north that dawn; Davin has set a stool with Liss's cushion for him at the map's Saltreach end for the first time: "I'll stand"; he stands by the loft ladder; Davin takes the cushion away.** **Dusk, the yard, the trough: Maren gives Rowan Haverly's edge one-handed (right, watching her own grip): "It's no use to me. I don't fight with it. I fight with him... I've carried it two years. I've drawn it four times. It's been dark for three of them... He'd want it on a gate. Not in a lion's mane." Rowan draws a hand's breadth: the quickened strokes dark. "It's dark." "It's dead ground. It'll be dark on the day... It's still steel." Rowan wears it beside her own; it pulls her a little to the left.** **The footings (Kessmoor surveyors, thirty, d.255, ten days' work): on the Ferry Flat (the water-meadow on the Hearthmere bank between the rampart's river end and the eddy) twelve black iron beds flush with the turf in a ring a quarter-mile across, white cords pegged as spokes to an empty timber cradle at the middle, and six tall black iron jib-posts along the eddy's bank to swing the chains out over the water.** **Midnight 270, moon two days off full, frost: Rowan stands alone in the empty cradle; thinks of her Book 2 cage (six black chains in curves over the river below Greyhold, white lines in every link; *Better that than loose*), the first year's trap with Thorne's boar in it, and her summer words *Chains on a Titan are what started this*; tells herself it's a fallback that will be taken home in the spring unfired; believes it; sleeps.**
