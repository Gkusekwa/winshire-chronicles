## Calder (64, 68, 72, 75)
- Ch 64 (day 318): **Before sunrise all five get up at the same breath (*They've been told*).** Calder up at first light on Kharrak, Lisska left, Aurveth right and climbing above everything. **Rowan's order: the words said again at dawn, not noon, "So they're fed before it starts"; the wards light (Calder says it late).** **The air: Arn's hundred pairs in flights of ten over the centre and both banks (Arn on Ildra leading the first ten); Yeva's four rocs over the Wyldmarch with cart-sized rocks; Aud Hallin's six stormkites high over Isvaren; Nell Tallis's, Black Ruth's and the Cranes' wyverns, hoods off, over Saltreach's end. "Nobody's sky."** **The stormkites' horn code (agreed at council by Aud Hallin, Arn and Rowan): one note west, two north, three south, four north-west, five south-west; then left, right.** **West: the Last Titan comes off the downs to the Hearthmere wall's western half and puts its head against the ward, which goes white in a field-sized patch round its head and holds; it walks north along the ward toward the river with its head on the shimmer "as a man walks along a wall in the dark with his hand on it", testing for the end (the river).** **North: the Wend above the line goes black; the Coil's light under the ice to the line's northern edge where the river runs between the wards; the Lady goes up the channel from the eddy past the Lamp faster than anything on water; both lost in steam.** **South: the Bell comes to a league below the line and lies there in white water.** **North-west: the Furnace's ridge heaves under the downs toward the Hearthmere bend; Aurveth sees it go deep under the knights, under the wall, "Toward the middle... I think it wants the engines"; Calder sends word by Lisska to the ridge to Rowan.** **South-west, second hour: the Horned Sun comes down off the hills onto the Saltreach wall's scrub end, gorse burning black before it; the Saltreach ward bends in "as a sheet bends in a wind" and goes white; Rennick's sellswords on the wall's top drop their crossbows, faces burning like noon in a field.** **Kharrak's white fire across a league along the Sun's front between it and the ward, a quarter-league a breath; light against fire; the frost goes off the whole south-east Vale at once, steam off everything; the Sun stops, turns its horn on Kharrak; Kharrak burns it on the horn and the face at a hundred paces; it goes back a quarter-league up the burning hillside, and comes again, all day. Calder's palms open and bleed on the plates.** **Lisska over the Saltreach camp puts a wind under the heat rolling over the fire-wall and sends it up "like smoke up a chimney" (*There. Easy*; it costs her breath).** **Noon: Nell Tallis's dozen and Black Ruth's wyverns dive out of the black scrub-smoke onto the Sun's back; three go into the light and don't come out.** **Through Aurveth: one of Yeva's rocs drops a rock on the Coil's head in the northern channel and comes out of the steam frosted white, shedding feathers "like snow off a branch", goes down on the Wyldmarch side and doesn't get up.** **Echo, afternoon (the Candle Downs side): along a league of the Hearthmere wall's western face where the Furnace heaves the ground at the bend, hundreds of horse-sized stone lions crouched shoulder to shoulder, backs to the wall, forefeet dug into the heaving ground, bracing it: the wall's own stone shaped; the shadow lying at the bend with forepaws pressed flat; a league off in the river, Maren on the lion's head bent with both hands flat on his stone.** He'll tell Wren, if. **Dusk: the Sun goes back to the southern hills and lies down "like a fire banked for the night"; Kharrak doesn't follow.** **On the ridge Calder pulls his hands off the plates and the skin comes away; Kharrak's whole face blistered white, eyes shut: *It's very bright... I couldn't stop it... I could only hold it.* *That's what we're doing. All of us.* *Holding... I don't like it. I'm good at it, though.*** (The Furnace's attack on the batteries and the Last Titan into the river follow in 65–66, same day.)

## Rowan (65, 73)
- Ch 65 (day 318): **Rowan rides the Hearthmere wall's top all day (a road's breadth, trodden smooth) from the river end to the bend and back on her white-blazed greyhart, Davin behind on a pony, four runners (ex-Hallowmere corn-exchange porters); Haverly's edge on her left hip, dark; "You couldn't see a line from a barn."** **The Last Titan walks north along the Hearthmere ward all morning, head on it, a field-sized white patch where it touches, "as a man walks along a fence looking for the gate".** **Where the white comes, the people under it must say the words again (Idony's *while the voices don't doubt* means all day); Rowan rides ahead of the white along the wall and starts the words at each stretch; her voice "a crow's" by noon.** **Noon at the Sarovel sector: the sixty olifants stand in a double row behind the wall's top, trunks curled, eyes shut; Mattias's unchosen behind on shying white steeds; the ward goes white a spear's length over the olifants and sags one breath under the Last Titan's leaning weight: four olifants in the front row go down and don't get up; the unchosen say the words louder (Mattias's voice cracking); the ward lifts.** **Noon, seen across the river through the Saltreach ward: a beast-flood driven down the Saltreach bank ahead of the Coil's cold out of the dead Wyldmarch and upper country (elk, deer, black shaggy wild cattle, boar, white-eyed wolves; thousands) at the Wyldmarch's river end; the ten thousand stonehorns, herd-men on the leaders, turn north into it in a wedge at a walk and trot (stonehorns don't gallop), and it splits round them "as a river breaks on a bridge's pier", east into the scrub and west into the river; two hundred of the clans dead where it reached the line first.** **Fourth hour (morning): behind the bend a hundred-pace gully opens across the militia's lines with scalding steam (the Furnace pushing under the downs); the wall holds (Rowan has ridden back and seen the stone lions in its face); the three hundred greyharts gallop along the wall's top and foot to the gap, Wynn first with Ash and Sorrow at her knee; the knights dismount in a line across it, hounds in front; the militia come back behind them; the ward holds.** **Fourth hour after noon: the Furnace comes up under the batteries at the Ferry Flat's western edge by the magazine: the turf splits, molten glass runs out "like porridge out of a pot"; a barn-sized plated ironback-like head, black plates red-molten at every seam, small white eyes, the broken Kessmoor cage-chain from the Scald still round its neck, links glowing.** **The four chilled-chain engines (squat drums; chain run through iron tanks of eddy water, steaming) throw chains round its neck and forelegs; steam screams where chilled iron meets the seams; it holds but drags them toward the Bridle's ring "as if it wanted them"; two hundred grey crews run off the bolt-throwers to fight for the Bridle with crowbars, shovels and fire-hooks "as men run in front of a fire to save their own house"; bolt-throwers fire heartstone-tipped bolts point-blank into its plates; eleven crews go under it.** Rowan rides down onto the Flat to the Bridle's edge: banked, unfired. **The Lady comes down from the northern channel, up out of the eddy onto the Flat, winds round the Furnace and pulls it into the eddy's edge: it screams like metal in a forge trough; a column of steam a league high and more; the river boils grey a quarter-league; quenched; it lies black, every plate cracked across "as quenched iron cracks when it's quenched too fast", the Scald chain cold on its neck, eyes grey. Dead: the first destroyer of the battle.** The Lady goes back to lie by the rope. **The line cheers, both banks; Rowan doesn't: the Last Titan has lifted its head off the ward and turned it toward the steam and holds it there; she remembers *Every one we kill, he eats*; says nothing; "she couldn't have done anything else".** **Dusk: Kessmoor lays its eleven dead in a row by the magazine under grey coats; the Bridle banked, chains still on the jibs, unfired; she's glad of it "all the way through".** **Davin's day-one count: sixty-one knights and militia at the bend; four olifants; two hundred of the clans; three wyverns; one roc; eleven of Kessmoor's.** **Echo, dusk: over the Saltreach scrub end against the black smoke, red, bronze and green turning low together, and three rust-grey-and-white rocs (Yeva's, what's left of four) among them.** Rowan rides the wall in the dark from fire to fire saying the words.
