## Calder (64, 68, 72, 75)
- Ch 64 (day 318): **Before sunrise all five get up at the same breath (*They've been told*).** Calder up at first light on Kharrak, Lisska left, Aurveth right and climbing above everything. **Rowan's order: the words said again at dawn, not noon, "So they're fed before it starts"; the wards light (Calder says it late).** **The air: Arn's hundred pairs in flights of ten over the centre and both banks (Arn on Ildra leading the first ten); Yeva's four rocs over the Wyldmarch with cart-sized rocks; Aud Hallin's six stormkites high over Isvaren; Nell Tallis's, Black Ruth's and the Cranes' wyverns, hoods off, over Saltreach's end. "Nobody's sky."** **The stormkites' horn code (agreed at council by Aud Hallin, Arn and Rowan): one note west, two north, three south, four north-west, five south-west; then left, right.** **West: the Last Titan comes off the downs to the Hearthmere wall's western half and puts its head against the ward, which goes white in a field-sized patch round its head and holds; it walks north along the ward toward the river with its head on the shimmer "as a man walks along a wall in the dark with his hand on it", testing for the end (the river).** **North: the Wend above the line goes black; the Coil's light under the ice to the line's northern edge where the river runs between the wards; the Lady goes up the channel from the eddy past the Lamp faster than anything on water; both lost in steam.** **South: the Bell comes to a league below the line and lies there in white water.** **North-west: the Furnace's ridge heaves under the downs toward the Hearthmere bend; Aurveth sees it go deep under the knights, under the wall, "Toward the middle... I think it wants the engines"; Calder sends word by Lisska to the ridge to Rowan.** **South-west, second hour: the Horned Sun comes down off the hills onto the Saltreach wall's scrub end, gorse burning black before it; the Saltreach ward bends in "as a sheet bends in a wind" and goes white; Rennick's sellswords on the wall's top drop their crossbows, faces burning like noon in a field.** **Kharrak's white fire across a league along the Sun's front between it and the ward, a quarter-league a breath; light against fire; the frost goes off the whole south-east Vale at once, steam off everything; the Sun stops, turns its horn on Kharrak; Kharrak burns it on the horn and the face at a hundred paces; it goes back a quarter-league up the burning hillside, and comes again, all day. Calder's palms open and bleed on the plates.** **Lisska over the Saltreach camp puts a wind under the heat rolling over the fire-wall and sends it up "like smoke up a chimney" (*There. Easy*; it costs her breath).** **Noon: Nell Tallis's dozen and Black Ruth's wyverns dive out of the black scrub-smoke onto the Sun's back; three go into the light and don't come out.** **Through Aurveth: one of Yeva's rocs drops a rock on the Coil's head in the northern channel and comes out of the steam frosted white, shedding feathers "like snow off a branch", goes down on the Wyldmarch side and doesn't get up.** **Echo, afternoon (the Candle Downs side): along a league of the Hearthmere wall's western face where the Furnace heaves the ground at the bend, hundreds of horse-sized stone lions crouched shoulder to shoulder, backs to the wall, forefeet dug into the heaving ground, bracing it: the wall's own stone shaped; the shadow lying at the bend with forepaws pressed flat; a league off in the river, Maren on the lion's head bent with both hands flat on his stone.** He'll tell Wren, if. **Dusk: the Sun goes back to the southern hills and lies down "like a fire banked for the night"; Kharrak doesn't follow.** **On the ridge Calder pulls his hands off the plates and the skin comes away; Kharrak's whole face blistered white, eyes shut: *It's very bright... I couldn't stop it... I could only hold it.* *That's what we're doing. All of us.* *Holding... I don't like it. I'm good at it, though.*** (The Furnace's attack on the batteries and the Last Titan into the river follow in 65–66, same day.)
