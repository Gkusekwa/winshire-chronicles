# Book 3, Part Six: The Vale of the Wend: shared drafting ledger

*Same rules as Book 2's part ledgers. Before writing a scene that touches another POV's days or people, read this file. After deciding any fact another chapter could contradict (who is where on which day, what someone said, a name, a wound, a number, a beast), add one line under your name. The plan wins over the ledger if they clash; note the clash.*

## Maren (66, 70, 74, 77)
- Ch 66 (day 318, dusk to small hours): **The Saltreach bank is alive now: the climbing glasses (61) were the Deep running up the tide-lines into the knot under the eddy as the five came in; the Hearthmere bank stays dead (drunk three hundred years); the Saltreach bank hums** (plant for 75). **Dusk: the Last Titan reaches the Hearthmere ward's end at the river, puts its head round it "as a dog puts its head round a door", and steps into the Wend in the gap between the wards, river to its shoulders, chain-ends trailing in the current, walking downstream to Orrun on his raised bed at the eddy's lower lip.** **Face to face, thirty paces of grey water: *Brother.* No answer; it looks at him "as you look at a stranger on a road". *It doesn't know me. It knew me. At the door. Under the hill. It called me little brother... I'm not little.*** (Answers Book 2 ch. 84's *You kept it well, little brother.*) **It comes at his shoulder "as stags come at each other"; the river thrown up in sheets that freeze and fall as ice on her; a chain-end swings out of the water like a flail across Orrun's flank (the iron's *hold* burns cold through the bond), twice in the night; he holds.** **The three shadows hold their sectors; she feels all four weigh on her at once for the first time.** **The Last Titan draws on the Saltreach bank's new Deep and a league of the bank begins to slide toward the river "as wet sand slides": the Wyldmarch's river end, Kessmoor's second battery (bolt-throwers tipping), the eastern wall's river end, stonehorns bellowing, people falling.** **Orrun leans his shoulder on his kin and lays his chin on the sliding bank; she slides down his face onto it; kneels; puts both hands flat (lifts the dead left out of her coat with the right and lays it down), watching them.** **The stand-word (Pell's yard at fourteen; once before at the Scald) said low into the live earth with a Titan behind it: a league of bank stops, nailed, shaking, and further: the Wend itself stands against the bank like glass for a breath. Toll: feeling gone from her right arm to the elbow (the left already dead to the shoulder).** **She shapes the field through Orrun's earth: three long ridges a quarter-league each, waist- to man-high, black soil steaming, between the river and the clans; a long deep trench at the water's edge so the slide goes into it, not onto the people** (she once watched Greyhold's engineers raise banks on the river for a week). **Cold to the heart: the cold goes through the Antlered's stone under her ribs into her heart; she feels it slow.** **Rhun's wolves lie in a ring against her; Rhun puts a heavy green cloak on her: "Knight. The knight.* "I'll remember."** **All night: Orrun at the lower lip, forefoot on the raised stone; his kin a quarter-league off in the eddy's middle; they look at each other across the water; three shadows still in their sectors; Maren behind the first ridge counting Orrun's heartbeats through the bond: slower than hers.**
