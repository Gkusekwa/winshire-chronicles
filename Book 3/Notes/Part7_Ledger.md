# Book 3, Part Seven: Coldtarn: shared drafting ledger

*Same rules as Book 2's part ledgers. Before writing a scene that touches another POV's days or people, read this file. After deciding any fact another chapter could contradict (who is where on which day, what someone said, a name, a wound, a number, a beast), add one line under your name. The plan wins over the ledger if they clash; note the clash.*

## Maren (80, 83, 86)

- Ch 86 (day 330, dawn to noon): **Maren has sat on the fallen stone above the passage every night since 325 (five nights; doesn't think she needs sleep); new snow a hand deep in the night lies on her sleeves and hands and doesn't melt; she isn't cold.** **She can feel every stone of the fallen mountain and the Scarfell beneath it, many-shaped, settling, waiting "as stone waits. ## Calder (78, 81, 84)
- Ch 78 (day 321): **Calder's voice back at dawn as a thread "like a door that hasn't been opened all winter"; *There you are.*** **The six roads run together above the Scarfell's foot into the Drover's Way, up through Harrow's End and over the Scarfell's lip toward Varrhold and Coldtarn; heads over the lip in the night; tails on the Way all morning; Tam's forty wagons last, Tam on the last box.** **Harrow's End empty (his mother's roofless house by the beck; the Eelweir below a ditch of mud where the river was; Fisk's house by the weir, door hanging); the Fenn top field on the slope between the village and the lip beside the Way: his grandfather's chest-high dry-stone walls that his father mended every spring, gone to weed, snowed (where he turned the elk).** **Noon: below the column's tail the snow goes off the moor in a line, heather smoking: the Horned Sun on the Way a league below Harrow's End, now two leagues of white day with the surge; it went round east of the lion in the night (the Last Titan rose off the hole at dusk 320, came north, and stopped two leagues west of the Scarfell's foot facing Orrun) and came up from Cotter's Cross onto the Way behind the tail.** **Kharrak: *I'll go. Back. To the field.* *I'm coming.* *No.* He lands on the lip and puts his head down: *Get off... Stand here. On the edge. Count them over. Like her... Somebody has to count them over... Farmer. Get off.* Calder gets off (the first time he's ever not come). *It was small. At the mouth. Wasn't it.* *It was small.* *Good.*** **Kharrak lands across the Way on the top field, wings out, tail over the wall, face to the light, and puts all his fire across the Way and the slope, a white wall a league long from the beck at the village's foot to the moor's shoulder: not at the Sun; between; the Sun presses; the field's dry-stone walls run to glowing slag "like candle-wax"; the snow goes off the slope; the Eelweir boils.** **Through the rust scar Calder feels everything go out of Kharrak: *Small?* *No. Not small. Not this time.*** **Calder on the lip counts the wagons over ("Thirty-one... Thirty-seven"); Kharrak's red going dark; Tam's last wagon over the lip at about the third hour after noon: "Forty"; Tam lifts one black-knuckled hand off the reins and goes on toward Coldtarn. *That's all of them, Kharrak. - Ch 81 (day 322, dusk into night): **Since noon Calder on Lisska has brought the tail of the tail across the high moor: sixty (the hurt, the old who'd walked twenty leagues, a few of Arn's riders on foot whose dragons died on the Vale); he counts now ("She'd taught him, without trying"); Aurveth very high, seeing.** **Dusk: the surge comes over the Scarfell from the south as weather: a sky-high black wall of wind, snow and hail flickering green, white, gold and blue "like lightning in a summer cloud, but all the colours", roaring like the Shatters; the sixty on the bowl's southern rim with the shore path a quarter-league below, the storm a quarter-league behind.** **Lisska: *Go on. Not before.* He gets all sixty down to the shore: *They're down.* *Good.* Her wind goes out all at once "As a breath goes out of you when you've held it too long"; the storm drops onto the bowl; she comes down slowly "as a leaf comes down in still air" into the tarn's black open middle, which closes over her; the ribbon floats a moment blue on black and goes down. That's the last."** **Echo: on the step beside Maren in the doorway: the lion across the slope's foot, chin on the step, eyes south; over the bowl's southern rim in the storm, coming down the shore path on foot, wings folded, long neck low, white and blazing, toward the slope and the door: his kin.**

## Idony (82)

Davin has six names (Vael, Ironhythe, Solenne, Corsa, Blackwater, Hallowmere); he puts his pencil on the first to cross it out and doesn't; writes the line under them; Idony lets them stand ("Somebody should have them").** **One left: with four out all three glasses lean the same way, south, toward the door, close: "One. One left." "Where?" He looks at the door.** **Echo: the hill shakes like a ferry on a swell; grit; the rune flares and holds; in the doorway Maren and Calder side by side on the step; below the step, filling the doorway's dark with white light, something huge at the slope's foot; and between it and the door the lion getting up.**

## Arn (85)
- Ch 85 (days 325–328): **Ildra doesn't leave: lies by Arn among the nineteen at the hall's back, follows him, chin on his boot, watching him with her gold eyes; he doesn't know what she's thinking ("That was the bond. You knew").** **D.325 Idony: "It's dropped. The wind... Snow. Only snow."** **Maren (three days and nights on the fallen block inside the door, not eating or sleeping that anyone saw) puts her bandaged hand on the door-stone and a man-wide passage opens slanting up through the fallen mountain beside the lintel "as water opens round a hand you push into it"; the first use of what Orrun gave her. "Not the door. Not yet. It's warm in here. It's winter out there."** **Outside: the western crag and the Scarfell's shoulder above it are down: a new grey slope under snow from the bowl's western rim to the tarn, burying the old slope, the step, the beck's mouth, Orrun, the Last Titan and Aurveth; the tarn a third smaller (the western shore, shallows and pouring-stone under the fall), its black middle still open and steaming in its ice ring; Hollow Beck coming out further west; no smoke anywhere (there was always a thread of it on the Scarfell).** **D.326 Ildra brings Arn a white hare and waits "As a dog waits"; "Thank you"; no fire has been lit since the door shut; he gives it back and she eats it.** **D.326 he stands at her shoulder; she puts her shoulder down (she knows him, not what he wants); she flies where his weight leans "like riding a horse" (he rode before the bond).** **Varrhold is gone: the night the mountain came down at Coldtarn the Scarfell's whole western shoulder moved and a slip came down over the hold's spur, hall, byres, pens and the ledges/Roosts into the valley; smooth snow on grey stone, no smoke, tracks or sound; the overflow sent there (79) was under it. The Varr chiefs' black oak chair (his father's, his grandfather's; Ulric wouldn't sit in it; Arn refused it on the coast) is under the mountain somewhere; nobody will sit in it again.** **On the way back: the hatching caves' mouth across the bowl has fallen in under snow. He doesn't tell Calder (whose eyes already saw it).** **D.328 noon, windless snow, the pour on the fallen stone of the new shore at the black water's edge; up the passage: the Clan's riders and their quiet dragons, Calder, Wren with Old Grey, Ada, Bel and her two, Kit and Jory, Liss (pin in her pocket), Tam, Fisk, Davin (case), Idony on his hurdle carried by four Kessmoor crews, Wynn and Sorrow, Osk and the wolf; Maren sitting on the fallen stone by the passage's mouth above.** **No ashes and no fire (none since Liss's lamp ran out). The old priest's words for a pour without ashes (told at the coast; Arn poured Ivo Brack and Sunniva so at Coldtarn in the autumn): *Then pour your hands... You hold them out. Empty. Over the water. And turn them over. So the water knows you'd have poured, if you'd had anything to pour.*** **Gannet first: "Saltreach gives to the water. Can I?" "Yes." (He lay under his cloak a pace from the fall's edge.) Jory and Tam carry him in his coat weighted with stones from the fall, the purse still inside; he goes down slowly into the black water; Kit holds out an empty flat palm: "Salt between us. Nothing owed."** **Arn's names: Kharrak. Lisska. Aurveth. / Ulric, son of Rurik, Champion of the Varr. / Gunna, daughter of Tarne, rider of the ledges, and Tamsin. / Bryn Tolle. Ivo Brack and Sunniva. Asta Dunn of the grey... All the riders. / Ilse Rowan, Marshal of the Accord. / Tobin, of Sarovel, who led the hands. / Gannet, of Blackwater. / (looking up at Maren, who doesn't move) Orrun. / And, not in the priest's words, the beasts of every kingdom that died on the line (hounds, greyharts, olifants, stonehorns, wolves, bears, rocs, wyverns, lynx and cats, and the ones the Kessmoor sheds took).** **Wynn: "Ash. Her name was Ash." Fisk adds Harrow's End names; Jory says *Hal*; Liss one nobody hears.** **"Who pours?" Nobody: no ashes, no heir, no urn. Arn holds out empty cupped hands over the water and turns them over: "Pour with me." Calder's scarred empty hands (fire under the skin) beside his, turned over; then Wren unasked; then Ada, Liss, Tam, Davin (case put down in the snow), Kit, Jory, the riders, everyone along the shore.** **The dragons don't make their pour-sound (they can't be told what this is); Ildra puts her nose to the water where Arn's hands turned and breathes on it (as Lisska breathed on the ash in the Thornwood), not knowing why.** **Ada sings *The old ones went up into the light, and the one fell into six* alone to the end and stops before the last line; nobody sings *Till the six are one again*.** **Echo, night 328 (the coldest of the winter; riders' breath freezing on their beards): Maren sits all night on the fallen stone above the passage in the too-big blue coat, no cloak or hood, hands in her lap; snow settles on her shoulders and hair and doesn't melt; she doesn't brush it off or shake; she isn't cold; at dawn the snow lies on her shoulders "like a mantle".**

## Kit (79)
- Ch 79 (days 321–322): **Kit, Gannet and the one-eyed dog (under Gannet's coat, feet bleeding on the ruts) come over the lip at Harrow's End at the second hour of night 321 and pass the top field by starlight: walls glassy black slag, snow gone, ground steaming, a grey winged hill across the road; nobody on the road stops.** **Dawn 322 Coldtarn down the beck's valley path: black steaming middle, white ice ring; the freed gone from the crags in the night, nobody knows where; the hall's door open in the western crag's foot with a line of unreadable script above it; a steep forty-pace slope of loose scree and frozen turf from the shore to the step, the only way up; nobody at the door.** **On the shore: Tam's forty wagons (Tam with his head in his hands: "She's at the Scarfell's foot. With the lion. With the other one coming"); a block of a few hundred Clan families in red and grey from the ledges and Varrhold, waiting, not going in (Arn's *in the order they come*); thousands coming over the bowl's rim.** **Kit climbs to the step in the warm air from the door: "In the order you came. The knight said... Five hundred... The rest to the caves. The hold at Varrhold. The ledges. The byres... The wagons came first." Tam starts lifting the old out. Jory (came in the night with the Staithe's four hundred): "I'll count." Davin, with Idony carried up the Way on a hurdle by four men, sits on the step with a pencil: "Names. Someone should have them."** **The salt: the last of the *Kittiwake*'s galley dish (coarse grey Blackwater pan-salt) tipped into her coat pocket at the Staithe; a pinch on every tongue at the step: "Salt between us. Nothing owed."** (Instead of *What's sworn is kept, or neither of us is fit to drink with*: a contract with no price; "The salt meant *I won't*.") **Tam: "What's this?" "Salt." "What for?" "So you know you don't owe me anything."** **Then the Clan's families (a girl with a basket of eggs she won't let go); Kessmoor runaways in grey; Osk's lame once-broken-backed wolf from Varrhold's pens licks the salt off her fingers. Jory: "Two hundred."** **The overflow goes down the beck to Varrhold's hold and pens, the caves across the bowl (an old woman in a shawl at the cave mouth with a lantern lit in daylight waving them in: Ada), the ledges, led by Arn and his riders and Tam.** **Noon 322 the ground shakes like a deck taking a sea; the tarn's middle heaves; a hard south wind with sideways snow over the rim and the sky darkening; Idony on his hurdle: "It's the surge. It's coming up the Scarfell. From the middle... It'll be all weather."** **The scree slope slides toward the tarn under the climbing line; Kit, Jory and Davin pull people onto the step; a Wendmouth girl of five in a patched red floating-market coat sliding alone, crying; Gannet goes down the moving scree "as a sailor keeps his feet on a deck in a sea", picks her up and climbs back, grinning for the first time since the far shore; four paces from the step a water-cask-sized boulder falls from the crag above the door into his legs; he throws the girl up "as you'd throw a line to a dock": "Kit. *Catch.*" She catches; he goes down with the scree into the shallows. Gannet dies.** **They bring him up on ropes; on his back in the snow, eyes open, still grinning a little (as when he taught her to splice as her father's bosun); Kit puts the last pinch on his tongue: "Salt between us. Nothing owed"; she leaves the purse of Ostrey silver in his inside pocket and closes his coat over it.** **The sky over the tarn held against the storm: about fifty Clan pairs circling low; Yeva's last two rocs over the slope; a black-green wyvern with no rider (Black Ruth's or Nell Tallis's, nobody knows) flying among the dragons, unchained.** **Echo: high above all, Aurveth, crag-sized, circling with her head down, staring at the tarn's frozen western edge where Hollow Beck comes out from under the scree, at something under the ice Kit can't see.**
