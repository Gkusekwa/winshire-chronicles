# Guide to *The Hunt Beneath* (Book 2)

*Taken out of the book's front matter in Oct 2026, to be published separately as one of the Guides to the Winshire books. The story so far and Who's who are written to be read before Book 2: nothing in them goes past where Book 2 begins.*

## The story so far

*What happened in Book One, The Watch Below.*

Before the six kingdoms there was one realm, and the old songs say something sleeps beneath it. On the Wend, the border river of the Marches, Hearthmere's knights kept their school at Greyhold and said their oath at noon: *keep the watch below.*

Maren Ashdown was twenty-seven, an unbonded late-oath student at Greyhold. Her father had died days after his bonded beast was killed: one life between two. For her trial she was sent to the Old Ruin's forbidden island to bring back proof of its monster. She found an ancient grey lion and would not harm it, so she failed. All winter she went back to sit near it. When the ice broke under her, it dragged her out.

Across the water Calder Fenn grew barley at Harrow's End and raised his daughter Wren with his mother, Ada. The Dragon Clan's old chief was dead, and his brother Ulric and his son Arn both wanted the chair. Arn's brother Teo arranged a peace meeting at a ford, and Calder found him dead in the water on the way. It looked like Ulric's work, and the Clan went to war with itself. In its first battle, on Calder's fields, three bonded riders died, and their dragons came to him in the barley stubble: Aurveth the old general, Kharrak the fighter, Lisska the scout. All three bonded with him at once.

The Restoration was rising on both banks, promising the one realm again, and its leader was seen in two places on the same day. At midwinter it told the kingdoms what Hearthmere had hidden for three hundred years: a Titan chained beneath it, bled to light its lamps. The knights' watch below was a guard on the chains, and from then on Hearthmere's magic failed. Maren learned that the thing in the chains was her lion's kin. Ordered to lure the lion into a trap, she broke her oath and was cast out.

Hidden in the Ruin's tower, Calder saw that the leader was two men, the twins Cassian and Corvin Vale. He heard them speak of Teo's killing, done by their man Rook, and worked out their plan: two locks, one under the Ruin and one under Greyhold, turned in the same breath. Lisska could speak only to him, so he sent her to the woman with the lion carrying a cut chain, a strip of shirt with TWO burned into it, and Wren's blue ribbon.

Then the war came over the river. When bond-forcers trapped the lion, Maren offered her own life into the binding in its place. The lion took it, and told her its name: Orrun. Greyhold was opened from inside. Lucan Haverly died in her arms, and the dying Warden Hale gave her the key to the way down.

Under the Ruin, Calder's hand was on Corvin's wrist when the key turned. Under the school, Maren killed Cassian as his lock gave way. The seal broke, the island fell into the dark, and Corvin escaped up an older stair. Through the bond Maren felt what three hundred years of chains had made of Orrun's kin: a thing that wants the world down.

Maren and Calder met for the first time underground and climbed out at dawn. The Titan rose from the river where the island had been and flew north-east, and every lamp in Hearthmere went out behind it. Maren gave Calder the name the Watch had for it: the Last Titan. Then, with her hand on the lion, she said the old words and meant something new by them. *Keep the watch.*

## Who's who

*As you first meet them in this book.*

### Maren, Calder and Idony

**Maren Ashdown**: twenty-seven, cast out of Greyhold for breaking the knights' oath, bonded to an ancient lion. She counts things: steps, breaths, odds.

**Orrun**: the grey lion of the Old Ruin, once called the Grey Mane, about ten feet at the shoulder. Stone moves for him, and at a cost he can raise a second lion out of earth or rock. He grieves for his kin.

**Calder Fenn**: thirty-two, a barley farmer from Harrow's End on the Saltreach bank, bonded by accident to three dragons. He can't read, and he wants to know why they came to him.

**Aurveth**: the oldest and largest of his three, a bronze, more than three hundred years old and three partners outlived. A torn wing and a horn-wound from the cavern keep her on the ground.

**Kharrak**: broad, scarred and battle-trained, once Dag Rorke's. He speaks in fragments and thinks restraint is cowardice.

**Lisska**: lean, quick and green, once Imra Sallow's. She jokes to keep grief off, sees everything, and wears Wren's blue ribbon on her horn.

**Idony Vael**: thirty-one, a Second-ring Reader of the College of Tides at Vael and a ferryman's son, unbonded and without a patron. For eleven years his tide-charts have shown a hole under Hearthmere.

### Hearthmere

**Liss Ashdown**: Maren's mother, thirty years on the ferry between Greyhold landing and Tolly's Staithe. She never stopped keeping an oil lamp.

**Tam Ashdown**: Maren's younger brother, a smith's apprentice from burned Millbrook.

**Odo Marl**: Millbrook's smith, Tam's master, both legs broken in the forge fire. His mark is two crossed hammers.

**Ilse Rowan**: Knight-Captain at Greyhold, who set Maren's trial and ordered the trap. She comes down from cracked Redwatch with the knights she has left.

**Wynn**: a knight of the gate, with his hounds Ash and Sorrow.

**Sergeant Ottery**: Maren's old river-watch sergeant at Redwatch, who never gets up for anyone below a captain.

**Grieve**: Master of Stables at Greyhold, unbonded, forty years there.

**Aske**: the younger of Greyhold's two licensed surgeons, about twenty-five.

**Sir Aldous Pryne**: a captain in Hallowmere blue who comes to Liss's ferry house with a writ.

**Tobin**: Maren's unbonded friend from Greyhold, loud about being unchosen. The Restoration won him, and on the night Millbrook burned he let her through its barricade.

**Davin**: the Kettle's potboy at Millbrook, who read the Restoration's paper aloud on the jetty and was let go for want of a law.

**Lord Ambrose Thale**: thin, old and careful under a heavy gold chain; he presides over the crown's council at Hallowmere.

### The Dragon Clan

**Arn Varr**: Teo's elder brother, holding Varrhold, in black and thinner. He sits beside the chief's chair, never in it.

**Ulric Varr**: the dead chief's brother and the Clan's best warrior, at Skarrow across the moor, called Teo's murderer since the ford.

**Gorm Askell**: Arn's steward: long, sour, ink-fingered, still limping from the ford.

**Gunna Tarne**: an old rider of the ledges who read Calder's account aloud in the Clan's yards. Her dragon is grey-green Tamsin, three hundred and twelve years old.

**Ivar Leck**: an old rider past flying, one of the ledges' setters, with his old dun dragon Brindle.

**Hulda Sarr**: an old woman of the ledges who can read a boot-print. Her dragon is black-and-grey Harl.

**Leif**: a boy of the ledges with a young brown dragon.

**Brede**: an old rider of the ledges, on his blue.

**Tor Brecc**: Hallam Brecc's grandson, nineteen, with Hallam's nose. Two midsummers ago he was chosen by Skell, a slender, nervous young blue.

**Orm Garrick**: Ulric's man: lean, old, a white scar through one eyebrow, on a grey hill-pony.

### Saltreach

**Ada Fenn**: Calder's mother, who sings the old songs, reads and writes, and keeps the hearth-charms.

**Wren**: Calder's daughter, nine, the one person never afraid of the dragons. She makes people say things twice.

**Bel**: Calder's sister from Upper Rush, Joss's widow. She hasn't forgiven Calder, and doesn't pretend.

**Pip and Nan**: Bel's children, six and four.

**Old Grey**: Joss's great grey dray-beast, once bound and driven to kill him. He goes where Wren goes. Mabbin and Sorrel are the other two greys.

**Old Fisk**: keeper of the Eelweir, Harrow's End's fishing landing, who won't leave his door.

**Coll Brask**: a sellsword of Delling's company out of Wendmouth, taken alive at Upper Rush and let go at dawn.

**Oswin Rusk**: factor of the Halloran trading house on Wendmouth's Gallows Quay.

**Captain Idris Vey**: a small, neat sellsword captain in grey gloves and spectacles, who once besieged Calder at Crow Scar. His hooded wyverns are Sable and Hask.

**Orla Marrow**: Speaker of the Tide Council at Corsa: broad, in blue silk, with a calm figurehead face.

**Brand Halloran**: master of the Halloran house, which has sat on the Tide Council for a hundred years; old, pale, very still.

### Isvaren

**Nessa Vael**: Idony's sister, thirty-five, square and sunburned. She has run the family ferry, the *Heron*, on Glasswater since their parents died, and doesn't hold with colleges.

**The Old Reader**: what Vael calls the Long Library's keeper, an ancient speaking bird the size of a large dog, black going grey, one eye milky. It calls Idony "ferryman" when it's fond of him.

**Beatrix Quell**: Master of the College of Tides, Fifth ring, about seventy, small and white-haired, with a voice like dry leaves. She chairs the Council of Colleges this season.

**Hollis Dane**: Magister of the Blue College, the Wardens' college: tall, heavy-handsome, short grey beard, five notches on his ring.

**Ysolde Marr**: a Warden of the Blue College. Her snow lynx is Frost.

**Brannagh**: the big, short-haired Warden on the Long Library's gate, with a lynx.

**The old cow**: the tusker cow who leads a herd under the High Crown: grey-faced, one tusk broken off and worn yellow, the other a loop longer than a man.

### The Wyldmarch

**Hesper**: a Keeper of the Thornwood, about fifty, alone in her end of the forest for twenty years, with a grey-streaked braid bound in red cord and a dozen knot-cords at her belt. She calls Orrun "Grandfather".

**Hush**: Hesper's duskcat, black and the size of a hound.

**Aun**: elder of the horse clans, very old and blind, white plaits to the ground, led by a girl of ten.

**Morwen**: elder of the forest clans, big and slow, with a brown bear.

**Tolly-Bright**: elder of the river clans, small and quick, and quick to laugh.

### Kessmoor

**Agathe Pike**: Master Engineer of the cordon round the Scald: sixty-odd, tall and heavy-shouldered, smoked spectacles pushed up on her forehead, soot ground into her knuckles.

**Harl Moss**: Inspector of the Assembly's Office of the Cordon: forties, neat and dry, with a brass gauge on a chain at his waistcoat.

### Sarovel

**The Bond-King**: Sarovel's king, who rides a royal unicorn in a gold collar at the head of his court.

**Lady Ismay Aurel**: the Bond-King's court mage, about thirty, in white and gold on a white horse, with bright, interested eyes.

**Seraphin**: the Bond-King's heir, a boy of about nine.

**Old Sebold**: a hand of the court for sixty years, half-blind, master of the unbonded workers below stairs.

**Mother Ysa**: keeper of the lighthouse on Sorrel Isle.

### The Restoration

**Corvin Vale**: the still twin: tall, thin, patient, never raises his voice. He turned the Ruin's lock with Calder's hand on his wrist, and went up an older stair.

**Cassian Vale**: the warm twin, the Restoration's public face, killed by Maren at the lock under the school. The crown hangs his body in chains on Greyhold's gate.

**Callis**: a knight of the gate, passed over for the inner circle, who opened Greyhold's river gate to Cassian. He is carried up from the cavern in irons, both arms broken.

**Absalom Reede**: a travelling bookbinder who ran the Restoration's meetings at Millbrook and prints its bills.

**Delling**: captain of a Saltreach sellsword company, the yellow tower, hired by the Restoration. His men have the paymaster's chest, and he sells anything.

**Rook**: the Restoration's agent who killed Teo, dead on the spit at the Ruin. He kept a book.
