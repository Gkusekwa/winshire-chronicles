# Winshire — Style Guide

*Working conventions for the prose. Items marked "proposal" are suggestions until agreed.*

## Point of view and structure *(established)*

- Two protagonists, **strictly alternating chapters**: odd chapters follow the student, even chapters the farmer (Book 1).
- Each protagonist's chapters **reference events happening to the other**: rumors, sightings, news, signs. The reader knows more than either protagonist until they meet.

## Voice *(proposal: style A with B's edge; see `Book 1/Notes/Voice_Samples.md`)*

- **Close third person, past tense.** Each chapter stays inside one protagonist's head and knows only what they know.
- **Base voice: grounded and plain.** Concrete things, ordinary words, dry humor, and a world that feels lived in: mud, wool, smoke, rough stone.
- **Maren's chapters** lean toward the lean, hard edge: short sentences under pressure, counting, distances, things she won't let herself feel.
- **Calder's chapters** stay plain and earthy, thinking in weather, soil, animals and his village. After the bond, three other minds crowd his chapters.
- **The mythic register is kept for the old world:** songs, the carved verse, legends, and Ada's stories. Used sparingly, it makes the past sound older than the present.
- **Mind-speech** (bond and dragon speech) goes in *italics*, with no quotation marks.

## Keeping it human *(working rules for every draft)*

**Show it on the page**
- Don't name an emotion when a body, an action or a choice can carry it. Never "a mix of fear and determination."
- Use specific nouns: "a grey cat the size of a calf," not "a magnificent beast."
- Similes come from the viewpoint character's own world: soldiering and the river for Maren; farms, weather and animals for Calder. Don't use any comparison they couldn't have thought of.
- Enter scenes late and leave early. End a scene on an action, an image or a line of dialogue, never a summary or a lesson.

**Rhythm**
- Vary sentence length on purpose. Fragments and run-ons are allowed when the mind works that way.
- Don't group things in threes by reflex (three adjectives, three clauses, three beats).
- Use few em dashes; prefer commas and full stops.
- Avoid balanced, symmetrical sentences ("not just X, but Y"; "It wasn't X. It was Y.").

**Dialogue**
- People talk past each other, interrupt, and leave things out. Nobody explains what both speakers already know.
- Use "said" almost always. Don't fill pauses with nods, soft smiles or sighs.
- Humor is allowed, even in grim chapters.

**Words and phrases to avoid**
- testament, tapestry, delve, palpable, intricate, symphony, ethereal, unspoken, visceral
- "the weight of…", "something shifted", "in that moment", "a dance of…"
- "let out a breath she didn't know she was holding", "eyes that held…", "a flicker of…"
- metaphorical "echoed" and "whispered" (wind whispering, history echoing)

**Process**
- Read each chapter aloud. Rewrite any sentence you wouldn't say.
- Every chapter gets a pass in Goodluck's own words. AI drafts are a starting point, not the finished voice.
- Amazon KDP asks whether a book's text, images or translations are AI-generated, so check its current content guidelines before publishing. If chapters start from AI drafts, keep notes on how much was rewritten.

## Length

- About **2,500–3,000 words per chapter**, about **110,000 words** for Book 1.

## Names and terms

- **Winshire** (never "WinShire"). Only ancient beasts, old songs, and a few scholars use the name; most people say "the Six Kingdoms."
- **Titans** for mountain-sized beasts; **the Last Titan** for the one beneath Hearthmere in Book 1.
- **The elder Titans** are known to people only as **gods**. Don't call them Titans on the page in Book 1.
- **The Marches** for the border region; **the Old Ruin** for the ruin on the border river.
- **The Restoration** (working name) for the antagonist; before chapter 34, always **"the leader,"** one man.
- Kingdom names other than Saltreach are working names.

## Reveals to protect (Book 1)

- Don't reveal that the Restoration has two leaders before chapter 34.
- Don't reveal that other Titans exist; the Last Titan is only legend until chapter 25.
- Don't reveal the Restoration's part in the Dragon Clan killing until the farmer overhears it in chapter 34.
- Nothing about the Council of Immortals, ever, in Books 1–3.

## Manuscript files

- One file per chapter in `Book 1/Chapters/`, named `Chapter NN - Title.md`.
- Each file begins with planning notes between `<!-- notes -->` markers, then a `---` line. **Write the prose below the line.** The notes don't go into the published book.
