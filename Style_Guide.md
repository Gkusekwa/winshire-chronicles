# Winshire — Style Guide

*Working conventions for the prose. Items marked "proposal" are suggestions until agreed.*

## Point of view and structure *(established)*

- Two protagonists, **strictly alternating chapters**: odd chapters follow the student, even chapters the farmer (Book 1).
- Each protagonist's chapters **reference events happening to the other**: rumors, sightings, news, signs. The reader knows more than either protagonist until they meet.

## Voice *(proposals)*

- **Third person, close (limited):** each chapter stays inside one protagonist's head, seeing only what they see and knowing only what they know.
- **Past tense.**
- **Two distinct voices:**
  - *The student:* disciplined, observant, measured; trained to read danger and hold back. Sentences tighten under pressure.
  - *The farmer:* plainer, earthier, practical; thinks in weather, soil, animals, and his village. After the bond, his chapters are crowded by three other minds.
- **Dragon and bond speech** (mind to mind) in *italics*, with no quotation marks.
- **Grounded fantasy:** rough stone, mud, wool, smoke. Magic and beasts should feel like they belong in a real, weathered world.

## Length

- About **2,500–3,000 words per chapter**, about **110,000 words** for Book 1.

## Names and terms

- **Winshire** (never "WinShire"). Only ancient beasts, old songs, and a few scholars use the name; most people say "the Six Kingdoms."
- **Titans** for mountain-sized beasts; **the Last Titan** for the one beneath Hearthmere in Book 1.
- **The elder Titans** are known to people only as **gods**. Don't call them Titans on the page in Book 1.
- **The Marches** for the border region; **the Old Ruin** for the ruin on the border river.
- **The Restoration** (working name) for the antagonist; before chapter 34, always **"the leader,"** one man.
- Kingdom names other than Saltreach are working names.

## Reveals to protect (Book 1)

- Don't reveal that the Restoration has two leaders before chapter 34.
- Don't reveal that other Titans exist; the Last Titan is only legend until chapter 25.
- Don't reveal the Restoration's part in the Dragon Clan killing until the farmer overhears it in chapter 34.
- Nothing about the Council of Immortals, ever, in Books 1–3.

## Manuscript files

- One file per chapter in `Book 1/Chapters/`, named `Chapter NN - Title.md`.
- Each file begins with planning notes between `<!-- notes -->` markers, then a `---` line. **Write the prose below the line.** The notes don't go into the published book.
