**Item 26, the College lamp colours:** Two different colour sets are mixed up here. The lamp colours are ch 3/8's (Blue College blue, College of Stars silver, Physicians green, Scribes amber) plus the Tides' soft blue (chs 29, 31, 36, 39). The collar colours in ch 36 are a separate set (Scribes black, Keepers white). The two can't be the same set, because white lamps are "the colour of no college at all" (ch 29). The real clashes were the names, and the Stars' colour against ch 8's master "in pale silver". Ch 36 was the cheapest file to fix (3 edits against 5 in chs 3 and 8). I also matched the Tides' collar to their soft blue. Ch 28's list (blue, green, gold, red, violet) is Maren's partial view and still fits.
- Changed (Book 2 ch 36 prose):
  - "the Tides' soft green, the violet of the Measures, the red of the Healers, the gold of the Sky-watchers," → "the Tides' soft blue, the violet of the Measures, the red of the Physicians, the silver of the College of Stars,"
  - "an old Master of the Healers at the west point" → "an old Master of the Physicians at the west point"
- Needs changing elsewhere: Book 2/Notes/Part3_Ledger.md, ch 36 line: "(collar colours: Blue blue, Scribes black, Tides soft green, Measures violet, Healers red, Sky-watchers gold, Keepers of the Script white)" → "(collar colours: Blue blue, Scribes black, Tides soft blue, Measures violet, Physicians red, Stars silver, Keepers of the Script white)".
