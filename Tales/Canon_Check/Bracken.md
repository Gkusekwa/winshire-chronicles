# Bracken: canon check

**Checked:** Book 1 ch 1, 5, 9, 25, 27, 29, 33, 37, 39, 41 read in full or at every relevant scene (Ferriby and First Frost word for word against the tale); ch 47, 57, 67, 69, 73 for later mentions. Grepped all three books and the Bible for Bracken, Ide/Darrow, Grip, Ash, Sorrow, Wynn, Callis, Turnip, the cat, Dun/Merrow, Redwatch, Ferriby, days 49–63. Checked against World.md's rulings (Redwatch on the bank with landing steps; Greyhold's dead burned on the shore; Ide dies dusk day 65), Characters.md and the Bestiary.

**Fixed:**
1. Ferriby, the raid. Old: the whistle "two short and then quiet" sounds from the roof *before* the patrol moves, then "one long one". New: the first signal is cut there, and later "two short. Then, after a while, one long one". Book 1 ch 33: Pell gives his orders first, and only then "Two short notes" (to the paddock), then "One long note" (to the green).
2. Ride back from Ferriby. Old: "The fork-girl made a short noise. Rooks." New: "The fork-girl nodded." Book 1 ch 33: "Maren nodded."
3. Turnip, three places. Old: "the grey with the knocking thing", "on the grey", "the grey with its old turnip". New: "piebald". Book 1 ch 5: "an elderly piebald gelding"; Characters.md "Elderly piebald".
4. Pell's lesson. Old: "In the afternoons she sat on the long bench". New: "Some mornings". Book 1 ch 9: "fourteen of them on the bench that morning".
5. The river before day 62. Old: the ice "made a sound at night… a long slow grinding". New: "a long slow creaking". Book 1 ch 41 notes: "The ice runs from day 62"; ch 37 (day 58) only "the first thin ice beginning to form along the edges of the reeds". Grinding means running ice, which comes later (the tale's own day-63 "ice grinding out at the edges" is correct and was left alone).
6. Added `Canon-checked against the books: 29 Oct 2026.`

**Defensible:**
- Ash. The tale never gives Ash a pronoun ("opened *its* shoulder" means the silent hound's), so it agrees with ch 39's "big grey bitch". Wynn's bonded hound is Sorrow (ch 51 "Wynn with Sorrow"). Calling Wynn "Ash and Sorrow's man" and Callis "Grip's man" names whose hounds they are, not who is bonded, so Callis stays unbonded.
- Long Barn hounds: the tale's five (Grip, Ash, Sorrow, the young bitch, Bracken) match ch 27's "the hounds, five of them".
- The honey-smoke on the Ferriby raiders, and the crate at the roadblock (ch 5) carried into the trees "in the summer": ch 5 is about day −21, still summer. Ch 73 confirms the smoke came off the Redwatch barge.
- Redwatch: yard, steps down to a stone landing, boats tied there. This agrees with ch 39 and with World.md's "on the bank, with landing steps below it".
- Dun dies on the seventh morning after Ferriby (49 → 56), Merrow dies at the change of watch, and the pyre is at dusk on 57 with the smoke going straight up. All of this agrees with ch 37.
- The landing at dawn on 63: everything checkable agrees with ch 39. That covers the six silent hounds two-by-two, the man in grey in the near barge's stern, Pell down by an axe-man and a hound at his legs, Bracken past Maren's knee, the axe-man into the river, the two hounds coming off Callis, the throat, *Thock*, Bracken letting go gently and lying down on his side, the scream from the steps, Ide ten paces off with her hand under her ribs on the left, and Maren dropping the lantern to catch her.

**Book-side issues:** none new. Ch 39 calls Ash a bitch and ch 63 calls Ash "he"; this is already in LOG.md.

**Verdict:** fixed (5 fixes).
