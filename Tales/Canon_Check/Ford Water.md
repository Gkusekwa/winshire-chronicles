# Canon check: Ford Water

**Checked:** read in full Book 1 chs 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16; the relevant passages of Book 1 chs 28, 39, 48, 54, 60; Book 2 ch 19 (Ulric and the ledger, the bay mare); Book 3 chs 15 and 18 (Arn always lost to Teo; the Choosing Ledge; the third ledge). Bible: World.md (calendar, Coldtarn, the Drover's Way distances, the Clan's dead, Imra and Lisska), Atlas.md (Teo's ford, the falls, the Ram's Head), Characters.md, Continuity_Sweep_Oct2026.md, Style_Guide.md; Plan.md entry 5. Grepped the three books and the Bible for Teo, Arn, Ulric, Haldor, Rurik, Hild, Asger/Brodd, Skerry, Haki, Brann Holt, Imra, Lisska, Sigrun, Gorm, Raske, the ford-house, the chair, the stable roof, "green menace", "go together", stallion/white sock, and the moon around days -15 to 14.

**Fixed:**
1. The ledge, day -4 (Imra on Lisska): "Not once. In nine years." → "Not once. In twelve years." Book 1 ch 10: "She's never said she likes anyone who wasn't Clan. Not once. In twelve years." (Also World.md: Imra bonded about twelve years before the mill; nine years is the island.)
2. Same scene: Lisska "put her nose against his chest so hard that he rocked back" → "put her nose against his chest, gently, so that he rocked back". Book 1 ch 10: "she pushed her nose, very gently, against his chest, so that he rocked back on his heels and Teo had to catch his arm".
3. The two fires: "like walking out on Coldtarn's ice in a hard winter, when the edges froze" → "like walking out on the beck's ice in a hard winter, when the edges froze". Book 1 ch 8: "It doesn't freeze. It's too deep to freeze." World.md: "Coldtarn, whose water never freezes".
4. Notes block: added "Canon-checked against the books: 29 Oct 2026." No Decided-here line changed.

**Defensible:**
- The ride down on day 4: six leagues from "a little after the third hour" to first light is about three hours, quick for "at a walk". The Bible gives only day rates (a steady horse, 14 a day). Left as is.
- Teo leaves Varrhold at noon on day 0 and reaches the fair at dusk, 14 leagues. That's a full day's steady ride in an afternoon, hard but possible (hard riding is 25 a day). Book 1 ch 12 only says he came at dusk.
- "the ford-house dark" at the ford: Book 1 ch 14 shows only the shieling, but Book 1 ch 54 has "the ford-house at Teo's ford", so the building is canon.
- Ulric's stalls "empty, the seven that had gone down to the Ram's Head": Rook's mare (Book 2 ch 19) would make it eight, but that's Teo's assumption in the dark, not a count.
- Ulric, not Teo's own idea, says a Varr should be seen smiling at every village: Book 1 ch 4 has Teo say "My uncle wants every village in the Marches to see a Varr smiling at it", so it agrees.
- Teo at fifteen "gone back to [the third ledge] and sat there laughing"; Book 3 ch 18 has him laugh "all the way back down the stair". Both can be true.
- Gorm's "twenty since the funeral" on about day -12 against Book 1 ch 10's "twenty-two" on day -4: a rising count, consistent.
- Moon: a waning moon four nights past the full on day 4, rising late, fits the cycle (new moon days -15 and 14; Book 1 ch 17).
- Rook appears only as in Book 1 ch 10 (the bucket, the halter, the nod); the bucket Teo barks his shin on reveals nothing. Must not reveal is kept.

**Book-side issues:**
- Book 1 ch 6: Teo says of the split hall "Three days, it's been like that", but the split began the night Haldor's body came in, before the pyre was lit (about day -27), and the pyre smoke had already lain three days by ch 2. Calder's visit, a few days after ch 4, is about eight days after it. Possibly "Three days" should be looser ("A week").
- Book 3 ch 18: "The next year Teo had stood there once, fifteen" (the year after Arn's sixteenth summer). With Arn about twenty-five and Teo twenty in Book 1, Teo would have been about twelve that year. The tale follows "fifteen".
- Book 1 ch 54: the ford-house is "where they laid the boy out", but ch 14 has Teo laid on the bank stones and taken up the Way on a hurdle at dusk. Minor.

**Verdict:** fixed (3 fixes).
