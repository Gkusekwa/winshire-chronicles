# Canon check: Nine Days

**Checked:** Book 2 ch 62 (in full), 52 (in full), 43 (caves scene and notes), 56 (notes, Gunna/Blackwater/Lisska passages), 54 (notes, moon/throat), 38 (roof scene), 12 (caves), 59 (notes), 82, 87, 88 (Wren/Ildra lines); Book 1 ch 50, 52, 54, 56 (Old Grey, the gate); Book 3 ch 2, 18, 45 (Ildra's look, the chirr). Bible: World.md, Style_Guide, Book 2 Chapter_Plan (Lisska's flights). Grepped all books for Wren, Ildra, little red one, Old Grey, Leif, Gunna, Tamsin, Brede, the tarn going black, day numbers 186/278–281/305–317, and the moon.

**Fixed:**
1. Opening, "She had lived in the caves since the autumn" → "since the spring". Book 2 ch 12: the Fenns come up to the caves on day 18 (spring); ch 21 and 24 have them "down from the caves" and "come up from the caves" in summer.
2. Day 186: "Papa stayed one night ... pretended to be asleep for ... In the morning he went down" → "Papa stayed till the afternoon went ... pretended not to hear ... Before dusk he went down". Book 2 ch 43: "He showed Aurveth the name. On the setters' ledge, at dusk"; notes: "Day 186 ... He shows Aurveth the name ... Ada reads him all eleven pages that night"; "Days 187–189 back at Greyhold landing". Decided-here line updated to match.
3. "Lisska came and went that autumn, with Papa's letters to the lion-woman tied on her leg" → "with Papa". Book 2 Chapter_Plan, Lisska's flights: only flight 3 (d.228, sent from away south, back to Calder at Corsa d.241) falls in the autumn, and Calder is away from the Scarfell most of it (ch 46–52).
4. Wren's slate: Leif "took it down ... on his brown" → "with his brown walking behind him". Book 2 ch 52: "He came down the path to the hold at noon with his brown dragon walking behind him".
5. Midwinter: "Wren went past them both with Old Grey and sat on her bowl" → "...turned her bowl upside down again with a great scraping effort, and sat on it." Book 2 ch 52, Calder watching: "She turned one of the stone hatching bowls upside down with a great scraping effort and sat on it."
6. Day 286: the letter "that Papa took down to the hold to read with Ada" → "that Papa read by himself on the setters' ledge before he took it in to Ada." Book 2 ch 54 notes: "He reads it on the setters' ledge, himself, slowly. Then takes it to Ada in the kitchen."
7. Notes: added "Canon-checked against the books: 29 Oct 2026."

**Defensible:**
- Ada's sore throat at the full moon (~293) and again at the dark of the moon on day 308. Ch 62 says Wren asked "at the new moon and at the full moon"; ch 54 says she asked at the new moon. With the dark of the moon on day 278 (ch 52), the full moon falls about 293 and the next new moon about 307–308, so both asks fit.
- Gunna home two days before Brede (as the tale decides it). Ch 56 notes say "with Gunna", but the prose has Gunna seeing them off with Brede and two riders, and ch 59 doesn't put Gunna at Gullhaven.
- Gunna's sheets "wouldn't say what for". She shows Calder on the setters' ledge (ch 56), not Wren in the caves.
- Aurveth isn't mentioned at the midwinter fire (ch 52 has her at the cave mouth). The tale never says she was absent.
- Old Grey "all one winter"/"from after the snow came": Book 1 brings him home about day 107, in snow, and he turns his head on day 121 at the gate (ch 56). Wren running up the lane shouting matches ch 56.
- "the year the tarn below went black with ash": a storyteller's legend in the wide opening, not a dated event.
- Tamsin is 312 (ch 56). Gunna's burned face and Tamsin's wing match ch 21 and 24. Ildra's look matches Book 3 ch 18 and World.md. Kharrak is red. The ninth-day and naming dialogue is word for word with ch 62.

**Book-side issues:**
- Ch 62: "They'd been open since the autumn and there'd been nobody behind them." This contradicts ch 43 ("her eyes half shut", day 186) and ch 52 ("In the autumn she'd been lying down... eyes half shut. Now she was standing... eyes open"). The tale follows ch 43 and 52 (open since the deep of winter).
- Ch 12 has the hatching hollows "worn into the rock" of the floor, yet ch 52 and 62 have Wren dragging and turning stone hatching bowls. The tale's loose wall-bowls reconcile it, but the books disagree with each other.
- Ch 88 notes: Bel "carried up from the hold in the autumn" a table, against ch 52: Bel "hadn't come up to the caves since the autumn". Minor.

**Verdict:** fixed
