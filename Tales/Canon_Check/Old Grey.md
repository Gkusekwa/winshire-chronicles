# Old Grey: canon check

**Checked:** Book 1 ch 2, 4, 6, 10, 36, 50, 52, 54, 56, 62, 64, 65, 69 in full at every Old Grey / Mabbin / Sorrel scene. Book 2 ch 2, 6, 11, 52 for later facts. Grepped all books and the Bible for Old Grey, Mabbin, Sorrel, Joss, Bel, Wren, the halter, Ewe Beck, the Staithe, days 99–142. Magic.md §6 and World.md (bound beasts without a binder).

**Fixed:**
1. Sorrel's age (two places). Old: "When the yearling had come, Sorrel, the yearling…"; "It was the yearling's call." New: "the foal had come… the foal"; "the young mare's call". Book 1 ch 4: "a young dray-mare called Sorrel, three years old".
2. Where Sorrel was. Old: "in the dark down the paddock". New: "in the far paddock". Ch 4: "out in the far paddock… loose in the paddock under the alders".
3. The beasts at the fire. Old: "the beasts in it screamed" (in the burning barn). New: "the beasts in the yard screamed". Ch 2: "They had them roped and out before we were even down the ladder, and then they fired the barns behind them."
4. The ear. Old: both men hold his head and something goes in and out of the ear. New: "the barley-dust one holding it and Joss with something going in and out of the ear". Ch 50: "He'd held the yearling's head while Joss stitched it."
5. The road south. Old: at the first grey light they're already by a salt-smelling river. New: "Days on, when the light came up grey". Upper Rush to Wendmouth is over a hundred leagues (Atlas; ch 50 "A hundred and twenty leagues on the drove road").
6. The good halter (three places). Old: "from the nail inside the barn door"; "the nail inside the barn door"; "hung on the nail inside the new barn door". New: "the peg by the door" / "its peg by the door". Ch 50: "the peg by the door empty where the good halter hung"; ch 52: "on its peg by the door, where Bel had hung it back".
7. The night Joss died. Old: the handler "didn't shout". New: "called out once, short". Ch 50: "The grey's handler… woke, and called out"; ch 57 (Vale): "a handler of ours woke and called out".
8. The unbound pen. Old: "beside the lines". New: "behind the lines". Ch 52: "a pen at the top of the slope, behind the lines".
9. The burning. Old: "Nobody came at the beast lines." (cut). Ch 52: Aurveth lands at the head of the beast lines and the hounds come at Calder there.
10. The head turns twice. Added after Wren points at the crag: "Once more, at his name, the head turned its hand's breadth toward the gate and stopped." Ch 64 (day 137): "He's turned his head twice. To the girl's voice."
11. The ear-moving evening. Old: "the three of them. Sorrel. Mabbin. Old Grey." New: "the three of them by the gate. Mabbin. Old Grey in the middle. Sorrel." Ch 62: "standing together by the gate… Old Grey in the middle".
12. The camp before the gate. Old: under Greyhold's walls, and the girl "had gone through a gate in the wall" with Ada. New: the walls are far up the river on a hill, and the girl comes to him every day and goes back to Ada. Ch 65/67/69: the Fenns are at the refugee camp by the water-meadows below Millbrook, two leagues from Greyhold; Ada, Wren and the children are in the crush outside the gate on day 142.
13. The attack on the camp. Old: "It came down the river from the south". New: "It came up off the river on the wind". Ch 69: the rising "came down the back channel in boats" from the town and fired the camp; the town is upriver of the meadows, not south.
14. "It had lifted its head once before for a voice" → "before" (it has now lifted it twice).
15. Added the canon-checked line.

**Defensible:**
- Wren hugs his foreleg at the stone jetty, and at Greyhold she stands by the Long Barn saying his name twice: ch 64, 69. Bel looks at the slack rope "as if somebody had put it there": ch 69 word for word.
- At the gate the tale has "a woman… smelled of iron and sweat and a great cat" taking Sorrel's halter, and Bel at Mabbin's head. Ch 69: "Maren got to Sorrel's halter"; "Bel was at Mabbin's head".
- Distances: Ewe Beck four leagues, the bell a league along the lane, the Staithe "six leagues": ch 52, 64.
- Sold at Wendmouth "with papers" (ch 50: "Bought fair, at Wendmouth, at the autumn sales. We've the papers"). Mabbin stays behind in the pens and turns up on Gallows Quay on day 50 (ch 36).
- The burning night has no moon. Ch 52: day 102 is the dark of the moon, and they go the next night.
- Must not reveal: the tale ends with his eyes still empty, which is ch 69's "His eyes stay empty".

**Book-side issues:**
- Book 2 ch 11, Bel: "He went to the Old Ruin in the autumn to bring the old grey home." Book 1 ch 50 has Joss going to the Ewe Beck camp on day 99, in deep winter (snow, beck ice), and never to the Old Ruin.

**Verdict:** fixed (14 fixes).
