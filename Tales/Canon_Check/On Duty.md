# Canon check: On Duty

**Checked:** Book 1 ch 47 (the lamps failing), 71, 75, 77 and 79 in full, plus the cat's earlier appearances (ch 27, 37, 39, 49, 51 for the winter lamps and warming-stones). Book 2 ch 2, 4 and 5 in full, ch 6's notes and ch 79's cat line. I also searched all three books and the Bible for the grey cat, Grieve, Hale, Thorne and the boar, Haverly and Kesh, Liss and the water-gate, Old Grey, Mabbin and Sorrel, Rowan's riders, the lamps without oil and the warming-stones. Against the Bible I checked Bestiary (great cats, duskcats, the grey cat on duty and gone by ch 79) and World.md (the seal, the lamps failing from night 90, the Settled block on Greyhold's dead). Must not reveal is respected: no "lion" for the cat, no kinship, no bowing, no bridge, tower steps or Grey Mother, no age.

**Fixed:**
1. **Dusk, night 143: the order of Liss's warning and Rowan's departure.** The tale had Rowan's riders leave before the cat goes down the water-gate stair and finds Liss shouting for Hale. Book 1 ch 75 needs Rowan still in Greyhold when the warning comes. Hale: "Rowan didn't believe it. Callis was on the gate with her that minute." Haverly: "She came to the water-gate at dusk ... Rowan's gone. She took every rider up to Redwatch at dusk." The section now opens "At dusk the cat went down the water-gate stair." The riders' paragraph and "The barn was half empty after" now come after "Then it went up." No words changed in either block.
2. **Orrun's arrival: when Lisska comes.** The tale had the green dragon come over the walls, and the men by the gate run, only after the flagstone lion rose. Book 1 ch 75 has her come over the yard and the men by the gate break and run first, then the barricade, then the yard-stone lion. That sentence now comes before "The big one turned his head toward the barn." It is cut from the end of "Then the big one went away north ... toward the falcon screaming."
3. **The yard-stone lion goes before Haverly dies.** I swapped two sentences: "The stones by the well lay down ... and were stones." now comes before "Then he was gone too." Book 1 ch 75: "Somewhere while Haverly talked, the thing by the well had gone back into the yard."
4. **Midnight: the island falls before Orrun's grief and before Lisska goes down.** The tale had the grief come down the line and Lisska go down, and only then "Then the hill dropped" (the island) and the waking. Book 1 ch 77: the lock turns, "she feels something fall: a whole island going down into the dark", then Orrun touches his kin and his grief comes through, then "Lisska comes down the stair behind them". I moved the block "Then the hill dropped. --- Not the hill ... nowhere to go that wasn't on top of it." to before "Far down the stair ... the big one's line went bright and hard". No words changed.
5. **Sunrise: the lamps.** "The cat had never seen them out. Nobody on the hill had." → "The cat had seen them falter all winter. It had never seen them go dark together and stay dark." Book 1 ch 47: on night 90 "every lamp in Greyhold goes out at once", and they dimmed afterwards. Ch 51: "the lamps on the wall had gone down to red". Ch 75 (this same night): "the old lamps were lighting and going out". World.md: lamps fail from night 90.
6. **The warm places.** After "like a hand held near a face" I added "Since the hard frost those had gone wrong, the stones and the lamps both." This is so the list of always-lit lamps and warming-stones doesn't contradict Book 1 ch 47 (the warming-stones "went cold for a whole night"; lamps dimming) or ch 59 ("nothing burned without it now").
7. Notes block: added "Canon-checked against the books: 29 Oct 2026." No Decided here line needed changing.

**Defensible:**
- **Night 143.** This matches ch 75. Thorne and the boar die together in the gateway at the third hour, and the bells stop. The barricade uses the boar's iron-banded stall door. Grieve is on a cart with a pitchfork. Old Grey is in the gap with a spearman a few paces off who won't come closer. Ada and Bel are behind with two children. Lisska is three steps down the stair with her wings over Haverly and Kesh.
- **Midnight and sunrise.** These match ch 77 and 79: the chains in the small hours, the inner wall splitting at sunrise, the lamps out, the draw going north-east. Hale's body sits on the bottom step against the wall with his stick across his knees, as ch 75 leaves him.
- **Days 0–6.** These match Book 2 ch 2, 4 and 5. Bel comes first with Nan on her hip, Ada with Pip by the wrist, then the greys, with Old Grey last and nobody leading him. Liss's lamp has a brass foot and is lit with a spill. There are fifty-three graves, the boar dragged up by eleven men and a dray-team, Kesh on Haverly's chest, Maren and Orrun apart at the foot of the hill. On day 3 the body is hung under the charter. On day 4 the brass-horn herald comes, and Aurveth tries her wing at dusk. Day 6's goodbye has everyone in the places ch 5 gives. The swim with Kharrak under her and Lisska over her, and Calder in the ferry with his hand on her neck, takes the best part of an hour. Maren is at the foot of the landing with the whetstone. At noon she goes to Millbrook with Tam.
- **Liss's boat.** Liss comes to the water-gate in her ferry with a pole. Ch 71/75 say only "in her boat".
- **The cat at Liss's step.** It isn't shown going back. Book 2 ch 79 later has it missing from the Long Barn, and the notes say so.

**Book-side issues:** none.

**Verdict:** fixed (6 fixes, plus the checked line).
