# Tales canon check (Nov 2026)

All 22 tales were checked against the books as they stood after the October 2026 fixes: every chapter each tale depends on, the Bible, and searches across all three books for every name, place and day number. One report per tale is in this folder.

**Result:** all 22 tales needed small fixes, and all are fixed in the tale files (about 80 changes, mostly a word, a number or a line). Each tale's notes block says "Canon-checked against the books". All 22 have since had their final prose pass and are at final draft (Nov 2026).

## Settled (Nov 2026): issues found in the books

The tales were fixed to agree with the books. These points were inconsistencies **inside the books** (or between a book and the Bible) that the check turned up. **All are settled** (2 Nov 2026), each with the smallest fix: the chapters' majority reading, the world's rules and the maps won. The decisions and every edit are in `Book_Fixes/` (batches A: items 1–11, B: 12, 14, 17–19, 23 in part, 24, 26, C: 16, 20–22, 23 in part, 25). Items 13 and 15 were Bible-only (World.md's fords now south-east of Ashford; the Bestiary's Stell is one of the ferry's far-shore landings). Items 11 and 25 turned out not to be contradictions.

Bible changes that came with them: World.md now states when a bonded partner dies (at once or within three days, never later), and that the Clan hatches in the newer caves under the hold while the old Coldtarn caves hold the winterers; the Bestiary no longer calls Aurveth Clan-bred and says where Book 3's sixty olifants came from; Characters.md has Gannet heavier by Book 3; the atlas puts Vael under the High Crown.

The list below is the record of what was found.

### Bonds and beasts
1. **Edric's four days** (Book 1 ch 1): he "lasted four days" after his hound died. The bond rule is death on the third day.
2. **"A breath. Less."** (Book 2 ch 34): the Old Reader says a dragon whose partner dies goes almost at once. Morrow lasts three days after Hallam (Book 1 chs 60, 62).
3. **Aurveth called Clan-bred** (Book 3 ch 5, Gorm's ledger, "Bred on the high ledges… bonded three times to riders of the Clan"; also the Bestiary). World.md and Book 1 chs 8 and 26 say she is older than the Clan, and Ennis wasn't a Clan rider.
4. **The yearling's eyes** (Book 2 ch 62): "open since the autumn"; chs 43 and 52 have them half shut in the autumn.

### The Clan and Varrhold
5. **Which caves the Clan hatches in.** World.md and Aurveth (Book 2 ch 12) treat the caves above Coldtarn as the Clan's hatching ground; Book 1 ch 40, Book 2 ch 12 and Atlas.md say they've been unused for a hundred years.
6. **The caves' side of Coldtarn** (Book 3 ch 85): "southern side"; World.md and the maps say the eastern rim.
7. **The pens** (Book 2 ch 12): "open and empty" in the autumn; ch 21 and Book 3 ch 18 imply the young live there until chosen.
8. **Two pours at the black water** (Book 3 ch 5): the second pour (Book 2 ch 78) was into the sea at Gullhaven.
9. **Teo's age at the Choosing Ledge** (Book 3 ch 18): "the next year" after Arn's sixteenth summer would make Teo about twelve, not fifteen.
10. **"Three days" split** (Book 1 ch 6): by then the hall has been split about a week.
11. **Where Teo was laid out** (Book 1 ch 54): the ford-house; ch 14 lays him on the bank stones.

### Places
12. **The far shore of Glasswater** (Book 2 chs 8, 37): the High Crown above the far shore; Atlas.md and chs 28, 32 put Vael on the north shore, so the far shore is the south shore under the Iron Fells.
13. **The fords above Ashford:** World.md says north-east of Ashford; the map has them south-east.
14. **The ledge chains** (Book 2 ch 4): hanging from rings set in the ledge; Book 1 chs 77–78 have them coming down from high above.
15. **Stell's landing** (Bestiary): "the far landing" of Nessa's ferry; the books only say "the far shore villages".

### Timing and counts
16. **The lights in the east** (Book 3 ch 2): "every night since" the six went up; ch 1 and the rest of ch 2 start them on night 4.
17. **Orrun's shadow at the Moot** (Book 2 ch 25): "half an hour", but it lasts from a morning start until noon.
18. **Refugees under the wall** (Book 2 chs 43, 45): Liss ferries "the rest" over before the flood, but Maren later pictures a hundred still waiting.
19. **Where Joss went** (Book 2 ch 11): Bel says the Old Ruin in the autumn; Book 1 ch 50 has the Ewe Beck camp in deep winter.
20. **The reef-stone's pier** (Book 3 chs 13, 17): "last pier" in the prose, "nearest" in the notes, "first pier" in ch 17.
21. **Dragons at Blackwater** (Book 3 ch 13): the farmer's "three dragons of his own"; in Book 2 ch 56 Aurveth stayed home. The total is nine either way.
22. **Olifants** (Book 3 chs 8, 46, 53): only the three royal olifants are left at Solenne, then forty and twenty more arrive with no source.
23. **Notes against prose:** Book 3 ch 38 (rocs "from the Worldsend" against "out of the east, along the coast"); Book 3 ch 7 ("a day east" against two days' walk); Book 2 ch 25 (the waking "a league into the forest" against a league from the Moot).

### Small and descriptive
24. **The black book's pages:** "old paper" (Book 1 ch 53) against "something older than paper" (Book 2 ch 4).
25. **Gannet's build:** "long, thin" in Book 1, "thick" in Book 3.
26. **The College lamp colours** (Book 2 chs 3, 8 against chs 31, 36): different colleges, names and colours.
