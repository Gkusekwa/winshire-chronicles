# Ringless: canon check

**Checked:** Book 1 ch 9, 45, 47, 57, 63 (Callis's arm), 65, 69, 71 (barricade, word for word), 75, 77, 79. Book 2 ch 2, 4, 5, 6, 12, 14, 15, 20, 22, 25, 28, 37, 40–46 (notes and the relevant prose; ch 45 at Tobin's arrival word for word). Atlas.md and the atlas data file (`Tools/atlas/world.py`) for the fords, Ashford, the Brackenwood, Hallowmere, the Scald and Greyhold. Grepped for Tobin, Kemble, Ludd, Ewan, Pollard, Rowan's leg, Davin, Fennick, the fords, the refugees, the ring, days 142–143 and Book 2 days 4, 8, 98, 105, 182, 232–233.

**Fixed:**
1. The Millbrook camp. Old: "where the tents had been all spring… The ones who'd come over on the ferry in the thaw". New: "since the winter… on the ferry and the ice". Book 1 ch 65 (Liss): "When the ice was in I walked them over on a rope… They've a camp at Millbrook". The thaw is night 119.
2. Rowan in the yard. Old: "She'd read the council's order to them in the snow, the day Davin was taken… *That is the order. It is not mine. I don't make it.*" New: "and heard her read the orders down to them in her quiet voice…". Book 1 ch 47: the order is read on day 93, and Tobin had been gone "since supper on midwinter's eve" (day 87).
3. The fords to Ashford (prose and Decided here). Old: "two days' walk" / "(two days on foot)". New: "three days'" / "(three days on foot)". On the atlas map, fords on the Wend below the Brackenwood are about 21–23 leagues straight from Ashford (155,105), so about 27 by road, or 3½ days at Atlas's 8 a day.
4. Ewan at the Ring. Old: Maren "knocks them off into the beck, one, two". New: "knocks one off into the beck, and the next two get down off theirs sooner than meet the lion". Book 2 ch 20: she takes Pask into the beck, and two Wardens "step off rather than face Orrun". The tale says Ewan "wasn't lying" and saw it, so his account has to match.
5. Ewan's ring. Old: "On the plains." New: "In the forest." The Hollow Ring is "a clearing deep in the Thornwood" (Atlas; Book 2 ch 20).
6. Ewan and Maren (two places). Old: "mucked out beside Maren every morning for a year"; "mucked out beside her for a year". New: "forked hay at the far end every morning of the winter Maren mucked it out alone"; "worked the same barn as her all winter". Book 1 ch 25: "Nobody helped her… She did it alone". She was on the midden for about three months, not a year.
7. The refugees under Greyhold's wall after the flood. Old: "Hundreds of them… the ones the crown had ordered back over the river and nobody had ferried back." New: "A hundred and more… the last of the ones the crown had ordered back over the river, that the ferry hadn't got across before the water." Book 2 ch 43: "A hundred and more"; Liss ferried them all summer, and her last crossing of forty was driven back onto the Greyhold jetty.
8. Added the canon-checked line.

**Defensible:**
- The barricade (Book 1 ch 71): every quoted line of Tobin's and Maren's matches, from "No. Let them by." to "Go on. Get Tam home." The tale summarises the middle speech ("He told her the rest… Since the summer, since before Upper Rush; Reede; the inner circle and the better names; the frost fair; her") and then gives its last lines verbatim.
- "When they throw you out of the gate with nothing, come and find us. We'll still be here." This is Book 1 ch 57 word for word.
- The ring on midwinter's eve fits ch 47 (Tobin gone since supper on midwinter's eve). "Gone two months" by Book 2 day 4 fits too.
- Cassian hung "in chains, by the arms, with a board", THE RESTORATION'S LEADER, from day 3 (Book 2 ch 4). Tobin sees it on day 4. The dead (Thorne with her boar at the gate, Hale by the archive door with a sword-cut, Haverly an hour on the stair, Kesh by a bolt and he by a spear) all match Book 1 ch 75.
- "The eighth night" (Tobin's mock report) is Book 2 ch 6's night 8.
- The companies cross in early summer while everyone watches the Ring (Book 2 ch 43). Ashford is held by the companies. Rowan is "back from the Ring in disgrace, sent to hold the Downs" (ch 43, 46). The leg is broken below the knee and knitting under Keepers' moss (ch 22), which fits her walking with a stick and splints in high summer.
- The Vael Pass snowed at the end of summer (Book 2 ch 37, days 173–176). The flood had passed Greyhold by day 182, and the new rope went up on day 189 (ch 43), which fits "a coil of new rope". Two dragons warned the valley, "a man on the red one" (ch 43). The Dye Street wall is ch 37.
- Lisska goes west at dusk on 232. The Titan stands at noon on 233, puts its head down, lays the egg on its back and goes west. The green lamps go white at dusk, the labourers go mad in the ditches, Tobin comes in at the east gate with the rope off the gatehouse rail, Maren is in sacking-wrapped boots, and Tobin's boots are tied with string. All of this matches Book 2 ch 45.
- "A hundred leagues and more" from the Marches to the Scald: on the atlas the straight line is about 89 leagues, so about 111 by road.
- The Moot "half a morning" (Ewan, from the clans' talk). The book says the shadow held "half an hour" by Idony's count, but also that it went at noon. This is hearsay in the tale, so I left it.

**Book-side issues:**
- World.md ("Settled in drafting the Tales") puts the fords above Ashford "north-east of Ashford". The atlas map puts the Wend below the Brackenwood south-east of Ashford, and every chapter that places the fords (downriver of the Brackenwood, on the Downs' eastern edge) agrees with the map. One of the two needs changing.
- Book 2 ch 43 vs ch 45: in ch 43 Liss takes "the rest" of the refugees over before the flood, while in ch 45 (day 232) Maren pictures "a hundred people… waiting since the spring". The tale follows ch 43's events: the last forty were driven back onto the Hearthmere jetty.
- Book 2 ch 25: Orrun's shadow lasts "half an hour" (Idony), but the ground opens in the morning and "The far Orrun went at noon".

**Verdict:** fixed (7 fixes).
