# Canon check: Skell

**Checked:** Book 2 ch 21 (in full), 24 (notes and prose on Tor, Skell, the bier and pour), 10 (notes; the cry going up; Gorm limping "from the ford"), 12 (the old caves, the pens), 33 and 38 (hatching-bowl and mother-hum lines); Book 1 ch 40 and 66 (the old hatching caves), 48 (eleven pairs, the steward), 60 (in full) and 62 (vigil and carrying). Book 3 ch 18, 45, 50, 51 (pens, caves, Gunna and Tamsin). Bible: World.md, Characters.md, Atlas.md, Bestiary.md. Grepped the books for Skell, Tor, Hallam, Morrow, Gunna, Tamsin, Gorm, Leif, eggs/hatching/pens, and days 98, 127–132.

**Fixed:**
1. The hatching. Old: "That was the caves above Coldtarn, on the eastern rim, where the Clan keeps its eggs..." New: "That was the hatching caves under the hold, the ones the Clan cut a hundred years ago, where it keeps its eggs now." Book 1 ch 40, Aurveth: "The old hatching caves. The Clan used them before they built the new ones under the hold, a hundred years ago, and nobody has been in them since." Book 2 ch 12: the hollows "were empty... A hundred years, Gunna had said." Atlas: "unused for a hundred years". Skell, hatched about four summers before Book 2, can't have hatched there. Follow-on lines changed with it:
   - "The bowls were worn smooth. Dragons had lain in them since before there were people to cut channels." → "The bowls had been cut by men, with warm sand in them." (Book 2 ch 33: "an egg in the warm sand of a hatching bowl".)
   - The drop "all the way down to the black water. The tarn. It lay below the caves' mouth..." → "all the way down to the glen."
   - The boat: "The black water under the caves. Coldtarn." → "The black water above the hold, that he'd flown over with the others in the spring. Coldtarn." And in the smoke, "The black water under the caves" → "The black water above the hold".
   - Decided-here line updated.
2. The steward in the winter before the ford: "came up the stair from the yard, limping, and stood" → "came up the stair from the yard and stood". Gorm Askell is hurt at the second ford (Book 2 ch 10: "limping. He'd been hurt at the ford"; Characters.md: "Hurt at the ford (60)").
3. Tor at his choosing: "Not in the line. Behind it. He'd been pushed out of it by the crowding, or he'd stepped out of it to see." → "At the very end of the line, and a step out of it. He'd been pushed by the crowding, or he'd stepped out to see." Book 2 ch 21: "He'd stood in it two midsummers ago, at seventeen, and a young blue called Skell had walked up the ledge and stopped in front of him and stayed."
4. Notes: added "Canon-checked against the books: 29 Oct 2026."

**Defensible:**
- Timeline: chosen at seventeen, two midsummers before Book 2's (Tor nineteen, ch 21). Haldor's burning in the autumn after his first bonded midsummer, then Teo's. The winter the ledges didn't fly, the ford on Book 1 day 130, Morrow's death at dusk on 132 ("the third day"), and Aurveth home on Book 2 day 12 ("less than a month") all agree.
- The ford: eleven in a row by the burned shieling; Kharrak and Aurveth (not Lisska, who is on the Ram's Head road) on the ridge; the ten not following "till I fell"; the nine shapes, with Gunna staying. The tale splits Morrow's message into two utterances; the words match Aurveth's relay in Book 1 ch 60.
- The midsummer night matches ch 21 throughout: forty young and thirty in the line, the girl from Skarrow, smoke an hour after dark from the north-east, the ropes and grapnels, the wyverns, Tamsin and the pot, the straggler, the axe, the open hand, the bolt, the head across his knees. The tale's "big young bronze" before the girl is never said to be the taken bronze.
- Tor's "It's the old ones that get three days" echoes Book 1 ch 62 (Ada). His "if the dragon goes first... it's a breath" is a rider's belief about dragons, borne out in ch 21. Ide's three days after Bracken (Book 1 ch 39) is a hound bond.
- Hatchlings brought down to the pens in autumn and young kept in pens until the choosing: ch 21 has the young "out of the pens". See the book-side note below.

**Book-side issues:**
- World.md ("How the Clan keeps its dragons") says the best hatching places "are the hatching caves above Coldtarn" and the Clan holds them, and Aurveth in Book 2 ch 12 says the same in the present tense. Book 1 ch 40, Book 2 ch 12 and Atlas.md say those caves have been unused a hundred years, in favour of new ones under the hold. The Bible should say which caves the Clan hatches in now.
- Book 2 ch 12: "In the autumn they'd been open and empty" (the pens). Ch 21 and Book 3 ch 18 imply young dragons live in the pens until they are chosen. Either the young have separate pens or the autumn line needs a word.
- Book 2 ch 34, the Old Reader: "A dragon whose partner dies goes with him... A breath. Less." This contradicts Morrow's three days after Hallam (Book 1 ch 60, 62) and World.md's examples. Ch 21's "a breath" is the other direction (the dragon first).

**Verdict:** fixed
