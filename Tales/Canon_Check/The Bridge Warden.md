# Canon check: The Bridge Warden

**Checked:** Book 1 chs 26, 28, 38, 62, 66, 70, 74 (the chains), 78, 79 notes; Book 2 chs 21, 34 (in full for the history), 41, 59, 64; Book 3 ch 5, 81; World.md (the Oneroad, the capital, Aurveth ruling), Atlas.md (Oneroad, Old Ruin 3.5 leagues above Greyhold, travel table, `world.py` ONEROAD line), Magic.md, Story.md, Bestiary. Grepped for Ennis, Aurveth, Aldric, the cat, the bridge, milestones, "nineteen", binders, honey-smoke.

**Fixed:**
1. Prose, the traffic "From the west, from the march-land and the hills beyond it and the long road from the south, a train of stone-carts" → "From the west, from the march-land and the hills beyond it, a train of stone-carts". The south road reaches the island from the Saltreach side: its "Nineteen to the bridge" stone is in the Wendwood on the east bank (Book 1 ch 38: "the old drove road up the east bank of the Wend, through the Wendwood"; Atlas: the Wendwood along the east bank).
2. Prose, the lord's carts: "iron: bar, and long links of chain, each link as long as a man's forearm, thick as a wrist" → "iron: bar and pig, and coils of lesser chain for the nets". The great chains are "each link taller than a man" (Book 1 ch 74 notes; ch 79 "links taller than men") and were "Forged in the cavern by the lord's smiths over a whole winter" (Book 2 ch 34). Carried down the stair "two men to a length of chain" → "two men to a bar", and "with the chain between them… the clink of the chain" → "with the bar between them… the clink of the iron".
3. Notes, Decided here: "with chains on carts" → "with iron on carts (bar for the winter's forging below, not finished chain)".

**Defensible:**
- Book 2 ch 34's bird: "A hundred men with fire on poles". The tale has about a hundred men in all, forty on the pole-pots. The bird is giving the archivist's round number for the company that drove the cat; left as is, but the author may prefer "a hundred" with fire.
- Book 1 ch 70 (Aurveth: "I was in the air over the bridge… I couldn't get down to him") vs the tale (she comes down and is netted): ch 70 is her broken memory, hedged "I think"; Book 2 chs 21 and 34 (smoked three days on the bridge, cut "with a knife and a word") agree with the tale.
- "Younger than Lisska" (Book 1 ch 38) at the Wendwood stone: in the tale she's about twelve then; Lisska is about twelve or thirteen at the mill. Close enough for a memory.
- The fort's gates: the tale has a west and an east gate and the north road coming into the round (Book 2 ch 41's six roads); Book 1 ch 66's "two gates" is the ruin as it stands now.
- Bible sweep facts (caves, hounds, moon, funerals, Redwatch) don't touch this tale. Nothing names the cat, the sleepers or the elders.

**Book-side issues:**
- Book 3 ch 5: Gorm's record reads "Aurveth. Bred on the high ledges. In Rurik's grandfather's time. Fed by the Clan… bonded three times to riders of the Clan." World.md (Settled in drafting the Tales) says she is older than the Clan and wasn't bred by it, and Ennis wasn't a Clan rider (Book 2 ch 34). Either the record is meant to be wrong (say so in the prose) or the line needs softening. The Bestiary also still calls her Clan-bred.
- Book 2 ch 34 has the chains both forged in the cavern "over a whole winter" and brought "with the chains" to the door when the cat was driven off. The tale now reads iron in at summer's end and the forging after; the book could say the same.

**Verdict:** fixed (3 changes, one of them in notes).
