# Canon check: The Covered Chair

**Checked:** read in full Book 3 chs 2 and 5; Book 2 ch 88 (the six on the moor, the go-up, the ream of paper) and ch 78 (the burning, the pour, the salt-store page); the relevant parts of Book 2 chs 10 (Arn at the gate by torchlight), 21, 24, 46, 51, 62; Book 3 chs 1, 3, 4 (the lights), 18 (the third ledge, the chair), 22 and 85 (Coldtarn, the caves); Book 1 chs 6, 8, 14, 16 (read for Ford Water). Bible: World.md (the hall, the grey cloth, the caves six leagues off, Ildra), Atlas.md and the atlas data (Coldtarn, the caves, the hall), Characters.md, the sweep. Grepped the books for Arn, Ulric, Gorm, Haki, Skerry, Ildra, Bjarke, Hulda Sarr, Brede, Leir, Gunna, Sigge Brack, Ivar Leck, Tor Brecc, the chair, the lights, the hum, quires and tally-paper.

**Fixed:**
1. The go-up, dusk day 0: the flattened heather reached "the ling on the slope below the Roosts" (Varrhold is five leagues and more off) → "a league of it, until the heather stood again and it was only air". Book 2 ch 88: "The heather flattening under them for a league in every direction". The tale itself says later "The heather was down for a league."
2. The sitting at the chair, day 2: the imagined quarrel, the red "left them the dead to burn" → "left them the last of Gullhaven to fly". Book 2 ch 78: the burning was at dusk on 350, both halves present; Arn left first on 352, and Ulric's half stayed "to fly the last of Gullhaven's people to the salt-store hill farms".
3. Gorm in the tally-house: "I've told your father so for twenty years" → "for ten years". Book 1 ch 16: Gorm "who'd counted Harrow's End's barley sacks at the hold every autumn for ten years".
4. Notes block: added "Canon-checked against the books: 29 Oct 2026." No Decided-here line changed.

**Defensible:**
- Lights on night 3 ("a glow", no hum) and the white sky and hum from night 4: matches Book 3 ch 2 ("on the fourth night, they were more than a glow"; the dragons humming again). See book-side note on the first nights.
- Wren calling Ildra across the tarn from the caves: the prose of every book treats Coldtarn as a bowl you can see across (Book 3 ch 85 "Across Coldtarn's bowl"), though the atlas draws it about three leagues wide.
- Arn "on his red off the high ledge": he rides behind its rider, as the sentence before says; it doesn't make him bonded.
- Gorm's quires "at midwinter for his letters to the lion-woman": Book 2 ch 51's letter is on Clan tally-paper, and Book 2 ch 88 has him writing to Maren a year before.
- Ildra following Arn "since the winter": Book 3 ch 2 says only "every time he came up"; she woke in Book 2 ch 62, late winter.
- The scenes shown in Book 3 chs 2 and 5 (the cave table, the riders told on day 8, the pens, the rider at noon, the stair and Wren, the flights, dusk 10, the doorway, days 11-12) agree in speech, order and people; the tale's additions (Wren and Ada leaving on day 11, the stall with Ulric) don't touch anything the books show.
- Nothing past day 12 is revealed: no voice from Ildra, no choosing, no pens opened, no Ulric's death.

**Book-side issues:**
- Book 3 ch 5: "two pours at the black water" for Arn and Ulric's two pours, but the second (Book 2 ch 78) was off the Gullhaven jetty into the sea. The first was at Coldtarn (Book 2 ch 24).
- Book 3 ch 85: the hatching caves are "Across Coldtarn's bowl, on its southern side"; World.md and every map put them on the eastern rim.
- Lights: Book 3 ch 2 has Calder think on day 3 that "Every night since the six went up off the moor the east had lit white at midnight", while Book 3 ch 1 (notes and prose) has them first seen on night 4 and ch 2 itself has only "a glow" before night 4.
- Book 3 ch 5's ledger has Aurveth "Bred on the high ledges. In Rurik's grandfather's time", but World.md says she is older than the Clan and wasn't bred by it. Readable as Gorm's books being wrong; the author may want that said.

**Verdict:** fixed (3 fixes).
