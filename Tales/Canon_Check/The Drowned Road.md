# Canon check: The Drowned Road

**Checked:** Book 3 ch 13, 14, 24, 25 in full; notes and the relevant prose of ch 16, 17, 20, 21, 27, 29, 60 and ch 1 (the lights). Book 2 ch 49 and 56 (notes and the relevant prose), ch 75 (drawback timing), ch 78 (290 drowned). Book 1 ch 7 (Gannet). Bible: World.md (the fixed rulings and the Tales block: piers counted down, the ninth, Gannet and the Teeth, Gullhaven and the Bell), Characters.md, Bestiary.md (reefwyrms), Atlas.md and the atlas data (Blackwater, Wendmouth, Gullhaven, the Bell reef, P41). Grepped the books and Bible for Merrin, Kittiwake, Kit, Jory, Hal, Wenna, Abram, Gannet, Fosse, Sallow, Corrie, Ferris, Lorn, Tansy, Nye, Sim, Teeth, tooth, Needle, the ninth, Saffery, and the day numbers 60, 62, 66, 76, 100, 242, 297–301, 349.

**Fixed:**
1. The Shatters, the last turn (the "At noon on the second day" scene). "At noon on the second day they came to the tooth. / A rock like a broken tooth, with a second rock behind it like its shadow, and a channel between the two…" became "At sunset on the second day they came to the last tooth. / A rock like a broken tooth, standing up alone out of the white water, and a channel beside it…"; "went through between the tooth and its shadow" became "went through by the tooth"; the reefwyrms "that afternoon" became "that evening", and "dropped back at dusk" became "at dark". Book 3 ch 21 makes the tooth and its shadow the *first* turn, at noon on the first day: "At noon Kit found the first turn. A rock like a broken tooth, with a second rock behind it like its shadow…". Ch 24 puts the end of the reef at sunset the evening before the first pier: "the last tooth of rock gone astern at sunset". The notes' Depends-on entries for ch 21 and 24 are updated to match.
2. Hal's drowning, the road home. "He came up from the ballast on the first morning going back and stood at her elbow" became "On the first morning going back he put his chin on the hatch-coaming over the ballast". Book 3 ch 13: Jory "sat on the stone ballast in the hold the whole way and didn't cry, and read her the milestones going back".
3. The flake sale. "She took the flakes to Wendmouth at the end of the summer." became "…late in the autumn." Sallow says the Halloran fall at Corsa was "last week", and that was Book 2 day 242 (ch 49). With midwinter at day 281, that is late autumn: in ch 49, Calder decides on day 248 to go up to Varrhold "before winter".
4. The boathouse raid. "A man went into the water off the shed's sea-door." became "…through the shed's side." Book 2 ch 56: Tamsin's tail sent the binder "off the boards and through the side of the shed and out into the black water".
5. Notes: added "Canon-checked against the books: 29 Oct 2026."

**Defensible:**
- The crossing count and ages agree with the books. Kit was on six crossings with Hal and two with Jory (ch 13), and this tale's crossing is the eighth (ch 25: "She'd seen it eight times"). Hal drowned eleven years before Book 3, when Jory was eight; Jory is 14 on the seventh crossing and 18 on the eighth, and 19 in Book 3. Kit is 28 at the drowning, so about 39–40 in Book 3. Corrie is 19, taken on "last year" (ch 21 has her at 20). Sim and Nye are 16 and 15, a year later 17 and 16 (ch 21, 29).
- The days at sea agree with Book 3 ch 24–25 and World.md: first pier on day 3, the ninth on day 16, the light on night 20, the last pier on day 21. The Needle has six fathoms at the top of the flood, the shore is four leagues off and they anchor a league off on four fathoms. Hal's longest stay was six days.
- Day 76 dusk agrees with Book 3 ch 14: the shout, the ladders, the ships standing off, the sellswords walking backwards, the lion among the fires, the old man helped down. Corrie rowing out to the *Kittiwake* doesn't break ch 14's "Nobody came that night", which is from Maren's side. Dabb is new and touches nothing in ch 14.
- The Blackwater raid agrees with Book 2 ch 56: nine dragons (Kharrak, Lisska, Tamsin and six of the ledges; Aurveth stayed home), dawn 297 from the landward side, wet cloths, Lisska singing with her cloth off, the bronze burned at dusk, Calder writing on night 299, Lisska west at dawn 300, the rest gone on 301.
- The Bell at the end of the winter, the drawback at noon, the 290 names and the spit gone all agree with Book 2 ch 75 and 78. The lights over the Worldsend come "in the first warm days" (Book 3 ch 1, days 4–5). The tale tells Kit's month of looking for salvors before the lights, but that month can overlap them.
- The Ostrey paper agrees with ch 13: signed at the end of the winter, presented on day 62, due on the hundredth. Its wording is quoted word for word. Day 76 is twenty-four days from the hundredth.
- Gannet agrees with World.md and Book 3 ch 60: one salvage summer at the Teeth, and he came east in the autumn. The smugglers' guess that "all six" Titans went into the Worldsend is rumour, matches Book 2 ch 88, and says nothing past Book 3's opening.
- The marks are never read, and the Lit Shore and the light are never named. The Council of Immortals is never named.

**Book-side issues:**
- Gannet's build. Book 1 (Characters.md: "Long, thin") and Book 3 ch 13–14 ("Thick. Fifty, perhaps"; "older. Heavier") are only two years apart. The tale bridges this with "Wendmouth fed me", but between Book 1 and Book 3 the books themselves jump.
- Where the reef-stone came from. Book 3 ch 13 prose has it dropped from "the drowned road's last pier", while ch 13's notes say "nearest standing pier" and ch 17 says the "first pier". The tale's Gatepost sits at the channel head and avoids the question.
- Book 3 ch 13 prose credits the farmer at Blackwater with "three dragons of his own and six of the Clan's", but Book 2 ch 56 leaves Aurveth at home: he had two of his own and seven of the Clan's. The total is nine either way.

**Verdict:** fixed (4 fixes, plus the checked line).
