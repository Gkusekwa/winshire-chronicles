**Defensible:**
- What Hale sees below is word for word with Book 1 ch 49. The stair is "longer than… the hill could hold" and "worn in the middle like the stairs in a chapel". On the ledge, a link is taller than he is, with "rust gone hard on it like bark". Six chains are counted "before Merrow lowered the lantern". The dark moves, there is one breath, the six chains move a hand's breadth with the hill, and Merrow says "That's what you're keeping, lad." The vast attention comes only through the door (ch 51), and the Must not reveal holds: no knight's lock, no second key, no margin signs, no drawing on the fourth stub.
- The tale's chains run from high above, past the ledge, down into the dark. This matches Book 1 ch 77 ("going down past them into the dark from somewhere high above") and ch 78. Book 2 ch 4 differs (see below).
- Merrow turns the key, Hale goes through, and Hale locks it after. This fits ch 51 and World.md's Watch-list ruling ("the new Watcher locks it behind them"). Writing his name in the old script, last of the list, fits Book 2 ch 4.
- Merrow is past fifty and grey as a badger, Dun is in the yard because he won't stay in the barn, Merrow sits across from Hale and "told me what I'd be swearing to", and Merrow's wife doesn't know. All ch 37. Grieve has two years at Greyhold here, which fits ch 37's "forty years before" Maren's arrival.
- The carved line over the door as the first archivist's joke, with Hale laughing at it: ch 35 ("it had taken him thirty years to understand it wasn't").
- Hale never looks at the bottom of the gutter: ch 53 ("never once looked at the bottom of the fourth").
- The Watch's name for the sleeper, "The Last Titan", and the lullaby lines: ch 37.
- The stubs are "the old vellum" in the tale. Book 2 ch 4 says the black book's pages are "something older than paper", while ch 53 calls the stubs "old paper". The tale agrees with Book 2.
- "All the hands of three hundred years": Book 2 ch 4 itself says "three hundred years of hooked script".
- Ch 75 has Hale read the lintel ward "the day I came to the archive". The tale doesn't say he didn't. It only shows him reading the keyhole ward on his first night.

**Book-side issues:**
- Chain geometry: Book 1 ch 77 and 78 have the six chains coming from high above past the ledge and on down. Book 2 ch 4 has them "going down from great iron rings in the ledge… and coming up again… on the far side", with the rings still there "in a row along its edge". These can't both be true of the same ledge. The tale follows Book 1.
- The black book's material: Book 1 ch 53 says "old paper", Book 2 ch 4 "something older than paper".

**Verdict:** fixed (1 fix).
