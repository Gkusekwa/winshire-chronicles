# Canon check: The Last Crossing of Winter

**Checked:** Book 1 chs 1, 3, 11, 47, 51, 63, 64, 69, 71 (in full), 75 (notes and Hale's scene), 79; Book 2 chs 2, 4, 5, 6, 77, 79; World.md (Tam and Odo ruling; Greyhold's dead; Redwatch on the bank), Characters.md, Atlas.md (Greyhold–Ruin 3.5, Millbrook 2). Grepped for Liss, Tam, Odo, Hale, Joby Marsh, Mother Ketch, Merl/Peg Dowe, Gil Mossop, Tolly, Greta, Edric, Callis, Thorne, Rowan, Abe/Fisk's boy, the water-gate, nights 141–144 and the moon.

**Fixed:**
1. Prose, Mother Ketch: "And my two boys. Off the flats, one winter." → "And my two boys. Off the eel-weir, in the flood, thirty years back." Book 2 ch 79: "It took my man. And my two boys. Off the eel-weir, in the flood, thirty years back."
2. Prose, Hale at the grille: "an old man, broad, bigger than she'd thought, with a white beard spread on his chest" → "an old man, tall and stooped, taller than she'd thought, with a short white beard". Characters.md: "Past sixty, tall, stooped, short white beard, bad hip, ash stick."

**Defensible:**
- Night 142–day 143 (ch 71): lantern, Tam, Wren's words at the red door, the lamp flaring, the burn-word, "That's not right", "For me. They know my boat", Maren's message and "Get her some bread" all agree word for word.
- Hale (ch 75: "At dusk. In her boat. Shouting for me by name… Rowan didn't believe it. Callis was on the gate with her that minute"): the tale has Liss shouting from after noon till dusk and Joby refusing to name Callis to Rowan with Callis beside her. Fits.
- The moon: night 143 is a few days before the full moon of about day 146; the tale puts thick cloud and Millbrook's smoke over it, so the ferry still crosses "by feel" (Book 2 ch 6).
- "Three leagues up" for the island and Redwatch: the Atlas says 3.5 straight, and Book 1 chs 3, 63, 69 say "three leagues". Matches the prose.
- Edric "dead within the week": Book 1 ch 1 has four days. Buried "in one hole at the end of the long field": Book 2 ch 4.
- The river running backward at Greyhold after the island falls: ch 79 has the Wend "pouring into it from both channels", so below the hole it runs upstream. This isn't the Book 3 hub reversal.
- Joby's grandson eleven here, twelve a year later (Book 2 ch 79). Lisska over the landing straight after the lion: ch 75 has her over Orrun on the run.
- Tam takes Odo down at dusk day 143 and brings him up on Book 2 day 1: per World.md's ruling.

**Book-side issues:**
- Book 1 ch 1: Edric "lasted four days" after his hound died, against the bond rule that the partner dies on the third day (World.md; Ide, Morrow). The tale's "within the week" doesn't press it, but the chapter may want "three days".

**Verdict:** fixed (2 changes).
