# Canon check: The Last Lane

**Checked:** Book 2 ch 25 (in full), 13, 15, 18, 22 and 27 (notes plus the Hesper passages: the knot, the moss, the bag, the lamps), 23 (the carving, the saying, the torch), 26 (the news at Varrhold); Book 1 ch 73 (the knowe); Book 3 ch 7 (Rhun). Bible: World.md (Lake Ysse, the Moot), Atlas.md, Bestiary. Grepped the books for Hesper, Hush, Aun, Morwen, Tolly-Bright, Rhun, Sedge, the lanes, the lamps, and days 31, 84–85, 99–106.

**Fixed:**
1. "She'd heard him roar before. On the knowe, the knight said, and at a gate" → "On an island, the knight said, the night his collar came off, and at a gate". Book 2 ch 25: "She'd heard him roar on the island, on the night the collar came off. She'd heard him roar at Greyhold's gate." Orrun doesn't roar at Hollin Knowe in Book 1 ch 73.
2. Notes: added "Canon-checked against the books: 29 Oct 2026."

**Defensible:**
- Everything shared with ch 25 agrees, word for word where the book quotes it: Aun's speech and Idony going pink; the boats, rafts and roof; the slope and the seven lanes; "A fire... We stood in the lanes" / "You stand in a lane..."; sixty tame stonehorns three deep in the western lanes; eleven bears and the pale one in the third lane; duskcats on the ridges; "Those." / "The near one. And the one past it."; the seventh-lane exchange ("That one's mine" through "If they don't, they don't"); barefoot up the lane at dusk; the birds at dawn; the full of the morning; the flood; the second lion in the sixth lane; the bull, the roundhouse, and the saying sung in the clans' tongue with the last of it in Maren's. The tale's extra beasts before the bull, and the Drinking Arm, are additions the book doesn't contradict. Leans-Left is "at the front" of the wave that turns all at once.
- Where the stag rises: ch 25's notes say "a league into the forest", but its prose says "rising out of the Thornwood a league off", and ch 27 puts Dane's lamps "on the forest's edge, a league north, where your glass said." The tale's stag rising over the three sparks at the edge follows ch 27 and World.md (edge about a league behind the Moot).
- Times and counts: seventy days with Maren (met night 31, now 104); twenty years alone (ch 13), Hush in her ninth winter, bonded eleven years; "for the last six days" (surge from dawn 99); Rowan's leg "four days ago... began to knit" (moss evening 99, knitting by morning 100); four hundred people at the Stones; the south stone, the hair as long as her arm, eleven in and nineteen out, the knot put in her coat with her left hand on day 85 (ch 18). The bag goes from Aun's door-post to Maren (ch 27). Lisska sniffing Hesper's hair, "it smells like the woods" (ch 15). Rhun's memory of the dream matches Book 3 ch 7, and Rhun isn't at the Moot.
- Must not reveal: the beast is only "the Old Stag", and nothing is said of the six beyond the carving and the saying.

**Book-side issues:** none found. (A minor one: ch 25's notes place the waking "a league into the forest", while its prose and ch 27 put it about a league from the Moot at the forest's edge.)

**Verdict:** fixed
