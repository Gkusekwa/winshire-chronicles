# Canon check: The Night the Glasses Broke

**Checked:** Book 2 ch 3 and 8 in full (ch 8's day-19 crossing word for word against the tale's end), ch 29 (Nessa, Tib, Mother Talley, the salt) and ch 36, 40 and 41 (later facts that must not be contradicted). I also searched all three books and the Bible for Nessa, Tib, Mother Talley, Pell, Ruddock, Tern End, Wendhead, Rope Street, the *Heron*, the Lady, salt and the College colours, and for days 0–19. Against the Bible I checked Bestiary (lantern owls, stormkites), World.md (Glasswater's outflow to Wendhead) and Atlas.md, plus the map (Wendhead, the Greenwold road's ford). The Plan entry (11) and the tale's Must not reveal are respected: Nessa throws no salt, there's nothing of the Coil, and Idony is seen only from outside.

**Fixed:**
1. The night, from the kitchen window: "The long grey roof of the Library on the hill's shoulder" → "along the top of the hill". Book 2 ch 28: "at the top of the hill, cut into it, a long low front of stone"; ch 38: "the Library's long slate roof running half a league along the hill's top". The "south shoulder" is where the College of Tides stands (ch 39).
2. Notes block: added "Canon-checked against the books: 29 Oct 2026."

**Defensible:**
- **The night.** This matches ch 3 and 8. Lamps go white at dusk, the ferry lamps and the quays too, and the observatory owls blaze on their ledges facing south. The Gallery breaks at midnight. Nessa sits up with the owl in the dark kitchen under a blanket ("dusk to dawn" is Nessa's own telling in ch 8). Stormkites come down in pairs before sunrise, a ferry goes out with its owl at dawn, and the porters put out lamps that are their own colours again.
- **Day 2.** This matches ch 3. It's a mild morning with the snow going off the roofs, the lake flat, black, very still. Idony is in his winter coat buttoned to the neck at the foot of the College stair. The owl is ordinary amber. Ledgers and crate are taken that morning.
- **Day 19.** Ch 8's lines are word for word. The tale has Nessa see him "from halfway in", where ch 8 (Idony's POV) says she "looked up and saw him". That doesn't say she hadn't seen him earlier.
- **The glasses' money.** Ch 3 says Pell owed Nessa money; ch 8 says she lent Idony the money three winters ago. The tale reconciles them (Pell's debt worked off in glass, "on my money"), and its "four winters back" for the debt sits before the three winters of glass-making.
- **Ruddock's timing.** From the Greenwold ford up to Wendhead, then up the valley track to Tern End by day 9, is about 50 leagues on foot after the news reached the ford. That fits 8 leagues a day (Atlas).
- **Day 12.** The Council "the day after tomorrow", said on day 10, matches ch 8's day-12 Council.
- **Tern End and Stell.** Ch 29: "the far shore villages". Tern End and Stell (in *Kite-Rider*) can both be *Heron* landings.
- **Note for the prose pass (not a canon fix).** Mags Penner's son-in-law "came down off the Pass road at dusk" with news that the toll-house lamps at the top were white, "a day off". He can't have seen that if he left a day earlier. This is tale-internal and I've left it.

**Book-side issues:**
- **College lamp colours and names.** Book 2 ch 3 (and ch 8, and the tale) gives the Blue College blue, the College of Stars silver, the Physicians green, the Scribes amber. Ch 36 gives the seven colleges as Blue (blue), Scribes (black), Tides (soft green), Measures (violet), Healers (red), Sky-watchers (gold), Keepers of the Script (white), with the Tides' lamps "soft blue" in ch 31 and 36. Ch 28 lists blue, green, gold, red and violet. So "Physicians" vs "Healers", "Stars" vs "Sky-watchers", and the Scribes' amber vs black disagree.
- The LOG's earlier notes stand: Nessa's salt (ch 29 vs ch 40), and Idony's room (ch 3, College roof, vs ch 41, attic over the boat-shed).

**Verdict:** fixed (1 fix, plus the checked line).
