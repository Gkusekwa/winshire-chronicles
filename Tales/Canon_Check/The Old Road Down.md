# Canon check: The Old Road Down

**Checked:** Book 2 ch 63 in full; notes and the relevant prose of ch 55, 57, 58, 60, 61, 86. Book 3 ch 8 (notes and opening prose), plus every olifant passage in Book 3 (ch 41, 46, 48, 53, 55, 63, 65, 70, 71, 77, 80, 85). Bible: Bestiary.md (olifants, tuskers, bond-steeds), World.md (the Sunward Hills ruling), Atlas.md and the atlas data (Solenne, the Gilt, the Sunward Hills, Sorrel Isle, the Titan's track t09–t10). Grepped the books and Bible for olifant, tusk, Bruna, the yard, Sunward Hills, Elderwood, Gilt, Sorrel, bond-steed, the white-bearded rider, and the days 287–318.

**Fixed:**
1. The wild cow's description. "The other long and yellow and uneven, never sawn, never capped, curving a little to the left." became "…never sawn, never capped." Bestiary.md describes olifants as having "straight tusks".
2. The wild cow's line. "back and back through mothers, so many that she lost count of them," became "back and back through mothers, more than she could hold,". "She lost count of them" is too close to the banned "She didn't count them."
3. Notes: added "Canon-checked against the books: 29 Oct 2026."

**Defensible:**
- The tale's timeline agrees with Book 2 ch 58–63: the Titan coming in from the west and lying down on the ridge (about day 300), the lamps going white and cracking in the stir, noon of day 315 (standing up, coming down onto the palace cliff, gone north-west), three days of burning, the heaves, the Horned Sun at dawn on 317 in the west over the palace end, the unicorns that afternoon (the old near-black mare with her horn-tip gone), the boats at dawn on 318, the three olifants turning across the gap by the fishing-stair, the mid-morning slide held by three lions, the heir at noon, the drain going up the Gilt in the afternoon, and the cat, the two hounds and a bond-steed bolting into the vineyards. The dark tower on Sorrel Isle with "a very small yellow light" agrees with ch 63's oil lamp.
- Book 2 never says whether the Titan walked or flew in over Sarovel, so the tale's walking pushes are open.
- The mind-speech rules hold: the cow knows Avo's sounds only as sounds, and the mother's word for "the one who sits" comes from her own chosen bond. "Is small" after the drain matches the riders' bonds going "dull" (ch 63).
- Nobody in the yard has seen a war stand. The stable-owner in ch 63 says it was his grandfather who saw one.
- Where the herd goes is left open, as Must not reveal requires. Book 3 ch 8 has only the three royal olifants in the low city in spring. Ch 46 has "forty of them, up the Sun Road from Solenne". The tale's yard herd of about forty went north along the ridge, which is the line of the Sun Road (World.md), so the two can be squared.
- The tale's notes say the hill voices stopped "the night the Titan lay down", but the prose has them stop as it came walking in from the west, a few days earlier. That is a conflict inside the tale, not with the books, so I left it.

**Book-side issues:**
- Book 3 ch 8 shows only the three royal olifants left at Solenne, and its notes say "the olifants don't come". Yet ch 46 has forty olifants "up the Sun Road from Solenne before it emptied", and ch 53 has twenty more in Perrin Sade's column from Solenne on day 230 (ch 55 counts sixty). The books never say where the extra sixty come from. If the tale's yard herd is meant to be those forty, a line in Book 3 might mark it. That is for the author to decide.

**Verdict:** fixed (2 fixes, plus the checked line).
