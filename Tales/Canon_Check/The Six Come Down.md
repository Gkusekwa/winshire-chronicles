# Canon check: The Six Come Down

**Checked:** Book 3 ch 1, 2, 4 and 5 (notes, plus the ch 4 prose with the horse-clan rider), ch 7 (notes and prose to the fords), ch 38 (the rocs and Yeva), ch 46, 47, 51 and 67 (later Yeva and roc lines); Book 2 ch 13 (the roc over the fire), 15 (the Stones, Lisska's landing, the elders), 17 (Lisska on the roc), 27 (the drain), 88 (notes, the six going up). Bible: World.md, Atlas.md (Plains, Ysse, Worldsend, travel table), Bestiary (rocs, stonehorns). Grepped the books for Yeva, roc, Tova, Rhun, Aun, Dast, the Ysse fords, the high pastures, and days 0–18.

**Fixed:**
1. The drain's grass. Old: "The grass still came up, but it came up grey, and stayed grey, and the mares ate it and got thin on it." New: "The grass still came up green, but there was nothing in it, and the mares ate it and got thin on it, and by the winter it had gone grey." World.md, Settled in drafting the Tales: "The Plains' grass: green after the drain (Book 2 ch 27), grey by the spring of Book 3"; Book 2 ch 27 notes: "The trees stay green and the grass grows, but the Keepers' knots won't hold."
2. Following from it, the next paragraph: "A hundred leagues of grey, from the Thornwood to the western edge" → "A hundred leagues of it, from the Thornwood to the western edge" (she has flown over it "every day since" the summer).
3. Notes: added "Canon-checked against the books: 29 Oct 2026."

**Defensible:**
- Dates: the six cross "three days before the year turned" (Book 2 day 362 = Book 3 day 0, ch 1 notes); lights from night 4 to night 7, none on night 8; the six come down at dawn on day 9 and turn west; Lisska goes east very high and comes back at dusk on day 6 (ch 2); the clans walk from day 9, nine days at four leagues a day, so day 18 is "the tenth morning of the walk" (ch 7).
- "We're two days off it" on day 18, while ch 7's notes say "a day east of the Ysse". Ch 7's prose has Maren walk east from the Ysse for two days (days 16–18) and the crossing begin on day 20, so the tale's "two days" agrees with the prose.
- Dast: sent "when the lights came" (ch 4, "about day 4"; the tale has noon on day 5, the morning after the first lights). Turned at the Greenwold road's ford with the riders, "the council's order. No clans. No herds." (ch 4 quotes the same), then down the Saltreach bank, at the Staithe at noon on day 9. Tova's "I didn't think she'd come... I sent him anyway" echoes ch 7's "I didn't think you would. I sent the boy anyway."
- Tova, Rhun (the broad grey Keeper with a wolf at each knee), the stonehorns the size of a cart, mares two and three to a saddle, ten thousand people, and the Ysse's four fords under snowmelt ("Too deep for a child. Too fast for an old man." is word for word with ch 7) all match ch 7. Yeva matches ch 38: forty, the scar across both lips, Tova's clan, felt, short hair, "They're careful with calves." The six as seen at dawn on day 9 match ch 7's dusk-18 descriptions (six places in the grey one's back, Kessmoor's chain on the Furnace, the horned one's light, the Bell's fallen dome).
- Ushra as the roc over the lion's fire ("It is looking at me", Book 2 ch 13) and the roc that looked at Lisska (ch 17). Yeva among the elk-riders whose bows came down at Aun's "one word" (ch 15), with four hundred people at the Stones.
- Aun's death and Tova put in her place: Aun was the horse clans' elder (Book 2 ch 15), Tova is the horse clans' elder in Book 3, and Aun doesn't appear in Book 3.
- Distances: the clans' route from the high pastures is about nine to eleven days at four leagues a day to the Ysse, then "eighty leagues of nothing" to the Wend (World.md). "A hundred leagues" is the drained Wyldmarch (Book 2 ch 27; the rider in Book 3 ch 4).
- Must not reveal: the elders, the Lit Shore and the south-west light aren't touched. "Grey Mother" is used only as the old saying.

**Book-side issues:**
- Book 3 ch 38's notes say the four roc-riders were "sent down the coast from the Worldsend by Elder Tova" on day 143, but the prose has them come "out of the east. Along the coast", and Tova is at the Wend camp by then. The tale has every wild roc leave the Worldsend on day 5 and Ushra with nowhere high to go. This is not a conflict inside the tale's days 0–18, but the author may want ch 38's notes to say where the rocs came from.
- Book 3 ch 7's notes ("a day east of the Ysse") and prose (two days' walk, crossing on day 20) differ slightly.

**Verdict:** fixed
