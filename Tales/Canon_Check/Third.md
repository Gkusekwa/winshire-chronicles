**Checked:** read Book 1 ch 6 and ch 8 in full (with Ford Water); the Ragnald, Sigrun and Aurveth passages of Book 1 chs 18, 20, 26, 28, 48, 60, 62, 70; Book 2 ch 21 (the choosing night, the Choosing Ledge, Tor Brecc); Book 3 ch 18 (Arn on the Choosing Ledge, the yearlings) and ch 22 (Sigrun's rock). Grepped all three books for Sigrun, Ragnald, Hallam, Morrow, Ase, Oda Leck, the hives, the pyre for two, "of age", choosing, "old girl", and "didn't count".

**Fixed:**
1. Ase at the longhouse door: "The priest's had the second half of it standing up there a year." → "two years." Book 1 ch 28 (notes and prose): Aurveth "lay a year on Coldtarn's shore and another in her cave", and Sigrun came "the second spring after he died". The tale's own Decided here says the empty half is "still standing... two years on".
2. Notes block: added "Canon-checked against the books: 29 Oct 2026." No Decided-here line changed.

**Defensible:**
- Ages: Ragnald dead 61 years before the mill, Sigrun chosen about two years after at 17 and 76 at the mill, so 15 when he died. Book 1 ch 8 has her remember him "a little, from when I was a girl", and ch 28 "She was a girl when he died". The tale gives her a fuller memory at fifteen, but the books' lines are an old woman's understatement, not a count.
- Ragnald's death: Book 1 ch 26, "I felt Ragnald die from the other side of the mountain. I was asleep. I woke screaming." The tale has her asleep in the cave and him over the shoulder of the hill on the south slope (ch 28: "forty hives on the south slope above the hold"). Close enough.
- The waiting: Book 1 ch 8, "They wait a few days, and then they burn them both on the same pyre." The tale's seven days is "a few days", and the pyre built for two follows ch 28.
- Where she lay: ch 8 has "She came up here, to this rock, and she lay down on the shore with her head in the water". The tale has her on the shore all year and on the rim above it on the first flight, with her head down over the water. Both fit the bowl.
- Kin-speech: Morrow and Aurveth "if they can see each other" agrees with World.md and Book 1 ch 60. Mind-speech is only between Sigrun and Aurveth after the bond. The smoke gift and the bronze mark agree with Magic.md and World.md.
- The choosing (a line on the Choosing Ledge from the fifteenth summer, yearlings and two-year-olds, the young dragon stopping and staying) matches Book 2 ch 21 and Book 3 ch 18. The tale leaves out the pens, as its Must not reveal says to, and has the young come "off the lower ledges".
- Hallam, 21 and bonded at 16 to a young Morrow ("her"), and Sigrun's summer nights on their ledge, fit Book 1 ch 48 ("We knew Sigrun. Sixty years. She sat on our ledge on summer nights").
- Must not reveal holds: the first partner is unnamed and faceless, the "hard place" is felt and not explained, nothing of what she waits for, no Council, no Titans.

**Book-side issues:**
- Book 3 ch 5 (Gorm's ledger): Aurveth "Bred on the high ledges. In Rurik's grandfather's time". This clashes with Book 1 ch 8 and ch 26 (on the Roosts "longer than anyone could remember"; her first partner "before the Clan was the Clan"), with World.md ("older than the Clan and wasn't bred by it") and with this tale's oldest story. It could stand as Gorm's books being wrong; the author may want to say so. (Also in The Covered Chair's report.)

**Verdict:** fixed (1 fix).
