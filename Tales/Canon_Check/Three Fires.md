# Canon check: Three Fires

**Checked:** Book 1 chs 4, 6, 10, 12, 16, 18, 20, 22, 26, 28, 40, 48, 56, 68 (18, 20 and 16 in full); Book 2 chs 17, 30, 35 (old moon); Book 3 ch 5, 18; World.md (Settled in drafting the Tales: Imra and Lisska, the Clan's dead), Characters.md, Bestiary.md, Continuity_Sweep. Grepped all three books and the Bible for Lisska, Imra, Kharrak, Dag, Aurveth, Sigrun, Teo, the stable roof, the hedge/bolt, Coldtarn, and the days 4–9.

**Fixed:**
1. Prose, "On the fourth evening a boy came up the Way" → "On the fifth evening". Lisska goes to the roof the night of the ford (day 4; Book 1 ch 16: Imra at the pyre on day 5, "She's been on that roof since last night"); the pyre is the tale's "second evening" (day 5, ch 16 notes); the mill is the night of day 8 (ch 18 notes: "this is the same night, day 8"). The fourth evening was day 7. The tale's own "four days" lines already fit day 8.
2. Notes, Decided here: "Days 5 to 8 Lisska keeps to the long stable roof" → "From the night of day 4 to day 8 …" (same source: ch 16 "since last night").

**Defensible:**
- Moon and light: "No moon", sharp stars, frost coming: matches ch 18 ("no moon, and the stars very sharp") and Book 2 ch 35's "old moon" (a thin waning crescent, not up at that hour).
- The battle's order, the deaths, the wounds, the bond lines and ch 20's dialogue all agree with chs 18 and 20 word for word where quoted. Lisska's "Stay" when they try to lift him is her own share of the chapter's *no, don't, stay*.
- Lisska "heard" Imra's "She'll stop" at the pyre: she gets speech only through Imra, who gives her everything "a little behind"; read as relayed.
- "The black water… never freezes because it's too deep": Lisska's folk reason; Book 3 ch 54 gives the true one later.
- "Not in all the years on the ledges": no year count, so clear of the nine/twelve-year question (World.md settles about twelve).

**Book-side issues:** none new. (The bond-length tension Book 1 ch 10 "nine years" vs Book 3 ch 5 is already settled in World.md.)

**Verdict:** fixed (2 changes).
