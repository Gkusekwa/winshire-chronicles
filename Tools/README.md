# Tools

Scripts that build the maps, the chapter-opener art and the audiobooks. Edit their data files and rebuild; don't edit the outputs by hand.

- **atlas/**: every map and `Bible/Atlas.md`, which `build.py` generates. `world.py` holds the geography and places; `marches_items.py` says what appears on which chapter-end map; `plan_data.py` holds Book 1's chapter numbers, POVs and titles. Run `python3 build.py` (needs `cairosvg`, `numpy` and the IM Fell English fonts). See `atlas/README.md`.
- **art/**: the abstract chapter openers, one per chapter, with no titles. `winshire_art.py` is the brush library and the first 41 compositions; `chapter_openers.py` lays out all 79 for Book 1 (odd chapters build on one circle, even chapters on three). Run `python3 art/chapter_openers.py "Book 1/Art/Chapter Openers/svg"` from the repo root, then convert to PNG. `book2_openers.py` (88) and `book3_openers.py` (86) do the same for Books 2 and 3; each book's `Art/Chapter Openers/NOTES.md` lists the POV signs and the spoilers kept out. `covers.py` draws the cover art for the three editions, and `cover_layouts/` holds the cover layouts and `render.js`, which renders the finished covers (see `Covers/README.md`).
- **ebook/**: `kindle_compress.py` makes the Kindle copy of an EPUB for upload: maps become 4-grey PNGs, chapter openers too, and other images JPEGs, so a 24 MB EPUB comes down to about 9 MB and KDP's per-megabyte delivery fee stays low. `python3 Tools/ebook/kindle_compress.py "Book 1"` reads every edition's EPUB in `Book 1/{Bone,Night,Band}/` and writes the copies to `Book 1/Kindle/`; the full-size files are left as they are.
- **audiobook-kit/**: the audiobook kit, for any book: it makes each book's annotations, lexicon and reading scripts from the chapters, and narrates them with Chatterbox, with checks, from a menu (`bash Tools/audiobook-kit/audiobook.sh --book "Book 2"`). Each book's files are in `Book N/audiobook/`. See `audiobook-kit/README.md`.

The chapter plans (`Book 1/Chapter_Plan.md`, `Book 2/Chapter_Plan.md`, `Book 3/Chapter_Plan.md`) are maintained by hand. The old plan generator was retired once Book 1 was drafted: it no longer matched the plan, and it would have overwritten the drafted chapter files.

Book 1's maps and `Bible/Atlas.md` were rebuilt from the updated data in October 2026. Books 2 and 3 have their chapter-opener art and chapter-end maps (Oct 2026). Their map entries are in `atlas/book2_items.py` and `atlas/book3_items.py`; build them with `python3 atlas/build.py --book 2 --out DIR` (likewise 3), then copy `svg/`, `png/` and `contact_sheet.png` into `Book 2/Art/Maps/Chapter endings/` and `front_matter/` into `Front matter/`. Plain `build.py` still builds Book 1 exactly as before. Each Book 2 and 3 plan row already names where its chapter-end map should centre (the **Ends at** column).

In November 2026 the map builder learned to keep names apart: it measures each name with the real fonts, tries spots farther out (joined to their place by a thin line) when nothing near fits, and treats the names drawn outside the placer (region names, the catacombs) as names. All three books' chapter-end maps were rebuilt with it and have no overlapping names.
